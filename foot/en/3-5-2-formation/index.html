The 3-5-2 formation: wing-backs and 3-5-2 vs 4-3-3

In short

The 3-5-2 uses three centre-backs, five midfielders (including two wing-backs on the flanks) and two strikers. Against a 4-3-3 it gives you a strong central midfield and two wide players, but the wing-backs must be very fit.

How the 3-5-2 lines up

Three centre-backs defend the middle. In front of them, five players fill the midfield: three through the centre and two on the flanks, called wing-backs. Two strikers play up front. The system is therefore both a back three and a five-man midfield.

The job of the wing-backs

They are the key players of this shape. According to the Premier League, their role depends on how high they play: when they push up they act almost like wingers and stretch the opposition defence; when they stay deeper they help protect the back line. In modern teams they press first when the ball is on their side and recover quickly when the play switches. They cover more ground than anyone else, so they need to be quick, good crossers and strong one-on-one.

What the other lines do

  • The three centre-backs. The Premier League notes that with three at the back a team can outnumber the first line of the opposition press, and that the centre-backs can slide out to cover the flanks.
  • The three central midfielders. They give passing options, control the middle and cover the space left by the wing-backs.
  • The two strikers. One holds the ball up, the other comes to collect it or attacks the space in behind.

An older version of the 3-5-2, recalled by the French encyclopaedia Universalis, had a sweeper behind two markers. Today teams tend to use three comparable centre-backs.

Strengths and weaknesses

  • Strengths: a numerical advantage when building up against a press of two or three, a strong presence through the middle, two strikers who occupy the opposition centre-backs, and the option of dropping into a back five.
  • Weaknesses: its solidity depends on the quality of the midfield work and the speed of transitions. If the wing-backs stay deep, the opposition can receive the ball in front of the back five. The wing-backs also have to put in a huge amount of running.

Who plays a 3-5-2?

According to the Premier League, Italian coach Antonio Conte is one of its best-known followers, with Juventus and Inter. Max Allegri's Juventus later switched to a diamond from 2014, according to the same outlet. Simone Inzaghi's Inter also used the 3-5-2 in the Champions League final against Manchester City.

3-5-2 vs 4-3-3: the matchup

Here is how the two shapes compare, line by line. It is an analysis based on structure, not a guaranteed result.

Area3-5-24-3-3
Attack2 strikers against 2 opposition centre-backs.3 forwards, including 2 wingers, against a back four.
Midfield3 central midfielders + 2 wing-backs.3 central midfielders.
FlanksWing-backs against opposition wingers and full-backs: constant duels.Wingers and full-backs against the wing-backs.
Defence3 centre-backs, sometimes 5 in a low block.2 centre-backs and 2 full-backs.

In the middle, the number of central players is the same, so the duel is decided on the flanks. If your wing-backs win their battles, you force the opposition wingers to defend and the 4-3-3 loses its press. If your wing-backs drop deep, the opposition wingers and full-backs can pin you back in a five and make you defend for long spells. The wing-back is the key.

When should an amateur team use it?

The 3-5-2 makes sense if you have two very fit wide players, three defenders you can trust on the ball, and two strikers who complement each other. It is less suitable if your wide players do not have the fitness to repeat the runs for 90 minutes. To compare your options, draw both lineups with the lineup maker.

How to beat a 3-5-2

These ideas come from the structure of the shape. They are not guarantees.

  • Attack the wing-backs: a quick winger and an overlapping full-back test them, especially after a switch of play.
  • Play between the wing-back and the wide centre-back: that is where space opens up when the wing-backs have pushed on.
  • Press their build-up with an extra forward: their numerical advantage at the back is then cancelled out.
  • A 4-2-3-1 can use its number 10 to draw a midfielder out and create an overload in front of their defence.

Frequently asked questions

What does 3-5-2 mean in football?

Three centre-backs, five midfielders (three through the middle and two wing-backs on the flanks) and two strikers.

What is a wing-back?

A wide player who has to defend his flank and attack by crossing. You find them in back-three shapes such as the 3-5-2.

Is the 3-5-2 good against a 4-3-3?

It depends on the wing-backs. The midfield is level, so the duel is played out on the flanks: if your wing-backs beat the opposition wingers, the formation works well.

Does the 3-5-2 turn into a back five?

Yes, when the two wing-backs drop back to line up alongside the three centre-backs.
