Convert WEBP to JPG

WEBP images saved from a website won't open? Convert them to JPG.

  • No file upload
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up
  • No watermark

Your files never leave your device: the conversion is done by your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no account, no watermark and no daily limit.

How to do it

  1. Add your WEBP files.
  2. Keep JPG (or choose PNG to keep transparency).
  3. Click "Convert" and download.

Why are so many images WEBP?

Most websites now serve their images as WEBP, a Google format that is lighter than JPG. When you save them, you get a .webp file that some programs, print shops and forms still refuse. JPG opens everywhere. Animated WEBP files are converted to a still image (the first frame).

Good to know

  • JPG is the most compatible format (websites, email, printing). It does not support transparency, which is replaced by white.
  • PNG is lossless and keeps transparency: ideal for logos, screenshots and images with text.
  • WEBP is the smallest. All recent browsers read it, but some older programs do not.
  • Hidden photo information (GPS location, phone model, date) is not copied into the converted file, which is handy before you share a photo.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The page loads a small program into your browser, and that program does the conversion on your phone or computer. Your files are never uploaded or stored: we cannot see them. You can check it in the "Network" tab of your browser's developer tools.

Are transparent WEBP files handled?

In JPG, transparency turns white. Choose PNG to keep it.

What about animated WEBP?

Only the first frame is converted: JPG does not support animation.
