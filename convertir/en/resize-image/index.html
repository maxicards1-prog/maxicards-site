Resize an Image Online

Change the size of your images in pixels or by percentage, one or several at a time.

  • No file upload
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up
  • No watermark

Your files never leave your device: the conversion is done by your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no account, no watermark and no daily limit.

How to do it

  1. Add your images.
  2. Choose "By pixels" and type the width you want (the height is worked out for you), or "By percentage" (50% = half).
  3. For an exact frame (1080 x 1080, 600 x 600...), fill in width and height: the image fits inside the frame without distortion, or is stretched if you choose that.
  4. Click "Resize" and download.

Common sizes

  • Square Instagram post: 1080 x 1080 px; portrait 1080 x 1350 px.
  • YouTube thumbnail: 1280 x 720 px.
  • Image for a website or blog: 1200 to 1920 px wide.
  • Profile picture: 400 x 400 px is enough almost everywhere.

The name of the resulting file shows its new dimensions (photo-1080x810.jpg). Enlarging an image beyond its original size does not create detail: it just gets blurrier. To make an image lighter without changing its size much, see Compress Image.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The page loads a small program into your browser, and that program does the conversion on your phone or computer. Your files are never uploaded or stored: we cannot see them. You can check it in the "Network" tab of your browser's developer tools.

How do I keep the proportions?

Fill in only the width (or only the height): the other side is calculated so the image is not distorted.

How do I get exactly 1080 x 1080?

Fill in both and choose &quot;Stretch to exact size&quot;. If the photo is not square, it will be distorted: it is better to crop it first.

Which format is the image saved in?

In its original format (JPG, PNG or WEBP); HEIC images are saved as JPG.
