Convert SVG to PNG

Turn an SVG logo or icon into a sharp PNG image, at the size you choose, with transparent background kept.

  • No file upload
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up
  • No watermark

Your files never leave your device: the conversion is done by your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no account, no watermark and no daily limit.

How to do it

  1. Add one or more SVG files.
  2. Choose the size: 2 × the original for a sharp result on high-density screens, or a fixed width (512, 1024, 2048 px).
  3. Choose PNG (transparency kept) or JPG (white background), then click “Convert”.

Why convert an SVG?

SVG is a vector format: the image is described by shapes, and stays sharp at any size. That is perfect for a logo on a website, but many places do not accept it: social networks, messaging apps, Word or PowerPoint depending on the version, print shops, online forms. PNG, on the other hand, is accepted everywhere.

Choosing the right size

Since SVG has no fixed resolution, you choose it. For a profile picture or a thumbnail, 512 px is enough. For printing or a presentation, take 2048 px. For more guidance, see what resolution to use for printing an image and which image format to choose.

Limits

  • An SVG that fetches fonts or images from the internet will not display them: for safety, the browser loads nothing external for an image. Text may then appear in another font.
  • SVG animations are not kept: you get a still image.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The page loads a small program into your browser, and that program does the conversion on your phone or computer. Your files are never uploaded or stored: we cannot see them. You can check it in the "Network" tab of your browser's developer tools.

Does the PNG keep transparency?

Yes, if you choose the “Transparent” background. JPG always has a white background.

My SVG has no size, what happens?

The tool uses the dimensions of its display area (viewBox). If it has none, it starts from 300 × 150 px, the default size in browsers.

Can I do the reverse, PNG to SVG?

No: turning an image into vector shapes takes tracing software, and the result is rarely faithful.
