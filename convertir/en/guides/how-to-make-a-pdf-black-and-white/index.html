How to Make a PDF Black and White

To print, choosing “Black and white” in the print window is often enough.

The free tool, no file upload:
PDF to JPGBlack and whiteJPG to PDF

To get a black-and-white file, turn the pages into gray images, then rebuild the PDF.

Updated November 23, 2026

You only want to print in black and white

No need to change the file. In the print window (Ctrl + P or Cmd + P), look for the “Color” or “Color mode” option and choose “Black and white” or “Grayscale”. Its name and location depend on the printer; it is sometimes under “Preferences” or “Properties”. It is the simplest way to save color ink.

You want a black-and-white PDF file

With our tools, in three passes, without uploading anything:

  1. PDF to JPG: each page becomes an image.
  2. Black and white image: choose “Grayscale” for a document with photos, or “Pure black and white” for high-contrast text.
  3. JPG to PDF: add all the images in order and rebuild the PDF.

One limit: the resulting PDF is made of images. The text can no longer be selected or copied. Keep the original for that.

On Mac, with Preview

Open the PDF in Preview, choose File > Export, PDF format, then a gray or black-and-white option under “Quartz Filter”. Save under a new name. The result depends on the document: check it before sending.

Grayscale or pure black and white?

  • Grayscale: every color becomes a shade of gray. Photos and charts stay readable.
  • Pure black and white: two colors only. Ideal for a text document photographed with a phone: the gray background turns white and the text stands out. Adjust the threshold if letters fade.

What about file size?

A pure black-and-white PDF is often lighter. If that is the goal, try Compress PDF first, which is more direct. For a document scanned with a phone, also read scan documents with your phone: many apps offer a black-and-white mode right at capture.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a PDF black and white for free?

Convert the pages to images (PDF to JPG), turn them black and white, then rebuild the PDF (JPG to PDF). All free and done in the browser.

How do I print a PDF in black and white?

In the print window, choose “Black and white” or “Grayscale” in the printer's color options.

How do I make a PDF black and white on Mac?

In Preview: File > Export, then choose a gray or black-and-white Quartz filter.

Why is the text no longer selectable?

Because the pages went through images. Keep the original PDF if you need to copy text.
