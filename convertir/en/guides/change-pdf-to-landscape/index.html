How to Change a PDF to Landscape

If the pages are simply lying on their side, rotate them 90 degrees.

How to do it

  1. Identify your case. Is the content lying on its side (you need to rotate), or is the page simply too narrow for the content (you need to change the layout)?
  2. Rotate the pages concerned. Open the rotate PDF tool, choose 90 degrees in the right direction, on all pages or only some.
  3. Download the new PDF. The file is created in your browser; the original is not changed.
  4. Check the reading direction. Open the PDF: the text should read normally, without tilting your head or your screen.
  5. Otherwise, change the source. For a true landscape layout, go back to the original document (Word, spreadsheet) and export to PDF again.

Two very different situations

When people say "make it landscape", they often mix up two things. Telling them apart saves time.

Case 1: the content is sideways

You scanned or photographed a document the wrong way, or a wide table was placed on a portrait page by turning the content. The page itself is right, but the display is on its side. You simply need to rotate the pages by 90 degrees. It is instant and lossless, because the PDF stays the same; only the display orientation changes.

Case 2: the layout must change

You want the content to fill a page that is wider than it is tall, for example for a table that overflows. Rotating will only lay the portrait page on its side: the content will stay just as narrow. You need to go back to the original application and change the page orientation: in Word it is a page layout setting (Orientation), in LibreOffice and Google Docs an equivalent page setup option, and then you must export the PDF again. Exact labels vary by version: look for "Orientation" in the page layout options.

Rotating only some pages

A document sometimes mixes portrait and landscape pages. The tool lets you choose which pages to turn. If you have just one off-kilter page, you can also extract it, rotate it, then put it back in place with page organization.

Orientation when printing

If your goal is to print in landscape, the print dialog generally offers an orientation choice, which does not modify the file. If the PDF is already landscape but prints badly, check that setting.

Common pitfalls

  • Rotating 180 degrees instead of 90: the page ends up upside down. Check before saving.
  • Pages of different sizes: a merged PDF can mix formats and orientations. See merge PDFs to prepare your files properly before assembling them.
  • Scan still too heavy after rotating: rotation does not change the file size. Use compression if needed, knowing the text will then no longer be selectable.

On a phone

Turning the phone rotates the display, not the file. To change the PDF itself, use the rotation tool in the browser, which also works on mobile.

Privacy

Rotation happens in the browser: the document is not uploaded to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Does rotating a PDF change its size?

No, only the display orientation of the pages changes. The file size stays roughly the same.

Can I make just one page landscape?

Yes: choose the page or pages to rotate rather than the whole document.

Why is my table still narrow after rotating?

Because the page stayed portrait. You need to change the orientation in the original document and export the PDF again.

Does rotation hurt quality?

No, it is only a change of page orientation.
