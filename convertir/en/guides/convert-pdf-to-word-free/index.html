How to Convert a PDF to Word for Free

This site does not convert PDFs to Word.

But Word itself opens PDFs, and so do Google Docs and LibreOffice: at no cost, with some caveats about layout.

Updated November 23, 2026

An honest note first

Our site does not produce Word, Excel or PowerPoint files. Rather than promise a conversion we do not offer, here are the free solutions that already exist on your device or in your account.

Solution 1: Word

In Word, go to File > Open and choose the PDF. The program announces that it will convert the document into an editable file. Microsoft says the text then becomes fully editable and that you can save the result as Word or as PDF again. The outcome depends on the PDF: formatting may not be faithful and some elements may be missing. Check every page.

Solution 2: Google Docs

Put the PDF in Google Drive, right-click it and choose "Open with Google Docs". The text becomes editable in your browser. It is free with a Google account, but complex layouts (tables, columns, rare fonts) are often disturbed. You can then download the document as a Word file.

Solution 3: LibreOffice

LibreOffice is a free office suite. It opens PDFs in its drawing module (Draw), which lets you edit text blocks one by one. It is useful for fixing a word or a date, less so for rewriting a long document.

The case of scanned PDFs

A PDF made from a scan is an image: there is no text to convert until character recognition (OCR) has been applied. This site does not do OCR. Some free OCR tools exist elsewhere, with varying results. If all you need is to get the text out of a digital PDF, try PDF to text: it extracts the content of digital PDFs, not scans.

If you only want part of the document

No need to convert everything: extract the pages you need, or split the PDF, then open only that part in Word. The result is faster and easier to fix.

Why conversion is imperfect

A PDF describes where each word sits on the page, not the structure of the text in paragraphs. Software therefore has to guess the columns, tables and headings. The more complex the document, the more you need to touch up. For a contract or a heavily designed resume, expect some retouching. If you still have the original Word file, reopen it instead of converting.

The other way around

For the reverse operation, which is far more reliable, read how to convert a Word document to PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Can Word really open a PDF?

Yes: Word offers to convert it into an editable document. The formatting may differ from the original, though.

Does this site convert PDFs to Word?

No. It does not produce Word, Excel or PowerPoint files.

Why is the layout broken after conversion?

A PDF does not contain a structure of paragraphs or tables: the software has to guess it, and mistakes are possible.

How do I convert a scanned PDF?

You need text recognition (OCR), which this site does not offer. Look for a dedicated OCR tool or a function built into your scanner.
