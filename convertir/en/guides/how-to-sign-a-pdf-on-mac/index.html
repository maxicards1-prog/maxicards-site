How to Sign a PDF on Mac

Open the PDF in Preview, show the Markup toolbar, click the Sign button and choose or create your signature.

How to do it

  1. Open the PDF in Preview. Double-click the PDF (Preview is the default app) or right-click > Open With > Preview.
  2. Show the Markup toolbar. Click the pen-tip icon at the top of the window.
  3. Create your signature. Click the Sign button, then create one: with the trackpad, the Mac's camera, or your iPhone depending on your macOS version.
  4. Place it and save. Click the signature to add it, move and resize it, then save (Cmd + S).

Three ways to create your signature

  • Trackpad: sign with your finger on the trackpad, then press a key when done. It looks like a finger-drawn signature.
  • Camera: sign in black ink on white paper and hold it up to the camera. Preview cuts the signature out: the most faithful method.
  • iPhone or iPad: on recent macOS versions, you sign on the phone's screen and the signature appears on the Mac.

Preview remembers your signatures: next time, just click the Sign button and pick the right one.

Keep the original

Preview saves straight into the open file. To keep an unsigned version, first choose File > Duplicate, or hold the Option key and choose File > Save As.

Without Preview: in the browser

On a work Mac where you would rather not change anything, or if you sign with a mouse, our Sign PDF tool works in Safari, Chrome or Firefox: draw the signature, click where it goes, download. The file is not sent anywhere.

Before sending

Reopen the signed PDF to check the page and position. If it is too big for email, shrink it with Compress PDF. To learn what a drawn signature is worth legally, read how to sign a PDF for free. On iPhone the method is different: how to sign a PDF on iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign a PDF on Mac without Adobe?

With Preview, the app that comes with every Mac: Markup toolbar, Sign button. Adobe Acrobat is not needed.

How do I make an electronic signature on Mac?

Create it once in Preview (trackpad, camera or iPhone): it stays saved and drops onto any PDF in one click.

Why is my camera signature blurry?

Sign with a black felt-tip pen, thicker than a ballpoint, on bright white paper in good light, and hold it straight in front of the camera.

Can I sign several pages?

Yes: go to each page and click your signature again from the Sign button.
