What Is the Maximum Email Attachment Size, and What Then?

Gmail and Outlook.com cap attachments at 25 MB per message.

Beyond that, you need to shrink the file, split it or send it as a link.

The official limits we were able to verify

Email serviceAttachment limitBeyond the limit
Gmail25 MBGoogle suggests using Google Drive
Outlook.com25 MBShare a link (OneDrive or another service)

For other email services (internet providers, work or school mailboxes), we do not give a number: it varies by provider and plan, and it can change. Check your provider's official help or test with a file first.

Two common traps

  • The limit applies to the whole message. Ten files of 3 MB count as 30 MB, which is too much.
  • The recipient has a limit of their own. You can send 20 MB without an error and still see the message bounce on their side. If it matters, aim well below 25 MB.

What to do when the file is too big

Make the file lighter

This is the first thing to try. For a PDF, use PDF compression: it turns pages into lighter images, which cuts the size sharply but makes the text non-selectable. For photos, image compression or resizing is often enough. Everything happens in your browser, with no file upload.

Split it into several emails

A long PDF can be separated with the split tool and sent in several messages. It works, but the recipient receives several files to piece together, so say so in your message.

Send a link instead of a file

Put the file in online storage (Google Drive, OneDrive or another service) and share the link. This is the right solution for videos and big folders. Check the link's access rights before sending: limit it to the people concerned if the content is personal.

What not to do

Do not change a file's extension to get around a block: some email services block executable formats, and it does not reduce the size anyway. And do not send the same message over and over if it seems stuck: you could flood the recipient's inbox.

Frequently asked questions

Does Gmail accept more than 25 MB?

Not as a direct attachment. Above 25 MB, Google offers to attach the file from Google Drive.

Is the 25 MB limit per file or for the whole message?

For all the attachments of the message together: add up the sizes before sending.

Why is my email rejected when I am under the limit?

The recipient's mail system may have a lower limit, and message encoding adds a little weight. Shrink the file further or send a link.

What is the limit for other email providers?

It varies by plan and can change: check your provider's official help rather than a number found on a forum.
