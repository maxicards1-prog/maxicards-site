Favicon size: dimensions, formats and HTML tags

Start from a square image of at least 512 px, then produce an ICO (16, 32, 48 px), a 180 px PNG for Apple and 192 and 512 px PNGs for the manifest.

Updated November 23, 2026

The basic rules

According to Google's documentation on favicons in search results, the favicon must be square (1:1 ratio) and at least 8 x 8 px, but Google recommends going above 48 x 48 px. The listed formats are BMP, GIF, ICO, PNG, JPEG, PPM and TIFF. SVG does not appear in this list: if the favicon must show in Google search, keep an ICO or a PNG.

Which sizes should you make?

SizeUse
16 x 16 and 32 x 32 pxBrowser tab and bookmarks (usual sizes, to put in an ICO file)
48 x 48 pxThreshold to exceed according to Google for search
180 x 180 pxHome screen icon on Apple devices (apple-touch-icon)
192 x 192 and 512 x 512 pxWeb app manifest icons; web.dev says these two sizes are enough in most cases

Start from a square image of 512 px or more, simple and readable even when very small: avoid small text and fine details.

ICO, PNG or SVG?

  • ICO: a single file that can hold several sizes. To create yours from an image, use PNG to ICO.
  • PNG: suited to Apple icons and the manifest. If you start from a vector drawing, SVG to PNG produces it at the size you want; resizing gives you the other dimensions.
  • SVG: sharp at every size. web.dev notes that Chromium-based browsers accept it for app icons, with a raster version as a fallback. Do not rely on it alone.

The HTML tags

Place them in the head section of your home page. Example with a classic ICO: <link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" sizes="48x48">. For an Apple icon: <link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">. If you add an SVG: <link rel="icon" href="/icon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">. Google accepts the values icon (recommended), shortcut icon, apple-touch-icon and apple-touch-icon-precomposed.

Pitfalls

  • The favicon must stay accessible: do not block it in robots.txt, and keep a stable URL.
  • Google states that display is never guaranteed, and that a change can take several days to several weeks to be picked up.
  • Your browser often keeps the old favicon in its cache: do a hard refresh to check.

Official sources: favicons in Google Search and manifest icons on web.dev.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum size of a favicon?

Google gives 8 x 8 px as the minimum, but recommends a size larger than 48 x 48 px.

Does a favicon have to be square?

Yes, Google asks for a 1:1 ratio.

Can I use an SVG as a favicon?

Recent browsers handle it, but Google's favicon page does not list it among the supported formats. Provide an ICO or a PNG.

Why doesn't my new favicon show up?

The browser cache often keeps the old one, and Google can take several days to several weeks to refresh its own.
