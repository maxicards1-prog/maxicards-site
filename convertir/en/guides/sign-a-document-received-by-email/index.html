How to Sign a Document Received by Email

Save the attachment, sign it with our Sign PDF tool, then reply to the email with the signed file attached.

The free tool, no file upload:
Sign PDFCompress PDFJPG to PDF

No printer, no scanner.

Updated November 23, 2026

How to do it

  1. Save the attachment. Computer: click the attachment, then Download. Phone: open it, then Share > Save to Files (iPhone) or Download (Android).
  2. Sign the document. Open the Sign PDF page, choose the file, draw your signature and tap where it should go.
  3. Download the signed PDF. The signed file keeps the original name, with a suffix so you can tell them apart.
  4. Reply to the email. Click Reply, attach the signed PDF (not the original!) and send.

The document is not a PDF?

  • A Word file: open it in Word, LibreOffice or Google Docs and export it as PDF (our guide convert Word to PDF), then sign the PDF.
  • A photo or scan in JPG: turn it into a PDF first with JPG to PDF, then sign.

You also need to fill in fields

If the document has checkboxes or text fields (name, date, “read and approved”), fill them in first in your PDF reader: Preview on Mac, Files on iPhone, Adobe Acrobat Reader or the browser on PC. Save, then sign the filled-in file.

Several pages to initial

Some contracts ask for initials at the bottom of each page. Our tool places one signature per pass: sign the first page, download, then reopen the resulting file for the next page. On iPhone and Mac, the Markup tool places the same signature on each page faster. Detailed methods: on iPhone, on Android, on Mac.

The email bounces because it is too large

A signed PDF usually stays small. If it goes over your email limit, use Compress PDF, or read email attachment size limits.

Your document stays with you

A contract or permission form often holds personal details. Here the PDF is signed in your browser: it is not uploaded to a server, unlike many signing websites.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign a document received by email without a printer?

Save the attachment, sign it with a PDF signing tool (our Sign PDF page, Preview on Mac, Markup on iPhone), then send the signed file back.

How do I sign an email document on my phone?

Open the attachment, save it, then open our Sign PDF page in the browser: you sign with your finger and the signed file is saved on the phone.

How can I sign a document without printing it?

By adding a drawn signature directly into the PDF, on a computer or a phone. The document never needs to be printed or scanned.

Is a simple electronic signature accepted?

For most everyday documents, yes. If the other party requires a qualified signature, they will usually send you a link to a signing service.
