How to Sign a PDF on iPhone

Open the PDF in the Files app, tap the Markup button (the pen tip), then “+” and “Add Signature”.

Updated November 23, 2026

How to do it

  1. Save the PDF to Files. Did the PDF arrive by email or message? Open it, tap the share button, then “Save to Files”.
  2. Open it and tap Markup. In Files, open the PDF and tap the pen-tip icon. Depending on your iOS version, Markup may also be in the share menu.
  3. Add your signature. Tap “+”, then “Add Signature”. Sign with your finger in the box and tap Done. The iPhone keeps it for next time.
  4. Place it and finish. Drag the signature where it belongs, resize it with the handles, then tap Done: the PDF is saved signed.

Second method: in Safari, no settings

If the Markup button does not show up, or if you want a method that also works on other devices, open our Sign PDF tool in Safari. Pick the PDF from Files, sign with your finger in the white box, then tap the preview where the signature should go. The signed PDF is saved to Files. The document stays on your iPhone: nothing is uploaded.

Signing a PDF you received in Mail

In the Mail app, tap the attachment to open it, then the Markup button. Once the signature is in place, the iPhone often offers to reply with the signed document attached. Handy for returning an order form or a permission slip in a minute. Our guide sign a document received by email and send it back covers this on every device.

Pitfalls to avoid

  • Signature too big: shrink it with the handles so it fits the signature box without covering text.
  • Wrong page: scroll to the signature page (often the last one) before tapping “+”.
  • File too large to send back: run it through Compress PDF.

Is this signature valid?

A signature drawn with your finger is a simple electronic signature. It is fine for most everyday paperwork. For a document that requires an advanced or qualified signature, you need a specialized provider: our guide how to sign a PDF for free explains the difference.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign a PDF on iPhone without an app?

With the Markup tool built into iOS, from the Files or Mail app: Markup button, “+”, “Add Signature”. Or in Safari with an online tool that works on the device.

How can I sign a PDF on iPhone for free?

Both methods on this page are free: Apple's Markup tool and our Sign PDF page in Safari.

Why can't I find Markup on my iPhone?

The file may not be a PDF, or it is open in an app that does not offer it. Save it to Files and open it from Files.

How do I change my saved signature?

In Markup, tap “+” then “Add Signature”: the signature list lets you delete one and create a new one.
