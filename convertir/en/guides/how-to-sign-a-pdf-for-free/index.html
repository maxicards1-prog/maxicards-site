How to Sign a PDF for Free

You can sign a PDF for free with Preview (Mac), the iPhone markup tool, Adobe Acrobat Reader or this site's signing page.

How to do it

  1. Open the PDF in the tool of your choice. On this site, drop the file on the signing page. On a Mac, open it in Preview. On an iPhone, open it from Files or Mail.
  2. Create your signature. Draw it with your finger or trackpad, or on a Mac with the camera or an iPhone, as Apple's help describes.
  3. Place it in the right spot. Move and resize the signature so it lands in the intended box without covering any text.
  4. Save the signed PDF. Download or save the file under a new name and keep the original intact.
  5. Check before sending. Reopen the signed document and make sure the signature is visible on the right page.

Free solutions, by device

On this site

The sign PDF page works in your browser: the document is not uploaded to a server, which is reassuring for a contract or a personal form. It works on every device.

On a Mac with Preview

Preview lets you add text and a signature to a PDF. According to Apple, you can capture your signature with the trackpad, the Mac's built-in camera, or an iPhone or iPad. Some PDFs do not support every feature: if the option does not appear, try another method.

On an iPhone

Open the PDF in the Files or Mail app, then use the markup tool (pencil icon) and add a signature. The exact labels vary with the iOS version: look for the "+" button or "Signature" in the markup toolbar.

On Windows and Android

Adobe Acrobat Reader lets you add a signature for free. If the reader offers paid features, plain signing is generally still available without a subscription, but check this in your version.

What is a "simple" signature worth?

The European eIDAS regulation distinguishes three levels: simple, advanced and qualified electronic signatures. A signature drawn on a PDF is at the simple level. The general principle is that an electronic signature cannot be denied legal effect as evidence solely because it is electronic. However, it is up to the person relying on it to show who signed and that the document was not changed afterward, which a simple image pasted onto a PDF proves poorly. Only a qualified signature is expressly treated as equivalent to a handwritten one. Rules differ from country to country (for example, the US has its own e-signature laws).

In practice: for a routine form, an authorization or an informal document, a simple signature is often enough. For an important contract, a lease or a deed for which the law or the other party requires a specific level, ask which type of signature is expected, or use a certified e-signature service. This guide is not legal advice.

After signing

If the signature is crooked, rotate the pages before signing. If the file becomes too heavy to send, see PDF compression: note that it turns pages into images, which makes no difference to a visible signature but would destroy a certified electronic signature.

Frequently asked questions

Is a signature drawn on a PDF legal?

It is a simple electronic signature, which can be accepted as evidence, but its weight depends on the context and on what you can demonstrate. It is not automatically equivalent to a handwritten signature.

What is the difference with a qualified signature?

A qualified signature is issued by a recognized provider and has a legal effect equivalent to a handwritten signature under eIDAS.

Is my document sent online when I sign here?

No, processing happens in your browser.

Can I sign a password-protected PDF?

This site does not unlock protected PDFs. If you have the right to do so, use the software that created the protection.
