How to Convert a Photo to PDF

Drop the photo (JPG, PNG or HEIC) on this site's tool: it creates a PDF in your browser, on any device.

How to do it

  1. Find your photo's format. Look at the extension: .jpg or .jpeg, .png or .heic (common on iPhone).
  2. Choose the matching tool. Use the JPG, PNG or HEIC to PDF converter, or simply drop the file on the site's home page.
  3. Add all your photos. For a multi-page document, add the images in the order you want.
  4. Start the conversion. The PDF is created in your browser, and your photos are not uploaded.
  5. Download and check. Open the PDF, check the orientation and readability, then send it.

Why convert a photo to PDF?

Many online forms and applications accept only PDF, or limit the number of files. A PDF gathers several pages into one file and opens the same way for everyone. It is handy for a photographed receipt, a prescription, an invoice or a form.

With this site

Depending on the photo's format: JPG to PDF, PNG to PDF or HEIC to PDF. If you are not sure, drop the file on the home page: the site suggests the right tools. No image is uploaded to a server; the conversion runs locally. A photo can weigh up to 80 MB.

HEIC is the default photo format on recent iPhones. If you would rather have a standard image, see HEIC to JPG.

With your device's own features

iPhone

The Photos app lets you print an image: by enlarging the print preview, you get a shareable PDF. Exact menus change with the iOS version, so if you cannot find the option, the site's tool is more reliable.

Android

Many phones offer "Print" then "Save as PDF" from the gallery or the share menu. Availability depends on the manufacturer.

Windows

Open the image, start printing and choose the "Microsoft Print to PDF" printer: it saves a PDF file instead of printing.

Mac

Open the image in Preview, then use File > Export as PDF, or Print and save as PDF.

Several photos in one PDF

Two methods. Either you add several images to the conversion, or you create one PDF per photo and then merge them. The second is useful if the photos come from several devices. For details, read merge PDFs without software.

Good habits

  • Shoot flat, under even light, to avoid shadows and distortion.
  • If the result is too heavy for a form, shrink the photos with image compression before converting.
  • A photo of text is not text: the resulting PDF is not selectable. This site does not offer OCR.

Frequently asked questions

Can I put several photos in the same PDF?

Yes: add them all in the tool, or create separate PDFs and merge them.

What is the best resolution for a PDF?

A sharp, readable photo is enough. There is no need to send 12 megapixels for a receipt: shrink it if the form limits file size.

Will the PDF contain selectable text?

No, it will be an image inside a PDF. The site does not do text recognition (OCR).

Are my photos sent anywhere?

No, the conversion happens in your browser.
