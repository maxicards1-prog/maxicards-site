JPG vs PNG vs WebP: which image format to choose?

For a photo, use JPG.

For a logo, a screenshot or a transparent image, use PNG. For a website, use WebP. For an icon at any size, use SVG. The table below covers each case.

A photo: JPG

JPG is designed for continuous-tone images, which means photos. Absolutely everything can read it. Its downside: it uses lossy compression, and repeated recompression damages the image. If your photo is a PNG and too large, PNG to JPG often shrinks it a lot.

A logo, a screenshot, a transparent image: PNG

PNG loses nothing and supports transparency: it is the right format for text, flat colors and sharp diagrams. Its files are heavier for a photo. To go the other way, use JPG to PNG; just know that an image already compressed as JPG will not get its original quality back.

A website: WebP

According to MDN, WebP is a good general-purpose option for the web: lighter than JPG and PNG at comparable quality, with transparency and animation. For your own images, JPG to WebP saves weight. Details in What is WebP?

iPhone photos: HEIC

This is the "High Efficiency" format of Apple devices. Great for saving space, less so for sharing. The guide What is HEIC? explains how to deal with it.

A resizable icon or logo: SVG

SVG describes shapes rather than pixels: it stays sharp at any size and weighs very little. It is not suited to photos. If a site demands a regular image, SVG to PNG produces one at the size you want.

GIF: keep it for simple cases

Limited to 256 colors, GIF is outdated for photos. MDN recommends PNG for still images, and WebP, AVIF or APNG for animations.

Quick decision rule

  • You send or publish a photo: JPG.
  • You have text, a logo or a transparent background: PNG.
  • You run a website: WebP, with SVG for icons.
  • You archive on an Apple device: HEIC, and convert when you share.

Frequently asked questions

Which format should I use to email photos?

JPG, because it opens everywhere and is relatively light. If needed, reduce the photo's dimensions before sending.

Why is my PNG so large?

PNG keeps all the information, which makes it heavy for a photo. Converting the photo to JPG usually shrinks it a lot.

Can I convert a JPG to SVG?

It is not a simple conversion: an SVG is a vector drawing, not a photo. Our site converts SVG to PNG, not the other way around.
