Extract Pages from a PDF

Pull one or more pages out of a PDF into a new file, without touching the original.

  • No file upload
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up
  • No watermark

Your files never leave your device: the conversion is done by your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no account, no watermark and no daily limit.

How to do it

  1. Choose the PDF.
  2. Type the pages to keep: 3 for one page, 2, 5-7 for several, 10- for “page 10 to the end”. They are kept in the order you write them.
  3. Choose “One PDF with these pages” or “One PDF per page”.
  4. Click “Extract” and download the result.

Extract, split or delete?

Three similar tools, three uses:

  • Extract (this page): you know which pages you want to keep, for example page 4 of a 30-page contract.
  • Split: you want to cut the whole document into several files (one page per file, or in batches).
  • Delete pages: you know which pages to remove, and you keep everything else.

An exact copy of the pages

Pages are copied without being recompressed: text stays selectable, images keep their quality and links keep working. The new file is often much lighter than the original, which helps for sending by email. You can also change the order by writing the pages in the order you want (5, 1, 2), or use Organize pages to do it with thumbnails.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The page loads a small program into your browser, and that program does the conversion on your phone or computer. Your files are never uploaded or stored: we cannot see them. You can check it in the "Network" tab of your browser's developer tools.

How do I extract a single page from a PDF?

Type its number in “Pages to extract”, for example 4, then click “Extract”.

Is the original modified?

No. A new PDF is created; your original file stays intact.

Can I extract pages from several PDFs at once?

One PDF at a time. To assemble pages from several PDFs, extract them, then use Merge PDF.
