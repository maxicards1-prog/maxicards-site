Add a Watermark to a PDF

Stamp every page with semi-transparent text: CONFIDENTIAL, COPY, DRAFT, or the intended use of a document.

  • No file upload
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up
  • No watermark

Your files never leave your device: the conversion is done by your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no account, no watermark and no daily limit.

How to do it

  1. Add your PDF.
  2. Type the watermark text, for example: “Document intended only for Agency X, rental application of January 12”.
  3. Choose the direction (diagonal or horizontal), the color and the opacity. Leave “Pages (blank = all)” empty to mark the whole document.
  4. Click “Add watermark” and download the PDF.

Protect a copy of an ID document

This is the most useful case: when you send a copy of an ID card, passport, pay stub or tax return for a rental, a registration or an application, add a watermark saying who the copy is for and why, with the date. That makes the copy much harder to reuse elsewhere without anyone noticing.

The settings

  • Opacity: 20 to 30% keeps the document readable; go up to 40-50% for a clearly visible mark.
  • Diagonal: the text crosses the whole page, which makes it hard to hide.
  • Pages: 1 to mark only the first page, 2-5 for a range.

The limits

The watermark is added on top of the page. Someone with a PDF editor could remove it. For stronger protection, run the PDF through Compress PDF afterward: the pages become images, and the watermark becomes part of the image. Emojis and non-Latin alphabets are not supported by the standard PDF font.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The page loads a small program into your browser, and that program does the conversion on your phone or computer. Your files are never uploaded or stored: we cannot see them. You can check it in the "Network" tab of your browser's developer tools.

What text should I put on a copy of an ID card?

The recipient, the purpose and the date, for example: “Copy for Agency X, rental application, 01/12/2027”. The copy then cannot be used elsewhere without it being obvious.

Can I use a logo as a watermark?

No, only text for now.

Can the watermark be removed?

With a PDF editor, yes. To blend it into the page, compress the PDF afterward: the pages become images.
