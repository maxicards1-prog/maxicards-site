Convert PNG to ICO

Create an .ico file that holds several icon sizes, for a website favicon or a Windows icon.

  • No file upload
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up
  • No watermark

Your files never leave your device: the conversion is done by your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no account, no watermark and no daily limit.

How to do it

  1. Add your image: a square PNG with a transparent background gives the best result (512 × 512 px ideally).
  2. Choose the sizes: “favicon” (16, 32, 48 px) for a website, or “Windows icon” (16 to 256 px).
  3. If the image is not square, choose to fit the whole image (transparent edges) or to fill the square.
  4. Click “Create ICO file” and download it.

One file, several sizes

An ICO file is not a simple image: it stores several versions of the same icon, each at its own size. The browser or Windows picks the right one: 16 px in a tab, 32 px in the taskbar, 48 px on the desktop. The tool creates each size separately from your image, with quality smoothing.

For a favicon

Put the favicon.ico file at the root of your site and add this line in the <head>: <link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" sizes="32x32">. For phone home screens, you also need PNG files at other sizes: our guide what size should a favicon be gives the full list. To prepare those PNG files, use Resize an image.

Good to know

  • At 16 px, details disappear: a simple logo with little text stays much more readable.
  • The images inside the ICO file use the PNG format, supported by Windows since Vista and by all current browsers.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The page loads a small program into your browser, and that program does the conversion on your phone or computer. Your files are never uploaded or stored: we cannot see them. You can check it in the "Network" tab of your browser's developer tools.

Which starting image should I choose?

A square PNG, at least 256 × 256 px, with a transparent background and a simple design.

Can a JPG become an ICO?

Yes, but JPG has no transparency: your icon will have a solid background.

Does the ICO file work on a Mac?

Mac browsers read ICO favicons. For macOS app icons, the expected format is different (ICNS).
