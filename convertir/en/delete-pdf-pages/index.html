Delete Pages From a PDF

Remove the pages you do not need and keep the rest in a single file.

  • No file upload
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up
  • No watermark

Your files never leave your device: the conversion is done by your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no account, no watermark and no daily limit.

How to do it

  1. Choose the PDF: its page count appears next to the name.
  2. Type the pages to delete: 2, 5-7 or 1, 3, 10-.
  3. Click "Delete these pages" and download the new PDF. The original is not changed.

What is it for?

  • Remove blank pages from a document scanned on both sides.
  • Take out a page with personal information before you share a document.
  • Delete the cover page or the terms and conditions from an invoice before filing it.

The pages you keep are copied with no loss at all: text, images and links stay intact. The summary tells you how many pages were removed and how many are kept.

Tip: finding the right page number

The number to type is the position of the page in the file (1st, 2nd...), not necessarily the number printed at the bottom of the page. Your PDF viewer usually shows it in the toolbar. To see every page as an image before you decide, you can first run PDF to JPG at "Screen" quality.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The page loads a small program into your browser, and that program does the conversion on your phone or computer. Your files are never uploaded or stored: we cannot see them. You can check it in the "Network" tab of your browser's developer tools.

Can I delete several pages that are far apart?

Yes, separate them with commas: 2, 8, 15-17.

Is my original file changed?

No. You download a new copy; the original on your device does not change.

What about changing the page order?

Split the PDF with &quot;Split PDF&quot;, then put the pieces back in the order you want with &quot;Merge PDF&quot;.

Can I delete every page except one?

Yes, but it is simpler to use &quot;Split PDF&quot; with the range of the page you want to keep.
