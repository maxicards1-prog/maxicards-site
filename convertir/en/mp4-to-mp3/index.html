Convert MP4 to MP3

Pull the soundtrack of a video from your phone or computer into an MP3 file.

  • No file upload
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up
  • No watermark

Your files never leave your device: the conversion is done by your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no account, no watermark and no daily limit.

How to do it

  1. Choose the video (MP4, MOV, M4A; WEBM depending on the browser).
  2. Choose the quality: 192 kbps suits both music and voice.
  3. Click "Extract MP3". The bar shows progress; expect a few seconds per minute of video on a computer.
  4. Download the MP3.

Limits, in full transparency

All the audio is decoded in your browser's memory. To stay reliable, the tool accepts videos of up to one hour on a computer and 20 minutes on a phone, and files up to 1 GB. Beyond that, it tells you before starting. The sound is saved in stereo at 44.1 kHz.

The tool does not download videos from YouTube or other platforms: it converts files that are already on your device, such as a video shot with your phone, a recording of a class or meeting, or a video memo.

Which quality should you choose?

  • 128 kbps: voice, podcast, meeting (about 1 MB per minute).
  • 192 kbps: a good choice for music (about 1.4 MB per minute).
  • 320 kbps: maximum MP3 quality (about 2.4 MB per minute). It cannot exceed the quality of the original sound.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The page loads a small program into your browser, and that program does the conversion on your phone or computer. Your files are never uploaded or stored: we cannot see them. You can check it in the "Network" tab of your browser's developer tools.

Can I convert a YouTube video?

No. The tool only converts files already on your device. Respect the copyright of the content you convert.

Does my iPhone video (MOV) work?

Yes, in Safari and in Chrome. If your browser cannot read a format, a message tells you.

Why is there a duration limit?

The whole sound must fit in the browser's memory. One hour of decoded audio already takes more than a gigabyte: beyond that, the page could crash.

Can I convert several videos?

Yes, add them all: they are converted one after the other and gathered into a ZIP.
