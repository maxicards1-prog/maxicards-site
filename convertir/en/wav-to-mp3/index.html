Convert WAV to MP3

A WAV file is heavy: as an MP3 it becomes 5 to 10 times smaller and plays everywhere.

  • No file upload
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up
  • No watermark

Your files never leave your device: the conversion is done by your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no account, no watermark and no daily limit.

How to do it

  1. Add one or more WAV files.
  2. Choose the quality: 320 kbps (default) for music, 192 kbps for a good compromise, 128 kbps for voice.
  3. Click “Convert to MP3” and download the files.

Why go from WAV to MP3?

WAV stores sound without compression: one minute of CD-quality audio (44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo) weighs about 10 MB. The same clip as an MP3 at 320 kbps weighs about 2.4 MB, and about 1.4 MB at 192 kbps. To email it, put it on a phone or publish it, MP3 is far more practical, and every device plays it.

Which quality should I choose?

  • 320 kbps: the maximum for MP3, for music you want to keep.
  • 192 kbps: hard to tell from the original on most headphones.
  • 128 kbps: enough for voice (podcast, memo, recorded class).

MP3 loses part of the information: keep your original WAV if you plan to edit it further. More details in our guide MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC: which format to choose.

Limits

Up to 60 minutes per file on a computer and 20 minutes on a phone: beyond that, browser memory is not always enough. The sound is converted to 44.1 kHz.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The page loads a small program into your browser, and that program does the conversion on your phone or computer. Your files are never uploaded or stored: we cannot see them. You can check it in the "Network" tab of your browser's developer tools.

Will the MP3 have the same quality as the WAV?

Almost, at 320 kbps: the difference is very hard to hear. But MP3 is a lossy format: keep the WAV for studio work.

Can I convert several WAV files at once?

Yes, add them all: you get one MP3 per file, and a ZIP to download everything.

What about the other way, MP3 to WAV?

It is not offered: converting an MP3 to WAV does not bring back the lost quality, it only makes a bigger file.
