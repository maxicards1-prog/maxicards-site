Compress a PDF

Shrink a PDF that is too large for an email or an online form.

  • No file upload
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up
  • No watermark

Your files never leave your device: the conversion is done by your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no account, no watermark and no daily limit.

How to do it

  1. Choose the PDF to shrink.
  2. Choose the level: "Medium" suits most documents; "High" helps you get under a strict limit (2 MB, 5 MB...).
  3. Click "Compress". The tool shows the size before and after, then offers the smaller file.

How the compression works

Each page is redrawn as an image at a reasonable resolution (80 to 150 dots per inch), then saved as JPEG. This is very effective for scanned documents and PDFs full of photos, which often lose 50 to 90% of their weight while staying readable on screen and in print.

To be honest about it: after compression, the text becomes an image. It can no longer be selected or searched, and links stop being clickable. For a pure-text PDF made in Word, which is already light, compression often gains nothing: the tool tells you so and does not hand you a heavier file.

What size limits will you run into?

  • Email attachments: often 20 to 25 MB in total (Gmail, Outlook).
  • Government portals and job application sites: often 2 to 10 MB per document.

If your document is still too large, cut it up with Split PDF. For standalone photos, use Compress Image instead.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The page loads a small program into your browser, and that program does the conversion on your phone or computer. Your files are never uploaded or stored: we cannot see them. You can check it in the "Network" tab of your browser's developer tools.

How much smaller will my PDF get?

It depends on the content: a scan or a PDF of photos often drops by 50 to 90%. A simple text PDF is already optimized and may not shrink; the tool will tell you.

Will the text stay readable?

Yes, on screen and in print with the &quot;Medium&quot; level. With &quot;High&quot;, small characters can get a bit blurry: check the result before sending it.

Why can't I select the text anymore?

To save space, pages are saved as images. If you need selectable text, keep a copy of the original.

My password-protected PDF won't work.

Protected PDFs cannot be read without the password. Open it with its password and save a copy without protection.
