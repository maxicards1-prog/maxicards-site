Why this site is different

Most online converters upload your files to their servers, process them there, then send them back. Here, nothing leaves your device: the page loads a small program that does the conversion right on your phone or computer. It is safer for personal documents (ID card, pay stub, contract, photos), often faster because there is no upload and no waiting, and it lets us stay free with no daily limit, no account and no watermark.

What the site can do, and what it can't

We only offer conversions that truly work in a browser: PDF (including signing, page numbering and reordering), images (JPG, PNG, WEBP, iPhone HEIC, SVG, ICO) and audio to MP3. Word, Excel and PowerPoint conversions are not offered: they need real office software to get a faithful result. Tip: Word, LibreOffice and Google Docs can all "Save as PDF" or "Download as PDF", for free and with no quality loss.
