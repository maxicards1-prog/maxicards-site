Work out your paces and prepare your race: free calculators, pace charts and week-by-week training plans.

Calculators

  • Pace calculatorEnter a distance and a time: you get your pace per kilometer and per mile, and your speed in km/h and mph.
  • Running time calculatorEnter the pace you plan to hold and the distance: you get the finish time and a table of splits to write down or print.
  • Pace converterChange any of the units: the others update right away. A conversion table is given below.
  • VO2 max calculatorEnter the distance you covered during the test: the tool gives your MAS in km/h, the matching pace and an estimate of your VO2 max.
  • Training paces from MASYour MAS gives all your paces: easy running, threshold, race paces and intervals, with a range for each.
  • Heart rate zonesEnter your age (and, if you know them, your measured max HR and resting HR): you get your 5 zones in beats per minute.
  • Race time predictorFrom a recent result, estimate your time over 5K, 10K, half marathon and marathon, and see the limits of the prediction.

Pace charts

  • 10K pace chartWhat pace should you hold to finish a 10K in 45, 50 or 55 minutes? The chart gives the pace, the speed and the halfway split.
  • Half marathon pace chartWhat pace do you need to finish a half marathon in 1:45 or 2:00? The chart gives the pace, the speed and the splits at 5, 10, 15 and 20 km.
  • Marathon pace chartWhat pace should you hold to finish a marathon in 3:30, 4:00 or 4:30? The chart gives the pace, the speed and the key splits.

Training plans

  • Beginner 5K planEight weeks, three workouts a week, from run-walk to a continuous 5K. A gradual plan, at your own pace.
  • 10K planThree workouts a week for eight weeks: easy running, quality work and a long run, with an easier week and a taper.
  • Half marathon planFour workouts a week for ten weeks: easy running, quality work, half marathon pace and a long run, with two easier weeks.
  • Marathon planFour workouts a week for twelve weeks for a runner with a good base: long runs, marathon pace work and a taper.

Simple tools, cited formulas

This site brings together the calculations a runner needs every week: working out your pace, predicting your race time, measuring your MAS, setting your training paces and your heart rate zones. Each tool shows its formula and its source on the Sources page. When a method is debated, we say so: an estimate remains an estimate.

Where to start?

  • You have a recent time: calculate your pace, then predict your other distances.
  • You want to train at the right speed: do a test, estimate your VO2 max and MAS, then open your training paces.
  • You are aiming for a race: choose a time in the 10K chart, the half marathon chart or the marathon chart, then follow a plan.
  • You are just starting: the beginner 5K plan alternates running and walking for 8 weeks.

Free, no account, in your browser

The calculations run on your phone or your computer: nothing you type is sent or saved. The tools are free, need no sign-up and are supported by advertising. They give general guidelines and are not medical advice: read the health disclaimer before starting or resuming exercise.

Distances and paces are given in kilometers and miles; the site was first written in French, which is why a few reference sources are French-language documents.
