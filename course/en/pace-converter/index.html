Convert pace and speed

  • Calculated in your browser
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up
  • No data sent

Change any of the units: the others update right away. A conversion table is given below.

How to use it

  1. Type a value in one of the blocks: speed in km/h or mph, pace in min/km or pace in min/mile.
  2. The other blocks recalculate immediately: no need to press a button.
  3. For a pace, fill in the minutes and the seconds separately (5 and 30 for 5:30).
  4. Then find the value in the conversion table to compare with your workouts.

Formula: speed (km/h) = 60 ÷ pace (decimal min/km); pace (min/mile) = pace (min/km) × 1.609344; mph = km/h ÷ 1.609344. The international mile is exactly 1,609.344 m. See Sources.

Why are there several units for the same thing?

Pace in minutes per kilometer says how long each kilometer lasts: it is what you watch on the road. Speed in kilometers per hour is what treadmills, maximal aerobic speed tests and many physiology tables use.

In the US and the UK, pace is given in minutes per mile and treadmills often show miles per hour: a runner at 8:00 min/mile runs at about 4:58 min/km.

How to convert by hand

  • From min/km to km/h: convert the pace to decimal minutes (5:30 = 5.5), then divide 60 by that number: 60 ÷ 5.5 = 10.9 km/h.
  • From km/h to min/km: divide 60 by the speed: 12 km/h gives 60 ÷ 12 = 5.0 minutes, or 5:00 per kilometer.
  • From min/km to min/mile: multiply by 1.609: 5:00 per km gives 8:03 per mile.
  • From km/h to mph: divide by 1.609: 12 km/h is about 7.5 mph.

On a treadmill

A treadmill usually shows speed in km/h or mph. To run at 5:30 min/km, set it to about 10.9 km/h (6.8 mph). Many runners add a 1% incline to make up for the lack of air resistance, but this setting is a common rule of thumb rather than a rule. To find the pace of a full race, use the pace calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert min/km to km/h?

Convert the pace to decimal minutes, then divide 60 by that number. At 6:00 min/km, 60 ÷ 6 = 10 km/h; at 5:30 min/km, 60 ÷ 5.5 = 10.9 km/h.

How do I convert min/km to min/mile?

Multiply the pace in seconds by 1.609344. 5:00 min/km is 300 seconds, which gives about 483 seconds, or 8:03 min/mile.

What speed in km/h is 5:00 min/km?

5:00 min/km is 12 km/h, or about 7.5 mph. It is also a pace of 8:03 min/mile.

How many meters are in a mile?

An international mile is exactly 1,609.344 meters, or 1.609344 km.

Is my data saved?

No. The calculation runs in your browser, on your device. Nothing you type is sent to our servers or saved in your browser.
