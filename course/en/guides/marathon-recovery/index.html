How to Recover After a Marathon

The short answer

Your body needs several days to recover from a marathon. Rest for the first few days, resume with walking or very slow jogging, and let your legs decide. There is no single proven duration.

What happens in the body

After a marathon, muscle fibers are damaged, energy stores are empty and the nervous system is tired. Blood markers of muscle damage stay elevated for several days, with individual variation. Soreness and heavy legs are therefore normal in the days that follow.

The first days

  • Walk that evening or the next day: it keeps you from stiffening up.
  • Eat and drink normally, with carbohydrates and protein: the body needs material to repair itself.
  • Sleep: it is the best recovery tool.
  • Avoid intense efforts and heavy strength sessions.

When to resume running

No duration applies to everyone.

A randomized study published in the European Journal of Sport Science in 2020 compared running, elliptical and complete rest after a marathon: resuming running 48 hours later did not appear to delay muscle recovery over eight days, and total rest throughout the week was not advised.

This concerns a specific sample of runners; for others, resuming may be later. Restart with short, very easy outings, and wait until muscle soreness has gone. To rebuild a real load, allow several weeks: it is, in a way, a taper in reverse.

After a half marathon

Recovery is shorter than after a marathon, but the same principles apply: walking, sleep, nutrition, slow resumption. Listen to your sensations rather than following a fixed calendar.

What to do next

Do not launch into a new plan the next day. Take the time to review: how the sessions went, fueling (see the guide), pace. When you are ready, a half marathon plan or marathon plan is still possible, with a gradual rise in volume.

Warning signs

See a doctor for specific or persistent bone pain, a very swollen leg, prolonged very dark urine, fever, chest pain, palpitations or unusual fatigue that lasts. This article is general and does not replace medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to recover from a marathon?

There is no single duration. Muscle damage resolves over several days, and rebuilding load takes several weeks. Listen to your legs.

Can you run 2 days after a marathon?

A 2020 study found that a jog at 48 h did not appear to delay muscle recovery in the group studied, but it depends on the person. Stay very slow and short.

What should I eat after a marathon?

A normal meal with carbohydrates and protein, and something to drink. There is no miracle food.

Why do my legs hurt after a marathon?

Soreness is normal after a long effort. Specific pain on a bone, swelling or pain that is getting worse should be examined by a doctor.
