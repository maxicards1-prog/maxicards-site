What Pace Should Your Tempo Run Be?

The short answer

A tempo run is run at threshold: the fastest pace you can hold for a long time before breathing runs away. It is often placed at 80 to 90% of MAS depending on the author, close to half marathon or 10K pace for a trained runner.

What is threshold?

The threshold is the intensity beyond which lactate builds up quickly in the blood. Depending on the method (lactate, ventilation, heart rate), several slightly different thresholds are defined.

For a recreational runner, the simplest is to talk about a pace that is "comfortably hard": marked breathing, sentences of a few words.

What pace?

Published ranges vary: you find 80 to 90% of MAS, sometimes 85 to 90% for a 10K runner. A simple rule many people use: roughly the pace a trained runner could hold in a one-hour race. So keep a range and adjust to perceived effort.

Typical workouts

  • 2 x 10 minutes at threshold, with 2 minutes of jogging between the two (ideal to start).
  • 3 x 10 minutes, with 2 minutes of jogging.
  • 20 to 30 minutes continuous, for an already trained runner.

These workouts are common coaching practices, not protocols validated by a specific study. Do one a week at most, never the day before a long run.

A useful workout or not?

Observations of elite athletes (Seiler and Kjerland, 2006) show little time spent at threshold, with most of the work being either easy or very hard. That does not mean threshold work is useless: for a 10K or half marathon runner, it stays very close to race pace. But avoid running "medium-fast" all the time.

Common mistakes

  • Starting too fast on the first block and falling apart on the third.
  • Running all your runs at this pace, with no real recovery.
  • Skipping the warm-up: see the 10-minute routine.

If pain appears or persists, stop the workout and see a health professional.

Frequently asked questions

What is the threshold pace for a 10K?

Often between 85 and 90% of MAS, close to 10K race pace for a trained runner.

How long can you hold threshold pace?

For a trained runner, about one hour is commonly accepted; a beginner holds it for less.

Should I do threshold runs when training for a marathon?

It is common in plans, because it brings the effort closer to race pace. Dose it according to your level.

Are threshold and tempo the same thing?

The words are not always defined the same way. They generally mean a hard but controlled effort, close to threshold.
