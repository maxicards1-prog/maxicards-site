Does Running 30 Minutes a Day Help You Lose Weight?

The short answer

Running 30 minutes uses energy, so it can contribute to weight loss, but the result depends on everything you eat and burn. The calories-burned calculation is an estimate, not a measurement.

How many calories does 30 minutes of running burn?

The most common method uses metabolic equivalents (METs). By convention, 1 MET is about 1 kilocalorie per kilogram of body weight per hour. The formula is: kcal ≈ MET × body weight (kg) × duration (h).

In the Compendium of Physical Activities (2011 edition), running at 6 miles per hour (about 9.7 km/h, a 10-minute mile) is rated at 9.8 MET.

Body weightCalculation (30 min = 0.5 h)Estimate
60 kg (132 lb)9.8 × 60 × 0.5about 294 kcal
70 kg (154 lb)9.8 × 70 × 0.5about 343 kcal
80 kg (176 lb)9.8 × 80 × 0.5about 392 kcal

These are worked examples for that single pace. The Compendium itself notes that its values do not replace an individual measurement (age, sex, running efficiency, body composition...), and more recent editions give slightly different values. Keep it as an order of magnitude.

How far do you run in 30 minutes?

Distance depends on pace: at 6 min/km (9:39 per mile), 30 minutes gives 5 km (3.1 miles); at 7 min/km (11:16 per mile), about 4.3 km (2.7 miles). Use the pace calculator or the pace-speed converter.

Why doesn't the scale always move?

  • Weight loss depends on the balance between what you eat and what you burn over the week, not on a single session.
  • Appetite can rise after a session: people sometimes compensate without noticing.
  • Body weight also moves with water, meals and sleep.

How often does it count?

The WHO recommends 150 to 300 minutes of moderate-intensity activity a week for adults, or 75 to 150 minutes of vigorous activity. Three 30-minute runs make 90 minutes, which is a good start. If you are a beginner, run-walk is a sensible way in: see how to start running.

This guide is general. For a weight-loss goal, a medical condition, medication or pregnancy, ask your doctor or a health professional.

Sources: Compendium of Physical Activities (Ainsworth et al., 2011); WHO (2020), Br J Sports Med (accessed 24/11/2026).

Frequently asked questions

Does running 30 minutes a day make you lose weight?

It uses energy and can contribute, but the result depends on your diet and your overall energy balance over the week.

How many calories does 30 minutes of running burn?

With the MET formula, about 294 kcal at 60 kg (132 lb), 343 kcal at 70 kg (154 lb) and 392 kcal at 80 kg (176 lb) at about 6 mph (9.7 km/h). It is an estimate.

How far is 30 minutes of running?

It depends on pace: 3.1 miles (5 km) at 6 min/km, about 2.7 miles (4.3 km) at 7 min/km.

Is running 30 minutes three times a week enough?

Three 30-minute sessions make 90 minutes a week, more than the WHO minimum of 75 minutes of vigorous activity; the effect then depends on the rest of your lifestyle.
