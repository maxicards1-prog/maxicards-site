How to Run in Hot Weather

The short answer

Slow down, run early in the morning or late in the evening, drink according to thirst and stop at the first warning sign. A body temperature of at least 40 C (104 F) with confusion is heat stroke: call emergency services right away.

Why heat penalizes running

To get rid of the heat produced by effort, the body sweats and sends blood toward the skin. In high heat, especially with humidity, this cooling is less efficient: your heart beats faster at the same pace and effort feels harder. It is normal to be slower.

Use the pace calculator to see your real speed without comparing it to a session in cool weather, and rely on perceived effort rather than the stopwatch.

Adapting your session

  • Time: run early in the morning or in the evening, outside the hottest hours.
  • Pace: slow down, shorten, or swap the hard session for an easy jog.
  • Route: choose shade, water points and loops close to home.
  • Clothing: light, light-colored clothes, a cap, sunscreen.
  • Heart rate: it climbs at equal pace; your heart rate zones become less reliable.
  • Acclimatization: the body adapts gradually to heat; increase volume gently.

Drinking

The 2015 consensus on exercise-associated hyponatremia (Hew-Butler et al.) recommends drinking according to thirst, which limits both dehydration and the risk of drinking too much. Avoid forcing fluids without thirst. For a long outing, carry water or plan aid points: see what to eat and drink during a marathon.

Warning signs

Signs of heat-related illness: headache, cramps, unusual fatigue, dizziness, nausea, incoherent speech. Stop, get into the shade, cool yourself (cool water on the body, airflow) and drink in small sips.

According to official guidance (info.gouv.fr and Santé publique France, French public health sources), heat stroke is a medical emergency: body temperature of at least 40 C (104 F) with altered consciousness (confusion, agitation, loss of consciousness). Call emergency services immediately (911 in the US and Canada, 112 in the EU, 999 in the UK) and cool the person while waiting for help.

A skin or forehead temperature reading can underestimate the core temperature: if in doubt, call.

Running in a group

Tell someone your route, run with a partner when possible and keep an eye on others. Older adults, children, people with chronic conditions or on medication should talk to their doctor before running in extreme heat.

To remember for a race

For a marathon or half in summer, see also marathon training: aim for a more cautious pace on race day.

Frequently asked questions

At what temperature should I stop running?

No single threshold is established, because humidity, wind and your acclimatization all matter. Follow local heat advisories and adapt or cancel the session.

How do I recognize heat stroke while running?

Body temperature of at least 40 C (104 F) with confusion, incoherent speech or loss of consciousness. It is an emergency: call emergency services.

Should I drink a lot when it is hot?

Drink according to thirst, without forcing. Drinking far more than necessary can be dangerous (hyponatremia).

Why do I run slower when it is hot?

The body spends energy cooling itself, so the same pace demands more effort. This is normal: slow down.
