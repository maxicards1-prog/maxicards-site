Is Running on an Empty Stomach a Good Idea?

The short answer

Running on an empty stomach burns more fat during the session according to a 2016 review, but that does not prove greater fat loss in the long run. For a short, easy run many runners tolerate it; for a long or hard session it is better to eat first.

What the research shows

The systematic review and meta-analysis by Vieira et al. (2016, British Journal of Nutrition) concludes that aerobic exercise done in the fasted state leads to higher fat oxidation during the effort than the same exercise after a meal.

That study measures what happens during exercise: on its own it does not show greater fat loss in the long term. The overall result depends on everything else in your day.

Running 30 minutes in the morning, fasted

For a 30-minute run at an easy pace, many runners run without eating. If you feel fine, there is nothing unusual about it. If you get dizzy, nauseous or very tired, eat something first or move the run. Needs vary a lot from person to person.

Long or hard sessions: better to eat

The joint position statement of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics, Dietitians of Canada and the American College of Sports Medicine (2016) presents nutrition as a way to improve performance and recovery, and recommends seeing a sports dietitian for a personal plan.

For an interval session or a long run, a meal or snack beforehand prevents running out of energy. See what to eat before running.

5K (3.1 miles) fasted, or after eating?

  • 5K fasted: possible for many people at an easy pace; listen to how you feel.
  • Running after a meal: digestion varies with the meal and the person. Leave a comfortable gap for you, tested in training and never on race day.

Keep in mind

  • Drink water before you head out.
  • Keep an easy pace: you should be able to talk (see easy-run pace).
  • Avoid fasted running if you have diabetes, are pregnant or are on medication, without medical advice.

This guide is general and does not replace medical or dietary advice, especially with diabetes, eating disorders or pregnancy.

Sources: Vieira et al. (2016), Br J Nutr; Thomas, Erdman, Burke (2016), joint position AND / DC / ACSM (accessed 24/11/2026).

Frequently asked questions

Is running 30 minutes on an empty stomach OK?

For an easy run, many runners do it without trouble. If you feel dizzy or nauseous, eat beforehand.

Can I run 5K on an empty stomach?

Yes for many people at an easy pace; listen to how you feel and stop if you feel unwell.

Can I run 30 minutes after eating?

It depends on the meal and the person. Leave a comfortable gap, tested in training.

How do I run fasted without feeling drained?

Keep an easy pace, drink water before leaving, and save long or hard sessions for days when you have eaten.
