Half marathon pace chart

What pace do you need to finish a half marathon in 1:45 or 2:00? The chart gives the pace, the speed and the splits at 5, 10, 15 and 20 km.

How to use it

  1. Find your target time in the first column of the table.
  2. Read the pace to hold in minutes per kilometer and per mile, and the speed in km/h.
  3. Note the splits: they let you check that you are running evenly during the race.
  4. For a time that is not in the table, use the pace calculator.

A half marathon is exactly 21.0975 km (13.1 miles). The times in the chart assume an even pace over this official distance; the splits are those of a runner who holds the pace exactly.

Running your half marathon in three parts

A half marathon naturally splits into three parts. During the first 5 kilometers, hold back: adrenaline makes you start too fast. From the 5th to the 15th km, hold the chart pace and take a little water at the aid stations.

The last 6 kilometers are where evenness decides your time: someone who managed the start well can keep the pace, or even speed up a little.

Over this distance, a gap of 5 seconds per kilometer makes 1 minute 45 seconds at the finish. A starting pace error of 10 seconds per kilometer can therefore cost more than 3 minutes.

What pace in terms of MAS?

Coaches generally place half marathon pace between 75 and 85% of MAS, depending on the runner's experience and weekly mileage. The training paces from MAS give you the exact range. To prepare, the 10-week half marathon plan includes workouts at this pace.

Your 10K time can also be a starting point: estimate it with the race time predictor or compare with the 10K pace chart.

Frequently asked questions

What pace for a half marathon in 1:45?

You need to hold about 4:59 min/km (8:01 min/mile), or 12.0 km/h. The 10K split is around 49:46.

How long is a half marathon?

21.0975 km (13.1 miles), exactly half of the marathon (42.195 km).

What time is a good half marathon for a beginner?

Many beginners finish between 2:00 and 2:30. The goal of a first half marathon is to finish it in good shape: the time comes later.

Should I drink during a half marathon?

Over more than an hour, a little water at the aid stations is common. Drink in small sips, according to thirst and heat; if in doubt, ask a doctor or a sports professional for advice.
