Teams mic or camera not working

In Teams, check that you are unmuted, that the right mic and camera are selected under Settings, Devices, and that your system allows Teams to use them.

Page updated November 26, 2026.

Start with a quick test

Run the mic and webcam test in your browser. If both work there, your devices and permissions are fine, and the problem is in Teams' settings. If one fails, fix it first with mic not working on Windows 11, mic not working on Mac, webcam not detected or camera not working on Mac.

Microphone

Microsoft's Teams help gives these steps for Windows: 1. Update Teams and Windows. 2. In the meeting controls, select Unmute mic if the mic icon has a line through it. 3. Check that a headset boom is not in the mute position and that an external mic's mute button is off. 4. Open Settings, Privacy & security, Microphone, and turn on Microphone access, Let apps access your microphone, the Teams entry in the app list, and Let desktop apps access your microphone. Then close all apps and restart. 5. In Teams, go to Settings, Devices, and pick your mic in the Microphone drop-down. Select Make a test call to check it. 6. During a meeting, select the arrow next to Mic, then More audio settings, to change the microphone.

On a Mac, Microsoft says to check System Settings, Privacy & Security, Microphone, and make sure Teams is selected.

Camera

Microsoft's help suggests: - Close other apps that might use the camera, such as Skype or FaceTime. For an external camera, unplug it and plug it back in. - On Windows, open Settings, Privacy & security, Camera, turn on Let apps access your camera, and turn on the Teams entry. If it is already on, switch it off and back on, then restart. - On a Mac, open System Settings, Privacy & Security, Camera, and confirm Teams is selected. - Install the latest Teams and Windows or macOS updates.

Teams in a browser

Allow the mic and camera for the Teams site in your browser's permissions, then reload. See how to allow microphone access and how to allow camera access. Microsoft notes that the Make a test call option is not available in Teams for the web.

If you are heard but the sound is poor

See mic too quiet, mic distorted or crackling and mic echo. For a dark or soft picture, see webcam dark or blurry.

If nothing works

Restart the computer and rejoin. If a work or school account is involved, an administrator may manage some settings, so ask your IT team. A joined test meeting with a colleague is a good final check. The video interview tech check lists what to verify before an important call.

This site is not affiliated with Microsoft. Menu labels can change between Teams versions, and the online test does not upload your sound or image.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I choose my mic and camera in Teams?

Open Teams Settings, then Devices, and pick them from the drop-downs. Labels may vary by version.

Why is Teams the only app where my camera fails?

Teams may lack permission in your system privacy settings, another app may be using the camera, or the wrong camera may be selected.

Is there a way to test my devices in Teams?

Microsoft's help describes a Make a test call option in Teams settings. It is not available in Teams for the web.
