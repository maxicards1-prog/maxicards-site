Zoom mic not working

If people cannot hear you in Zoom, check that you are unmuted, then pick the right microphone in Zoom's Audio settings.

Page updated November 26, 2026.

Start with a quick test

Open the mic test in your browser and speak. If the level bar moves, your mic works and the problem is in Zoom or its permissions. If it does not, fix the system first: see mic not working on Windows 11 or mic not working on Mac.

Quick checks

Zoom's help starts with the basics: - Fully insert the microphone cable. If Zoom does not detect it, unplug it and plug it back in. - For a Bluetooth device, confirm it is on and connected. - If the Audio icon in the meeting controls shows muted, click it to unmute. Also look for a physical mute switch on your mic or headset. - Raise the computer's volume, because it can be muted even when Zoom's speaker setting is on.

Pick the right mic in Zoom

In the desktop app, click your profile picture, then Settings, then the Audio tab. Under Microphone, use the test option, choose your mic from the drop-down, and adjust the Input Volume slider. Labels may vary by version. During a meeting, click the upward arrow beside Mute to switch microphones.

Allow Zoom to use the mic

If your device does not appear in Zoom, the permission is probably missing, according to Zoom's help. - Windows: open Settings, then Privacy, and under App permissions choose Microphone. Zoom's article names the switches for apps and desktop apps to access the microphone. On Windows 11, Microsoft's equivalent labels are Let apps access your microphone and Let desktop apps access your microphone, under Privacy & security. - Mac: open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Microphone, and turn on Zoom.

Zoom in a browser

If you join through a web browser, allow the microphone for the Zoom site: see how to allow microphone access in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

Other apps and software

Another app may be using the mic. Close recorders, other call apps and streaming tools, then rejoin. Zoom's help also mentions that some security software can block access to the mic, so check its settings if everything else looks right.

Sound quality problems

If you are heard but sound quiet, harsh or echoey, see mic too quiet, mic distorted or crackling and mic echo.

If nothing works

Restart Zoom, then the computer. Update or reinstall Zoom if the problem started after an update. Join a test meeting and try a headset to compare. Before an important call, the video interview tech check lists what to verify.

This site is not affiliated with Zoom. The online test runs in your browser and does not upload your voice.

Frequently asked questions

Why can everyone hear me except in Zoom?

Zoom may be set to a different microphone, or it may lack permission. Check Settings, then Audio, and your system microphone privacy settings.

How do I switch microphones during a Zoom meeting?

Click the upward arrow beside Mute and choose another microphone.

Does the online mic test work with Zoom?

It checks that your mic and browser permission work. Use Zoom's own Audio settings for options inside the app.
