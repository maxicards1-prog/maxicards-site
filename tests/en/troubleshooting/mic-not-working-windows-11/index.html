Mic not working on Windows 11

Most of the time the mic is blocked by Windows privacy settings, muted by a switch, or the wrong input device is selected.

Page updated November 26, 2026.

Start with a quick test

Open the online mic test, start it and say a few words. The result tells you whether your browser gets sound, whether access was denied, or whether no microphone was found at all. The steps below follow those three cases.

Case 1: access is denied

Two permissions stack on top of each other: Windows and the website. In Windows 11, open Settings, then Privacy & security, then Microphone. Microsoft's help lists these switches: Microphone access, Let apps access your microphone, and Let desktop apps access your microphone. A browser counts as a desktop app, so that last one matters. Turn all three on. Then allow the mic for the site in your browser: see how to allow microphone access in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

Case 2: no microphone found

  • Check the connection: push the plug all the way in, try another USB port, or make sure a Bluetooth headset is connected.
  • Look for a mute button on a headset boom or cable. Some laptops also have a keyboard key that cuts the mic.
  • Go to Settings, System, Sound, and look under Input. Microsoft says connected microphones are listed there. A Bluetooth mic has to be paired first, using Add device.
  • Unplug the device, plug it back in, and run the test again.

Case 3: the mic is detected but the level stays flat

  • In the test's device list, pick a different microphone. Windows sometimes uses a built-in mic instead of your headset.
  • In Settings, System, Sound, select your mic under Input. Microsoft's steps are to open its properties, choose Start test under Input settings, speak, choose Stop test, then Play to hear the sample. Adjust the Input volume slider if needed.
  • Close other apps that may be holding the mic, such as a video call, a recorder or a game. Depending on the driver, only one program can use it, and the browser may report that the device is already in use.

Try the built-in troubleshooter

Microsoft's page also describes a troubleshooter. In Settings, System, Sound, scroll to Advanced, and select Input devices under the troubleshooting heading. Menu names can differ slightly between Windows 11 updates.

If nothing works

Restart the PC, then try a different browser. If the mic works in one place but not on a single site, the site permission is the problem. If it fails everywhere, plug it into another computer or phone: if it fails there too, the microphone itself is probably faulty. If Windows shows no device at all, Microsoft suggests a driver reinstall through Device Manager, under Sound, video and game controllers, then Scan for hardware changes. Once it works, the guide Set up your mic for video calls helps with the right level.

The test runs in your browser. Your voice is not sent to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my mic work in Discord but not in my browser?

Each app has its own permission. Allow the mic for the website in the browser, and make sure Let desktop apps access your microphone is on in Windows privacy settings.

My headset is detected but its mic is not. What should I do?

Pick the headset mic in the test's device list. On a Bluetooth headset, the mic may only appear in a hands-free mode.

Where is the microphone setting in Windows 11?

Open Settings, then System, then Sound, and look under Input. Privacy switches are under Settings, Privacy & security, Microphone.

Does the online test record me?

No. The sound stays in your browser and is not uploaded or stored on a server.
