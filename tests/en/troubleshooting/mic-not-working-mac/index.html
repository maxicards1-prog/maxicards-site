Mic not working on Mac

On a Mac, a silent mic is usually blocked in System Settings under Privacy & Security, or the wrong input device is chosen in Sound settings.

Page updated November 26, 2026.

Start with a quick test

Open the online mic test in your browser, start it and speak. If it says access was denied, the problem is a permission. If it says no mic was found, look at the connection and the input device. If the level bar stays flat, look at the input settings. The steps below follow those cases.

Check app permission

Apple's help says to open the Apple menu, choose System Settings, click Privacy & Security in the sidebar (you may need to scroll), then click Microphone. Each app that has asked to use the mic is listed with a switch. Turn on your browser, or the app you are trying to use. Quit and reopen the app afterward if it still does not hear you. The orange dot or Recording Indicator in Control Center shows when the mic is in use.

Allow the site in your browser

The browser also asks per website. See how to allow microphone access in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. If you use Safari, its website settings follow a similar path, but labels may vary by version.

Choose the right input device

Apple's help gives these steps: choose Apple menu, System Settings, then click Sound in the sidebar. Click Input, then select your device in the list. A built-in display mic may appear as your Mac model name followed by Microphone. Drag the input volume slider and speak while you watch the level. Lower it if a loud source distorts, raise it if your voice is faint.

No device in the list

  • Reconnect the USB, wired or Bluetooth device, and check that it is switched on.
  • Try a different port or cable.
  • Look for a mute switch on a headset or microphone.
  • Restart the Mac if the device still does not appear.

The level is flat in one app only

That app may be using a different input device or have its own input slider. Check its audio settings. If two programs compete for the mic, close the one you are not using. For specific apps, see Zoom mic not working, Teams mic and camera not working and Discord not picking up your mic.

The sound is too quiet or distorted

Adjust the input volume in small steps. Read mic too quiet and mic distorted or crackling for placement and level tips.

If nothing works

Restart the Mac and test again, then try another browser. Test the mic on another computer or phone: if it fails there as well, the microphone is probably faulty. If you are on a managed work or school Mac, an administrator may control these settings. Once it works, Set up your mic for video calls helps you fine-tune the level.

The test runs in your browser, and your voice is not uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the microphone permission on a Mac?

Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Microphone. Each app is listed with its own switch.

How do I choose which mic my Mac uses?

Open System Settings, then Sound, then Input, and pick the device from the list.

Why does the mic work in one app but not another?

Each app has its own permission and often its own input setting. Check both the Microphone privacy list and the app's audio settings.

How can I tell when my mic is in use?

Apple says the Recording Indicator in Control Center shows when the microphone is in use or was used recently.
