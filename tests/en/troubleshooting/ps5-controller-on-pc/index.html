Connect a PS5 controller to a PC and test it

Plug the DualSense in with a USB data cable or pair it over Bluetooth with the Create and PS buttons, then press a button in the gamepad test to see it.

Page updated November 26, 2026.

Start with a quick test

Connect the controller using one of the methods below, then open the gamepad test and press a button. A browser only reveals a controller after the first press, as a privacy measure, so a blank page does not mean it is broken.

By USB cable

This is the simplest route. Use a USB-C cable that carries data, since some cables only charge. Sony notes that haptic feedback on PC needs a USB connection, and only works when the game supports it. The controller should then appear as a gamepad.

By Bluetooth

Based on PlayStation's support page (read Nov 26, 2026): 1. Make sure the player indicator is off. If it is on, hold the PS button until it goes out. Unplug any USB cable. 2. Press and hold the Create button, then press and hold the PS button until the light bar blinks. 3. Turn on Bluetooth on your PC and choose the controller from the list. 4. When pairing finishes, the light bar blinks and the player indicator lights up. One controller can store up to four pairings. If pairing fails, Sony points to its reset page, linked from the support article.

What the test shows

The gamepad test recognizes the controller family from the name your browser gives it. For a DualSense, buttons can appear with PlayStation names (cross, circle, square, triangle) rather than Xbox letters. It also measures sticks, analog triggers and the dead zone. Vibration is tested only where your browser allows it.

In games

Controller support varies by game. Sony says to look at a game's store page for full or partial controller support on Steam, for example. Adaptive triggers, the built-in speaker and microphone work only if the game supports them, and a headset jack needs a wired connection. A browser test cannot predict how one specific game behaves.

If the controller does not show up

  • Press a second button; some browsers wait for a real press.
  • Try another cable or another USB port.
  • Close launchers and remapping tools that may hold the controller exclusively.
  • Read controller not recognized on PC.
  • If a stick moves by itself, see controller stick drift.

Keep the firmware current

If the controller disconnects or loses buttons, check whether the maker offers a firmware update. Sony says you can update the controller on PC with the PlayStation Accessories download. Update over a USB cable and leave the controller connected until the process finishes, so the update is not interrupted halfway.

If nothing works

Try the controller on another device. If it fails there too, follow Sony's reset steps, then contact PlayStation support.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my controller only appear after I press a button?

Browsers hide gamepads until the first input, so a site cannot detect a controller without your action.

Why does my DualSense show Xbox button names?

The test picks the labels from the controller name the browser reports. If it is not recognized as Sony, it may show the generic Xbox-style layout.

Does vibration work over Bluetooth on PC?

Sony states that haptic feedback on PC needs a USB connection, and only in games that support it.
