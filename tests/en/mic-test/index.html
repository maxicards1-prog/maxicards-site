How to test your microphone

  1. Click “Start test” and allow microphone access when your browser asks.
  2. If you have more than one microphone, pick the right one in the list: headset mic, webcam mic or the built-in laptop mic.
  3. Say a sentence at your normal speaking volume for about three seconds. The bar and the waveform move, then the verdict appears: it works, or it doesn't, with a suggested fix.
  4. Click “Record 5 seconds” to hear yourself the way people on a call hear you.

How to read the result

  • No sound: the mic is open but nothing arrives. See mic not working on Windows 11 or mic not working on a Mac.
  • Too quiet: the peak stays below about -20 dB. See mic too quiet.
  • Clipping: the peak hits the maximum (0 dB). See distorted or crackling mic.
  • Good level: somewhere in between. The ideal level depends on the app. This test mainly checks that the mic responds and that the sound is neither too low nor crushed.

The thresholds (silence below -50 dB, quiet below -20 dB, clipping at -1 dB) are our own reference values, not a standard. The dB figures are dBFS, where 0 dB is the loudest level a digital recording can hold.

Why the automatic fixes are turned off

Browsers can clean up your sound on their own: noise suppression, echo cancellation and automatic volume. During this test we switch them off so you see what the microphone really sends. In Zoom, Teams or Discord those features are often on, so your sound may be a little better there than it is here.

If access is denied

An access-denied message means your browser or your operating system is blocking the microphone for this site. Follow how to allow the microphone in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. On a Mac, also look for Privacy & Security > Microphone in System Settings (labels vary by version). A “device already in use” message means another app is holding the mic: close it, then try again.

Using a headset? See how to test a headset mic. Hearing an echo? See mic echo.

Privacy

The microphone only opens after you click “Start test”. The sound is measured inside your browser, and the 5-second recording stays in memory in this tab. It is not uploaded anywhere and it disappears when you close the page. The “Stop” button closes the microphone.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my mic not working on this test?

Most often the wrong microphone is selected, the browser has no permission, or another app is using the mic. Allow access, try another microphone in the list and close apps such as Zoom, Teams or Discord.

Why doesn't my microphone show up in the list?

The list only fills in after you allow access. Click “Start test”, allow the microphone, then open the list. If it is still missing, check the connection and your system privacy settings.

Does the test work with a Bluetooth headset?

Yes, if it appears in the list. When the mic opens, the system may switch the headset to a hands-free mode that lowers the sound quality. That is normal.

Is my recording saved or sent anywhere?

No. It stays in memory in the tab and disappears when you close or reload the page. Nothing is uploaded.
