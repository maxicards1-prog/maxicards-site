The test to run before a video call: camera and microphone checked at the same time.

Updated November 26, 2026 · the test runs in your browser, nothing is sent.

What this test is for

This is the test to run five minutes before a video call on Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Discord or WhatsApp on a computer. It opens the camera and the microphone at the same time, the way a calling app does, and tells you for each one whether it works.

How to do it

  1. Click “Start test” and allow both the camera and the microphone.
  2. Look at the preview: framing, light and sharpness.
  3. Say a sentence. The level bar should move. After about three seconds the test gives its verdict for the microphone.
  4. If something fails, the test tries the camera alone and then the microphone alone, so you know which one is the problem.

A quick check before you call

  • Your face is lit from the front, not from behind.
  • The camera is at eye level.
  • The right microphone is selected in the list (not the monitor's or your phone's).
  • Other apps that use the camera are closed.

The guides how to set up your mic for video calls and webcam lighting and framing go through each setting. Preparing for a job interview? Read the video interview tech check.

When one of them fails

A camera that works alone but fails together with the mic usually means one permission is missing, or the selected mic is busy in another app. The two verdicts are shown separately for that reason. App-specific problems have their own pages: Zoom mic not working and Teams mic or camera not working.

What this test cannot tell you

It checks the camera and the microphone as your browser sees them. Zoom, Teams and Meet each have their own settings (which mic, which camera, background effects), so check those inside the app too. This page also cannot test how good your internet connection is.

Problems that show up mid-call

If people say they can't hear you, check the microphone selected inside the calling app first, then the mute button, then the system's input volume. If your picture looks wrong, look at the camera chosen in the app and whether another program is using it. Running this test first usually tells you which side to investigate.

Privacy

The camera and the microphone only open after you click. Nothing is recorded or uploaded: the picture and the sound stay in this tab. The “Stop” button closes both.

Frequently asked questions

Does this replace the test built into Zoom or Teams?

No. It checks the camera and microphone in your browser. Each app has its own settings (which mic, which camera), so check those in the app as well.

Why does the camera work but not the mic?

One of the two permissions is probably missing, or the selected mic is busy in another app. The test shows a separate verdict for each.

Can I run the test while Teams or Zoom is open?

You can, but an app that already holds the camera may block it for the browser. Close the app or turn its camera off for the test.

Is it safe to test my camera and mic here?

Yes. They only open after you click, and the picture and sound stay in your browser. Nothing is recorded or sent.
