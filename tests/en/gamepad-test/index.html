How to test a gamepad

  1. Plug the controller in by USB, or pair it over Bluetooth in your system settings.
  2. Press a button on the controller with this page in front. Browsers only show a controller after its first button press.
  3. Press every button: it lights up. Squeeze the triggers: the value rises from 0 to 1, because they are progressive controls, not simple buttons. Push the sticks in every direction.
  4. To test for drift, let go of the sticks and click “Measure rest position”: for three seconds the test watches whether a stick wanders away from the center by itself.

PS5, PS4, Xbox and Switch labels

The browser numbers buttons the same way whatever the controller (the “standard” layout). The “Button labels” menu translates those numbers into the names on your controller: Cross, Circle, Square, Triangle on a PlayStation pad; A, B, X, Y on an Xbox pad. On a Switch pad the A and B letters sit in different places than on an Xbox, so the test names buttons by their position.

If the browser doesn't recognize the controller as “standard”, the names may be wrong. A note appears above the list when that happens.

Deadzone and drift

A stick at rest should report zero, but a worn stick sometimes reports a small value. Games ignore small movements with a deadzone. When the resting value is bigger than that zone, the character or camera moves by itself: this is drift. The test flags probable drift when the average distance from the center is above 10%. That threshold is our own reference value, not a standard. See controller stick drift.

Rumble

The “Test rumble” button sends a short vibration when the browser allows it. Depending on the browser, the system and how the controller is connected, rumble may not be available from a web page even though the controller is fine.

Frequently asked questions

Why isn't my controller showing up in the test?

Press a button with the page in front: browsers hide a controller until a button is pressed. Also close apps that may grab it, such as a game launcher.

How do I check my controller for stick drift?

Let go of the sticks and click “Measure rest position”. If drift is confirmed, try cleaning around the stick, raising the deadzone in the game, then repair or replace the controller.

How do I test analog triggers?

Press them in slowly: the value under the button name goes from 0.00 to 1.00. If it jumps or stays stuck, the trigger is faulty.

Rumble doesn't work in the test. Is my controller broken?

Not necessarily. Not every browser or connection type lets a web page control rumble. Try a USB cable with Chrome or Edge.
