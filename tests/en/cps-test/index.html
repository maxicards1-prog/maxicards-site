    How the test works

    Pick a length: 1, 5 or 10 seconds. The timer starts on your first click. Click as fast as you can inside the box until time runs out. The result is the number of clicks divided by the length: 40 clicks in 5 seconds is 8 clicks per second (CPS).

    Tips for a fair result

    • Use the same mouse and the same surface when you compare attempts.
    • Short runs (1 s) give higher numbers than long ones (10 s), because nobody can hold a peak rhythm. Only compare attempts of the same length.
    • A click counts on every press of the left button (or every tap on a touch screen).
    • A mouse that double-clicks by itself will skew the result: check it with the mouse test.
    • Your clicks are counted when the button goes down, so holding the button doesn't add anything.

    What counts as a good score?

    There is no official answer. Scores depend on the length, the mouse, the technique and how tired your hand is. Read what is a good CPS for how to think about it, and compare yourself mainly with your own earlier attempts.

    Click techniques

    Players talk about “jitter clicking” (shaking the arm), “butterfly clicking” (alternating two fingers) and “drag clicking” (dragging a finger across the button). These techniques can tire your hand and wear out the mouse switch, and some online games limit them. Take breaks if your wrist or fingers hurt.

    Warm up first

    A few slow clicks before the real run help you find a comfortable hand position. Rest for a few seconds between attempts, and stop if your hand starts to ache.

    Related tests

    Want to test your reflexes instead of raw speed? Try the reaction time test.

    Choosing the length

    The 1-second run is a burst: good for a quick comparison, but luck matters a lot. The 5-second run is a balanced default. The 10-second run shows endurance and is usually the lowest score. Whatever you choose, keep the same length when you want to see whether you are improving.

    Privacy

    The test counts your clicks inside the page, in your browser. Your best attempts stay in memory while the page is open: nothing is recorded or sent.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is CPS?

    It means “clicks per second”: the number of clicks divided by the length of the test.

    Why does my score drop over 10 seconds?

    You can't hold a maximum rhythm for long: your hand gets tired. Always compare attempts of the same length.

    How do I test my clicks per second?

    Pick 1, 5 or 10 seconds, then click inside the box as fast as you can. The timer starts at your first click and the score appears when it ends.

    Does the test work on a phone?

    Yes, each tap counts as a click. Touch screens aren't comparable with mouse clicks.
