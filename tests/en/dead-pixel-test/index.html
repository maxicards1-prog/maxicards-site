Online dead pixel test

The screen turns a single solid color, one at a time. Nothing flashes: the color only changes when you decide.

Or pick a color

In full screen: arrow keys or a click change the color, Esc exits, I shows or hides the help text.

Nothing is sent or stored on a server: everything stays in your browser.

Full-screen solid colors: look for dots that don't change. Nothing flashes.

How to look for a dead pixel

  1. Clean the screen with a soft cloth: a speck of dust looks like a faulty pixel.
  2. Set the brightness to a comfortable level and turn off any screen saver.
  3. Click “Start the full-screen test”. Move through the colors with the arrow keys or by clicking. On each color, look at the whole surface up close, sweeping from left to right.
  4. On black, look for a bright dot. On white, look for a dark dot. On red, green and blue, look for a dot of another color, or a black one.
  5. Press Esc to exit.

Dead pixel vs. stuck pixel

  • A dead pixel stays off: it looks black, and is easiest to see on a white background.
  • A stuck pixel stays lit on one color: a red, green, blue or white dot, easiest to see on a black background.
  • A stain or glow that spreads over several centimeters is not a pixel. It is a backlight uniformity problem. Use the refresh rate and screen test, which includes a uniformity check.

For more detail, see dead pixel vs. stuck pixel.

Why nothing flashes

Some sites offer to “fix” a stuck pixel with a rapidly flashing animation. We don't offer one: those patterns can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy, and there is no guarantee they work. This test only shows fixed colors.

What to do if you find one

Write down the color of the dot, its position and the background where it shows up. Then look at your monitor's warranty: each manufacturer has its own rule about how many faulty pixels it accepts, written in its terms. If you are buying a screen, run this test as soon as it arrives and while you can still return it. The guide how to check a new monitor walks you through it.

On a phone or tablet

Some mobile browsers don't allow full screen on a web page. In that case the test fills the whole window: scroll to hide the browser bars, or use a computer.

Privacy

The test only displays colors. Nothing is recorded or sent.

Frequently asked questions

How can I tell a dead pixel from dust?

Clean the screen with a soft cloth. If the dot stays in the same place on several colors, it is a faulty pixel. Dust moves or disappears.

Why doesn't full screen work on my phone?

Some mobile browsers don't allow full screen for a web page. The test then fills the whole window: scroll to hide the browser bars, or use a computer.

Is a dead pixel covered by the warranty?

It depends on the manufacturer and the model. Read your monitor's warranty terms: they state how many faulty pixels are accepted.

Can I fix a stuck pixel?

We don't offer flashing fixes because they can be a risk for people sensitive to flashing lights, and results aren't guaranteed. If the pixel bothers you, check your warranty.
