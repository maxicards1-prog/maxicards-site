Video interview tech check: 6 steps before you join

Test your mic and camera the day before, sort out light and background, and keep a backup plan: that is most of a smooth video interview.

Page updated November 26, 2026.

Test it now: Mic + webcam test · Mic test · Webcam test

The method in 6 steps

  1. Run a mic and webcam test: Open the mic and webcam test the day before. Allow both, speak, and watch the level bar and the picture.
  2. Sort out the sound: Wear headphones to avoid echo and bring the mic closer to your mouth. See how to set up your mic for video calls.
  3. Sort out the picture: Light in front of you, camera at eye level, head and shoulders in frame. See webcam lighting and framing.
  4. Choose the background: Pick a simple, tidy background with no bright window behind you. Close the door and let the people around you know.
  5. Check your connection: Plug your laptop into power, close heavy apps and, if you can, use a network cable or sit close to your router.
  6. Prepare a backup plan: Keep the host's phone number, a charged phone with the meeting app installed and the meeting link saved somewhere you can reach quickly.

Why the day before

A test the day before leaves time to fix a problem such as a blocked permission, the wrong mic or a camera another app is using. On the day itself you rarely have time. The test takes a few minutes.

The order of checks

  1. The microphone, because people forgive an average picture far more readily than a voice they cannot hear.
  2. The picture, with light before camera.
  3. The connection, because a weak one makes everything stutter.
  4. The surroundings: noise, background, interruptions.

Know the platform

Interviews run on different apps: Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and others. Open the one you will use ahead of time, join a test meeting if the app offers one, and check which mic and camera it has selected. The app can pick a different device from the one you tested in the browser, so confirm it in the app's audio and video settings. For app-specific problems, see Zoom mic not working and Teams mic or camera not working.

Typical problems

  • "Nobody can hear me." The wrong mic is selected in the app, the mic is muted, or permission is blocked. See Discord mic not working for a method that applies to other apps too, plus allow the microphone in your browser.
  • "They cannot see me." The shutter is closed, another app is using the camera, or permission is blocked. See webcam not detected and allow the camera in your browser.
  • "I hear an echo." See mic echo.

Lighting and background

Check your picture at the time of day of the interview, because the daylight in the room changes. If you cannot avoid a window behind you, close the blinds partly or turn so the light falls on your face. A plain wall or a tidy shelf is better than a busy room. If your app offers a background blur, test it beforehand, since it can look odd around your hair and hands.

What to wear and where to sit

Choose clothes that look good on camera and contrast with your background. Sit on a stable chair, at a table at a comfortable height, so you can keep still and look relaxed. Put a note on the screen edge with the interviewer's name and the key points you want to cover.

One minute before

Silence your notifications, close apps that use the mic and camera, and glance over your notes. Look at the lens when you speak instead of at the faces on screen. That is what gives the impression of eye contact.

On the day, in one minute

Open the test page one last time, talk, watch the level bar, then close it so the mic and camera are free for the meeting app. Only one app can usually use them at a time. Keep a glass of water within reach, and place your notes beside the lens rather than below the screen so you do not keep looking down.

During the call

If the sound or picture gets worse, say so calmly and move to your backup plan. The people on the other side know that it happens.

Privacy

The tests on this site only start when you click and send nothing: the recording and the photo stay on your device.

Frequently asked questions

How long before an interview should I test my setup?

The day before is ideal, with a last quick check a few minutes before you join.

What if my camera fails at the last minute?

Tell the interviewer right away and offer to join from your phone or by audio call, depending on what they accept.

Should I use a headset for a video interview?

It usually helps. A headset avoids echo and keeps the mic close to your mouth.

Why can I be heard in the test but not in the meeting app?

The app may have a different microphone selected or a blocked permission. Check its audio settings and your system privacy settings.
