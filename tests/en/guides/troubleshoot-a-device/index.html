How to troubleshoot a device that won't work

To find what is failing, isolate one thing at a time: another port, another computer, another browser, then an online test that reads the key or signal.

Page updated November 26, 2026.

Test it now: Mic test · Webcam test · Keyboard tester · Mouse test · Gamepad tester

The method in 6 steps

  1. Write down the symptom: What exactly happens: nothing, on and off, or something wrong? One precise sentence stops you going in circles.
  2. Test in the browser: Open the matching test: mic, webcam, keyboard, mouse or gamepad. If the test works, the device is fine and the problem lies with the app.
  3. Change the port or socket: Try another USB port, plugged straight into the computer rather than into a hub. Run the test again.
  4. Try another computer: Plug the device into another machine, or a phone if it allows. If the fault follows the device, it is the device. If not, it is your computer or its settings.
  5. Check permissions and software: Browser permissions, system privacy settings, apps holding the device and remapping software: close or disable them one at a time.
  6. Update, restart, then contact support: A restart fixes more than people expect. If the device really is the problem, contact the seller or manufacturer with your notes in hand.

The principle: one change at a time

When nothing works, it is tempting to change everything at once. Then you cannot tell what fixed the problem or what caused it. Change one thing per step, then run the test again.

The four suspects

  • The device itself: wear, a fault, a weak battery.
  • The cable or connection: a charge-only cable with no data, a bad USB port, unstable Bluetooth.
  • The system: a driver, privacy settings, a device that is disabled or wrongly selected.
  • The software: browser, app or a macro tool getting in the way.

The tests on this site read the signal as your browser receives it. If the device shows up correctly there, you can rule out the first three suspects, at least for the browser.

By type of device

  • Mic and camera: mic not working on Windows 11, mic not working on Mac, webcam not detected, camera not working on Mac.
  • Keyboard: key not working.
  • Mouse: mouse double-clicking.
  • Controller: controller not recognized on PC, stick drift, Xbox controller on PC.
  • Screen: dead pixel vs stuck pixel, check a 144 Hz monitor.

Reading the test result

When the test shows the signal, ask what is different in the app: a different device, a muted switch, a setting that applies only there. When the test shows nothing, the cause is lower in the chain: the device, the cable, the port or the system. This single comparison saves a great deal of guesswork, and it is why we start with the browser test.

When it is the software

If the test passes but the app fails, look at the app's own settings first: input device, permissions, a hardware acceleration option or a conflicting plugin. Update the app and your browser, then restart the app. If one app fails and others work, reinstalling that app or resetting its settings is often quicker than chasing a system fault.

When it is the system

If the test fails on your computer but the device works on another, look at the operating system. Check that the device is enabled and set as the default, that privacy settings allow access, and that the driver is up to date. After an update, a setting can be reset, so do not assume nothing changed. Restart once the change is done.

Keep your proof

Note the purchase date, the model and a description of the fault, and take a photo or screenshot of the test result. It speeds up a warranty claim.

What the test cannot do

It does not replace a professional hardware diagnosis. It cannot see the state of a driver or the internal settings of a game. It only tells you whether the browser receives the signal it expects.

A few extra tips

Try the simplest explanations first: a muted device, a switch on the cable, a dead battery or a loose plug. Unplug and reconnect once before anything else. If you changed a setting just before the problem started, undo it. When you ask for help online, mention the model, your operating system and your browser, and what the test showed. That gives people something specific to work with.

Privacy

Each test runs in your browser and sends no sound, picture or keystroke anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Which device should I check first?

The one with the clearest fault. Use the matching test: it tells you right away whether the signal arrives.

When should I contact the warranty?

When the fault follows the device from one computer to another, after you have ruled out the cable, the port and the software.

Why does a device work in the test but not in my app?

The app may use another device, lack permission or be blocked by another program. Check its settings and your privacy settings.

Can a charge-only cable cause problems?

Yes. Some cables carry power but no data, so the device charges but does not respond. Try another cable.
