Simple tests that stay on your device

A mic nobody can hear, a webcam that looks too dark, a key that stopped working, a monitor you just unboxed: you want to know fast whether the problem is the device, the system or the app. Each test gives a clear answer, with a plain-English diagnosis and a link to the matching help page.

What is different here

  • Nothing is sent: sound, image and keystrokes stay in your browser.
  • You stay in control: the mic and camera start when you click and stop when you decide.
  • Useful error messages: access denied, device not found, device already in use: each case has its own explanation and steps.
  • No flashing: screen tests use solid, steady colors.

These tests are indicative. A confirmed hardware fault is a matter for the seller or the manufacturer.
