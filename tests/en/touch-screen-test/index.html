How to test a touch screen

  1. Place one finger in the area: a numbered circle follows it.
  2. Add two, three, four, then five fingers at the same time, and up to ten if you can. The maximum detected is displayed.
  3. Drag your fingers across the whole surface and into the corners: each finger should leave a continuous trace, with no jumps or breaks.
  4. Repeat over the whole area. A part that doesn't respond points to a fault in the panel or its protector.

What the number of points means

The number of “points” is how many fingers the screen can follow at once. The device reports a value itself. The test shows that value next to the maximum you actually put down. If the screen reports 10 points and only detects a few, check the screen protector, how clean the panel is and your system's mode.

If touch responds badly

  • Clean the screen with a soft, clean cloth, with the device switched off.
  • Take off the screen protector or the case, then test again.
  • Try with clean, dry hands.
  • Restart the device.

Pen or finger

The “contact type” tile shows what kind of contact the browser reports. A mouse click is reported as a mouse, not as a touch screen, so a desktop PC without a touch screen shows no fingers. That is expected.

Still not working?

Follow the steps in the guide how to troubleshoot a device that won't work. If a zone never responds, even when clean and without a protector, the touch layer or its sensor may be faulty: check your warranty or a repair shop.

Tips for a fair test

Test with the device on a table, not held in one hand while the other hand covers part of the screen. Charging cables, cases and thick gloves can all change how the panel behaves. If you use a stylus, test with it too: pens often report their contact differently from fingers. Run the test twice, once after a restart, to rule out a temporary glitch.

Privacy

The test shows the position of your fingers inside the page. It does not record or send them.

Frequently asked questions

My computer has no touch screen. What will happen?

A mouse click is reported as “mouse”, not as a touch screen. The test detects no finger, which is normal.

How many touch points should a touch screen detect?

It depends on the device: the system reports a value. The test shows that value next to the maximum you actually placed.

Part of my screen doesn't respond. Is it serious?

If it never responds, even when clean and without a protector, it is a fault of the touch panel or its sensor. Check your warranty or ask a repair shop.

Why does my touch screen not register multiple fingers?

A screen protector, dirt or moisture on the glass, or a system setting can cut multi-touch. Clean the screen, remove the protector and restart, then test again.
