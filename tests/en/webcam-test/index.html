How to test your webcam

  1. Click “Start test” and allow camera access when your browser asks.
  2. Your picture appears. The camera's indicator light usually turns on while it is in use.
  3. Check the resolution received and the measured frames per second. If the picture is black, the test tells you.
  4. Click “Take a photo” to judge sharpness, then save it to your device if you want to.

What the test measures

  • Real resolution: what the browser actually receives, not what is printed on the box. It can be lower than the resolution you asked for.
  • Frames per second (FPS): measured while you watch the preview. In a dim room many cameras lower their frame rate to see better, which is normal.
  • A black image: if the picture is almost black, the camera responds but sees nothing. Think of a closed privacy shutter, a dark room or a covered lens.

If it doesn't work

  • Access denied: see how to allow the camera in your browser. On a Mac, also look for Privacy & Security > Camera in System Settings (labels vary by version), or read camera not working on a Mac.
  • No camera found, or device busy: see webcam not detected.
  • Dark or blurry picture: see webcam dark or blurry and the guide webcam lighting and framing.

Why the resolution can be lower than expected

The browser asks the camera for a resolution, and the camera answers with what it can do. An old USB cable, a slow USB port, or another app already using the camera can also pull the resolution down. If you pick 4K and receive 720p, that is the result of this negotiation, not a bug in the test.

Our frame-rate reading is an estimate made while this page is in front. It is a good guide, not a lab measurement.

Privacy

The camera only opens after you click “Start test”. The picture stays in your browser: it is not recorded and not uploaded. The photo is created in memory in this tab, and it only goes to your device if you click the save link. The “Stop” button closes the camera.

Frequently asked questions

Is this webcam test safe?

Yes. The camera only opens after you click “Start test”, the picture is processed in your browser and nothing is recorded or uploaded. The “Stop” button closes the camera.

Why is the resolution lower than the one I selected?

The browser asks your camera for a resolution, and the camera replies with what it can do. A worn USB cable, a slow USB port or another app using the camera can also limit it.

Does the test work with an external webcam?

Yes. Allow access, then choose it in the “Camera to test” list.

Why is my picture flipped?

The mirror preview is on by default, as in most video call apps. Untick it to see the picture the way other people see it. The photo you take is not mirrored.
