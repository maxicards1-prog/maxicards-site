Why adding time goes wrong

Hours and minutes work in base 60, not base 10. Adding 2.45 and 3.30 on a regular calculator gives 5.75, but 2 h 45 min plus 3 h 30 min is 6 h 15 min. This calculator converts every entry to minutes, adds or subtracts them, and turns the result back into hours and minutes. Entries you leave blank count as zero, so you can use as many or as few of the eight rows as you need.

Formats you can type

  • 7:30 or 7h30 for 7 hours 30 minutes
  • 45min or 45 minutes for 45 minutes
  • 2 hours 15 minutes or 2h15m
  • 7.5 for 7.5 decimal hours (7 h 30 min)

Worked examples

Adding: 7 h 30 min + 6 h 45 min + 1 h 20 min = 14 h 95 min, which carries over to 15 h 35 min. That is 935 minutes, or 15.58 decimal hours.

Subtracting: choose the minus sign next to a row. 10 h 15 min − 45 min = 9 h 30 min, a handy way to take a lunch break out of a shift.

For a whole week with start and end times, the time card calculator is faster. To convert the total for payroll, see the decimal hours converter.

Good for

Totaling the length of videos or tracks, adding up study or practice sessions, summing the hours on several job tickets, or checking how long a multi-leg trip takes in total. Any total of 24 hours or more is also shown as full days plus the remainder.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add hours and minutes?

Add the minutes and the hours separately, then carry every 60 minutes into one hour. 1 h 40 min + 2 h 35 min is 3 h 75 min, which is 4 h 15 min.

How do I add 7.5 hours and 45 minutes?

7.5 hours is 7 h 30 min. Add 45 minutes to get 8 h 15 min, or 8.25 in decimal hours.

Can I subtract time too?

Yes. Change the plus sign to minus on any row. The total can be negative if you subtract more than you add.

How many minutes are in 15 h 35 min?

15 × 60 + 35 = 935 minutes.
