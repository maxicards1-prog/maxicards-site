Worked examples

  • Room: a 12 ft by 10 ft room has an area of 12 × 10 = 120 ft², which is 120 × 0.092903 = 11.15 m².
  • Circle: with a radius of 5, the area is π × 5² = 78.54 and the circumference is 2 × π × 5 = 31.42.
  • Triangle: a base of 8 and a height of 5 give 8 × 5 ÷ 2 = 20.
  • Triangle with three sides: the calculator uses Heron's formula, √(s × (s − a) × (s − b) × (s − c)), where s is half the perimeter. Sides of 3, 4 and 5 give s = 6 and an area of √(6 × 3 × 2 × 1) = 6.

Converting units

Area units are squared, so convert carefully: 1 ft² = 0.092903 m², 1 yd² = 9 ft² and 1 ft² = 144 in². Multiplying lengths in feet gives square feet directly.

Perimeter is the distance around a shape. It is useful for fencing, baseboards or trim, while area is what you need for flooring, paint or sod.

For an L-shaped or irregular space, split it into rectangles, find each area and add them up. When you buy flooring or paint, allow some extra for cuts and waste.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate square footage?

Multiply the length by the width, both in feet. A 15 ft by 12 ft room is 15 × 12 = 180 square feet.

How do I convert square feet to square meters?

Multiply by 0.092903. For example, 100 ft² is 9.29 m².

How many square feet are in a square yard?

There are 9: a yard is 3 feet, and 3 × 3 = 9.

How do I find the area of an irregular room?

Divide it into rectangles or triangles, calculate each area and add them together.
