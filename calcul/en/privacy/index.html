Privacy policy

What we collect (almost nothing) and how ads work.

Last updated: November 24, 2026.

Data controller

MAXICARDS, SASU, 60 rue François Ier, 75008 Paris, France. Contact: contact@maxicards.net.

What this site does not collect

There are no accounts, no forms and no analytics tool of our own (apart from advertising, see below). The numbers you type stay in your browser and are never sent to us.

The share link

When you change a calculation, your numbers are copied into the page address on your device, so that the “Copy the link” button can reopen the same calculation. This address is not sent to anyone unless you share it yourself. It may remain in your browser history.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). To serve pages, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type, possibly outside the European Union. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Google AdSense advertising

This site shows ads provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and identifiers to serve ads, limit how often you see them, measure their results and, with your consent, personalize ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • A Google consent message asks for your choice before any advertising cookie is set. You can change it at any time from the consent link shown on the site.
  • Learn more: how Google uses cookies in advertising.
  • To adjust or turn off ad personalization: Google Ad Settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct, erase or restrict your data, and object to its processing, by writing to contact@maxicards.net. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).
