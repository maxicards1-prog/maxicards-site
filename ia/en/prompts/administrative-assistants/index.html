ChatGPT Prompts for Administrative Assistants

Ten prompts to write faster, organize your day and make your documents more reliable, without exposing sensitive data.

In short

  1. Write meeting minutes
  2. Write a professional email
  3. Reword a letter
  4. Plan a complex meeting
  5. Create a tracking sheet
  6. Write an internal procedure
  7. Manage the day's priorities
  8. Translate and adapt a message
  9. Summarize a long document
  10. Prepare a business trip

Letters, minutes, schedules, reminders: an administrative assistant writes and organizes all day long. An AI can prepare first drafts, reword and structure your documents.

You often handle confidential information. The prompts below use fictional or generic data that you replace. Always review the result before sending it. Also keep a copy of your favorite templates to reuse from one week to the next.

  • Replace names with job titles or initials.
  • State the tone: formal, friendly, firm.
  • Have important letters approved.

Replace the parts in [brackets] with your own details, then paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot or Le Chat. To adapt a prompt, use the prompt generator.

1. Write meeting minutes

To turn notes into a readable document.

Here are my meeting notes: [paste the notes without sensitive data].
Write one-page minutes: date and attendees (by role), agenda, topics discussed, decisions made, actions to take with owner and due date, date of the next meeting. Add nothing that is not in my notes; flag ambiguous passages.

2. Write a professional email

For a clear, polite and effective message.

Write a professional email to [goal: request a document, confirm an appointment, follow up, inform]. Recipient: [role]. Information to include: [dates, documents, deadline].
[formal or friendly] tone, 100 words maximum, a precise subject line, a clear request and an appropriate closing. Suggest a shorter version for an internal chat message.

3. Reword a letter

To make a letter clearer or more diplomatic.

Here is a draft letter: [paste the text]
Rewrite it in a clear business style: short sentences, appropriate courtesies, structured with a subject line, background, request and deadline. [tone] tone. Keep all the facts. Flag missing information (references, dates, attachments).

4. Plan a complex meeting

To organize an event with several participants.

I am organizing a [length] meeting with [number] participants from [departments]. Goal: [goal]. Constraints: [room, video conference, lunch, availability].
Suggest: an agenda with the time for each item, a backward schedule of tasks (14 days before to 1 day after), the emails to send and their timing, and a checklist for the day itself.

5. Create a tracking sheet

To track requests, files or orders.

I need to track [type of files: time-off requests, supply orders, invoices to process] in a spreadsheet. Volume: [number] per month. Users: [number].
Propose the structure of the sheet: columns, dropdown lists, statuses, follow-up colors, useful formulas to detect delays. Explain how to set it up, step by step, with the formulas to copy.

6. Write an internal procedure

To document a recurring task.

Help me write a procedure for the task: [task]. Here is how I do it today: [unsorted steps].
Turn them into a procedure: purpose, people involved, equipment or access needed, numbered steps, points of attention, what to do if there is a problem. Short sentences in the imperative. Ask me any questions you need.

7. Manage the day's priorities

When several requests arrive at the same time.

Here are my tasks for today: [list with deadlines and requesters by role]. My available time: [hours].
Sort them by priority (urgent and important, important, can wait, delegate). Propose an order of work, the messages to send to negotiate a deadline, and a protected time slot for deep work.

8. Translate and adapt a message

To communicate with an overseas partner.

Translate the following message into [language] for [type of recipient]: [paste the message]
Adapt the tone to a professional context, keep dates and numbers as they are, and suggest a simplified version for a non-native reader. Flag expressions that could be misunderstood. A human reviewer must validate any important document.

9. Summarize a long document

To save time before a meeting.

Here is a [type: report, contract, memo] document, without personal data: [paste the text]
Give me a 150-word summary, then a list of the dates, amounts and obligations mentioned, and a list of 5 questions to ask. Summarize only what is in the text; flag ambiguous passages. I will read the full document for the important points.

10. Prepare a business trip

To organize a trip without forgetting a detail.

I am preparing a trip for [role] to [city] from [dates] for [purpose]. Constraints: [budget, travel policy, meeting times].
Propose a checklist: bookings, documents, receipts to keep, appointments, a plan B in case of delay. Add a template itinerary sheet to print. I will check the schedules and prices on the official sites.

Check before sending

An AI can be wrong about a date, an amount or an internal rule. Review every letter before sending and compare it with your sources. For binding documents (contracts, legal or HR letters), have them approved by the responsible person.

Data not to paste

You often have access to sensitive information. Do not paste personal data (identities, addresses, national ID numbers, bank details, employee or customer files), confidential contracts or strategic information. Replace names with job titles and amounts with approximate values. Privacy laws apply, and your employer may have rules on the use of AI: find out before you start.

Limits

In these prompts the AI has no access to your files or your inbox: it only knows what you give it. It prepares, you decide and you sign, even for the simplest letters.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use these prompts with my company's documents?

Only if your company's rules allow it and without sensitive data. Anonymize the content, or ask your manager or your data protection officer which tools are authorized.

Can the AI manage my calendar?

Not with these prompts: it has no access to your calendar. It can help you plan and write coordination messages, but you stay in charge of bookings and invitations.

How do I check a document summary?

Compare it with the original text, especially for numbers, dates and obligations. Ask the AI to quote the relevant passage for each important point, then find it in the document.
