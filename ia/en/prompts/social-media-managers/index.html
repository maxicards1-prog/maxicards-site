ChatGPT Prompts for Social Media Marketing

Ten prompts to plan your content, find ideas and write copy tailored to each platform.

Posting regularly takes ideas, time and consistency. An AI can help you plan, adapt a message to several formats and proofread your copy.

It does not know your brand: give it your target audience, your tone and your constraints. The prompts below ask for them explicitly. Then adapt them to your industry and your audience.

  • Describe your audience and your brand voice.
  • State the platform and the format.
  • Keep final approval: numbers, promises and visuals.

Replace the parts in [brackets] with your own details, then paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot or Le Chat. To adapt a prompt, use the prompt generator.

1. Monthly content calendar

To plan your posts without running out of ideas.

You are a social media manager. Create a [number]-week content calendar for [brand, business] on [platforms]. Target audience: [description]. Goals: [awareness, traffic, sales]. Frequency: [number] posts per week.
For each post: date, platform, theme, format (carousel, short video, photo), hook, call to action.
Mix useful content, behind-the-scenes, social proof and offers. Present a table.

2. Content ideas from a theme

When you are short of inspiration on a topic.

Suggest 15 content ideas for [platform] on the theme "[theme]", aimed at [target audience].
For each idea: a catchy title, the angle (educational, funny, testimonial, myth versus reality), and the recommended content format.
Avoid cliches and ideas that have been overdone. Sort the ideas by production effort, from quickest to longest.

3. Write a LinkedIn post

To turn an experience into a professional post.

Write a LinkedIn post of 150 to 200 words based on this real experience: [describe what happened and the lesson].
Structure: a first line that makes people want to read on, a short story, a concrete takeaway, a closing question to start a discussion. Sincere tone, short sentences, no jargon, three emojis at most.
Do not add any fact I did not provide. Suggest two different hooks.

4. Short video scripts

To prepare Reels, Shorts or TikToks.

Write 3 short video scripts of 30 seconds for [brand] on the topic [topic], aimed at [target audience].
Each script: a 3-second hook, 3 to 4 shots described with the spoken text, a call to action. Include the on-screen text and the length of each shot.
[tone] tone, fast pace, no false promises. My equipment: [smartphone, no special set].

5. A clear, useful newsletter

To write a regular newsletter without spending all day on it.

You are an editorial manager. Write a newsletter for [audience] from these items: [3 pieces of news or information].
Structure: subject line (3 options), a 2-sentence hook, three short blocks with subheadings, a single call to action, a sign-off.
Length: 250 words. [tone] tone. Remind me to include the unsubscribe link and any legally required information.

6. Ad copy to test

To create several versions of an ad to compare.

Create 5 ad variations for [advertising platform] promoting [product or service] to [target audience].
For each variation: headline (30 characters maximum), main text (90 characters maximum), call to action. Each variation uses a different angle: benefit, problem, proof, real urgency, curiosity.
No unverifiable promises, no medical or financial claims. My actual offer: [offer].

7. Reply to a negative comment

To keep a professional tone when facing criticism.

A customer posted this public comment: "[comment]". Our actual situation: [what happened and what we can do].
Write three replies of 60 words maximum: empathetic, factual, and one that offers to continue in a private message. Courteous tone, never defensive, no false apology. Tell me which one fits depending on how serious the complaint is.

8. Analyze campaign results

To interpret numbers and decide on next actions.

Here are the results of my campaign: [reach, clicks, conversions, costs, duration]. Original goal: [goal].
Help me analyze them: what works, what does not, 3 possible explanations to verify, 3 tests to run, with a metric to judge each test.
Stay cautious: say when the sample is too small to conclude.

9. Define a content strategy

To clarify your brand's tone and themes.

Help me define my content strategy. Business: [description]. Audience: [description]. Competitors: [types, without naming them]. What sets us apart: [real elements].
First ask me 6 questions. Then propose: 4 content pillars, a tone (3 adjectives and 3 things to avoid), sample sentences in that tone and a template for a typical post.

10. Repurpose content into several formats

To get the most out of an article or a video.

Here is content I have already published: [paste the text or summary].
Turn it into: a LinkedIn post, a thread of 5 messages, a 30-second short video script, 3 quotes to illustrate, a newsletter paragraph.
Each version must be adapted to its format and must not repeat the original word for word. Add no new information.

What to check

Check every number, every date and every claim before publishing. An AI can invent a statistic or a study. For health, finance or performance claims, follow the regulations and avoid unproven promises.

Law and transparency

Sponsored content must be labeled as such. Do not use a person's image or voice without their consent. AI-generated visuals must be checked before publication, especially for rights and for resemblance to existing works.

Data not to paste

Do not paste customer files, prospects' email addresses, private messages or confidential numbers. To analyze results, use aggregated or anonymized data. Privacy laws apply to your contact lists.

Limits

Generated texts all sound alike. What makes the difference is your point of view, your stories and your real proof. Use the AI to move faster, not to say the same thing as everyone else.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to say that a text was written with AI?

There is no general obligation for a text that you reviewed and stand behind, but some platforms or industry rules require labels for generated or sponsored content. Check the rules of each platform.

How do I keep my brand's tone?

Describe your tone with adjectives and paste two or three examples of texts you like. Ask the AI to follow them, then correct. The more examples you give, the more consistent it gets.

Can AI predict what will work?

No. It offers ideas and hypotheses, but only your tests and your results show what works with your audience. Run small experiments and keep what works.
