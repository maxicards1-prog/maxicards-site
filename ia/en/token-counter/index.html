Token Counter for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini

Everything happens in your browser: your text is not sent to any AI or any server, and there is no account, no API key and no limit.

What is a token?

AI models do not read letters or words, but tokens: chunks of text a few characters long. A short, common word ("the", "house") is often a single token; a long or rare word is split into several. Spaces, punctuation and emojis count too. Check "Show the tokens" to see each token in color.

Tokens matter for two things: API billing (a price per million input and output tokens) and the context limit (how much text a model can read at once).

An exact count for GPT, an estimate for Claude and Gemini

The counter uses the o200k_base tokenizer, the one used by OpenAI's GPT-4o and later models, through the open source gpt-tokenizer library, loaded from this site. The count is therefore exact for those models. Anthropic and Google have not published their tokenizers for offline use: they offer token counting through their API (with a key). For Claude and Gemini, the number shown is an estimate: the real number may be a little different.

Do other languages use more tokens than English?

Often, yes, because tokenizers were trained mostly on English text: accented letters and words from other languages are split into pieces more often. Paste the same text in two languages to compare: the "characters per token" figure shows the gap.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. The tool runs entirely in your browser, on your device: nothing is sent to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or to our servers, and nothing is saved. You can check it in the Network tab of your browser's developer tools.

How many tokens is a word?

It depends on the text and the language. Paste yours: the tool shows the exact number of tokens and the number of characters per token, which is better than a rule of thumb.

Is this counter exact for ChatGPT?

Yes for the text itself: it uses the same tokenizer (o200k_base) as recent GPT models. In a real conversation, a few formatting tokens are added (roles, system instructions).

Why does an emoji count as several tokens?

An emoji is encoded on several bytes, and the tokenizer often splits it into 2 to 4 tokens. Rare characters behave the same way.

Is there a size limit?

No, apart from your device's memory. A whole book works on a computer; the color view is limited to the first 3,000 tokens to stay smooth.
