Prompt Improver: Check and Fix Your Prompt

Paste your prompt: the tool checks 12 points and tells you what to add to get better answers.

  • Nothing is sent
  • No API key
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up

Everything happens in your browser: your text is not sent to any AI or any server, and there is no account, no API key and no limit.

How to do it

  1. Paste the prompt you use in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.
  2. Read the points marked ✗: each one says what is missing and how to fix it.
  3. Copy the improved version, fill in the [brackets], and compare the new answer with the old one.

What the tool checks

The analysis relies on simple rules drawn from the prompt writing guides published by OpenAI, Anthropic and Google: a task that starts with an action verb, a role, context, an audience, a format, a length, a tone, an example, constraints, a request to flag uncertainty, no vague words ("stuff", "kind of", "etc.") and enough detail. For a long pasted text, it also checks that the text is clearly separated from the instructions.

What the tool does not do: it does not understand the meaning of your request the way an AI would. It looks for clues in the text. A prompt can score 100 and still be unclear if the information it gives is wrong or too general; on the other hand, a very simple question ("What is the capital of Peru?") needs no role and no format. Use the score as a checklist, not as a grade.

Example

"Write a post about my new product" gets a low score: no role, no context, no audience, no format. The improved version becomes: "You are [an expert in the field]. Write a post about my new product. Context: [who I am...]. Audience: [...]. Format: [...]." Once the brackets are filled in ("I sell handmade candles in Austin, Instagram post for my customers aged 25 to 45, 80 words, 2 emojis at most"), the answer is a different story.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. The tool runs entirely in your browser, on your device: nothing is sent to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or to our servers, and nothing is saved. You can check it in the Network tab of your browser's developer tools.

Is the score calculated by an AI?

No, by fixed rules written by hand, which makes it instant, free and private. It does not judge the quality of your ideas, only whether the elements that help an AI answer well are there.

Should I aim for 100 / 100?

Not necessarily. A short factual question needs neither a role nor a format. Above 60, mainly check that the context and the format are there: they change the answer the most.

Does the tool work for prompts in other languages?

This English version looks for English wording. For prompts written in French, use the French version of the tool (FR link at the top of the page); for other languages, the score will be less reliable.
