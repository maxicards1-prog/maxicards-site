How to read these prices

  • Input: everything you send to the model (your question, the instructions, the documents, the conversation history).
  • Output: what the model writes. It costs 5 to 8 times more than input, depending on the model. For models that "think", reasoning tokens are billed as output.
  • Cached input: when you often send the same beginning of a request (instructions, a document), it can be read from a cache, at a much lower price.

Three ways to pay less

  1. Pick the smallest model that does the job: to sort, extract or classify, a small model often costs 10 to 40 times less than a large one (see the table).
  2. Limit the length of answers: output is the most expensive part.
  3. Batch processing: all three providers offer a discounted rate for non-urgent requests processed later.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. The tool runs entirely in your browser, on your device: nothing is sent to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or to our servers, and nothing is saved. You can check it in the Network tab of your browser's developer tools.

Are the prices up to date?

They were checked on November 23, 2026 on the official pages of OpenAI, Anthropic and Google, linked under the table. Providers change their prices often: check their page before a big commitment.

Are taxes included?

No. The three providers list their API prices in US dollars, before tax. Sales tax or VAT may be added depending on where you are, and your bank may charge a conversion fee if you pay in another currency.

ChatGPT Plus or the API: which one should I choose?

A subscription is for chatting with the AI in its app, for a fixed price. The API is for building the AI into a program, a website or an automation, and you pay for what you use. They are billed separately.
