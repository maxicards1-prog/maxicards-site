Custom Instructions Generator for ChatGPT

Set once and for all how the AI answers you: tone, length, level of detail, and what to avoid.

  • Nothing is sent
  • No API key
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up

Everything happens in your browser: your text is not sent to any AI or any server, and there is no account, no API key and no limit.

How to do it

  1. Enter your job, your level and what you use AI for.
  2. Set the tone and the length of the answers.
  3. Copy the two blocks.
  4. Paste them into your assistant's personalization settings (see below).

Where do these instructions go?

  • ChatGPT: in the settings, under personalization. Block 1 goes in the part that describes you, block 2 in the part that describes how ChatGPT should answer.
  • Claude: in your profile settings, in the personal preferences field; or in a Project's instructions for a specific use.
  • Gemini: in the saved instructions for Gemini, or in a Gem for a specific use.

Menu names often change from one version to the next: look for "personalization" or "instructions" in the settings.

Why it helps

Without instructions, the AI starts from scratch in every conversation: answers that are too long, a tone that does not sound like you, examples that do not fit your country. With a few well-chosen lines, every answer takes your job and your preferences into account, without repeating them every time. The "start with the short answer" instruction is often the one that saves the most time.

What not to put in them

Do not write sensitive information there: Social Security or ID numbers, your address, health data, passwords, confidential information about your clients or your employer. These instructions are sent with every conversation.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. The tool runs entirely in your browser, on your device: nothing is sent to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or to our servers, and nothing is saved. You can check it in the Network tab of your browser's developer tools.

Is there a length limit?

Yes, each assistant limits the size of these fields, and the limit changes between versions. The counter under each block helps you stay short: a few clear lines beat a long text.

Do the instructions apply to old conversations?

No, usually only to new conversations started after the change.

Can I have several sets of instructions?

Yes, with ChatGPT and Claude Projects or Gemini Gems: each one has its own instructions, for example one for work and one for school.
