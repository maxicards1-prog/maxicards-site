Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026. This page is an English translation of the French privacy policy; in case of difference, the French version prevails.

Data controller

MAXICARDS, SASU, 60 rue François Ier, 75008 Paris, France. Contact: contact@maxicards.net.

Your text is never sent to us

The tools run in your browser, on your device. The text you type or paste is not sent to MAXICARDS, to an AI or to any third party, and it is not saved: it disappears when you close the page. A security rule of the site (Content-Security-Policy) also prevents the pages from sending data to any service other than Google for advertising.

What the site does not collect

The site has no account, no form and no audience measurement tool of its own (apart from advertising, see below), and it stores nothing in your browser's storage.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). To serve the pages, GitHub processes technical data such as the IP address and the browser type, possibly outside the European Union. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Google AdSense advertising

This site shows ads provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and identifiers to serve ads, limit how often they repeat, measure their results and, with your consent, personalize ads based on your visits to this site and other sites. Advertising has no access to the text you type into the tools.

  • A Google consent message asks for your choice before any advertising cookie, where the law requires it. You can change your choice at any time from the consent management link shown on the site.
  • To learn more: how Google uses cookies in advertising.
  • To adjust or turn off ad personalization: Google Ad Settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask for access to, correction, deletion or restriction of your data, and object to its processing, by writing to contact@maxicards.net. You can also file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).
