How Much Does ChatGPT Cost?

ChatGPT has a free plan and several paid plans.

In short

Here is what each one adds, how to decide, and where to cancel.

The short answer: ChatGPT is free, with no subscription needed. Paid plans (Go, Plus, Pro) raise the limits and open more tools; none is required to try it. Prices change and vary by country, so check chatgpt.com/pricing, or our AI subscription comparison. The developer API is billed separately.

The individual plans

PlanWhat the official page says
FreeUnlimited text chats (subject to abuse guardrails); uploads, image creation, voice and deep research are limited
GoEverything in Free, with more messages with tools, more uploads, more images and more voice
PlusHigher model limits, priority access during high-traffic periods, voice, image generation, file uploads and deep research tools where available
ProA top plan for heavy use; see the pricing page for its content

There are also plans for organizations (Business, Enterprise), priced separately. Our comparison page lists the prices we checked, with the date.

Is Plus worth it?

Nobody can answer for you, but here is a simple test. Start with the free plan and note when you get stuck: a limit message, refused file uploads, no images left. If that happens several times a week and costs you time, a subscription makes sense. Otherwise, there is no rush. OpenAI's help says that when you move to Plus, Pro or Business, the limits you reached on the free plan are reset, so you lose nothing by waiting until you need more.

To decide, also read what the free version of ChatGPT includes: many common needs (drafting, summarizing, explaining) fit inside it. If you are weighing assistants against each other, which AI chatbot should I use compares uses.

How to cancel

According to OpenAI's help, you manage your subscription, view invoices and can cancel from the web app.

  1. Sign in to ChatGPT with the account tied to your Plus or Pro subscription.
  2. Click your profile icon, then Settings.
  3. Open Account and follow the options to manage or cancel the plan.

Note that the email address receiving invoices may differ from your ChatGPT account email. If you paid but the account still shows the free plan, or if you did not subscribe directly on OpenAI's site, the help page describes the steps to follow.

What about the API?

According to OpenAI's help, API usage is not included in Plus and is billed independently. If you build apps and want to estimate a cost from the amount of text, use the API price calculator and the token counter.

Features and limits read on OpenAI's official pages on 26/11/2026.

Frequently asked questions

How much is ChatGPT Plus?

Check the current figure on chatgpt.com/pricing or our comparison page: prices vary by country and change. Plus is the most common paid plan.

How much is ChatGPT Pro?

Pro is the top individual plan, for very heavy use. Its current price and content are on OpenAI's pricing page.

Is ChatGPT Plus worth it?

It depends on your limits: try the free plan first and pay only if you keep hitting its limits. Your limits are reset if you upgrade to Plus.

How much does ChatGPT cost per month?

Nothing for the free plan. Paid plans are billed monthly; see the pricing page for the current amounts in your country.

How to cancel ChatGPT Plus?

Sign in, click your profile icon, open Settings, then Account, and follow the options to manage or cancel the plan.

How much does ChatGPT Business cost?

Business is a plan for organizations and is priced separately, per seat; see OpenAI's pricing page for the current terms.
