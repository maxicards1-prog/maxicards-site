What Is Gemini AI?

Gemini is Google's AI assistant.

In short

You use it in a browser or an app to write, summarize, learn or create images.

Short answer. Gemini is Google's artificial intelligence assistant. On a computer you open it at gemini.google.com; on an Android phone you use the app, which requires signing in. It helps you brainstorm ideas, get simpler summaries of complex topics and create first drafts of outlines, emails and blog posts (Google help page, checked 26/11/2026).

Yes, Gemini is an AI

Gemini is a generative AI tool: you write a request in plain language and it produces text, an analysis or, in some cases, an image. The name "Gemini" refers both to Google's family of models and to the consumer app that lets you use them. In Google's help center the app is presented as a way to supercharge your ideas.

How to access it

  1. On a computer: go to gemini.google.com. The help page lists Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Opera and Edge as supported browsers.
  2. On Android: open or install the Gemini app. The help page says the mobile app on Android requires signing in.
  3. Sign in with a Google Account if you want chat history, Gems, image generation and file upload: the help page says these require a Google Account.

For getting started, plans and limits, read is Gemini free.

Can you use it without a Google account?

Partly. The official help page says some features work without signing in, but chat history, Gems, image generation and file upload need a Google Account, and the Android app asks you to sign in. Availability changes by country and version, so open the official page to see what is offered where you live.

Gemini on a phone

When people ask "what is Gemini on my phone", the answer is Google's assistant app, which can answer questions and help you write, brainstorm or summarize. We did not find an official page describing step by step how to remove it or replace it with another assistant on every phone model, so we do not detail that procedure. Menus vary by device and version: look in your phone's assistant settings.

Age, work and school accounts

According to the Google help page, on a personal Google Account the person managing Family Link can turn off Gemini access for users under 13, or the age that applies in their country. A work account requires a qualifying Google Workspace edition and a user aged 18 or older, and a school account depends on the institution's administrator enabling the service. For images, other age rules apply: see can Gemini edit photos.

What to do with Gemini

  • Write: emails, outlines, article drafts. To ask well, see how to prompt Gemini.
  • Understand: ask for a simplified explanation of a hard topic, then examples.
  • Create images with Nano Banana, covered in how to use Nano Banana.
  • Compare with other assistants: ChatGPT vs Gemini and which AI chatbot should I use.

A simple first test

To judge Gemini, give it a task whose answer you already know. For example:

Explain [a topic you know well] in 5 sentences as if I were 15, then suggest 3 questions to test my understanding.

Check the clarity, any mistakes and the level of detail. If you know nothing about the subject you cannot spot errors, which is the best reason to start with a familiar topic.

Precautions

Like any generative AI, Gemini can produce wrong but plausible answers: reread and verify important facts. Avoid entering personal or confidential data unless you have read your account's privacy settings. For paid plan prices, the AI subscription comparison lists the prices the site recorded on a given date, which may have changed since.

Finally, do not confuse Gemini with other things that share the name, such as the zodiac sign or the space program: this page is only about Google's AI assistant.
