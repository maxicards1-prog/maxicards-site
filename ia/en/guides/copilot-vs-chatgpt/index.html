Copilot vs ChatGPT: What Is the Difference?

Copilot is Microsoft's assistant, ChatGPT is OpenAI's.

In short

They look alike on screen, but their place in your tools is not the same.

The short answer: they are two products from two companies: Copilot belongs to Microsoft and is built into Windows, Edge and Microsoft 365; ChatGPT belongs to OpenAI and lives mostly on its own site and apps. Both have a free version and subscriptions. The AI models used change often: check them in the app rather than trusting outdated lists.

When to prefer one or the other

Copilot, if you live in the Microsoft ecosystem

If you mostly work in Word, Excel or Outlook, Copilot has the advantage of being inside those apps for owners of a suitable Microsoft 365 subscription. For occasional use, the free version, usable without an account, is convenient.

ChatGPT, if you want a standalone assistant

ChatGPT is designed as a workspace of its own: chat, images, voice, files, search, and a work mode connected to your apps. See how to use ChatGPT.

An honest test

Give the same request to both and compare: same text, same instruction, same constraints. The prompt generator helps you write one identical, complete request. Judge accuracy, clarity and respect for the format. Results vary from task to task, and no general comparison holds for long.

Does Copilot use ChatGPT?

We do not claim it: the official Microsoft pages we read do not name the models behind Copilot, and only mention a choice between several AI models in the app. Agreements between companies and the models on offer change; the right habit is to look at the list shown in the Copilot app, or Microsoft's information page.

What is the same on both sides

  • Both can be wrong: always check what matters.
  • Both call for care with confidential data until you know the privacy settings.
  • Both answer better to a precise request: see how to write a good prompt.

Frequently asked questions

Is Copilot the same as ChatGPT?

No. Copilot is Microsoft's assistant and ChatGPT is OpenAI's. They are separate products with separate plans.

Copilot vs ChatGPT which is better?

It depends on the task and your tools: Copilot suits Microsoft 365 users, ChatGPT is a standalone workspace. Test the same request on both rather than trusting rankings, which age fast.

Copilot vs ChatGPT for business?

Both have organization offers: Microsoft describes Copilot Chat with eligible licenses, and OpenAI lists Business and Enterprise plans. Compare the current terms on their pages.

Copilot vs ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Claude?

Four different assistants from four companies. Our guide on which AI chatbot to use compares typical uses; test your own requests on each.

Is Copilot an LLM?

Copilot is an assistant product built on AI language models. Microsoft's pages we read do not detail which ones; check the model list in the app.

Is Copilot generative AI?

Yes: it writes text, answers questions and creates images, which is what generative AI does.
