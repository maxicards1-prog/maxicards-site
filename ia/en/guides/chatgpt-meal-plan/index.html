How to Plan Your Weekly Meals With ChatGPT

ChatGPT is a good organizing assistant: meal ideas, grocery lists, recipes from what is left in the fridge.

In short

For a diet or a health question, you need to see a professional.

  1. List your constraints
  2. State tastes and refusals
  3. Flag real allergies
  4. Ask for a 7-day table
  5. Adjust

The question “what's for dinner tonight?” comes back every day and is more tiring than you would think. An AI can settle it for the whole week: you give your constraints (time, budget, tastes, equipment), it proposes a menu table and the matching grocery list. This guide is about organization, not nutrition.

Important: a menu suggested by an AI is not health advice. Allergy, intolerance, illness, pregnancy, a weight-loss or weight-gain diet, feeding a baby or an elderly person: ask a doctor, a dietitian or another health professional, and don't follow a medical meal plan that came out of an AI.

How to do it

  1. List your constraints: number of people, meals concerned (lunch, dinner), weekday prep time, budget, equipment (oven, stovetop, microwave, slow cooker).
  2. State tastes and refusals: foods nobody likes, favorite dishes, cultural habits or beliefs.
  3. Flag real allergies and check every recipe and every label yourself. The AI can be wrong about an ingredient.
  4. Ask for a 7-day table, then the grocery list grouped by aisle.
  5. Adjust: replace dishes you don't like, and ask it to reuse leftovers from one day to the next.

Prompt for a weekly menu

You are a meal organization assistant, not a health professional.
Prepare my menus from Monday to Friday for [number] people: lunch and dinner.
Constraints: dinners ready in under 30 minutes, budget of about [amount] dollars for the week, equipment: [oven, stovetop, microwave].
What we like: [list]. What we don't like or don't eat: [list].
Allergies to respect strictly: [list, or "none"]. I will check each recipe myself.
Present the result in a table (day, meal, dish, prep time). Include two meals that reuse leftovers from other days.
Do not give any diet instructions or nutritional intake targets.

Prompt for the grocery list

From the menu table above, make the grocery list for the week for [number] people.
Group it by aisle (produce, dairy, pantry, frozen, meat or seafood, dry goods).
Give approximate quantities, then a section "already at home?" for the basics I may already have (oil, salt, rice, pasta, spices).
For two expensive ingredients, suggest a cheaper alternative.

Prompt "with what I have in the fridge"

I have in my fridge and cupboards: [precise list with quantities and approximate expiry dates].
Suggest 3 simple meals for tonight that use first what needs to be eaten first.
For each meal: ingredients to add (3 maximum), steps in under 8 lines, total time.
Remind me to check cooking times and food storage myself, especially for meat, fish and leftovers.

Good habits

  • Check food safety: cooking, storage, expiry dates. The official instructions on the product and from health authorities come first.
  • Check quantities: a recipe for 4 given by an AI may be poorly proportioned.
  • Keep the menus that went over well in a document, so you don't have to ask for them again.
  • For a family, involve the kids in choosing: it works better than an imposed plan.

Adapt menus to real life

A plan that is perfect on paper often fails on a tired Wednesday night. Plan a “joker” evening (simple dishes, frozen food, leftovers) and a bigger day for cooking larger batches. You can ask the AI to plan both kinds of evenings, and to suggest prep to do on the weekend to save time during the week.

Take my weekly menu and add: a "joker" dinner in under 15 minutes, a prep task to do on Sunday (which one, how long, how to store it following the usual guidelines), and a take-along lunch idea.
Give the prep time for each day and the moment when I need to take something out of the freezer the day before.

Frequently asked questions

Can ChatGPT put me on a diet?

It replaces neither a doctor nor a dietitian. For a diet, an illness, an allergy or a pregnancy, ask a health professional.

Can it make my grocery list?

Yes: give it the menu table and ask for a list grouped by aisle, with the basics to check at home.

Are the suggested recipes reliable?

Not always. Check quantities, cooking, times and ingredients, especially if there is an allergy.
