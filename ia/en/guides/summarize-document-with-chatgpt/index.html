Summarize a PDF, Word File or Text With ChatGPT

Summarizing is one of the most practical uses of AI, as long as you frame the request well and remember that a summary can omit or distort an important detail.

In short

  1. Check that the document can be shared.
  2. Attach the file or paste an excerpt
  3. Specify the audience, the purpose and the format
  4. Ask for the points to verify
  5. Check

A forty-page report, meeting minutes, an instruction leaflet: we often want the essentials in a few lines. ChatGPT can produce a summary, but a bare “summarize this document” gives an average text that doesn't say what matters to you. Better to specify for whom, what the summary will be used for and in what form.

What ChatGPT accepts (help page consulted on Nov 23, 2026)

According to OpenAI's help page on file uploads, you can attach DOCX, PDF, TXT, Markdown, HTML and JSON files, among others, as well as spreadsheets and presentations. For text documents, the stated limit is 2 million tokens per file, and 512 MB per file for documents. Free accounts are limited to 3 file uploads per day, and these limits can be lowered at peak times. A Google Docs file must first be exported as PDF or DOCX. To see what a length of text represents in tokens, use our token counter.

How to do it

  1. Check that the document can be shared. Contract, medical record, customer or employee data, “confidential” document: don't send them to an online service without permission.
  2. Attach the file or paste an excerpt (one chapter at a time for a very long document).
  3. Specify the audience, the purpose and the format of the summary.
  4. Ask for the points to verify: where in the document to find each claim in the summary (page, section title).
  5. Check by rereading at least the passages on which you will decide or act.

Structured summary prompt

You are an executive assistant who prepares briefing notes.
Here is a document of [number] pages about [topic]: [attach the file or paste the text].
I must present it to [whom] to decide on [decision to make].
Give me: 1) a summary in 5 sentences maximum; 2) the 5 key points as a list; 3) the important figures and dates in a table; 4) the decisions or actions requested; 5) what remains unclear or missing in the document.
For each key point, indicate the section or page of the document it comes from.
Use only what is written in the document. If information is not there, write "not specified."

Prompt for meeting minutes

Here are my raw meeting notes: [paste your notes, without unnecessary personal data].
Turn them into clear minutes with: the subject and date, the decisions made, the actions to take (who, what, by when, as a table), the open points.
Do not add any name, date or decision that is not in my notes. If an action has no owner, write "to be assigned."

Prompt to summarize a long document in several passes

I am going to give you a long document in [number] parts. For each part, answer only with a 3-sentence summary and the list of important terms.
When I have sent the last part and written "END," you will write a 10-line overall synthesis and flag any contradictions between the parts.
Here is part 1: [text]

Limits to keep in mind

  • A summary is not the document. A clause, an exception or a figure can disappear. For a contract, an invoice or a regulatory text, read the original.
  • Errors are possible, including added details that are not in the text. Ask for the references and check them.
  • A scanned or badly formatted PDF may be misread: check that the text is actually picked up.
  • Confidentiality: according to OpenAI's help page on ChatGPT, conversations on the individual services may be used to train models, with data settings to check in your preferences. Read ChatGPT and personal data.

Choose the right summary length

Depending on the use, the summary doesn't take the same form: five lines to know whether the document concerns you, a page to inform a team, a table to compare several documents. Tell the AI which form you want, otherwise it will choose a medium length that is useful to no one.

Here are two documents on the same subject: [document A] and [document B].
Compare them in a table: common points, differences, figures that diverge, elements present in only one.
Don't draw conclusions for me. End with the questions I should ask the author of each document.

Frequently asked questions

Which files can I send to ChatGPT to summarize?

OpenAI's help page lists PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown, HTML and JSON, among others. Size and upload limits depend on your plan.

Is the summary reliable?

It can omit a point or add a detail. Ask for the source sections for each point and reread the passages that matter.

Can I summarize a contract?

For a first read without personal data, it is possible, but a summary does not replace reading the contract or the advice of a legal professional.

What do I do with a document that is too long?

Split it into chapters, summarize each part, then ask for an overall synthesis based on the summaries.
