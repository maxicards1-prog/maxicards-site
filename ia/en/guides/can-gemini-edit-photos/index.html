Can Gemini Edit Photos?

Yes, Gemini can edit an image you upload, subject to account, age and availability conditions.

In short

Here is how, and why it sometimes refuses.

Short answer. Yes. According to Google's official help page (checked 26/11/2026), you can create images from a description, edit images that Gemini generated, or upload your own image and ask for edits. You need to be signed in to the Gemini app, be 18 or older to generate and edit images, and be in a supported country and language.

How to do it, step by step

  1. Open Gemini and sign in with your Google Account.
  2. Upload your photo into the conversation (file upload requires an account).
  3. Describe one clear change at a time, for example "Keep the face and clothes, replace the background with a beach at sunset".
  4. Look at the result, then ask for a precise adjustment: "brighter", "remove the object on the right".
  5. Download the version you like.

The writing method is covered in Nano Banana (Gemini): create and edit images. For description ideas, try the image prompt generator.

The conditions to know

  • Account. You must be signed in to Gemini. School accounts may have access restrictions.
  • Age. The help page says you must be 18 or over to generate and edit images, and 13 (or the applicable age in your country) or over to generate images. Editing is not available to users under 18.
  • Country and language. Availability is limited to the supported languages and countries of each app.
  • Models. The help page names several versions of the image model (Nano Banana 2, Nano Banana Lite, Nano Banana Pro). The Lite version is not designed for multiple image references or multiple edits, and redoing an image with Nano Banana Pro requires a Google AI plan.

Why Gemini will not edit my photos

Several causes are possible, all tied to the conditions above:

  • you are not signed in, or you use a restricted school account;
  • the account belongs to someone under 18, for whom editing is not available;
  • the feature is not yet offered in your country or language;
  • your daily quota is reached (see below);
  • the request seems to break the terms of service or the prohibited use policy: the help page says Gemini may remove images when its systems detect a possible violation.

If none of this applies, rephrase. A vague instruction ("improve the photo") works less well than a precise instruction about a single change.

Why Gemini stopped generating images

The first thing to check is your quota. The help page mentions a daily quota of Nano Banana 2 images: once it is reached, redoing an image with Nano Banana Pro is unavailable. The page we read gives no exact number of images per day, and we do not invent one. The limit depends on your plan and can change. Check your account, then use the AI subscription comparison to compare plans.

Animated GIFs

The help page also mentions creating short animated GIFs (about three seconds), rolling out gradually, limited to some countries and requiring a Google AI plan. In that context Gemini does not produce long videos, so read the current conditions on the official page before counting on it.

Example edit requests

On this photo, remove the person in the background on the left and keep everything else identical.
Make this photo brighter and warmer without changing the faces or the composition.

One instruction per message is usually easier to correct than a list of ten changes. If the result drifts away from the original, start again from the original photo.

Good practice and limits

  • Edit only photos you have the right to use, and respect people's image: do not upload a third party's photo without their consent.
  • A realistic edit is not proof: if you publish a modified image, say so when it matters.
  • The help page we read mentions no watermark or label on generated images, so do not assume one exists.
  • Always keep the original: save the starting photo before testing changes.

Frequently asked questions

Can Gemini edit photos?

Yes. You can upload your image and ask for changes, provided you are signed in, an adult and in a supported country, according to Google's help page of 26/11/2026.

How do I edit a photo with Gemini?

Sign in, upload the photo in the conversation, describe one precise change at a time, then adjust with short requests before downloading the result.

Why won't Gemini edit my photos?

Common causes: not signed in or a restricted school account, under 18, feature not available in your country, quota reached, or a request judged against the terms of use.

How many images can Gemini generate per day?

The Google help page we read gives no number. It mentions a daily quota that depends on your plan. Check your account for your own limit.

Can Gemini make videos?

The help page only mentions short animated GIFs of about three seconds, in some countries and with a Google AI plan. For anything else, read the official conditions.
