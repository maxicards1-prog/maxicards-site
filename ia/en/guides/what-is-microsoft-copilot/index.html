What Is Microsoft Copilot?

Copilot is Microsoft's artificial intelligence assistant.

In short

Depending on where you meet it, it is not quite the same product: here is how to tell them apart.

The short answer: Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant. Its consumer version is free: you ask it questions, ask it to write or create images, on the web, in an app or in the Edge browser. In Microsoft 365 subscriptions, Copilot is also built into Word, Excel and other apps, with usage limits.

One brand, several places

The name “Copilot” covers several uses, hence the confusion. According to Microsoft's support pages, you can tell them apart like this.

WhereWhat it is
Free Copilot (website, app, Edge)An assistant that gives direct answers, helps with writing and creates images. Available at copilot.cloud.microsoft, as an app (Windows, macOS, iOS, Android) and in Edge
The Microsoft Copilot appDescribed by Microsoft as an AI app for work: questions, creating images and Word, Excel or PowerPoint files, a choice between several AI models, multistep tasks with Cowork
Copilot in Microsoft 365AI features inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook for owners of a Personal, Family or Premium subscription, with usage limits
Copilot Chat (work or school)A version for organizations, included with some Microsoft 365 licenses; work data (files, email) requires an extra paid license

What you can do with Copilot

Microsoft's support page on the free version mentions help with writing, brainstorming and summarizing, answers based on real-time web search and, in Edge, summaries and insights for web pages. The Copilot app adds uploading or creating files and delegating tasks. For ideas on what to ask, the prompt generator and the prompt library work with any assistant, Copilot included.

Do you need an account?

Microsoft says sign-in is not required for the free version, but signing in with a personal account adds chat history, image creation, longer conversations and voice. You can sign in with a Microsoft account, an Apple ID or a Google account, or with a work or school account.

How to do it: try it

  1. Open copilot.cloud.microsoft in your browser, or install the Copilot app from the Microsoft Store (Windows), the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Write a precise request, with the context and format you want, exactly as with ChatGPT.
  3. Read the answer, check the facts, then ask for corrections.

Copilot and ChatGPT are not the same thing

They are two assistants from two different companies. The Copilot app page says it lets you choose between several AI models without listing them: check the app to see which ones your account offers. To compare uses, read which AI chatbot should I use, or our page Copilot vs ChatGPT.

Good to know before using it

  • Features vary by account type, country, device and subscription; Microsoft changes them often.
  • Like any AI, Copilot can be wrong: check facts and do not paste confidential data.
  • If you do not want the app on Windows, it can be uninstalled: see how to uninstall Copilot on Windows 11.

Frequently asked questions

What is Copilot app?

It is Microsoft's AI app: ask questions, create images and files, choose between several AI models and delegate multistep tasks with Cowork. It runs on Windows, Mac, iOS and Android.

What is Copilot on my computer?

On Windows it is the Microsoft Copilot app, an AI assistant you open from the PC. According to Microsoft it can be uninstalled from Settings, Apps.

What is Copilot on my laptop?

The same app or the website copilot.cloud.microsoft. If your laptop has a Copilot key, you can change what it launches in Settings.

What is Copilot Cowork?

Microsoft presents Cowork as a Copilot app feature for delegating multistep tasks while keeping control.

What is Copilot used for?

Questions, writing help, brainstorming, summaries, image creation and, with a Microsoft 365 plan, AI features in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook.

What is Copilot Microsoft?

Microsoft's AI assistant, available free on the web, in apps and in Edge, and built into Microsoft 365 for subscribers.
