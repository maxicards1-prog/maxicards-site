How to Prepare a Business Plan With ChatGPT

ChatGPT can help you structure and write your business plan.

In short

It knows neither your local market nor your numbers: yours are the ones that must carry it.

  1. Write your project in 10 lines
  2. Ask ChatGPT for the structure
  3. Have it write section by section
  4. Gather the data yourself
  5. Do the calculations with your assumptions
  6. Have a human review it

A business plan serves to convince (a bank, an investor, a partner) but also to force you to look your project in the eye. An AI can save you time on structure and writing. It cannot invent your market, your prices or your forecasts. A business plan filled with plausible but false numbers is worse than a simple business plan.

What a business plan contains

A typical business plan presents the project from several angles: the founders, the product or service, the market and customers, the operations and equipment, the team and its cost, the financial forecasts and the legal structure. On the numbers side, the usual minimum is a projected income statement, a funding plan and a break-even calculation. Before writing, check the guidance published by the small business agency or business support network in your country, and follow their template if a lender asks for one.

How to do it

  1. Write your project in 10 lines: who your customer is, what problem you solve, how you make money.
  2. Ask ChatGPT for the structure suited to your type of business, then compare it with the list above.
  3. Have it write section by section, from your facts, not from nothing.
  4. Gather the data yourself: market prices, local competitors, real costs, quotes. Mark as “to verify” everything that comes from the AI.
  5. Do the calculations with your assumptions, in a spreadsheet, and ask the AI to review the logic, not to decide the numbers.
  6. Have a human review it: an adviser from a business support network, an accountant, an entrepreneur in the same field.

Prompt to clarify the project

You are a demanding business start-up adviser. I'm going to present my project: [description in 10 lines].
First ask me 10 questions about what is unclear or unproven: target customer, real problem, price, competition, resources, risks.
Don't write a business plan yet. After my answers, you will summarize my project in one paragraph and list the 5 most fragile assumptions I need to test.

Prompt to write a section

Write the "Offer and market overview" section of my business plan, in 350 words, in a clear and professional style.
Use only this information: [my product, my target customers, my planned prices, what I observed in the field, my known competitors].
Do not invent any figure, study or market share. Wherever a numeric data point would be useful, write [DATA TO SOURCE] and tell me where to look (type of organization, type of document).
End with 3 questions a banker would ask me about this section.

Prompt to test the plan's strength

Here is my simplified forecast: [assumptions on price, volumes, monthly costs, starting investment].
Play a skeptical lender. List the possible inconsistencies, the overly optimistic assumptions and the costs often forgotten in this type of business.
Don't redo my calculations and don't suggest any figure: ask me questions that force me to justify each assumption.

Confidential data: be careful

A business plan contains strategic information. Don't paste confidential data (trade secrets, detailed accounts, information about third parties) into an online AI without having read its terms. According to OpenAI's help page on ChatGPT, conversations on the individual services may be used to train models; look for the data controls in your settings. You can also work with an anonymized version: rounded amounts, names replaced.

What AI does not replace

  • Real market research: talking to your future customers, looking at the prices charged.
  • Personalized legal, tax and financial advice: go to a professional or a business support network.
  • Forecasts: they rest on your assumptions, which you must be able to defend.

Prepare to meet a lender

A business plan is defended out loud. Use the AI as a practice partner: ask it to play the other side and ask you the uncomfortable questions (why this price, what happens if sales are twice as slow, who are your real competitors). Write down your answers and fix the document where you hesitate.

You play an adviser meeting a business founder for the first time. Here is my project in 10 lines: [project].
Ask me one question at a time, starting with the hardest. After each of my answers, tell me what is convincing, what remains vague, and what I should specify in writing in my document.
Stop after 8 questions and give me a 5-point assessment.

Frequently asked questions

Can ChatGPT write my whole business plan?

It can draft sections from your information, but it knows neither your local market nor your numbers. The data and assumptions must come from you.

What financial elements are needed at a minimum?

Usually a projected income statement, a funding plan and a break-even calculation. Check what your lender or support network asks for.

Can I trust an AI-generated market study?

No. Generated figures can be wrong. Look for verifiable sources and note them in your document.
