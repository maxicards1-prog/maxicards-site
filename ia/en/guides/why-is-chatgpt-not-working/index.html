Why Is ChatGPT Not Working?

When ChatGPT stops responding, the cause is often an outage on OpenAI's side, a usage limit or a chat that has become too long.

In short

Here is how to sort it out in minutes.

The short answer: three causes come up most: an outage or overload at OpenAI (check status.openai.com), a usage limit reached on your account, or a conversation that has become too long. If the status page is green, reload the page, reopen the app, then try a private browsing window.

How to do it: a six-step diagnosis

  1. Check OpenAI's status page (status.openai.com). ChatGPT's own help points to this page when an error appears on the first message of a new chat. If an incident is under way there is nothing to fix on your side: try again later.
  2. Read the message on screen. If ChatGPT says a limit was reached, it shows when it resets. Uploads, image creation, voice and data analysis each have a limit separate from text chats, according to OpenAI's help.
  3. Check whether the conversation is full. The “context window full” or “chat memory full” notices concern the current conversation, not your account. OpenAI's help says you can start a new chat right away and paste in only the passage you need.
  4. Reload the page or restart the app, then reopen the conversation from your history.
  5. Try a private browsing window and sign in to the same account. If it works there, a browser extension or cached data was the problem.
  6. Switch network or device (Wi-Fi then mobile data, computer then phone) to see whether the issue comes from your connection.

The most common causes

An outage on the service side

When many people connect at once, the service can slow down or return errors. It is not related to your device. The status page shows whether ChatGPT is affected and when the incident is resolved. Paid plans offer priority access during high traffic, according to OpenAI's help on Plus; that does not remove outages but may reduce interruptions.

A usage limit

The free plan offers extended text chats, but tools (images, files, voice, deep research) are limited. If one tool stops responding while text messages still go through, it is probably a limit. More in what the free version of ChatGPT includes.

A conversation that is too long

Your messages and the replies both take up room. A few long exchanges can take more than many short ones. Instead of losing everything, open a new chat and paste only what matters: the instruction, the text to work on and your constraints.

A connection or browser problem

An outdated browser, an extension that blocks scripts or an expired session can stop the page from loading. A private window is the quickest way to rule this out.

When the answer is bad rather than blocked

If ChatGPT answers but misses the point, the problem is different: see why ChatGPT gives wrong answers. A more precise request with context and format fixes most of it; the prompt improver can help you rephrase.

If nothing works

Note the exact error message, the time and the device, then use OpenAI's official help center (help.openai.com). Avoid third-party sites promising to “repair” ChatGPT: they have no access to your account and may try to collect your login details. Frequently asked questions

Why is ChatGPT not working?

Most often an outage or overload at OpenAI, a usage limit, or a conversation that is too long. Check status.openai.com first, then reload and try a new chat.

Why is ChatGPT slow?

Usually heavy traffic or an incident on OpenAI's side: check the status page. A very long conversation or a heavy browser tab can also slow things down.

Why is ChatGPT not working today?

An outage is possible: OpenAI's status page says so. Otherwise check whether a usage limit on your plan has been reached.

Why is ChatGPT not loading?

Try a reload, a private browsing window and another network. If those fail, check the status page for an incident.

Why is ChatGPT not responding?

Look for a limit message, then try a new conversation. If the same short message fails in a fresh chat, check OpenAI's status page.

Is ChatGPT down?

Only the official status.openai.com page tells you reliably. Third-party outage trackers show complaint spikes, not always a real outage.
