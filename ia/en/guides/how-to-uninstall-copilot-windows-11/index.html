How to Uninstall Copilot on Windows 11

Yes, the Copilot app can be removed like any other.

In short

Here are the steps according to Microsoft, and what they do not cover.

The short answer: yes. On Windows 11, open Settings, Apps, Installed apps, find Copilot, open its “...” menu and choose Uninstall. That is the method Microsoft documents. It removes the app; it does not, by itself, turn off a Microsoft 365 subscription or the Copilot features it includes.

How to do it: uninstall the Copilot app

  1. Open Settings (Windows key + I).
  2. Go to Apps, then Installed apps.
  3. Find Copilot in the list.
  4. Click the three dots “...” on the right of the app.
  5. Choose Uninstall and confirm.

On Windows 10, Microsoft's support page says to go to Settings, Apps, select Copilot, then Uninstall. The official page states these steps for Windows only; they do not cover Mac or mobile.

If you only want it out of the way

You can keep the app without using it. If your keyboard has a Copilot key, you can change what it launches: in Settings, Personalization, Text input, the setting “Customize Copilot key on keyboard”. Microsoft's documentation lists the choices Search, Custom, or the currently mapped app; “Custom” opens the list of apps that can be assigned to the key. Menu names vary with your Windows version and language: search for “Copilot” in the Settings search box.

On a work computer

On a PC managed by a company, an administrator can prevent installation or remove the app. Microsoft documents two methods for administrators: an AppLocker rule that blocks installation and launch of the Microsoft.Copilot package, or removal with a PowerShell command. It adds that the AppLocker rule should replace the older “Turn Off Windows Copilot” policy setting, which is due to be deprecated. If your PC is managed by IT, ask them rather than trying yourself.

If you remove Copilot and want it back, Microsoft says to reinstall it from the Microsoft Store.

What uninstalling does not do

  • Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint or Outlook: these features depend on your Microsoft 365 subscription and each app's settings, not on the Windows app. Check the help for the app concerned or manage your subscription.
  • Copilot on the web or in Edge: they stay reachable as long as you do not use them less.
  • Your past conversations: they are tied to your account. Check your Microsoft account privacy options before deleting anything.

Frequently asked questions

How to uninstall Copilot Windows 11?

Settings, Apps, Installed apps, the “...” menu next to Copilot, then Uninstall. This is the method Microsoft documents.

Can I remove Copilot from Windows 11?

Yes, the Copilot app can be uninstalled. It does not remove Copilot features that belong to a Microsoft 365 subscription.

How to uninstall Copilot Windows 10?

Microsoft's support page says: Settings, Apps, select Copilot, then Uninstall.

Can I remove Copilot from my computer?

Yes, from Settings, Apps. On a work PC, ask your IT administrator, who may manage it with policy.

Can I remove Copilot from Word?

Copilot in Word depends on your Microsoft 365 plan and the app's settings, not on the Windows app. Check Word's help or your subscription.

Can I remove Copilot from my laptop?

Yes, with the same steps as on a desktop PC. If the laptop has a Copilot key, you can also change what it launches.
