How to Install ChatGPT on Your Phone and Computer

Official apps, the web version, fake apps to avoid: here is how to install ChatGPT cleanly and what to set up the first time you open it.

In short

ChatGPT can be used in several ways: directly in the browser, or with an app.

ChatGPT can be used in several ways: directly in the browser, or with an app. Many fake apps imitate its name in the stores. This guide shows you where to find the official apps, how to spot them and what to set up before you begin.

What the provider offers

The official download page (chatgpt.com/download) presents:

  • an iPhone app, to download from Apple's App Store;
  • an Android app, to download from Google Play;
  • a download section for desktop. OpenAI's official help describes the desktop app for macOS (macOS 14 minimum), also available for Windows;
  • a browser extension for Chrome on the Chrome Web Store, which is not a desktop app.

Some mobile features are reserved for paid plans: the download page states, for example, that ChatGPT Work on mobile is reserved for the Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise plans. Plans and prices are in our plan comparison (checked on 11/23/2026).

How to do it

On iPhone or Android

  1. Open your phone's browser and type the address chatgpt.com/download yourself. Do not go through an ad or a link received in a message.
  2. Tap the link or scan the QR code shown for the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
  3. On the store page, check that the publisher shown is OpenAI before installing.
  4. Install, open the app and sign in with your account or create one by following the screens.

On a computer

  1. Go to chatgpt.com/download, typed by hand in your browser.
  2. In the “desktop” section, use the download button offered for your system. If it is a Mac, the page detects the chip (Apple Silicon or Intel) and offers the right installer, according to the official help.
  3. Open the downloaded file and follow the installation. Never accept a file found on a third-party site.
  4. Sign in with your account.

Without installing anything

You can also use ChatGPT in the browser, at chatgpt.com. Bookmark this site: it is the simplest way to avoid imitations.

Spotting a fake app

  • Always go through chatgpt.com/download rather than searching the name in the store: search results mix real and fake apps.
  • Look at the publisher name on the page: it must be OpenAI.
  • Be wary of a subscription forced on you at first launch, a slightly altered name, or an app that asks for permissions unrelated to its role.
  • Never give your password to a site or app that is not at the official address.
  • In doubt? Uninstall and start again from the official page.

Settings to check right after installing

  1. Data. In the settings, under “Data controls,” check the model improvement setting. Details are in our guide ChatGPT and personal data.
  2. Custom instructions. Describe how you work (tone, length, language) without putting sensitive data in them: the custom instructions tool helps you write them.
  3. Memory. Look at what the assistant remembers, and turn off or delete what should not be kept.
  4. Account security. Choose a long, unique password, and turn on two-step verification if the option is offered in the security settings.

First try: a prompt to copy

Once installed, test with a complete prompt rather than a vague question:

You are an assistant for beginners. I just installed ChatGPT on [phone / computer]. Suggest 5 two-minute exercises to learn how to use it, from the simplest to the most useful for [my daily life: my emails, my classes, my job]. For each, give the exact prompt to copy and what I should check in the answer.

To build your own prompts, the prompt generator is free, and the guide how to use ChatGPT picks up where this one leaves off.

When you do not need to install

  • For occasional use, the browser is enough.
  • On a shared computer (work, library), prefer the browser and sign out afterward rather than installing an app.
  • If you are looking for another assistant, see which AI to choose.

Before confirming an installation, always take the time to reread the store page: publisher name, number of reviews, update date and requested permissions. This thirty-second check avoids most bad surprises.

The provider's pages change: if a button or label differs from this guide, follow the official page rather than this text.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I download the official ChatGPT app?

From the chatgpt.com/download page, which points to the App Store for iPhone and Google Play for Android. Check that the publisher shown is OpenAI.

Do I need to install ChatGPT to use it?

No. It can also be used directly in the browser at chatgpt.com.

Is there a ChatGPT app for computers?

Yes, the provider offers a download section for desktop; the official help describes an app for macOS (14 minimum) and Windows. Get it only from the official page.

How do I avoid fake ChatGPT apps?

Go through the official download page, check the publisher name on the store page and be wary of any subscription forced on you at first launch.
