How to Use ChatGPT as a Beginner

Ask a first question, follow up, correct, verify: here are the habits for getting the most from ChatGPT in your first days, without jargon.

In short

  1. Open ChatGPT
  2. Ask a simple, concrete first request
  3. Read the answer looking for what is missing
  4. Follow up in the same conversation
  5. Verify
  6. Open a new conversation for each topic

ChatGPT is a conversational assistant: you write a message, it replies, and you continue the exchange as you would with a person. OpenAI's official page says it can be used for free, with subscriptions available to go further (details in our guide to the free version). Here we focus on practice: getting started well, and above all running a good conversation.

How to do it

  1. Open ChatGPT at chatgpt.com or in the official app, then follow the account creation or sign-in screens. Screens change regularly: follow the ones that appear.
  2. Ask a simple, concrete first request with the context (see the example below).
  3. Read the answer looking for what is missing, not only for what is written.
  4. Follow up in the same conversation: “shorter,” “with an example,” “as a table.”
  5. Verify important facts before using them.
  6. Open a new conversation for each topic to avoid mix-ups.

Your first real request

Avoid “tell me about Italian cooking.” Prefer a task with a usable result:

I'm having four friends over on Saturday night, one of them vegetarian. I cook a little but haven't been doing it for long, and I want to spend less than an hour in the kitchen while they are here. Suggest a menu (starter, main, dessert) with a shopping list sorted by aisle and a prep schedule to do before they arrive. Approximate budget: [amount].

This request contains everything needed: number of people, dietary constraint, skill level, time available, expected result. To learn the general method, read how to write a good prompt.

Following up: the real skill

The biggest gain comes from the conversation. A few useful follow-ups:

  • “Shorten it to 100 words.” to cut down.
  • “Give me three variations.” to choose.
  • “Explain your answer like I'm a beginner.” to understand.
  • “What questions do you need answered to respond better?” so the AI asks for what it does not know.
Before answering, ask me the five questions you need so that your answer fits my situation. I will answer them, then you will write the complete response.

This last prompt is very effective for a new subject: instead of guessing, the AI runs the interview.

What ChatGPT does well

  • Rephrase, summarize and proofread a text you give it.
  • Explain a concept at different levels.
  • Suggest outlines, ideas, lists and message templates.
  • Act as a practice partner: review questions, role-play for an interview.

A complete example conversation

Here is how a conversation goes in practice when preparing a request for an appointment at your bank. First message:

I want to ask my banker for an appointment to talk about a [project] project. Write an email of 6 lines maximum, polite and direct, with three possible time slots: [slots]. I don't want to go into my budget in the email.

Second message, after reading: “Keep the substance, but start with the request, not the polite greeting.” Third: “Suggest a more specific subject line.” In three exchanges, you get a text to your liking, without writing it all yourself and without giving any financial information.

Its limits, to know from the start

  • It can be confidently wrong. Numbers, dates, quotes, references: check them.
  • It does not replace a professional for health, law or money. Use it to prepare your questions.
  • It depends on what you give it. Without a reference text, it answers in general terms.
  • Available features depend on the plan. The official plans page says that several free-plan features (file uploads, image creation, voice, deep research) are limited.

Good privacy habits

Do not paste personal data (identity, address, numbers), confidential documents from your employer, or passwords. Replace names with neutral labels (“client A”). Privacy settings are in the app's settings, and their labels change: look there for the section on data. We cover the subject in the guide on personal data.

Moving to the next level

Once you master follow-ups, try specific uses: writing an email, studying a course. Frequently asked questions

Is ChatGPT free?

Yes, OpenAI says it is free to use, with optional subscriptions. Some features of the free version are limited, see our dedicated guide.

Do I need to know how to code to use it?

No. You just write in everyday language. The more precise your request, the better the answer.

What if the answer is wrong?

Say so and specify what is wrong, provide the right information or a reference text, then try again. If the conversation gets tangled, start a new one.

Can I trust it for homework or an official document?

Use it to understand and prepare, not to hand in work in your place: follow the rules of your school or employer, and reread everything.
