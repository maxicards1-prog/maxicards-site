School and studies

  • Study with ChatGPTChatGPT can be a patient study partner: it explains things another way, quizzes you and checks your answers. Make it work from your own course material, and stay in control.
  • Essay with ChatGPTAI can help you understand the prompt, defend your outline against objections and spot your weak points. The one thing not to ask it is to write the essay for you.
  • Learn English with ChatGPTA partner available at any hour, patient, who corrects without mocking: that is what ChatGPT can offer for practicing English. As long as you frame the conversation well.
  • College essay and ChatGPTAn application essay must show who you are and why you want this program. AI can help you think it through and proofread, but having it write the essay would make it generic.

Jobs and careers

  • Write a resume with ChatGPTChatGPT can help you put your experience into words and tailor your resume to a job posting. It doesn't know what you did: you provide the facts, it helps you present them.
  • Cover letter and ChatGPTA letter generated in one click is recognizable by its stock phrases. Used as a thinking partner, though, AI helps you understand the job posting, choose your arguments and proofread.
  • Interview prep with ChatGPTPracticing out loud, with varied questions and honest feedback: AI is a good simulation partner. It doesn't know your interviewer, but it keeps you from walking in cold.
  • LinkedIn profile with ChatGPTA clear profile is read in a few seconds. AI can help you find the right words for your headline, summary and messages, as long as you give it true facts.

Work and business

  • Excel formulas with ChatGPTChatGPT can suggest a formula from a description of your spreadsheet. The quality of the answer depends on how precise your description is, and you must always test the result.
  • Write an email with ChatGPTA good email takes a few lines, but writing it takes time. ChatGPT can propose a draft as long as it knows who you are writing to, why, and what you expect in return.
  • Make a PowerPoint with ChatGPTAI is very useful for building the outline and writing slide text. The layout is done in PowerPoint or a similar tool, with your own eye for visuals.
  • Summarize a document with ChatGPTSummarizing is one of the most practical uses of AI, as long as you frame the request well and remember that a summary can omit or distort an important detail.
  • Business plan with ChatGPTChatGPT can help you structure and write your business plan. It knows neither your local market nor your numbers: yours are the ones that must carry it.
  • Proofread with ChatGPTChatGPT can spot mistakes and suggest rewordings. To keep your voice and let nothing slip by, you need to ask for exactly the right type of correction.
