What Is Midjourney?

Midjourney is an AI service that turns a written description into images, and even videos.

In short

You run it from a website.

Short answer. Midjourney is an artificial intelligence service that turns text into images and videos. According to its official getting started guide (checked 26/11/2026), you log in at midjourney.com, choose a plan, then type your description in the Imagine bar on the Create page: Midjourney then makes a set of four images.

How it works, step by step

  1. Subscribe. The official guide says you need to subscribe to one of the plans before creating your first image. Prices and plan details are on the official pages; we do not copy them because they change.
  2. Log in at midjourney.com and go to the Create page.
  3. Describe your image in the Imagine bar at the top of the page. That is your prompt.
  4. Generate. After pressing Enter (or the send button on mobile), Midjourney creates a set of four images. When progress reaches 100 percent, it is done.
  5. Refine. You can upscale, make variations, zoom out, pan or use the editor, according to the documentation.

What the platform offers

  • Personalization: moodboards and personalization profiles so images match your style.
  • Images in prompts: you can guide a creation with images (image prompts, style references, an edit model that creates or modifies images from images and written instructions).
  • Video: turn images into videos, whether they are Midjourney creations or your own images.
  • Settings: aspect ratio, model version, draft mode, styles and several other parameters through the settings icon.
  • Organization: an Organize page to download, sort, filter and put your creations into folders.

Is it free?

The official guide we read presents subscribing as a first step and does not mention a free trial. We therefore cannot say that one exists right now. If you are looking for one, check the official plans page, which takes precedence over any third-party article. To compare with tools that offer a free tier, see Nano Banana in Gemini or images in ChatGPT, whose conditions are described in those guides. The AI subscription comparison does not replace Midjourney's own pricing page.

Discord, apps and installation

The official guide talks about the website as the place to create, and about the Discord server as a place to share your work, talk with the community and ask for help. We do not detail any other way to generate images because we have not verified the current procedure. Read the official documentation before installing anything, and beware of fake apps or "free versions" offered by third parties.

Does Midjourney understand other languages?

The documentation we read does not say which languages are supported. The safest approach is to test a short description in your own language and, if the result is not right, rewrite it in English, the language of most official examples. Our guide on writing Midjourney prompts gives a method and examples, and the image prompt generator helps you build the description.

Writing a good description

The official guide itself points to the "Prompt Basics" and "Art of Prompting" articles, noting that writing prompts is an art of its own. In practice, a good description specifies the subject, the setting, the light, the visual style and the framing. A starting example:

A small neighborhood bookshop in the evening, lit window, light rain on the sidewalk, warm mood, watercolor illustration style, wide shot

Change one element at a time to understand what influences the result, then keep your best wordings.

Rules and precautions

  • Read the Community Guidelines that the official guide points to: they set what is allowed.
  • Check the commercial use terms for images on the official page before using them in a professional project; we do not detail them here because we did not read a source on it.
  • Do not upload other people's images without the right or consent.
  • A generated image is not proof: do not present it as a real photograph.
