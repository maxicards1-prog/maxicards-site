How to Proofread Grammar and Style With ChatGPT

ChatGPT can spot mistakes and suggest rewordings.

In short

To keep your voice and let nothing slip by, you need to ask for exactly the right type of correction.

  1. Choose the level
  2. Give the context
  3. Ask for the list of changes
  4. Reread the result
  5. Keep your original version

“Fix this text” is an ambiguous request. Do you want only spelling mistakes, or also punctuation, repetition, overly long sentences, tone? Without details, the AI often rewrites far more than intended and the text loses your personality. The key is to separate three levels: correct, improve, rewrite.

Three levels of request

LevelWhat you ask forWhen to use it
CorrectionSpelling, grammar, agreement and punctuation errors, without changing the sentencesAn email, a report, a resume that is already well written
ImprovementRepetition, heavy phrasing, clarity, keeping the meaning and styleA text that is correct but heavy
RewriteNew tone or new length, revised structureA shorter, more formal or warmer version

How to do it

  1. Choose the level from the table and say it explicitly.
  2. Give the context: type of text, recipient, desired tone.
  3. Ask for the list of changes so you can accept or reject each correction.
  4. Reread the result: the AI can change a meaning, remove a nuance or introduce an error.
  5. Keep your original version to compare.

Pure correction prompt

Correct only the spelling, grammar, agreement and punctuation mistakes in the text below.
Do not reword sentences, do not change the vocabulary or the tone, do not delete anything.
First give me the corrected text, then a two-column "before / after" table with the rule involved for each correction.
If a sentence seems ambiguous to you, don't change it: flag it at the end.
Text: [paste your text]

Style improvement prompt

Here is a text intended for [recipient]: [paste the text].
Improve it without changing its meaning or my ideas: remove repetition, shorten sentences longer than 30 words, replace heavy phrasing with simple wording.
Keep my formal tone and my level of language.
Give the improved text, then the 5 most important changes with the reason for each, so that I learn to spot them myself.

Prompt to adapt the tone

Rewrite the following text in a more [formal / warm / direct] version, for [recipient], keeping all facts, names and figures exactly as they are.
Target length: [number] words.
Mark in brackets any passage where you had to guess a piece of information, without inventing it.
Text: [paste the text]

What AI does badly

  • The subtleties of language: rare agreements, fine punctuation, regional usage. It can also “correct” an expression that was right.
  • Proper names, numbers and references: reread them yourself.
  • A long text: split it into parts so the correction stays attentive.

If you submit a text to a panel, a teacher or an employer, follow their rules on AI use. Fixing your mistakes is not having a text written for you.

Learn from your corrections

The best long-term use is not to receive a corrected text but to understand your recurring mistakes. Ask the AI to sort your errors by type (agreement, confused words, punctuation, wording borrowed from another language) and to give you the rule in one sentence. After a few texts, you will know what to reread first.

Here are three texts I wrote: [texts without sensitive data].
Give me an assessment of my mistakes: sort them by category, give the rule in one sentence and an example from my texts for each category.
End with the three rules to remember first and a small exercise of 5 sentences to correct, whose solution you will keep until the end.

One last read-through yourself

Even with a good correction, read your text aloud, preferably the next day. The ear catches the awkward sentences that a tool lets through. Also check that proper names, dates and numbers are accurate: they do the most damage when wrong.

Clean up before correcting

A text copied from a website or an AI can contain extra spaces or hidden characters. Our text cleaner removes them in your browser. If you write for the web, then convert your text with the Markdown to HTML converter. For an email, see writing an email with ChatGPT; for online content, the prompts for web writing.

Confidentiality

Don't paste text containing personal or confidential data. According to OpenAI's help page on ChatGPT, conversations on the individual services may be used to train models, with data settings to check in your preferences.

Frequently asked questions

Does ChatGPT replace a spell checker?

It can fix many mistakes, but it can also miss some or alter a correct text. Ask for the list of changes and reread.

How do I stop it from rewriting my whole text?

Specify &ldquo;correct only the mistakes, do not reword&rdquo; and ask for a before/after table of the changes.

Can I proofread a confidential text?

Avoid pasting personal or confidential data. Anonymize names and sensitive figures before asking for the correction.
