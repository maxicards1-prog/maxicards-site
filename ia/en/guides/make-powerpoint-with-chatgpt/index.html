How to Make a PowerPoint Presentation With ChatGPT

AI is very useful for building the outline and writing slide text.

In short

The layout is done in PowerPoint or a similar tool, with your own eye for visuals.

  1. Define the audience and the message
  2. Set the duration
  3. Ask for an outline
  4. Have the content written
  5. Replace generic examples
  6. Lay it out in PowerPoint

A successful presentation rests on a clear message, not on nice templates. Before opening PowerPoint, you need to know who you are talking to, what you want them to remember and how much time you have. ChatGPT helps you go from those elements to an outline, then to text ready to paste into your slides.

How to do it

  1. Define the audience and the message: who is listening, what do they already know, what decision or idea should they take away?
  2. Set the duration: count about one slide per one to two minutes of speaking, and adjust to your style.
  3. Ask for an outline slide by slide, with the message title of each (a sentence that states the idea, not a single word).
  4. Have the content written: three short bullets at most per slide, and speaker notes for what you will say aloud.
  5. Replace generic examples with your real figures and sources.
  6. Lay it out in PowerPoint: paste the text, pick a clean template, add your images and charts.

Prompt for the outline

You are a consultant who helps build clear presentations.
Topic: [topic]. Audience: [who, level of knowledge]. Speaking time: [number] minutes.
Goal: at the end, the audience must [decide / understand / remember]: [main message].
Suggest an outline of [number] slides. For each: a title that expresses the idea in one sentence, three bullets maximum, and the recommended type of visual (diagram, key figure, photo, table).
Start with a hook slide and finish with a "key takeaways" slide with the next steps.
Before answering, ask me any questions you need.

Prompt for the content and notes

Here is the approved outline of my presentation: [paste the outline].
For each slide, write: the final title (under 10 words), three bullets of under 12 words each, and speaker notes of 3 to 4 sentences to say aloud, in a natural style.
Do not invent any figure or source: wherever a data point is needed, write [ADD FIGURE] and tell me what type of data to look for.
Here is my starting information: [your data, without anything confidential].

Working from an existing document

OpenAI's help page consulted on Nov 23, 2026 indicates that ChatGPT accepts uploads of PDF and DOCX files and PPTX presentations. So you can give it a report to draw a presentation outline from, or an old presentation to rework. What ChatGPT sends back depends on your plan and the available features: if you don't see an option to download a presentation file, simply ask for the text slide by slide and copy it into PowerPoint. According to the same page, free accounts are limited to 3 file uploads per day. Remove any confidential information before uploading: see our guide on summarizing a document with ChatGPT.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Slides full of text: if you read the screen, the audience tunes out. Put the idea on the slide, the detail in your notes.
  • Invented figures: an AI can give a wrong figure with confidence. Every data point must come from a source you have checked.
  • An outline that follows the order of your documents rather than the order of your audience's questions.
  • Not rehearsing out loud: read your notes once while timing yourself.

Polish the opening and the ending

The audience remembers mostly the beginning and the end. Ask the AI for three different hooks (a question, a figure you have sourced, a short story from your experience) and choose the one that sounds like you. For the end, a single idea to remember and a clear action beat a summary of everything that was said.

Here is my presentation: [outline and slide titles].
Play a skeptical participant. Ask me the 8 hardest questions they could ask me at the end, ranked from most to least likely.
For each one, suggest a two-sentence answer outline, without inventing figures: if I'm missing a data point, tell me.

Prepare the delivery

Read your notes aloud while timing yourself, then cut what runs over. A short, sharp presentation leaves more of a mark than an exhaustive one. Also prepare a version without a screen in case the equipment fails.

Frequently asked questions

Can ChatGPT create the PowerPoint file directly?

It depends on your plan and the features shown in your interface, which we could not verify. The safe method: ask for the text slide by slide and copy it into PowerPoint.

How many slides should I plan?

Start from the duration: about one slide per one to two minutes of speaking, then adjust. Fewer, well-chosen slides beat a long parade.

Can I send my existing presentation to ChatGPT?

OpenAI's help page says the PPTX format is accepted. First check that the content is not confidential.
