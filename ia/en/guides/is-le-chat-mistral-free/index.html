Is Le Chat by Mistral Free?

Yes: Mistral offers a free plan with no commitment.

In short

Here is what it includes, what Pro changes and how to start.

The short answer: yes, Le Chat by Mistral AI (renamed Vibe) has a free plan: access on web and mobile, limited messages and web searches, basic image generation and editing. The paid Pro plan raises the limits. Prices can change: check Mistral's pricing page or our AI subscription comparison.

What the free plan includes

Mistral's pricing page describes the Free plan as a personal agent for everyday tasks, a way to test what Vibe can do. It lists: access on web and mobile, limited messages and web searches, limited coding sessions, basic image generation and editing, and more than a hundred connectors. Mistral does not give a number of messages there: the exact limits are not shown, so be wary of figures circulating on forums.

The name has changed: since August 12, 2026, according to Mistral's help, Le Chat became Vibe, at the same place (chat.mistral.ai) and with the same account. See what is Le Chat by Mistral.

What Pro changes

The pricing page presents Pro as broader access: more messages and web searches, faster responses, all-day use of the coding mode, and chat and email support. Mistral also states that its plans are subject to fair usage limits and its terms. There is in addition an education rate for verified students at accredited higher-education institutions, valid for a maximum of 12 months and reserved for people who have not used Vibe or Le Chat before, as well as Team and Enterprise plans for organizations. For prices, see the comparison page above.

How to do it: try it for free

  1. Open chat.mistral.ai: Mistral says you land in Vibe.
  2. Create an account or sign in. The button for the free plan on the pricing page reads “Start Free”.
  3. Choose Chat mode for a classic conversation.
  4. Write a precise request: goal, context, format. The prompt generator saves time.
  5. Read the answers, verify what matters and report errors with the thumbs-down button.

On a computer or a phone

Vibe's overview page offers apps to download for macOS and Windows, and for iOS and Android, as well as a web version. That page also mentions other platforms as “coming soon”; the exact state of downloads changes, so start from the official page rather than a third-party site. Never install the app from a link you received in a message: go through Mistral's site or your phone's official store.

Free does not mean careless

  • Do not paste personal or confidential data; privacy rules are in the help center, under “Trust, Security, Compliance”.
  • Always read critically: Mistral writes that Vibe can give incorrect answers.
  • If the free plan is not enough, compare before paying: free ChatGPT, free Gemini, or which AI chatbot should I use.

Frequently asked questions

Is Le Chat Mistral free?

Yes: the free plan gives access on web and mobile, with limited messages and web searches and basic images. The exact limits are not published.

Is Mistral AI free?

Mistral offers a free plan for its assistant, Vibe (formerly Le Chat). Paid plans raise the limits; the API for developers is a separate matter.

Is Mistral Le Chat private?

Mistral hosts privacy and data-governance answers in the Trust, Security, Compliance section of its help center. Read it before sharing sensitive data.

Is Le Chat Mistral better than ChatGPT?

It depends on your tasks. Compare both on identical requests; general rankings age quickly.

Is Le Chat Mistral safe?

Mistral warns that answers can be wrong and recommends checking them. Read the privacy information in its help center before sharing sensitive data.

Is Mistral AI good?

Judge it on your own tasks: accuracy, clarity, respect for the format. Test the free plan before paying.
