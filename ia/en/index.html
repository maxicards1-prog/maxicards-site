The tools

  • Prompt generatorFill in a few fields, or pick a template if you like: your prompt builds itself as you type, ready to copy.
  • Prompt improverPaste your prompt: the tool checks 12 points and tells you what to add to get better answers.
  • Token counterPaste a text: the number of tokens, characters and words shows up instantly, along with what this text costs in each API.
  • API pricingEnter the size of your requests and your volume: the cost of each model is shown, from cheapest to most expensive.

  • Image promptDescribe your subject, pick the style, the lighting and the framing: the prompt is tailored to the tool you use.
  • Custom instructionsSet once and for all how the AI answers you: tone, length, level of detail, and what to avoid.

Why these tools are different

Many "AI generators" send your text to a paid AI, ask you to create an account or limit the number of tries. Here, no AI is called: each tool runs in your browser, with clear rules explained on its page. The result: it is instant, free, unlimited, and your text never leaves your device. You then choose the assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot or Le Chat.

Checked figures

API prices are taken from the providers' official pages, with the date they were checked and a link to the source. Subscription prices are the ones shown in France, in euros, and are labeled as such. The token counter uses the real tokenizer of GPT models; when a number is only an estimate (Claude, Gemini), the page says so.
