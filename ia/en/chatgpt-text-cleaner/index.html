Clean Up Text Copied from ChatGPT

No more ** and ### in your documents: paste the text and get a clean version, or formatting ready for Word.

  • Nothing is sent
  • No API key
  • Free, no limits
  • No sign-up

Everything happens in your browser: your text is not sent to any AI or any server, and there is no account, no API key and no limit.

How to do it

  1. In ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, copy the answer (Copy button or selection).
  2. Paste it into the box: the clean text appears right away.
  3. Choose the result: clean text, HTML code, or cleaned-up markdown.
  4. Copy the result, or use "Copy with formatting" for Word, Google Docs or an email.

What the tool cleans

  • Markdown: **bold**, *italic*, # headings, lists, > quotes, tables, code blocks, links.
  • Hidden characters: zero-width spaces, soft hyphens, text direction marks. You cannot see them, but they throw off a character count or block some forms.
  • Em dashes (—): very common in AI-generated text. The tool can replace them with a simple hyphen or a comma.
  • Optional: non-breaking spaces, curly quotes turned into straight quotes, emojis.

Clean text or formatting?

For a text message, a form, a product description or a field that only accepts plain text, choose clean text: headings become simple lines and lists keep "•" bullets. For a document, choose "Copy with formatting": Word and Google Docs get real headings, real bold and real lists. For a website or a CMS, the HTML code is ready to paste into the HTML editor.

Tip: ask for text without markdown in the first place

You can also write in your request: "Answer in plain text, no markdown, no bold, no headings". AIs usually follow it, but not always over a long conversation: the tool is still handy to fix the text. To set this once and for all, add the instruction to your custom instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. The tool runs entirely in your browser, on your device: nothing is sent to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or to our servers, and nothing is saved. You can check it in the Network tab of your browser's developer tools.

Why does ChatGPT add asterisks and # signs?

They are Markdown marks: **bold**, # heading, - list. In the app they turn into formatting, but some copy and paste targets (a form, a text message, a CMS) keep them as they are. The tool turns them into clean text or real formatting.

How do I paste into Word without losing the bold?

Use "Copy with formatting": the text is copied like a web page, with its headings, bold and lists. Paste it into Word, Google Docs or an email: the formatting is kept, without a single asterisk.

Are the hidden characters a ChatGPT watermark?

Nothing proves it. These characters (zero-width spaces, direction marks...) often come from copying and pasting from a web page or an app. They can upset a form or a character counter: the tool counts them and removes them.
