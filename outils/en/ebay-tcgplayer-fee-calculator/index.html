How much do you really keep from a card sale once fees, shipping and grading are taken out?

How to use the calculator

  1. Enter what you paid for the card (or the share of the pack it stands for) and the price you sell it for.
  2. Pick the platform. Use “Custom” for an eBay Store plan, another marketplace or a local shop: enter its commission in % and its fixed fee.
  3. Add the shipping the buyer pays, what shipping really costs you, packing supplies and, if the card was graded, the grading cost.

You get your net profit, your margin, your return on cost and the minimum sale price to avoid a loss. The table compares the same sale on each platform.

The fees we use

eBay.com, seller without a Store: in the trading card categories (non-sport trading cards and collectible card games), the final value fee is 13.25% of the total amount of the sale up to $7,500, and 2.35% on the part above $7,500. The total amount of the sale includes the item price, the shipping the buyer pays and the sales tax. On top of that comes a per-order fee of $0.30 for orders of $10 or less, and $0.40 above $10. Store subscribers pay different rates depending on their plan: use “Custom”.

TCGplayer, Level 1-4 marketplace seller: the marketplace commission is 10.75% of each item sold, capped at $75 per item, since February 10, 2026 (it was 10.25% with a $50 cap before). The payment processing fee for domestic card and PayPal payments is 2.5% of the order plus $0.30. Higher-level, Pro and Direct sellers have other rates: use “Custom”.

Sales tax is not included in the calculator. Because eBay charges its percentage on the tax too, and the card processing fee covers the whole order, the real fees on a taxed order are slightly higher than shown.

Worked example

You paid $20 for a card and sell it for $45, shipping left aside. On eBay.com, the fees come to $6.36 (13.25% of $45 plus the $0.40 order fee): you make $18.64, a 93.2% return on your $20. On TCGplayer, the fees are $6.27 ($4.84 commission, then 2.5% of $45 plus $0.30 for processing): you make $18.73. If the card cost you $20 more to grade, your eBay profit drops to $-1.36.

Good to know

Marketplaces can report seller income to the tax authorities (in the US, on Form 1099-K once you pass the reporting threshold). This calculator is not tax advice: check the rules where you live.

Frequently asked questions

Does eBay charge its fee on shipping too?

Yes. On eBay.com the final value fee is a percentage of the total amount of the sale, which includes the shipping the buyer pays and the sales tax. That is why the calculator adds the shipping charged to the fee base.

Is the TCGplayer commission capped?

Yes. For Level 1-4 marketplace sellers the commission is 10.75% per item, capped at $75 per item since February 10, 2026. The payment processing fee (2.5% + $0.30) comes on top.

What purchase price should I enter for a card I pulled from a pack?

You can enter 0 to see the gross gain, or a share of the pack price. Our price per pack calculator gives you the average cost of a pack and of a card.

How is the break-even price calculated?

It is the lowest sale price at which fees, shipping, supplies and grading are all covered, so your profit is zero or more. The calculator finds it for you.
