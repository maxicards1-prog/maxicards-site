How to use the calculator

  1. For each deal (booster box, Elite Trainer Box, used lot…), enter the price, the number of packs it holds and the shipping cost.
  2. Enter the number of cards per pack, printed on the wrapper.
  3. If you know it, enter the price of a single pack at your store: the calculator shows how much each deal saves or costs you.

The cheapest deal per pack is highlighted. Shipping is included in the total, because it often changes the ranking.

Packs per product: common formats

A booster box usually holds 36 packs. An Elite Trainer Box holds 9 packs in recent sets, along with accessories (dice, card sleeves, Energy cards). Other boxes and bundles vary a lot: always count the packs listed on the box. For a collection box, the price per pack looks higher because you also pay for the accessories or the promo card.

Worked example

A 36-pack booster box at $144 with free shipping comes to $4.00 per pack. With 10 cards per pack, that is $0.40 per card. If a single pack costs $4.99 at your store (a price used for the example), buying the box saves you 36 × $4.99 − $144 = $35.64 compared with 36 packs bought one by one.

Price per card: what is it for?

The price per card lets you compare your purchases with single-card prices. If you sell bulk commons for a few cents and keep the hits, you know right away what you need to get back to break even. To work out the profit on one specific sale, eBay or TCGplayer fees included, use our fee calculator.

Sources and updates

The calculation only uses your numbers. Product contents (booster box, Elite Trainer Box) are those printed on the boxes of recent sets; they can change from one set to another. Page checked on November 24, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Is a booster box always cheaper than single packs?

Often, but not always: box prices vary a lot by set and by store. Enter your real prices and the calculator does the comparison.

Should I count shipping?

Yes. A few dollars of shipping change the price per pack, especially for a small lot. The calculator adds it to the total.

How many cards are in a pack?

It depends on the product and the set: read the number printed on the wrapper and enter it in the “Cards per pack” field.
