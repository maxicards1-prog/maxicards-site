Tools to collect and play smarter

Opening packs, selling a card, hunting a shiny Pokémon: behind every hobby there are numbers. This site makes them simple. How many packs for a coin-flip chance at a Special Illustration Rare? How much do you really keep from a card sold for $45 on eBay or TCGplayer? Is the booster box a better deal than single packs? Each tool answers in a few seconds, with an explanation and a worked example.

Checked, dated numbers

Pull rates, platform fees and shiny odds come from sources we cite on every page, with the date we checked them. When a number is not official (pull rates for physical packs, for example), we say so. When a number changes, we update the page.
