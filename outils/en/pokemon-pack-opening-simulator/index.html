Open a virtual booster box and see how many rare cards you could have pulled.

What the simulator does

The simulator opens virtual packs and shows you the rarities you pulled. It shows no images and no card names: only rarities, drawn at random with the known rates. It is a simple way to see what opening a booster box can look like before you buy one, or to compare your real opening with the average.

How to use it

  1. Pick the pack type: physical cards from the Mega Evolution series, or TCG Pocket.
  2. Pick the number of packs: 1, 10, 36 (the size of a booster box) or 100.
  3. Press “Open packs”. Every press runs a brand new draw.

The table compares what you pulled with the expected average. For physical cards, only packs with a Double Rare or better are shown.

How the randomness works

Random numbers come from your browser's cryptographic generator, the same one websites use for security. Nothing is rigged and nothing is sent over the internet.

Physical cards: in each pack, every rarity has its own chance of showing up (for example 1 in 101 for a Special Illustration Rare). This is an approximation: in a real pack, rare card slots follow rules the publisher does not disclose. The averages, however, match the published estimates.

TCG Pocket: the simulator uses exactly the rates shown in the game for the 4th and 5th cards. The first 3 cards are always 1-diamond cards.

Example: a 36-pack Mega Evolution booster box

On average, 36 packs give about 7.2 Double Rares, 4.0 Illustration Rares and 3.0 Ultra Rares. A Special Illustration Rare only shows up 0.36 times per box on average: you have about a 30% chance of pulling at least one. Run the simulation several times: you will see much better boxes and some very disappointing ones. That is normal, that is chance.

Sources and updates

  • Physical cards: TCGplayer estimates from more than 5,000 Mega Evolution packs, reported by Kotaku and Rubygator (October 7, 2025).
  • TCG Pocket: offering rates shown in the game, listed by PocketCards.net.

Data checked on November 24, 2026. Spotted a mistake? Write to us from the contact page.

Frequently asked questions

Does the simulator show real cards?

No. It only shows rarities, with no images or card names. This is a fan site that uses no official images.

Will a real booster box give the same result?

Not necessarily. The simulator draws each pack at random with the known average rates. A real box can be better or worse, and the publisher does not disclose how rare cards are spread across packs.

Can I simulate another set?

Not yet: only the Mega Evolution and Genetic Apex rates are built in, because those are the ones we could check. For another set, the pull rate calculator accepts custom odds.
