Am I lucky?

What are the odds of pulling the card you want in N packs? And how many packs do you need for a coin-flip chance?

How to use the calculator

  1. Pick the rarity you are after: physical cards from the Mega Evolution series, or TCG Pocket cards (regular Genetic Apex pack). For another set, choose “Other” and enter the odds as “1 in X”.
  2. To target one specific card, enter how many different cards share its rarity. The calculator divides the odds by that number.
  3. Enter how many packs you plan to open. The result updates on its own.

The math, made simple

Every pack is an independent draw: what you opened before changes nothing for the next one. If a card shows up with probability p per pack, the chance of never pulling it in N packs is (1 − p)N. So the chance of pulling at least one is 1 − (1 − p)N. The number of packs needed for 50% or 90% comes from the same formula.

A common mistake: a “1 in 100” card is not guaranteed in 100 packs. The chance of pulling at least one is only 63.4%.

Worked examples

Special Illustration Rare, Mega Evolution series. It shows up about once every 101 packs. With a 36-pack booster box, your chance of pulling at least one is 30.1%. You need 70 packs for a coin-flip chance and 232 packs to reach 90%.

Any Crown card in TCG Pocket. In a regular Genetic Apex pack, the 4th card is a Crown with a 0.040% chance and the 5th card with 0.160%. That makes about 0.20% per pack. Over 100 packs, the chance of seeing at least one is 18.1%.

Frequently asked questions

Does opening packs one by one or all at once change my odds?

No. Every pack is an independent draw. The order and the timing of your openings do not change the probability.

Are the pull rates for physical cards official?

No. The Pokémon Company does not publish pull rates for physical packs. The figures come from large samples of opened packs (more than 5,000 for Mega Evolution) and remain estimates.

Why don't 100 packs guarantee a 1 in 100 card?

Because every pack can miss. The chance of pulling at least one 1-in-100 card in 100 packs is 63.4%, not 100%.

How do I target one specific card instead of a whole rarity?

Enter the number of different cards that share the same rarity in the set or pack. The calculator divides the rarity's odds by that number, assuming every card of that rarity is equally likely.
