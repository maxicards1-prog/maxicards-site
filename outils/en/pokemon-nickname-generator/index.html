Pokémon Nickname Generator

Pick a style, then press “Suggest nicknames”.

    No need to keep the species name: find a nickname that fits within the game's limit.

    How to use the generator

    1. Type your Pokémon's species (optional): the generator adds ideas based on its name and its types.
    2. Pick a style (cute, funny, epic or classic first name) and the maximum length of your game.
    3. Press “Suggest nicknames” as many times as you like; click a nickname to copy it.

    Each idea shows its number of characters, so you know right away whether it fits in your game.

    How many letters can a Pokémon nickname have?

    GamesMaximum length (Latin alphabet)
    Gen 1 to 5 (Red and Blue to Black 2 and White 2)10 characters
    Gen 6 onward (X and Y to Scarlet and Violet)12 characters

    Spaces and punctuation count as characters. The games also limit how many digits a nickname can contain, and offensive words are blocked by a filter.

    Finding a good nickname: our tips

    • Play on the type: Ember for a Fire type, Frost for an Ice type, Volt for an Electric type. The generator does it for you when you type the species.
    • Twist the species name: a short, cute version of the name lets anyone recognize the Pokémon at a glance.
    • Think ahead: “Tiny” does not suit a level 100 Charizard. An epic name holds up for the whole adventure.
    • For a Nuzlocke: nicknaming every Pokémon is one of the basic rules; it makes every loss hit harder. See our Nuzlocke rules.

    Need to name a whole team? Roll one with the random team generator.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many characters can a Pokémon nickname have?

    12 characters since Pokémon X and Y (Gen 6), and 10 characters in older games, for languages that use the Latin alphabet such as English.

    Can you change a Pokémon's nickname?

    Yes, if you are its original Trainer: depending on the game, through a dedicated character or straight from the Pokémon's menu. A Pokémon received in a trade keeps the nickname given by its original Trainer.

    Will the game accept the suggested nicknames?

    They respect the length you chose and contain no offensive words. Very rarely, a game's filter may still refuse a word: just pick another idea.
