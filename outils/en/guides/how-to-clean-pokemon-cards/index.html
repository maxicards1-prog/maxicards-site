How to clean Pokémon cards without damaging them

In short The best cleaning is the lightest: a dry wipe, no product. Here is what to do, what to avoid, and the risk for a valuable card.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Protection and storage · by the MaxiCards team

Quick answer: for dust, use a clean, soft, dry cloth, in one direction, without pressing. No spray, alcohol or household cleaner: they can dull the card, and some treatments make a card ineligible for a grade.

The basic move

  1. Wash and dry your hands well; hold the card by the edges.
  2. Lay it on a clean, flat surface.
  3. Wipe with a clean, dry microfiber cloth, in one direction, without rubbing or pressing.
  4. Put the card back in a clean sleeve.

Stubborn dust can be blown away with a gentle air blower. If the card is very dirty, it is better not to insist.

What to avoid

  • cleaning spray, alcohol, nail polish remover, wipes;
  • water, even a little: the cardboard and the varnish do not handle it well;
  • circular rubbing, which can scratch the shiny surface of holo cards;
  • an eraser on a "black dot" or a stain: it removes material.

Why this caution?

According to SI Collectibles, wiping off a fingerprint does not alter a card, but some chemicals or treatments can make it ineligible for a numerical grade at PSA, which worries about their effect on the cardboard, the ink and the varnish. Collectors debate where cleaning ends and altering begins: if in doubt, do nothing more than a dry wipe.

What about a "cleaning kit"?

Kits sold for cards rely on the same principles: soft cloth, no strong liquid. For a valuable card, use no product before checking the grading company's policy, explained in how to get a Pokémon card graded.

The best protection is to stop it getting dirty in the first place: sleeve, toploader, binder. And if the card has a stain that lowers its condition, look at the condition scale.

Frequently asked questions

How to clean pokemon cards?

A clean, dry microfiber cloth, one direction, no pressure, clean hands. Nothing more for a valuable card.

How to clean pokemon cards without damaging them?

With a soft dry cloth, or an air blower for dust. Avoid water, alcohol and household products.

How to clean pokemon cards before grading?

Only a gentle dry wipe. Some products can make a card ineligible for a grade, so check the grading company's policy first.

How to clean pokemon cards at home?

Dry microfiber cloth and clean hands are enough. Do not try to remove ingrained stains.

Can you remove a black dot from a pokemon card?

Do not scratch it and do not use an eraser: that removes material. A black dot is a condition flaw to disclose when selling.

Is cleaning spray safe for pokemon cards?

No. A spray can dull the varnish and warp the cardboard.
