Radiant Pokémon cards: what are they?

In short A Radiant Pokémon is the shiny version of a Pokémon, introduced with the Sword & Shield series. It is rare, powerful and limited to one per deck.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Reading a card · by the MaxiCards team

Quick answer: a Radiant card shows a shiny Pokémon. It has the "Radiant Rare" rarity, is a Basic Pokémon, and you can have only one per deck.

What sets it apart

  • It was introduced with the Sword & Shield—Astral Radiance set, according to Poképédia.
  • Its look: shiny, with a texture close to full art cards; the illustrator's name and the number are in white instead of black.
  • It is more powerful and rarer than a normal card.

A Basic Pokémon, even if it evolves

All Radiant Pokémon are Basic Pokémon, even those that should evolve. Poképédia gives the example of Radiant Greninja, playable without Croagunk in play.

Only one per deck

The game rule is clear: "You can't have more than 1 Radiant Pokémon in your deck." To build your list, see how to build a Pokémon deck.

Which sets have them?

Poképédia lists them in several Sword & Shield sets: Astral Radiance, Silver Tempest, Lost Origin, Crown Zenith, and in Pokémon GO. These cards are no longer in recent booster packs.

Radiant, shiny: the same thing?

In the video games, a shiny is a Pokémon of a different color that you try to catch (see shiny hunting methods). For cards, a Radiant Pokémon is the "shiny" version of a card. Do not confuse it with an ordinary holo card: see holo, reverse holo, full art.

How much are they worth?

It depends on the condition and the Pokémon: see the estimation method rather than a list price, because prices move every day.
