Pokémon ex, GX, V, VMAX: what is the difference?

In short ex, GX, V, VMAX: these are families of powerful cards, each tied to a series. Here is what sets them apart, simply.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Reading a card · by the MaxiCards team

Quick answer: these are stronger cards than a normal one, with lots of HP, but they give your opponent more Prize cards when Knocked Out. GX cards come from Sun & Moon, V and VMAX from Sword & Shield, and the current ex from Scarlet & Violet.

How to recognize them

FamilySeriesKey points
Pokémon-GXSun & MoonYour opponent takes 2 Prize cards; a GX attack usable once per game.
Pokémon VSword & ShieldLots of HP, strong attacks and abilities; 2 Prize cards.
Pokémon VMAXSword & ShieldEvolution of a V (Dynamax or Gigantamax); counts as a Pokémon V.
Pokémon exScarlet & VioletStrong card worth 2 Prize cards; Mega ex give 3.

This information comes from Poképédia and from an announcement relayed by Pokébip (Mega ex). The exact number of Prize cards for a card is always written on the card: read its rule box.

Prize cards, in two sentences

At the start of a game, each player sets aside Prize cards. When an opposing Pokémon is Knocked Out, you take one, or more for the cards above: that is the price of their power. The full rules are in how to play Pokémon cards.

And Tera cards?

These are all Pokémon ex with a different symbol and an extra rule: while they are on the Bench, damage from attacks (yours and your opponent's) is prevented, according to Poképédia. They appeared in the Scarlet & Violet series.

Does it change the value?

Not in itself. Value depends on the rarity of the printed version (Illustration Rare, Hyper Rare...) and on condition. Rarities are described in rarity symbols, and the shiny versions in holo, reverse holo, full art. To know whether a card is still playable, see the Standard format.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between pokemon vmax and gx cards?

A GX comes from the Sun & Moon series and has a GX attack usable once per game. A VMAX comes from Sword & Shield: it is the evolution of a Pokémon V (Dynamax or Gigantamax).

Pokemon ex vs gx vs v vs vmax: which is the strongest?

It depends on the card and the format. Compare HP and attacks, and read the Prize card rules written on the card.

What are v and vmax pokemon cards?

Pokémon VMAX represent Dynamax or Gigantamax Pokémon and count as Pokémon V.

What is the rule for Tera pokemon ex cards like Terapagos ex?

Tera cards are Pokémon ex that take no damage from attacks while they are on the Bench, according to Poképédia.
