Pokémon TCG sets in order: Scarlet & Violet to Mega Evolution

In short From March 31, 2023 to the November 6, 2026 release, here is every main Pokémon Trading Card Game set in order, with its release date and card count.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Sets · by the MaxiCards team

Since 2023, the Pokémon TCG has run two big series: Scarlet & Violet, then Mega Evolution, which took over in autumn 2025. This page lists the sets in release order. Card counts come from the set pages on the Pokémon fan wiki Poképédia (cross-checked with Bulbapedia where possible), and the upcoming set comes from announcements relayed by specialist press.

Quick facts (as of November 24, 2026): the newest set is Mega Evolution—Delta Reign (November 6, 2026). The next announced set is Mega Evolution—Flowing Fists, scheduled for February 2027.

How to read the table

The card count is the full set size, secret rares included. A "secret" card has a number higher than the printed set total. For example, card 122/086 exists in a set with 86 numbered cards. Secret cards are usually Illustration Rares, Ultra Rares and the highest rarities. To understand the symbols, see the guide on Pokémon card rarity symbols.

The small "half-sets" are sold mostly in special boxes and premium products, and they are in the table like any other set. Basic Energy cards and the mobile game Pokémon TCG Pocket (a different game) are not counted here.

Special set: the 30th Celebration

In the middle of the Mega Evolution series, a standalone set came out on September 16, 2026 for Pokémon's 30th anniversary. It has its own guide: Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration cards.

The next announced set

Mega Evolution—Flowing Fists is scheduled for February 6, 2027. It brings the Z Mega Evolution Pokémon to the card game (they come from the Mega Dimension content of Pokémon Legends: Z-A), with Mega Lucario Z as the headliner. The total card count has not been given yet. Future sets are also expected to launch on Saturdays instead of Fridays. A date can still move a little at individual stores, so check with yours before making a trip.

What to do with this list

  • Identify a card: the set symbol and the number at the bottom of the card tell you which set it is from. The guide how to read a Pokémon card shows where to look.
  • Know what you can play: not every set is legal in Standard. See the Standard format and rotation.
  • Pick a product: booster box, Elite Trainer Box or bundle? The guide booster box vs Elite Trainer Box vs bundle compares what is inside.
  • Compare product costs: enter the price and the number of packs in the price-per-pack calculator.

Why card counts vary so much

A main set has roughly 100 to 190 numbered cards, plus secret cards on top. The half-sets differ a lot in size: Paldean Fates has 245 cards and Prismatic Evolutions 180, while Shrouded Fable stays small at 99. The bigger the set, the more patience it takes to finish it from packs alone. The booster odds calculator helps you estimate your chances of pulling a specific card.

Card counts come from community wikis, so they can be corrected later, for example when a promo card is attached to a set. If in doubt, the checklist on the official Pokémon TCG site is the final word.

Frequently asked questions

What is the latest Pokémon TCG set?

As of November 24, 2026, the latest main set is Mega Evolution—Delta Reign, released on November 6, 2026. The 30th Celebration set came out on September 16, 2026.

What is the next Pokémon TCG set?

Mega Evolution—Flowing Fists, scheduled for February 2027. It introduces the Z Mega Evolution Pokémon to the card game.

What is the biggest Scarlet & Violet set?

According to Poképédia, Paldea Evolved is the biggest with 279 cards, ahead of Paradox Rift (266) and Surging Sparks (252).

How many cards are in a recent Pokémon TCG booster pack?

A Mega Evolution series booster pack contains 10 cards, including one Pokémon TCG Live code card, according to the set pages on Poképédia.
