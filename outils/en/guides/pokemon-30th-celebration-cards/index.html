Pokémon 30th Celebration cards: everything confirmed

In short Pokémon turned 30 on February 27, 2026. Here is what was announced and released for the card game: the September 16, 2026 set, its rarities and its products.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Sets · by the MaxiCards team

Many people search for "Pokémon 30th anniversary cards". This page keeps only what is confirmed by The Pokémon Company International's press release or by specialist press. Anything not confirmed, such as a total card count or exact dates for some products, is flagged as such.

Timeline

  • February 27, 2026: during a Pokémon Presents, an anniversary TCG set was announced with a simultaneous worldwide launch, a first for the card game. The first visuals featured Mewtwo and Mew.
  • June 1, 2026: official detailed announcement of the set, named 30th Celebration.
  • September 15, 2026: the new cards opened in the Pokémon TCG Live app, according to French press reports.
  • September 16, 2026: in-store release, at the same time in participating countries.

What is in a booster pack

According to the official press release, each pack contains five foil cards, one foil Basic Energy card and one Pokémon TCG Live code card. Every card in the set is foil, energy cards included. To understand the different foil effects, see the guide on holo, reverse holo and full art cards.

The three highlights of the set

1. The 30 Pikachu cards

Each pack contains one of 30 unique foil Pikachu cards. Each illustration is by a different artist. The goal is obvious: collect all 30 versions, a challenge in itself. With 30 different illustrations, duplicates work like in any collectible series. Try it with the booster opening simulator.

2. The "Futuristic rare" rarity

This is a brand-new rarity with bright colors and new visuals by Japanese artist YOSHIROTTEN. The first two cards shown were Mew and Mewtwo. Because the rarity is new, compare any single card you plan to buy against official images to avoid fakes.

3. The 30 classic cards

Thirty memorable cards from the history of the game return with a foil treatment. The cards revealed first include Charizard from Base Set, and Pikachu and Zekrom-GX from Sun & Moon—Team Up. These cards are not legal in Standard, but they can be played in formats that allow their original prints. See the guide on the Standard format and rotation.

The set also shows Pokémon in day and night scenes, such as Espeon ex and Umbreon ex. Other cards mentioned in announcements include Illustration Rares of Zorua, Lycanroc, Nidorina, Victini and Zeraora, as well as Sylveon ex and Greninja ex.

Announced products

According to announcements relayed by French retailers and press, the product range was spread over several months. Contents and dates can differ between countries, so check your retailer.

Elite Trainer Box (Sept. 16)9 booster packs, a Nidorina promo card, accessories
Poster Collection (Sept. 16)3 booster packs, 3 promo cards, a double-sided poster
Pokémon ex box (Sept. 16)4 booster packs, Sylveon ex or Greninja ex promo

Dates for the later products vary by source (some say October 30 for the Battle Decks, others November 6), so rely on your retailer's page rather than an unconfirmed date. To compare what these products contain, read booster box vs Elite Trainer Box vs bundle, then work out the cost per pack with the price-per-pack calculator.

What is NOT confirmed

  • The total card count: the official press release does not give it. Some sites quote a total, but we do not repeat it without an official source.
  • Fast-food and other promotions: tie-in promotions were reported by the press, but we could not verify dates or details for your country here.
  • Any card value or price: cards from a new set swing a lot in the first weeks. Look at sold listings, not at promises.

Beware of "30th anniversary" cards sold online

An anniversary attracts counterfeits and invented "editions". A card whose effect or logo does not match the list above is suspect. Read how to avoid Pokémon card scams before buying single cards.

Frequently asked questions

When was the Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration set released?

On September 16, 2026, at the same time in participating countries. The cards were available in the Pokémon TCG Live app the day before, according to French press reports.

What is the Futuristic rare rarity?

A new rarity introduced in the anniversary set, with bright colors and new visuals by YOSHIROTTEN. Mew and Mewtwo were the first two cards revealed.

Are the 30th Celebration classic cards playable?

They are not legal in Standard, but they can be played in formats that allow their original prints, according to the official press release.

How many Pikachu cards are in the 30th Celebration set?

Thirty foil Pikachu illustrations, each by a different artist. Every booster pack contains one of them.
