How many cards are in a Pokémon booster pack?

In short A recent booster pack holds 10 set cards, plus a code card. Here is the detail, and why some sources count 11 or 12 cards.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Sets · by the MaxiCards team

Quick answer: a recent booster pack contains 10 cards from the set and 1 code card for the online game. Depending on how you count, you will also read "11" or "12": the Energy card and the code card change the total.

What the sources say

Poképédia (a French-language fan wiki) describes the make-up of a pack since the Sun & Moon series as "10 + 1 Energy card + 1 code card", which makes 12 cards in total. The page for the set Mega Evolution—Delta Reign states packs of 10 cards, with a single code card per pack, and adds that basic Energy cards are "available in its packs" without saying how many.

The two statements agree: the core of a pack is 10 cards. The code card comes on top, and a basic Energy card may be added depending on the set. To be sure, count your first pack or read the packaging.

What you find in the 10 cards

  • at least one rare card (or a higher rarity);
  • one reverse holo card (foil on the background), which can be any card from the set;
  • the rest are commons and uncommons.

This breakdown comes from the same Poképédia page. To understand the printed symbols, see Pokémon card rarity symbols.

What is the code card?

It is a card you do not play with: it carries a code to enter in the online game. The how-to is in the guide where to redeem a Pokémon card code.

What about a 3-pack blister or a booster box?

A 3-pack blister holds 3 booster packs, and a booster box 36. Just multiply. The guide booster box vs Elite Trainer Box vs bundle compares these products, and the price-per-pack calculator gives the cost of each card.

For Japanese or Korean packs, or special editions, the number can be different: read the mention on the pack rather than trusting a figure from another language.

Sources and updates

  • Poképédia, Booster (in French), accessed November 24, 2026
  • Poképédia, Mega Evolution—Delta Reign (in French), accessed November 24, 2026

Frequently asked questions

How many cards in a pokemon booster pack japanese?

We did not find a reliable source to quote, so no figure here. Read the mention on the pack: the make-up can differ from an English or French pack.

How many cards in a pokemon card pack?

A recent pack contains 10 set cards and a code card, according to the Poképédia set pages. A 3-pack blister holds 3 booster packs.

How many cards in a pokemon booster box?

A booster box holds 36 booster packs. Multiply the number of cards in one pack by 36, and check the product description.

How many cards does a pokemon booster pack have?

10 set cards, plus a code card; a basic Energy card may be added depending on the set page.
