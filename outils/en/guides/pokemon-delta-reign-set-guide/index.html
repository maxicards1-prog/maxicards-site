Pokémon Delta Reign (ME06): cards, products, tips

In short Delta Reign is the newest main set. It brings back Mega Rayquaza and introduces Legendary Stadium cards.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Sets · by the MaxiCards team

Mega Evolution—Delta Reign (code ME06) was released on November 6, 2026. It is the sixth main set of the Mega Evolution series, after Pitch Black. Here is what to know before you buy or hunt for a specific card.

The numbers

  • Release: November 6, 2026 (digital version in Pokémon TCG Live from November 5; pre-release tournaments from October 24 at participating independent stores).
  • Cards: 141 according to the Poképédia set page, that is 103 numbered cards and 38 cards beyond the printed total (numbers 104 to 141).
  • Booster pack: 10 cards, with one code card chosen at random from five designs (Pikachu, Mega Gardevoir, Mega Lucario, Mega Venusaur, Mega Kangaskhan).
  • Four booster artworks: Mega Golisopod, Mega Golurk, Mega Malamar and Mega Rayquaza.

The new feature: Legendary Stadiums

According to the press release relayed by Pokébip, Legendary Stadium cards combine two Stadium cards. They play as a single card and, placed side by side, form one large illustration. Packs that contain a Legendary Stadium include both cards needed. The set has six Stadium cards, which makes three Legendary Stadiums.

For a collector, this changes how you complete the set: you need both halves to get the full picture. Read the numbering on both cards carefully before buying one half as a single.

The headline cards

The big return is Mega Rayquaza, back in the card game for the first time in about a decade. It comes with three other Mega Evolution Pokémon ex: Mega Golurk ex, Mega Malamar ex and Mega Golisopod ex.

The press release also details the rarities:

  • 4 Mega Evolution Pokémon ex and 6 Pokémon ex;
  • 6 Stadium cards (the three Legendary Stadiums);
  • 14 Illustration Rare Pokémon;
  • 17 Ultra Rare Pokémon and Trainer cards;
  • 6 Special Illustration Rare Pokémon and Supporter cards.

The Poképédia page also lists "Mega Hyper Rare" cards in the part beyond 103. To learn how to read these levels, see rarity symbols.

Products

As with earlier sets, you can find single booster packs, an Elite Trainer Box and various collections. A fan-run release calendar also lists a 3-pack blister, a 6-pack bundle and a 36-pack booster box. Treat those as unconfirmed until you see them at a store. To compare formats, read booster box vs Elite Trainer Box vs bundle.

Based on earlier sets in the series, an Elite Trainer Box holds nine booster packs and a promo card. Check the contents printed on the box before you buy.

How to complete it

  1. Set your goal: the 103 numbered cards are reachable by opening packs. The 38 cards beyond that take far more openings.
  2. Estimate how many packs you need for a specific card with the pack odds calculator.
  3. Compare the cost of each format with the price-per-pack calculator: the cost of a pack changes depending on whether you buy a box, a bundle or a booster box.
  4. Test without spending using the opening simulator to see how many duplicates to expect.
  5. Buy the last cards as singles: it is often cheaper to buy the missing card than to open ten more packs.

Things to keep straight

  • The 8 Basic Energy cards numbered 001 to 008 can show up in packs but are not part of the 141 cards. Do not count them on your want list.
  • The code card comes in five designs. It unlocks cards in the app: see where to enter a Pokémon card code. It does not count in the set total.
  • The four booster artworks (Mega Golisopod, Mega Golurk, Mega Malamar, Mega Rayquaza) are packaging variants of the same set. Check the product box to see what it contains.
  • The digital version in Pokémon TCG Live arrived one day before the in-store release (November 5), and pre-release tournaments began on October 24 at participating independent stores.

What comes next

The next announced set is Flowing Fists (February 2027). Find all dates in the list of Pokémon TCG sets. As always, cards are not a safe investment: collect what you like.

Frequently asked questions

When does Delta Reign come out?

November 6, 2026, with pre-release tournaments starting October 24 at participating independent stores.

How many cards are in Delta Reign?

141 cards according to the Poképédia set page: 103 numbered cards and 38 beyond the printed total. The official press release does not give a total.

What is a Legendary Stadium card?

Two Stadium cards that play as a single card and form one large image when placed side by side. Packs that contain one include both halves.

Is Mega Rayquaza in this set?

Yes. Mega Rayquaza ex returns to the Pokémon TCG for the first time in about a decade, according to press coverage of the announcement.
