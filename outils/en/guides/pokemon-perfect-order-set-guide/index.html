Pokémon Perfect Order (ME03): the complete guide

In short Perfect Order, released March 27, 2026, takes the Mega Evolution series to Lumiose City with Mega Zygarde ex as the headliner.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Sets · by the MaxiCards team

Mega Evolution—Perfect Order (code ME03) was released on March 27, 2026. It is the third main set of the Mega Evolution series, after Ascended Heroes (January 30, 2026), and the first set in Lumiose City, the setting of Pokémon Legends: Z-A.

The numbers

  • Release: March 27, 2026; pre-release from March 14 to 26; digital version in Pokémon TCG Live from March 26.
  • Cards: 124, that is 88 numbered cards and 36 secret cards numbered 089/088 to 124/088.
  • Booster pack: 10 cards, four artworks (Mega Zygarde, Mega Starmie, Meowth, Mega Clefable), with a code card that has its own dedicated back.

Mega Evolution Pokémon ex

The press release relayed by Pokébip recalls the main rule for these cards: they have a lot of HP and powerful attacks, but your opponent takes 3 Prize cards when one is Knocked Out. Four Mega Evolution Pokémon ex make their TCG debut: Mega Zygarde ex, Mega Starmie ex, Mega Clefable ex and Mega Skarmory ex.

Poképédia shows that Mega Zygarde ex exists in several versions (numbers 047, 104, 120 and 124), as do Mega Starmie ex, Mega Clefable ex and Meowth ex. For a collector, "having Mega Zygarde ex" can mean several very different cards, and the number makes the difference. Read how to read a Pokémon card to understand it.

Rarities

  • 4 Mega Evolution Pokémon ex and 9 Pokémon ex;
  • 11 Illustration Rare Pokémon;
  • 18 Ultra Rare Pokémon and Trainer cards;
  • 6 Special Illustration Rare Pokémon and Supporter cards.

Products

TCGplayer lists a booster box of 36 packs, an Elite Trainer Box (9 packs, TCG supplies and an Alakazam promo, MSRP $49.99), a Booster Bundle of 6 packs (MSRP $26.94) and a Build & Battle Box (one of four 40-card decks plus 4 packs, MSRP $21.99). Those MSRPs are the launch prices listed on that page. Market prices were much higher at launch and change constantly, so check current prices before you buy. To choose, read booster box vs Elite Trainer Box vs bundle.

How to complete it

  1. Decide your target: the 88 numbered cards, or also the 36 cards above them (including several versions of the same Pokémon).
  2. Spot the multiple versions: if you want Mega Zygarde ex, say which version, because they are not equally hard to find.
  3. Estimate how many packs you need with the pack odds calculator.
  4. Compare formats with the price-per-pack calculator.
  5. Simulate your openings with the simulator to gauge the number of duplicates.

Things to keep straight

  • The Lumiose City setting: this is the first set of the series to use the setting of Pokémon Legends: Z-A. It works as an entry point if you discovered Mega Evolutions through the video game.
  • Numbers above the total: 089/088 to 124/088 do exist. They are the 36 secret cards.
  • Code card: the set-specific back distinguishes it from other code cards, and it does not count in the 124. See where to enter the code.
  • Energies: 8 associated Basic Energy cards (001 to 008) are separate from the count.
  • A starting point for players: with 9 Pokémon ex and 4 Mega ex, the list is short. Browse all of it before buying singles, and check legality in Standard format and rotation. Standard rotation took effect on April 10, 2026.

Pre-release and digital version

Play! Pokémon pre-release events ran at participating independent stores from March 14, 2026, two weeks before the March 27 release. The digital version in Pokémon TCG Live (iOS, Android, macOS, Windows) arrived on March 26, the day before the in-store release. It lets you collect the new Pokémon ex, play them and earn in-game bonuses.

Why people still buy it

Perfect Order is where Mega Zygarde, Mega Starmie, Mega Clefable and Mega Skarmory first appear as cards. If you want those Pokémon specifically, it remains the reference for their collector versions. With only 124 cards, it is quicker to complete than a big Scarlet & Violet set such as Paldea Evolved (279 cards). Products still in stock vary by store: ask for the exact contents before buying, and use the price-per-pack calculator to compare.

A landmark in the series

Perfect Order is the first Lumiose City set, followed by Chaos Rising, Pitch Black and Delta Reign. The list of Pokémon TCG sets gives all the dates. A modern card is not a safe investment: put the fun of collecting first.

Frequently asked questions

When was Perfect Order released?

March 27, 2026, with pre-release events from March 14 at participating stores.

How many cards are in Perfect Order?

124 cards: 88 numbered cards (001 to 088) and 36 secret cards numbered 089/088 to 124/088, according to Bulbapedia and Poképédia.

How many Prize cards does a Mega Evolution Pokémon ex give up?

Three, when it is Knocked Out, according to the press release relayed by Pokébip. In exchange, these cards have a lot of HP and powerful attacks.

Which Mega Evolution Pokémon ex debut in this set?

Mega Zygarde ex, Mega Starmie ex, Mega Clefable ex and Mega Skarmory ex, according to the press release.
