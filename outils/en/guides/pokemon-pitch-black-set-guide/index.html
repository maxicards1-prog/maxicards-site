Pokémon Pitch Black (ME05): cards, products, tips

In short Pitch Black, released July 17, 2026, features Mega Darkrai ex and three other Mega Evolution Pokémon from Pokémon Legends: Z-A.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Sets · by the MaxiCards team

Mega Evolution—Pitch Black (code ME05) was released on July 17, 2026. It follows Chaos Rising and comes before Delta Reign. It is the smallest of the recent main sets in the series.

The numbers

  • Release: July 17, 2026. Pre-release tournaments from July 4 at participating independent stores; digital version in Pokémon TCG Live from July 16.
  • Cards: 120 according to Poképédia, that is 84 numbered cards (001/084 to 084/084) and 36 cards numbered 085 to 120.
  • Booster pack: 10 cards including a code card, with four artworks: Mega Chandelure, Mega Darkrai, Mega Excadrill and Mega Zeraora.

With only 84 numbered cards, the "base" part of the set is quick to complete. The difficulty comes almost entirely from the 36 rare cards on top.

The featured Pokémon

The press release relayed by Pokébip presents a set built around Mega Evolution Pokémon from the Mega Dimension content of Pokémon Legends: Z-A. The stars are:

  • Mega Darkrai ex, the headline card of the Elite Trainer Box;
  • Mega Zeraora ex;
  • Mega Chandelure ex;
  • Mega Excadrill ex.

The Poképédia page also lists other Mega Evolution Pokémon ex and several more Pokémon ex. The press release counts 6 Mega Evolution Pokémon ex and 4 Pokémon ex.

Rarities

According to the press release, the rare part of the set includes:

  • 11 Illustration Rare Pokémon;
  • 18 Ultra Rare Pokémon and Trainer cards;
  • 6 Special Illustration Rare Pokémon and Supporter cards.

Poképédia adds that the numbered part beyond 84 also contains "Mega Hyper Rare" cards. The press article does not describe any new game rule for this set. If someone claims a "new Pitch Black mechanic", check it on the official set page. To understand the rarity levels, read rarity symbols and holo, reverse holo and full art.

Products

Booster packs, a Mega Darkrai Elite Trainer Box and various collections were announced in the press release. A fan-run release calendar adds a pre-release kit (July 4), a 3-pack blister, a 6-pack bundle, a 36-pack booster box and a half booster box of 18 packs. Confirm these at your store. It also mentions a "Zarude" two-pack combining Pitch Black and Chaos Rising packs for August 7.

There are also 8 Basic Energy cards tied to the series (001 to 008). They can show up in packs but are not counted in the set total.

How to complete it

  1. Split your goals: the 84 numbered cards first, the 36 rare cards afterwards.
  2. Work out your odds for a specific rare card with the pack odds calculator.
  3. Pick the cheapest format per pack with the price-per-pack calculator, because the price of a pack differs between products.
  4. Preview an opening session with the simulator: it shows how many duplicates to expect.
  5. Protect and sort your cards as soon as you open them: how to protect your Pokémon cards and how to store and organize them.

Things to keep straight

  • Read the number: a card numbered "100/084" is in the set, but it is one of the 36 cards beyond 084. The printed total (084) is smaller than the real number of cards.
  • Tell versions apart: the same Pokémon can exist as a regular card, an Ultra Rare and a Special Illustration Rare. The artworks are not identical, so find the right one before ordering.
  • Do not count the Energies: the 8 associated Basic Energy cards (001 to 008) are not part of the 120 cards.
  • Who is it for? A collector who loves Darkrai or Zeraora will find several versions of those Pokémon. A player will read the list of Pokémon ex for a deck and check the rule in Standard format and rotation.

Pre-release and digital version

The Play! Pokémon pre-release tournaments on July 4 let some players discover the set two weeks before the general release, at participating independent stores. The digital version arrived on July 16 in Pokémon TCG Live (iOS, Android, macOS, Windows). You can collect the new Pokémon ex there, play with them and earn login bonuses. It is a free way to see what the cards look like before buying physical packs.

In the series

To place Pitch Black among the other releases, see the list of Pokémon TCG sets. The next set, Delta Reign, came out on November 6, 2026. Reminder: buying cards is not a guaranteed investment.

Frequently asked questions

When was Pitch Black released?

July 17, 2026. Pre-release tournaments started on July 4 at participating independent stores.

How many cards are in Pitch Black?

120 according to the Poképédia set page: 84 numbered cards plus 36 cards numbered 085 to 120.

What is the headline card of Pitch Black?

Mega Darkrai ex, featured on the Elite Trainer Box, along with Mega Zeraora ex, Mega Chandelure ex and Mega Excadrill ex.

Does Pitch Black add a new game rule?

The press release relayed by Pokébip does not describe one. The set uses the Mega Evolution Pokémon ex already present in the series.
