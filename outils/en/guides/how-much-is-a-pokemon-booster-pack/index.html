How much is a Pokémon booster pack, and is the price fair?

In short The price of a pack changes with the store, the format and the timing. Instead of a figure that goes out of date fast, here is the method to calculate and compare it.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Value, buying and selling · by the MaxiCards team

Quick answer: there is no single price. Divide the product's price by the number of packs it contains, then compare with other stores. We deliberately give no price list: they change from one seller to another and from one month to the next.

Why the price varies

  • The format: single pack, 3-pack blister (sometimes with a promo card), bundle of 6 packs, booster box of 36 packs. The price per pack is not the same.
  • The store: big retailer, specialist shop, online store. Each sets its own price.
  • Demand: a recent or sought-after set costs more, especially right after release or when it is out of stock.
  • Age: a pack from an old set is no longer sold at its original price. Very old packs are collector's items, like 1st generation packs.

The method in three steps

  1. Note the product's price and the number of packs it contains (3 for a blister, 36 for a booster box).
  2. Divide: product price ÷ number of packs. The price-per-pack calculator does it for you and also gives the price per card.
  3. Compare with at least two other sellers, shipping included.

A promo card included in a blister is not always worth the price difference: take it out of the calculation.

Be wary of prices that are too low

A pack or booster box far below the market price may be resealed or already opened. The signs and the reflexes are detailed in how to avoid Pokémon card scams.

Is a pack worth its price?

A pack is mostly a lottery: the content varies, and so does the value of the cards. To estimate your odds of pulling a specific card, use the booster pack odds calculator. Cards are not a safe investment.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a pokemon booster pack msrp?

There is no single price: it depends on the store, the format and the set. Check the price at several sellers and divide by the number of packs.

How much is a pokemon booster pack retail?

Retail prices vary by store and over time. Compare at least two sellers, shipping included, rather than relying on a fixed figure.

How much is a pokemon booster pack in japan?

We have no reliable up-to-date source to quote for Japanese prices. Check a seller or the publisher's site at the time of purchase.

How much is a pokemon booster box?

A booster box holds 36 packs and its price varies by seller. Use the price-per-pack calculator to compare it with a blister or bundle.
