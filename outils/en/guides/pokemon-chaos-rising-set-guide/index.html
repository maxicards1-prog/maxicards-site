Pokémon Chaos Rising (ME04): the complete guide

In short Chaos Rising, released May 22, 2026, stars Mega Greninja ex, which is also the rarest card in the set.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Sets · by the MaxiCards team

Mega Evolution—Chaos Rising (code ME04) was released on May 22, 2026. It comes after Perfect Order and before Pitch Black.

The numbers

  • Release: May 22, 2026; pre-release tournaments from May 9; digital version in Pokémon TCG Live on May 21.
  • Cards: 122 according to Poképédia and Bulbapedia, that is 86 numbered cards (001/086 to 086/086) and 36 secret cards (087 to 122).
  • Booster pack: 10 cards, four artworks (Mega Dragalge, Mega Floette, Mega Greninja, Mega Pyroar).
  • Energies: 8 associated Basic Energy cards (001 to 008), found in packs but not counted in the set.

What makes it different

The press release relayed by Pokébip highlights two things. First, the Mega Evolution Pokémon ex category, here five cards, including Mega Floette ex, Mega Greninja ex, Mega Pyroar ex and Mega Dragalge ex, to which Poképédia adds Mega Gallade ex. Second, Ultra Rare Mega Evolution Pokémon ex whose artwork shows the silhouette of the Pokémon's original form. That is a detail to watch when you compare two versions of the same card.

The rarest card is Mega Greninja ex as a "Mega Hyper Rare", numbered 122/086. The number is higher than the printed total, which is normal for a secret card. If this numbering confuses you, read how to read a Pokémon card.

Rarity breakdown

  • 5 Mega Evolution Pokémon ex and 5 Pokémon ex;
  • 11 Illustration Rare Pokémon;
  • 18 Ultra Rare Pokémon and Trainer cards;
  • 6 Special Illustration Rare Pokémon and Supporter cards;
  • 1 "Mega Hyper Rare" card according to Poképédia.

Poképédia says the Ultra Rares are numbered 098 to 115, so they sit in the "secret" part of the set.

Products

The press release announces booster packs, an Elite Trainer Box (Mega Greninja theme) and collections. A fan-run release calendar mentions a pre-release kit (May 9), a 3-pack blister, a 6-pack bundle, a 36-pack booster box, a Mega Lucario ex League Battle Deck and a Mega Greninja ex Premium collection (July 3). Confirm these at your store. To decide which to pick, read booster box vs Elite Trainer Box vs bundle.

How to complete it

  1. Aim for the 86 numbered cards first: with packs and a few trades, it is a realistic goal.
  2. List the secret cards you like instead of chasing all of them: 36 rare cards is a lot to pull at random.
  3. Estimate with the pack odds calculator how many packs it takes to hit Mega Greninja ex or another specific card. The answer is a probability, not a guarantee.
  4. Work out the price per pack of the product you are considering with the calculator.
  5. Practice with the booster opening simulator to see what a run of openings looks like.

Things to keep straight

  • The number goes above the total: 122/086 is not a printing error. It is how secret cards are numbered.
  • Four different booster artworks: the Mega Dragalge, Mega Floette, Mega Greninja and Mega Pyroar designs dress the same set contents. Read the product box to see what it holds.
  • The 8 Basic Energy cards (001 to 008) are not counted: do not add them to the 122 cards.
  • Mega ex give up 3 Prize cards: if you play, remember these cards give your opponent more when Knocked Out (rule described in the Perfect Order press release). To build a deck, read how to build a Pokémon deck.

Pre-release and digital version

The press release lists Play! Pokémon pre-release tournaments from May 9 at participating independent stores, thirteen days before the general release. The digital version in Pokémon TCG Live (iOS, Android, macOS, Windows) arrived on May 21. It lets you collect the new Pokémon ex, battle with them and earn login bonuses. It is a good way to spot the cards you want before opening a single physical pack and to avoid impulse purchases.

A transition set

Chaos Rising sits between Perfect Order, the first set set in Lumiose City, and Pitch Black, which uses the Mega Dimension theme. It has 122 cards: a little fewer than Perfect Order (124) and a little more than Pitch Black (120). If you started the Mega Evolution series at ME03, it extends your collection naturally. If you are starting now, the list of sets helps you choose where to begin.

Going further

The list of Pokémon TCG sets places Chaos Rising in the series. For the highest rarities, remember that a rare card is not necessarily valuable: play and collect for fun first.
