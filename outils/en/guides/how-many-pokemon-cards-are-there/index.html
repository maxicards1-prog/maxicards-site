How many Pokémon cards are there in the world?

In short There is no single official figure. It depends on what you count: the different cards, or every card printed.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Sets · by the MaxiCards team

Quick answer: about 17,400 different cards in English as of June 2025 (an estimate), and 64.8 billion cards printed as of March 31, 2024, in all languages. These are two different measures.

Different cards or printed cards?

Two questions hide behind this one:

  • How many different cards? The site Dexerto added up the main English sets and got 17,432 cards (article of June 23, 2025). This total excludes promos, Japanese cards and other languages, and remains an estimate.
  • How many cards made? According to Poképédia, 64.8 billion TCG cards had been produced as of March 31, 2024, in 15 languages, in 93 countries. That figure counts copies, not different cards.

Why there is no official total

The total depends on the language, promos, cards from special products, reverse holo versions and alternate rarities. Every new set adds a few hundred cards: for example, the list of sets gives the card count of each one. Today's total will be out of date tomorrow.

How to count the cards in a set

The number at the bottom of a card reads like "122/086": the first number is the card, the second is the "numbered" total. A card whose number goes above that total is a secret card. See how to read a Pokémon card.

The cards of the Scarlet & Violet set, for example, run from 001 to 198 for the main series, then up to 258 with the special rarities according to Poképédia.

And the number of full art cards?

No reliable total is published. The types of shiny cards are explained in holo, reverse holo, full art.

Frequently asked questions

How many pokemon cards are there in the world?

There is no official total. An estimate from June 2025 gives 17,432 different cards in English, and 64.8 billion cards had been printed as of March 31, 2024.

How many pokemon cards are there in total?

It depends on what you count: different cards (about 17,400 in English according to Dexerto) or printed cards (64.8 billion according to Poképédia).

How many pokemon cards are there in a pack?

A recent booster pack holds 10 set cards plus a code card, according to Poképédia set pages.

How many pokemon cards are there in existence?

No one publishes a reliable total of every card ever made: promos, languages and special products all change the count.
