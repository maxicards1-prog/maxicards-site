Pokémon ACE SPEC cards: what are they?

In short An ACE SPEC card is a particularly powerful support card, with a simple rule: only one per deck.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Playing the TCG · by the MaxiCards team

Quick answer: a deck can hold only one ACE SPEC card, all different cards combined. These are Item, Pokémon Tool, Stadium or Special Energy cards whose effect is stronger than average.

The rule

According to a guide on the subject (in French), an ACE SPEC card can appear only once in a deck, and the official rulebook it cites answers "no" to the question of whether you can put two different ACE SPEC cards. An ACE SPEC Item therefore cannot be paired with an ACE SPEC Tool, Stadium or Special Energy. Other cards keep the usual limit of four copies of the same name, with basic Energy exempt.

Which card types?

  • Item cards;
  • Pokémon Tool cards;
  • Stadium cards;
  • Special Energy cards.

The differences between these types are explained in Trainer cards: Item, Supporter, Stadium, Tool and in Energy cards.

How to recognize one

The main marker is the text "ACE SPEC" printed in large letters on the card, with a rule band. According to the guide cited, there is no rarity symbol specific to ACE SPEC cards. Read the rule text on the card rather than relying on the look alone.

A concrete example

If you put one ACE SPEC card in your deck, you can no longer add another, even of a different type. Choose the one whose effect best fits your strategy. The full method is in how to build a Pokémon deck.

Can you play them everywhere?

It depends on the format and the allowed sets. To know what is legal today, look at the Standard format and rotation.

Frequently asked questions

What are ace spec pokemon cards?

A support card (Item, Tool, Stadium or Special Energy) with a powerful effect, limited to one in a deck.

How many ace spec pokemon cards per deck?

Only one ACE SPEC card per deck, even if they have different types or names.

What does ace spec mean in pokemon tcg?

It is the label on a powerful support card that you can include only once in your deck.
