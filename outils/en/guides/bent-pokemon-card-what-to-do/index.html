Bent Pokémon card: can you straighten it?

In short A curved card is not necessarily lost, but trying to "fix" it is risky. Here is what is reasonable, and what can make things worse.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Protection and storage · by the MaxiCards team

Quick answer: a slight curve can sometimes correct itself if you store the card flat, with no product or tool. A sharp crease cannot be repaired, and any visible attempt can get the card labeled "altered" by a grading company.

Bent, warped, curved: three cases

  • Slight curve: the card is not perfectly flat, but the cardboard is not broken.
  • Raised corner or dent: a small surface or edge flaw.
  • Sharp crease: a white line or a break in the cardboard. This flaw is permanent.

What to do for a slight curve

  1. Take the card out of wherever it warped and make sure it is dry.
  2. Put it in a clean sleeve, then flat in a binder or a rigid box. See how to protect your Pokémon cards.
  3. Leave it alone: forcing it does no good. Do not bend it the other way.

What to avoid

  • an iron, a hair dryer, heat or moisture;
  • makeshift presses with heavy objects: the card can get marked or stick;
  • glue, tape and any product.

Why so much caution? The site SI Collectibles explains that pressing is accepted by PSA for comics and magazines, but treated as alteration for sports cards. Flattening a warped card is one of the cases that divide collectors. For a valuable card, try nothing before reading the policy of the grading company you have in mind.

What is the impact on value?

A crease or a curve lowers the condition of the card: see the Mint and Near Mint conditions. To know whether it is still worth selling, use the method in how to estimate the value of a card. Bent cards stay perfectly playable in a sleeve.

Frequently asked questions

Bent pokemon card fix: what can I do?

If the curve is slight, store the card flat in a sleeve and do not force it. If the cardboard is broken, the flaw is permanent.

Is a bent pokemon card value affected?

Yes, it lowers the card's condition, so its possible value. It stays playable in a sleeve.

Bent pokemon card psa: can I restore it before grading?

A sharp crease cannot be repaired. Restoration attempts can get a card labeled altered by a grading company.

How do you straighten a curved pokemon card?

With no product or tool: flat, in a sleeve, in a dry place. Avoid heat, moisture and homemade presses.

Can you repair a bent pokemon card?

You cannot really repair broken cardboard. For a valuable card, it is better to sell it or keep it as is and disclose the flaw.
