Your shinies found

    Count your resets and encounters, and watch your cumulative odds of having seen a shiny, live.

    How to use the counter

    1. Type the name of the Pokémon you are hunting and pick your method: the odds change with the game and the bonuses.
    2. Press +1 (or the Space bar on a keyboard) at every reset, encounter or hatched egg. For encounters in groups, set the step to +2 up to +5.
    3. When the shiny shows up, press “Shiny found!”: the hunt is saved to your history and the counter goes back to zero.

    The counter is saved in your browser, on this device: you can close the page and pick up later. Nothing is sent over the internet.

    The odds of finding a shiny Pokémon

    SituationOdds per encounter
    Gen 2 to 5, no bonus1 in 8,192
    Gen 6 onward, no bonus1 in 4,096
    With the Shiny Charm (Gen 6 onward)3 in 4,096, about 1 in 1,365
    Masuda method (Gen 6 onward)6 in 4,096, about 1 in 683
    Masuda method + Shiny Charm8 in 4,096, that is 1 in 512

    The Shiny Charm adds two extra rolls to each encounter; the Masuda method (breeding two Pokémon from games in different languages) adds five for eggs. Some games have other bonuses (mass outbreaks, special abilities…): in that case choose “Other” and enter the “1 in X” odds given for your game.

    Worked example

    At 1 in 4,096, you need 2,839 encounters for a coin-flip chance and 9,431 to reach 90%. After 4,096 encounters, the chance of having seen at least one shiny is only 63.2%: more than one hunter in three still has nothing at that point. With the Shiny Charm, the 50% mark drops to 947 encounters, and to 355 with Masuda + Shiny Charm.

    Every encounter is independent: having already done 5,000 resets does not make the next one luckier. The percentage shown only tells you what share of hunters would already have found their shiny with the same number of tries.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does the counter keep my resets if I close the page?

    Yes, on the same device and browser: the counter is saved in the browser's local storage. If you clear the site's data or use private browsing, it starts again from zero.

    Why do I still have nothing after 4,096 resets?

    It is common: at 1 in 4,096, about 37% of hunters still have no shiny after 4,096 encounters. Chance has no memory.

    Can every Pokémon be shiny?

    No. Some Pokémon received from events or during the story cannot be shiny in a given game. Check before starting a long hunt.

    Can I count several hunts at once?

    The counter follows one hunt at a time. When you find the shiny, it is saved to the history with the number of encounters and the date.
