Random Pokémon Team Generator

Set your rules, then press “Generate a team”.

Generations

    Six Pokémon picked at random under your rules, for a challenge, a Nuzlocke or just a change of team.

    How to use the generator

    1. Tick the generations to include (Gen 1 = Red and Blue, Gen 9 = Scarlet and Violet) and, for a monotype team, pick a type.
    2. Set the options: final evolutions only (no Caterpie on the team), legendaries or not, no repeated types for a balanced team.
    3. Press “Generate a team”. Tick “Keep” under a Pokémon to hold it for the next roll, and reroll the others.

    The draw is truly random (the browser's random generator) and picks from the 1,025 Pokémon of the National Pokédex, with their official English names and current types. Regional forms and Mega Evolutions are not counted separately.

    Challenge ideas with a random team

    • Randomlocke: roll your team at the start of a new game and use only those six Pokémon. Mix in the Nuzlocke rules for extra difficulty.
    • Monotype: set a required type and finish the game with Pokémon of that type only.
    • Challenge with friends: everyone rolls a team from the same generation, then you battle locally.
    • Collection team: roll six Pokémon and look for their cards in your binders or at your local store.

    To check that a rolled team does not collapse against a single type, run its Pokémon through the weakness calculator. And to name them, try the nickname generator.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does the generator include every Pokémon?

    Yes, all 1,025 species of the National Pokédex up to Gen 9 (Pecharunt, #1025). Regional forms and Mega Evolutions are not drawn separately.

    What is a final evolution?

    A Pokémon that does not evolve any further: Charizard, but not Charmander or Charmeleon. Pokémon that do not evolve at all, like Tauros, also count as final evolutions.

    Can I keep a Pokémon and reroll the others?

    Yes: tick “Keep” under the Pokémon, then press “Generate a team” again.

    How do I make a random monotype team?

    Pick a type in “Required type”: only Pokémon that have this type (primary or secondary) will be drawn.
