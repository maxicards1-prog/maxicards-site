About

A free fan site with sourced numbers.

Pokémon Card Tools is a free site of small calculators and guides for Pokémon card collectors and players, for physical cards as well as TCG Pocket. It is the English edition of our French site.

Who runs the site

The site is published by MAXICARDS, a French company that runs the MaxiCards YouTube channel, a French-language channel about Pokémon cards. Full details are in the legal notice.

Our principles

  • Free and no account: no tool asks you to sign up.
  • Your data stays with you: calculations run in your browser; the shiny counter is saved on your device only.
  • Cited sources: every rate or fee shown links to its source and the date it was checked. Estimates are presented as estimates.
  • A fan site: no official images or logos. The site is not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company.

How the site is funded

The site shows advertising (Google AdSense) to cover its running costs. Advertising never changes the results of the calculators. See the privacy policy.

Found a mistake, or have an idea for a tool?

Write to us from the Contact page. We fix numbers as soon as a source changes.
