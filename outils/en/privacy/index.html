Privacy policy

What the site collects (almost nothing) and how advertising cookies work.

Last updated: November 24, 2026.

Data controller

MAXICARDS, SASU, 60 rue François Ier, 75008 Paris, France. Contact: contact@maxicards.net.

What the site does not collect

The site has no accounts, no forms and no audience measurement tool of its own (apart from advertising, see below). The numbers you type into the calculators stay in your browser and are never sent to us.

Storage on your device

The shiny hunt counter saves your count, your settings and your history in your browser's local storage (“localStorage”), on your device only. We have no access to it. You can erase it at any time by deleting the site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). To serve the pages, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type, possibly outside the European Union. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Google AdSense advertising

This site shows ads provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and identifiers to serve ads, limit how often they repeat, measure their results and, with your consent, personalize ads based on your visits to this site and other sites.

  • A Google consent message asks for your choice before any advertising cookie, where the law requires it. You can change your choice at any time from the consent management link shown on the site.
  • Learn more: how Google uses cookies in advertising.
  • To adjust or turn off ad personalization: Google Ad Settings.

YouTube videos

Some French-language pages of the site offer a video from our YouTube channel. Before you click, only an image hosted on this site is shown: nothing is requested from YouTube. If you click, the video loads from youtube-nocookie.com (Google's privacy-enhanced mode), which does not set tracking cookies until you play it; playback is then subject to Google's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct, erase or restrict your data, and object to its processing, by writing to contact@maxicards.net. You can also file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).
