Which consoles can you play GTA 6 on?

Updated November 24, 2026

GTA 6 released on November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only.

There is no PS4 version, and no Switch 2 or mobile version has been announced.

The consoles that run the game

  • PlayStation 5: yes, at launch.
  • PS5 Pro: yes, with improvements according to two guides, notably PSSR image reconstruction and ray-traced shadows.
  • Xbox Series X and Series S: yes, at launch.

The consoles with no version of the game

  • PS4: no. No PS4 version is offered.
  • Xbox One: no native version. Xbox's official page states that the game does not run natively on Xbox One but can be played through Xbox Cloud Gaming.
  • Nintendo Switch 2: no version announced.
  • Mobile (iPhone, Android): no version announced.

How Xbox cloud play works

The Xbox page says you need cloud play time, obtained through a Game Pass subscription, a purchase of play time or ad-supported play. The game is paid for separately.

You have a PS4 or an Xbox One

On PS4, there is no way to play it: you need a newer console. On Xbox One, the only way is streaming through Xbox Cloud Gaming, which requires cloud play time. Buying the game remains separate from this play time.

What about PC?

There is no PC version at launch, and no official date. Rumors point to the first quarter of 2027, not confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two. Everything is in our GTA 6 on PC page.

Which console to choose?

If you are hesitating, compare the editions and their bonuses with the Ultimate edition and look at the game length. For settings, our recommended settings apply on PS5 as well as Xbox, and the PS5 and Xbox buttons are covered separately.
