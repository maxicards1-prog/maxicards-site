GTA 6 Missions List

Updated November 24, 2026

How many missions, how many chapters, and the list in order from the start of the story.

How many missions are in GTA 6?

The story has 91 main missions spread over 5 chapters, according to the matching lists from GAMES.GG and Pro-Gamer.gg. Other guides count 93 to 97 missions because they add optional missions.

Chapters 3 to 5

Frequently asked questions

How many missions are in the GTA 6 story?

91 main missions in 5 chapters (22, 11, 26, 25 and 7), according to matching lists. Some guides count up to 97 by adding optional missions.

What is the first mission in GTA 6?

The Prologue, with Lucia in prison, then "Gone Fishing", Jason's first mission with Donnie.

How many chapters are in GTA 6?

Five chapters. The last one is the shortest, with 7 missions.
