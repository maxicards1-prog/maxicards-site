How to Steal a Fighter Jet in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

The fighter jet is stolen at Mile Zero Air Station, in the southwest of the map, in Mariana County.

It is reachable very early, from chapter 1 after a first Jason mission.

Where the base is

Mile Zero Air Station is in Mariana County, in the southwest of Leonida. The guides say you can go there from chapter 1, right after the first mission with Jason, or after the mission "Gone Fishing". The fighter jets are parked near the hangars and the runway; they are not always in the same spots.

Ways in

  • Through the main gate, by ramming the barrier with a vehicle, then heading for the row of planes.
  • By water: arrive by boat on the west side, take out the guards and get past the fence.
  • By a dirt ramp near the southeast entrance, which lets you jump the fence (according to one guide).

The wanted level

The sources do not agree: from four stars at the entrance, you go up to five once in the plane according to one guide; another says five stars right away. Just remember that the military reacts hard, with soldiers and aircraft chasing you. Body armor helps. See the wanted level to understand how it escalates.

Escaping

Take off as fast as you can: the plane can be destroyed if you hang around, and fighters will chase you. In our playthrough, we stole a fighter jet from this air base; the video below shows it.

Make a manual save before going in: the operation is risky, see saving your game.

Preparation tips

Bring body armor, full health and a fast vehicle to get through the barrier. Scout where the planes are parked beforehand, because their location varies. The sources say the army also responds with tanks: so do not stay on the ground.

What next?

The guides do not say clearly whether the plane stays yours after the theft: accounts mention a crash, a parachute jump or a shot-down plane. For easier aircraft, see where to find a helicopter or a plane. To spot the area on the map, see the map.
