GTA 6 FPS on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox

Updated November 24, 2026

GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X, with no 60 FPS mode.

The PS5 Pro holds that target most steadily; the Xbox Series S drops lower.

The answer in one table

Rockstar confirmed a 30 FPS target before release, and the Digital Foundry analysis, published the day before launch, confirms it console by console. The figures below are rough orders of magnitude: two sources summarize the measurements slightly differently, especially for the Series S.

ConsoleInternal resolutionSmoothness
PS5 Pro1440p, reconstructed by PSSRstable 30 FPS in normal play
PS51440p, simple upscaling30 FPS most of the time, drops possible in big explosions
Xbox Series X1440p upscaled30 FPS targeted, drops at busy intersections
Xbox Series Sabout 720p to 1080paround 20-25 FPS in heavy scenes

Is there a 60 FPS performance mode?

No. The analyses published at launch say that no console offers a 60 FPS mode, or a 40 FPS mode, and that there is no choice between a quality mode and a performance mode either. A rumor of a 60 FPS mode circulated before release: it did not turn out to be true. So do not look for this setting in the options: you can, however, adjust other assists, see our recommended settings.

What does the PS5 Pro change?

On PS5 Pro, the target is still 30 FPS. The gains are visual: an image reconstructed by PSSR, closer to 4K, and ray-traced shadows exclusive to this console. According to the measurements cited, smoothness is also steadier than on the base PS5.

And on Xbox?

The Xbox Series X also targets 30 FPS at 1440p. The Series S gives up resolution (around 720p) and has the sharpest drops in big fights. A “Quick Resume” bug on Xbox can remove the 30 FPS cap, but it speeds up the game: it is not a real mode.

And on PC?

GTA 6 has not been released on PC: see our update on the PC version. To decide what to play on, compare the consoles; for disk space, the game size; and for a good start, our tips.

Frequently asked questions

What FPS does GTA 6 run at on PS5?

30 FPS targeted, with possible drops in very busy scenes. There is no 60 FPS mode.

Does GTA 6 run at 60 FPS on PS5 Pro?

No, the PS5 Pro also targets 30 FPS. It brings a better image and steadier smoothness.

What FPS does GTA 6 run at on Xbox Series X and S?

30 FPS targeted on the Series X. The Series S drops lower, to around 20-25 FPS in heavy scenes.

How will GTA 6 run on PS5?

At 1440p and 30 FPS, with decent but not perfect smoothness during explosions.
