GTA 6 Side Quests by Chapter

Updated November 24, 2026

GTA 6 has no dedicated side quest menu: they show up on the map, by phone call or by poster.

Look for the missions with an hourglass: they are time-limited and can disappear.

How to find and track them

The guides agree on three points: there is no separate "strangers" menu, progress is shown on the map through the pink story book icon, and each quest can start with a call, a message, a poster or a marked location. After one step, the next may take several weeks of in-game time, even a whole chapter, to arrive. Keep going with the story: the quest giver will contact you.

Hourglass = urgent. A mission marked with an hourglass on the map disappears if you leave it too long. Do it before moving ahead in the story.

For the trophy tied to these quests, read Strangers and Freaks.

Chapter 1

  • Shirley: two favors, Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter. Missable: do them before Tourist Trap.
  • Kenton: treasure dives, after Island Dreams. They can still be done later and have their own trophy.
  • Kirk: the search for the Skunk Ape, 3 missions, at night, with a follow-up in chapter 2. See the Skunk Ape.

Chapter 2

  • Kyle: three missions around a farm, which continue in chapter 3.
  • Moses: vehicle deliveries, starting from the introduction mission (one or two missions depending on the guides).
  • Bret Vogel: hunting, 2 missions, with a "Fair Game" trophy.
  • Billy: a longer series (5 to 7 missions depending on the guides), with the "The Lone Rider" trophy.

Chapter 3

  • Yago: street races, 4 missions. Race Against Yago is missable.
  • Andy: a series reserved for Lucia (5 to 7 missions depending on the guides), "Road to Rehabilitation" trophy.
  • Yasmani: 4 burglary missions.
  • Randy (1 mission, "Supply & Demand" trophy), Tyrone (2 missions, "Spin On This!" trophy) and Dunk (1 mission).

Chapter 4

  • Pilot Pete: plane deliveries, reserved for Jason (4 or 5 missions depending on the guides).
  • Eddie Acosta: one mission, an invitation to a party.

The guides mention no new character quest in chapter 5, and some details (Shayna, Papa Audacity) are backed by only one guide: we do not expand on them.

Tips

  • Make a manual save before the series finales and at the end of a chapter: missions cannot be replayed.
  • After the story, most quests stay accessible, except the missable ones.
  • Side missions like fishing or races are also in the mission list and in the Easter eggs.

For the main story, see chapter 1 and chapter 2.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a side quest menu in GTA 6?

No, the guides say there is no dedicated menu. You check progress on the map, with the pink story book icon.

What does the hourglass on the map mean?

A time-limited mission: it can disappear for good if you wait too long. Do it before continuing the story.

Which side quests are missable?

Shirley's two favors in chapter 1 and Race Against Yago in chapter 3 are cited by several guides.

Why is a side quest no longer progressing?

The contact may take several weeks of in-game time to call you back. Do something else and wait for their call or message.
