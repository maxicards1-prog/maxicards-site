Transgressor '67 and Wyman's classic cars in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

The Transgressor '67 is rebuilt by finding three wrecks in Port Gellhorn, then paying Wyman $1,500.

The classic car series is not available without the Ultimate edition.

The requirements

  • This car series is reserved for the Ultimate edition: the standard edition does not give access to it (two guides).
  • It starts in chapter 2, after you take the Vapid Dominator Buggy '67 at the Paradise garage, in Watson Bay.
  • Wyman then writes to you. Tracking is in the touchpad, Exploration tab, "Find Cars for Wyman".

The three Transgressor '67 wrecks

It all happens in Port Gellhorn, in Kelly County. See the city page to find your way around.

  1. The Tanglewood Lane trailer park, north of the Sarena's Bargain Store shop.
  2. A garage behind a house decorated with a metal sunflower, northwest of the city, across from the Kolt & Associates hideout.
  3. Behind the Dirt Merchants mod shop, at the southern tip of Port Gellhorn, near the broken fence.

The guides give these locations approximately: if the wreck is not at the exact spot, search the surrounding area.

The steps

  • Scan each wreck on foot with WAiNK. This does not work from a vehicle, and the order does not matter.
  • Once all three are scanned, accept the $1,500 payment to Wyman in the BuckMe app, see the bank.
  • Wait for the rebuild, then pick up the car at Wyman's scrapyard when he calls.

The Dominator Buggy '67 that starts everything is the first car in the series. Remember to register each finished car.

Keeping it

Register it at a Pay 'n' Spray or put it in one of your garages; otherwise it goes back to Wyman. See registering a vehicle and buying a garage.

Wyman's other cars

Two guides agree on the number of wrecks to find: the Riata '70 (3), the Mamba GT '64 (4), the Reynolds Kit Car (5). The Deviant '72 comes after the others. We do not give their locations, because only one guide details them.

The other wanted cars

For the Tulip, the Growler, the Itali RSX, the Nightblade, the Jugular and the Urus, our sources give no verified location. We prefer to publish nothing rather than make something up; we will add to this if matching guides appear.

Frequently asked questions

Where do you find the Transgressor '67 in GTA 6?

By scanning three wrecks in Port Gellhorn (Tanglewood Lane trailer park, the house with the sunflower, behind Dirt Merchants), then paying Wyman $1,500.

Do you need the Ultimate edition?

Yes: two guides say Wyman's car series is reserved for the Ultimate edition.

How much does the Transgressor '67 cost?

$1,500, paid to Wyman through BuckMe after the three scans.

What happens if I don't register it?

It goes back to Wyman's scrapyard: register it at the Pay 'n' Spray or park it in a garage.
