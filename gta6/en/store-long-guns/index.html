How to Store Long Guns in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

In GTA 6, you can't carry everything: long guns are stored in the trunk of your personal vehicle.

We saw this in our game, and the guides confirm it.

Why store your weapons?

The game limits what you carry: two handguns and two long guns, one worn on your back and one in your hands. Everything else has to be kept safe somewhere else. See the weapon wheel for the details on the slots.

The trunk of a vehicle you own works as storage: that's where you leave a rifle to equip it later. In our game, Jason stores a weapon at the back of a pickup this way.

To take a weapon back, do the same thing in reverse: it is equipped from the trunk before you head out again.

The weapons you own reappear in this trunk, and the trunk also protects a picked-up, unowned weapon. It's all explained in saving your weapons.

Which vehicle?

It has to be a personal vehicle, meaning a vehicle you own. A vehicle you simply stole does not belong to you: see keeping a stolen car and registering a vehicle.

Locker or trunk?

The locker in an Ammu-Nation gun store does a different job: it lets you get back and manage the weapons you bought. The trunk is your field storage. For the stores themselves, see gun stores.

Cheats are elsewhere: weapon cheats. The section index is vehicles and weapons.

Why this limit?

The game wants you to plan your outings: you choose the long guns you take before leaving the vehicle, like packing a bag before a heist. A weapon left in the trunk stays out of sight and doesn't weigh you down. If a mission calls for extra firepower, make a stop at the trunk before you leave.

Good to know

  • Ammo, armor and explosives bought at a gun store arrive in this same trunk.
  • The trunk isn't only for weapons: you can leave other items in it too.
  • Choose your long guns before leaving the vehicle.

Step by step

  1. Go to the trunk : Stand at the back of your personal vehicle.
  2. Open the trunk : Use the button shown on screen to open it.
  3. Store : Choose the "Store long guns" command.

Frequently asked questions

Where do you store a rifle in GTA 6?

In the trunk of your personal vehicle, with the "Store long guns" command.

How many weapons can you carry?

Two handguns and two long guns, according to the guides we read.

Can you store a weapon in a stolen vehicle?

You need a personal vehicle. A vehicle that is only stolen isn't yours until you have registered it.
