How to Read the Table

  • Combination: the D-pad arrows, to be entered in order. They are the same on PS5 and Xbox.
  • How to unlock: the challenge to complete for the cheat to work. A code entered before its challenge does nothing.
  • Confirmed: the six guides we follow give exactly the same combination.
  • Confirmed, variant reported: at least four guides give the same combination, but a variant is going around. If ours does not work, first check that the challenge is completed.
  • Not found yet: the code exists in the game menu, but nobody has published a reliable combination yet. We add it as soon as it is cross-checked.

Frequently asked questions

How many cheat codes are there in GTA 6?

61 codes: 10 for the player, 11 for combat, 21 for vehicles, 3 for weather and time, 16 screen filters. 17 combinations are known as of November 24, 2026.

Are the cheats the same on PS5 and Xbox?

Yes, the arrow combinations are the same. Only the buttons that open the entry screen change: L1 then R1 on PS5, LB then RB on Xbox.

Why is my cheat not working?

Three possible reasons: the main story is not finished, the challenge that unlocks this cheat is not completed, or an arrow was entered wrongly. Check the Progression menu, Cheat Codes tab.

Can you enter the cheats with a keyboard or a phone number?

No. Unlike GTA 5, GTA 6 cheats are entered only with the D-pad arrows. A list with words or phone numbers is fake.
