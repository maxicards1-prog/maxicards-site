Updated November 24, 2026

The most profitable methods, when they unlock, what they pay, and how to keep your money.

Chapter by chapter

Chapter 1 (Key Lento)

The gangs' illegal operations, marked on the map, are the best source of money early on. Shipwreck treasure in Kenton pays well without attracting the police. Convenience stores pay little.

Chapter 2 (Port Gellhorn)

Moses buys stolen cars that have no tracker: scan them with WAiNK and don't damage them, because damage lowers the price. Bret pays for hunting, even more for rare species.

Chapter 3 (Vice City)

The real money starts here: jewelry stores ($10,000 to $25,000 each) and sports cars for Randy ($30,000 and up, in excellent condition). See our heists page.

Chapter 4

The big bank vaults unlock at the end of the chapter. They are the most profitable method (up to about $205,000), but they trigger a 4-star wanted level. Pete's air deliveries require you to know how to fly.

Don't lose your money

  • Dying or getting arrested means losing your cash. Don't walk around with a large sum.
  • Before you have an account, hide your money under the floorboards in Jason's room.
  • The BuckMe bank account opens in chapter 1, with the mission "Buck Me". After that, deposit your money at an ATM after every job.
  • After a heist, your partner can carry a bag or go resell the loot while you shake off the police.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best way to make money in GTA 6?

A big bank vault, up to about $205,000, but it only unlocks at the end of chapter 4. Before that, jewelry stores ($10,000 to $25,000) and sports cars for Randy ($30,000 and up) in chapter 3.

How do you make money early in GTA 6?

In chapter 1, do the gangs' illegal operations ($1,000 to over $10,000) and the Kenton shipwreck treasure ($1,400 to $2,600). Deposit your money as soon as the BuckMe account is open.

Do you lose your money if you die in GTA 6?

Yes, the cash you are carrying is lost if you die or get arrested. Money deposited at the bank (BuckMe) is safe.
