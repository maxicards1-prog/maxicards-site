How to Enter a Cheat Code in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

The exact buttons on PS5 and Xbox, the two conditions for a cheat to work, and what it blocks.

The Buttons, Step by Step

2. Open the entry screenhold L1 then press R1hold LB then press RB

The guides do not all describe step 2 the same way: some say to hold both buttons together. If the entry screen does not open, keep L1 and R1 (or LB and RB) held down for the whole entry. Use the D-pad, not the stick.

Two Conditions Before a Cheat Works

  1. Finish the main story. The cheat menu stays closed throughout the story of Jason and Lucia (91 missions).
  2. Complete the cheat's challenge. Each cheat has its own challenge: 100 workout sessions for body armor, 25 gold medals at the shooting ranges for “no reload”, and so on. The list is in the Progression menu, Cheat Codes tab, and in our table of all 61 cheats. Goals already completed during the story count.

What a Cheat Blocks

Warning: entering a cheat disables saving, trophies (PS5) and achievements (Xbox) until you quit the game. Save manually before cheating.
  • During a session where a cheat is active, you cannot save, and trophies and achievements stop unlocking.
  • To get everything back, quit the game and load a clean save.
  • Consequence: what you earn during a session with cheats, including the $250,000 from the money cheat, is not kept.
  • Tip: make a manual save in a separate slot before cheating, and do not cheat on the playthrough you are using to go for the platinum.

What About PC or Online?

There is no PC version yet (see here), and no GTA Online for GTA 6. Cheats only apply to story mode on PS5 and Xbox Series.

Frequently asked questions

How do you enter a cheat code in GTA 6 on PS5?

Press up on the D-pad to take out the phone, hold L1 then press R1, and enter the combination with the D-pad. The cheat must be unlocked and the story finished.

How do you enter a cheat code in GTA 6 on Xbox?

Same method as on PS5: up on the D-pad for the phone, hold LB then press RB, then the combination on the D-pad.

Do cheats disable trophies?

Yes, for the whole play session: trophies, achievements and saving are blocked. Quit the game and load a save to get them back.

Can you use cheats during the story?

No. The cheat menu only opens once the main story is finished.
