GTA 6 Collectible Cards: Sets and Trophy

Updated November 24, 2026

GTA 6 has 18 sets of collectible cards, 480 cards in total.

Completing 3 sets earns the “Don't Touch My Deck” trophy, and you need to complete 15 (372 cards at minimum) for 100%.

How many cards are there?

Three guides give the same numbers: 18 sets, 480 different cards, and between 12 and 36 cards per set. The cards are in fixed spots in the world; they do not respawn. In the Progress > Collections menu, you can see the list of sets and your progress (we saw this menu in our playthroughs, with “Collectible Cards,” “Kenton Treasures” and “WAiNK Locations”).

Where to find them

  • Indoors: on the counters, tables and shelves of rooms you can enter.
  • Outdoors: in garage toolboxes and in the yellow phone booths fixed to walls.

A card can only be picked up once. A duplicate does not advance the set, and color variants count as separate cards. Note the full name of the set, because several start with the name of the same city.

Trophies and 100%

  • Don't Touch My Deck: complete 3 sets. The fastest way is to aim for the smallest ones (Backyard Wrestling, Delights Dancers and one set of 24), for 54 cards at minimum.
  • 100%: 15 sets out of 18 are enough, which makes 372 cards at minimum. So you can skip the three biggest sets of 36 cards.
One guide links another trophy to the cards, but its name and condition vary between guides: we are not listing it until it is cross-checked. The official list is on the trophies page.

Selling your cards

Pawn shops buy cards. The price depends on rarity, from about $20 to over $1,000 for one card according to the guides. A sold card stays in your collection: it does not set the set back. So you can sell it right away if you need cash. Details are on the pawn shop page, and more ideas are in how to make money.

Frequently asked questions

How many collectible cards are there in GTA 6?

480 cards spread across 18 sets, with 12 to 36 cards each.

How many sets do you need for 100%?

15 sets out of 18, so 372 cards at minimum.

Where do you find the cards?

In fixed spots: on counters, tables and shelves indoors, and in toolboxes and yellow phone booths outdoors.

Does selling a card make you lose your spot in the set?

No. A card sold at a pawn shop stays in your collection.
