Bigfoot in GTA 6: How to Find the Skunk Ape

Updated November 24, 2026

GTA 6's “Bigfoot” is called the Skunk Ape.

You can run into it freely in the swamps south of Cottonmouth Lake, then Kirk's quest, with three night missions, lets you hunt it: it starts with a poster at the bar in Hamlet.

Bigfoot or Skunk Ape?

In the game, locals talk about the Skunk Ape, Florida's “stinky ape,” rather than Bigfoot. The creature's nickname in the quest is Mr. Mangrove. Our easter eggs page sums up the encounter; here is the full path, step by step, with the conditions for each mission.

Step 1: the free encounter

No mission is needed. As soon as you can explore freely (chapter 1), go to the swamps south of Cottonmouth Lake, below Hamlet, in Mariana County. On a dirt road, a deer carcass attracts the creature. It runs away if you get close: follow it to an effigy surrounded by mist. The mist knocks you out and you wake up not far away.

The region is covered on the Grassrivers page.

Step 2: start Kirk's quest

The quest is part of the Strangers & Freaks (see this page).

  1. Read one of the posters by Kirk, a cryptid specialist: one is at Skeeter's bar in Hamlet. Kirk then becomes a phone contact.
  2. Call him: a marker appears on the map, in the swamps near the Pelican River.
  3. Play at night: the mission only starts once night has fallen. If it is daytime, sleep at home to move the clock forward.
Reward: about $1,000 for the first mission, according to three guides.

The three missions, in order

MissionStated conditions
Search for the Skunk Ape (Skunk Apeology 101)Chapter 1, at night. Jason or Lucia according to most guides (one guide says Jason only).
Search for Tree EffigiesAt night, after the first one.
Help Kirk Find Mr. MangroveAt night, with Lucia only, from chapter 2. After the second mission, wait a few in-game days: Kirk texts you and the quest shows up on the map.

All three missions count toward the Strangers & Freaks trophy and the first one is required for 100% (see the trophies). We do not reveal the final choice or what it earns you: it is better to discover it while playing.

Practical tips

  • Play at night: if the sun comes up, sleep at home rather than waiting.
  • Plan to play Lucia for the last mission: it is not available with Jason. See how to switch characters.
  • The swamps and their landmarks are described on the map page.

Frequently asked questions

Where do you find Bigfoot in GTA 6?

In the swamps south of Cottonmouth Lake, south of Hamlet (Mariana County). On a dirt road, it eats a deer carcass before running away.

Do you have to play at night?

Yes for Kirk's missions: they only start at night, and you can sleep at home to move the clock forward. The free encounter has no known time condition.

How do you start Kirk's quest?

Read one of his posters (one is at Skeeter's bar in Hamlet), then call him from the phone: a marker appears in the swamps.

How much does the first mission pay?

About $1,000 according to three guides.

Can you do the last mission with Jason?

No. According to the guides we read, the “Help Kirk Find Mr. Mangrove” mission is reserved for Lucia.
