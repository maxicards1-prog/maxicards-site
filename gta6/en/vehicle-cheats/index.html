Which Challenge for Which Rare Vehicle?

  • Hauners P-996 Lazer fighter jet: complete all the challenges in the game, the longest one.
  • Hauners Rhino tank: all the survival challenges.
  • Bravado Banshee: all the combat challenges.
  • Progen Emerus: all the vehicle challenges.
  • Nagasaki Sea Sparrow helicopter: only 5 exploration challenges, one of the quickest to get.

Cars During the Story

  • Scan a car with the WAiNK app before stealing it: it shows the security features (alarm, tracker), the resale value and the registration cost.
  • A car fitted with a tracker cannot be modified or registered.
  • To keep a car, you need a garage or a free space; the Scooter Bros app has a registered car delivered.
  • In chapter 3, Randy buys the sports cars on his list for $30,000 or more each, if they are in excellent condition: see fast money.

On PS5: press ↑ on the D-pad to take out the phone, hold L1 then press R1, and enter the combination. On Xbox Series: same thing with LB then RB. Cheats only work after you finish the story, and each one must first be unlocked by its challenge (Progression menu > Cheat Codes). Details: how to enter a cheat code.
