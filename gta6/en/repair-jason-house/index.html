Repairing Jason's house in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

The railing is fixed on the back terrace of Jason's house in Key Lento, near the workout equipment.

Do it, like the three other repairs, before Lucia gets out of prison, because afterward it is impossible.

Where the railing is

The repairs are done at Jason's house, in Key Lento, in the Leonida Keys. For the railing, go out the back and down to the lower part of the terrace. Turn toward the house: the missing bars are next to the sports equipment and the ladder. Wait for the action prompt. One guide places it instead at the top of the second outdoor staircase, near the weight bench: the guides disagree, but it is the same area, and you just need to look around the workout equipment.

Tip. If the prompt does not appear, stand on the lower terrace, facing the house, rather than along the upper railing.

The four repairs

RepairWhereGood to know
StepsFront outdoor staircase, near the planksRequired to progress and enter the house.
Roof leakOn the roof, by the ladder near the doorOptional, missable.
RailingBack terrace, near the workout equipmentOptional, missable.
Wall and paintJason's bedroomCounts as one repair, the wall then the paint.

There is nothing to buy: the tools are already on site. The railing is fixed with the nail gun, by holding the R2 button (RT on Xbox) when the prompt appears.

Why do them, and until when

  • Each repair improves the relationship between Jason and Lucia. If you skip them, Lucia's starting meter, when she gets out of prison, is much lower.
  • No guide mentions a cash reward or a trophy.
  • There is no timer, but the repairs are missable as soon as Lucia is out: the activity is no longer available afterward. So make a save before going to pick her up (see saving the game).

How to make them appear

They become possible once you have control of Jason after the first missions (see chapter 1). The activity is not announced: open the map and look at the optional objectives at the house. You can do them over several visits. To understand the couple's relationship, see Jason, Lucia and the contacts.

Frequently asked questions

Where do you fix the railing in GTA 6?

On the back terrace of Jason's house in Key Lento, near the workout equipment. Face the house and wait for the prompt.

Can you miss the house repairs?

Yes: once Lucia is out of prison, they are no longer available. There is no timer before that.

What do the repairs pay?

No money according to the guides we read: they improve your starting relationship with Lucia, which will be lower if you skip them.

How many repairs are there?

Four: the steps, the roof leak, the railing, and the wall with its paint in the bedroom.
