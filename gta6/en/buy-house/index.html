Can You Buy a House in GTA 6?

Updated November 24, 2026

No, you cannot buy a house, an apartment or a villa in GTA 6.

The only properties for sale are eight garages; the safehouses of Jason and Lucia unlock for free with the story.

Why no houses?

Jason and Lucia are fugitives. Guides sum up the situation this way: the game gives you places to sleep as the story goes on, rather than letting you build up assets. No business to buy has been announced either, and no guide describes any passive income tied to a property.

What you can buy: garages

Eight garages are on offer, from $4,000 to $40,000 depending on the guides ($160,000 for all of them). Each garage offers one vehicle slot. The details are on our dedicated garage page.

Free safehouses, chapter by chapter

These places are obtained on their own, at no cost. They are not permanent: some disappear as the story moves forward. Nothing is bought, so there is no rent to pay.

Where to store your things?

  • A vehicle's trunk: long guns can be stored there (see vehicles and weapons).
  • Garages: your cars are stored there, and they also serve as a drop-off point for loot.
  • Jason's hiding place: for money, early on (see losing your money).

So what do you spend your money on?

With no house to buy, money goes elsewhere. Garages total $160,000. The rest goes to weapons (in our games, an assault rifle was listed at $1,450 in a gun store), clothes (the Thrillbilly Mud Club shop) and vehicle customization. For gun store prices, see vehicles and weapons.

Also remember that any purchase over $1,000 is paid with BuckMe: keep enough to pay in your account (BuckMe bank).

The list of safehouses varies slightly from one guide to another. If one is missing in your game, that is normal: it all depends on your progress. The "Little Boxes" trophy only concerns garages (trophy list).
