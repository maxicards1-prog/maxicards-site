Where to Find Hidden Money in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

Several places in GTA 6 hide easy money: the back of the Key Lento liquor store, the plane wrecks in Grassrivers, a warehouse near Hamlet and underwater treasure.

Here is what they pay, according to guides.

Key Lento: behind the liquor store

This is a hideout you can access after helping your neighbor Shirley bring back rum. Go around the store through the neighboring fence, take out the guards, then talk to the parrot, which points to the money near the generator. Expect about $1,300 in an air vent, $900 in the back room safe and about $100 in the register.

Plane wrecks in Grassrivers

When a smoking plane crashes, follow the smoke and look under the wreck: about $1,000 worth of cocaine is waiting for you. Leave quickly, as rival criminals and the police often show up. The loot then has to be sold to a fence (see where to sell).

Broken-down vehicles

On the roads of Leonida, stop by drivers in trouble, unless one of them asks you to leave. The trunk may hold a bag of illegal goods worth about $1,280. Payment arrives in your mailbox the next day.

South Vice-Dale County warehouse

About three miles south of Hamlet, it holds weapons, a bag of marked bills, cocaine, cash in a register and in a safe, and a rare collectible card (collectible cards). Guides value the whole lot at about $13,000, not counting the card, which a pawn shop buys for about $6,000.

Underwater treasure in the Keys

Purple icons mark the treasure hunts around the Leonida Keys. With Kenton, a diving suit and a scanner, you recover gold coins (about $200 each), candlesticks and chests. One hunt paid about $2,600. For the gear, see diving. You will also find these treasures in our Progress > Collections menu.

Other simple ideas

  • Jason's safehouse is used to store money early on (protecting your money).
The amounts are guide estimates, not guarantees. These places also remain dangerous: deposit before you go. More methods on making money.

Frequently asked questions

Where can you find money easily early in GTA 6?

In Key Lento, behind the liquor store (about $2,300 in total), then in the Grassrivers plane wrecks and on broken-down vehicles.

How much does the South Vice-Dale County warehouse pay?

About $13,000 in cash and loot, not counting the rare card, according to two guides.

What should you do with drug or jewelry loot?

Sell it to a fence: only dollars count, not the loot bag.
