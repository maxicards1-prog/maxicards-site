How to Save Your Game in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

To save in GTA 6, open the pause menu, go to the “Game” tab, then choose “Save Game” and a slot.

The game also has an autosave, but it is infrequent: it is better to save by hand.

Saving by hand, step by step

Manual saving is done from the pause menu. You don't need to reach a safehouse or go to sleep. The four guides we cross-checked describe the same path: the Options button on PS5, the Menu button on Xbox Series, then the “Game” tab and the save option. You then pick an empty slot, or a used slot to overwrite it. Wait until the save is finished before closing the menu.

Watch out for overwriting: replacing a slot erases the progress it contained. If in doubt, pick an empty slot instead.

How many slots?

The guides mention 15 manual slots. One detail differs: most add a separate slot reserved for the autosave (16 in total), while one guide counts the autosave among the 15. Either way, you have plenty of room to keep several points of your game.

The autosave

GTA 6 also saves on its own, in a separate slot that is overwritten every time. The guides say it triggers after a completed mission and, according to two of them, after certain events such as a death or an arrest. It is not a replacement for a real save: don't rely on it alone, especially before an important moment.

When can't you save?

  • During a mission: the option is greyed out. You have to wait until you are back in the open world, then save before or after the mission.
  • In the middle of the action: if the police are after you, first learn how to lose them, then save once things are calm.

Why save before big decisions

Two guides state that there is no menu to replay a mission you have already finished. Manual saves are therefore the only way to go back. A simple tip: create a new slot after each chapter, and keep one from before an important choice. Our missions list helps you spot the end of each chapter.

Careful when loading: one guide warns that loading an old manual save can replace the autosave. If you just want to test going back, first save your current progress in a free slot.

What if you cheat?

Cheats have their own rules, explained in our page on how to activate cheat codes. Remember to save before trying one, so you keep a clean game. For more good habits from the start, see also our beginner tips.

Step by step

  1. Open the pause menu : Press Options (PS5) or Menu (Xbox Series) while you are in the open world, outside a mission.
  2. Go to the Game tab : Open the “Game” tab (the name quoted by the guides), which groups saving and loading.
  3. Choose Save Game : Select the save option (“Save Game”).
  4. Choose a slot : Pick an empty slot, or an existing one if you accept overwriting it, then wait until the save is finished.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an autosave in GTA 6?

Yes, in a separate slot that is overwritten every time. It mostly follows completed missions: save by hand as well.

How many manual saves can you have?

The guides mention 15 manual slots. Only one counts the autosave among those 15; the others add it on top.

Can you save in the middle of a mission?

No, the option is greyed out during a mission. Go back to the open world, before or after the mission, to save.

Can you replay a finished mission with an old save?

Yes, it is the only way described by the guides, since there is no menu to replay missions.
