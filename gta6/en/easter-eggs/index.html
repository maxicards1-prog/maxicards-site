Creatures and mysteries

The Skunk Ape, the swamp Bigfoot

Where: in the swamps south of Hamlet (Grassrivers region, Mariana County according to the guides), on a dirt path.

How to do it: The creature is eating a deer carcass at the side of the path and runs away when you get close. Follow it: it leads you to an effigy surrounded by mist. Breathing in the mist makes you pass out; the creature comes to examine you, then you wake up a bit farther away. The guides mention no particular time of day or required mission.

What you get: no reward reported, but it is the most talked-about mystery in the game.

Sources: PowerPyx (November 18, 2026), ComicBook.com (November 20, 2026), GamingBible (November 20, 2026), GAMES.GG (November 20, 2026)

Kirk's quest: hunting the "stink ape"

Where: it starts in Hamlet, with a poster from Kirk, a creature hunter (in the French version of the game: "Aider à chercher le singe-qui-pue"). We started it ourselves.

How to do it: The quest has several steps: binoculars, tracks in the swamps and effigies. At the end, the game gives you a choice. We won't reveal what comes next: it is one of the best surprises in the side story.

What you get: the reward varies between guides: we will add it once it is cross-checked.

Sources: PowerPyx (November 18, 2026), ComicBook.com (November 20, 2026), BuzzFeed (via Yahoo) (November 20, 2026)

The UFO and the "The Truth Is Out There" trophy

Where: on a country road near the Starlet Motel hideout, in the Port Gellhorn region.

How to do it: At night, after 2 a.m. (in-game time), once you have reached chapter 2 according to most guides, look for an abandoned car with its emergency lights on and its doors open: it doesn't appear every time. Follow the clothes to the light, help the woman left behind by the UFO, then bring her car back. We'll leave the rest to you.

What you get: the "The Truth Is Out There" trophy (achievement on Xbox).

Sources: Beebom (November 19, 2026), GAMES.GG (November 23, 2026), ComicBook.com (November 20, 2026), BuzzFeed (via Yahoo) (November 20, 2026)

The abandoned research base

Where: in Pelican Valley, near the swamps.

How to do it: After the UFO, Jason can find an abandoned research site, "Area 51" style: going in quickly raises your wanted level. The guides describe a follow-up with an alien to bring back to the UFO. The code for one door circulates in two different versions: we will publish it once it is confirmed.

What you get: experience according to one guide; nothing cross-checked for now.

Sources: ComicBook.com (November 20, 2026), BuzzFeed (via Yahoo) (November 20, 2026)

Nods to movies, TV shows and other games

The real Easter egg at the top of the radio tower

Where: the radio tower south of Vice City (South Vice-Dale County), presented as the highest point in the game.

How to do it: Get past the fence at the foot of the tower and climb: allow 6 to 7 minutes to reach the top. Up there is a nest of eggs. Take an egg and throw it: it lands on a car's windshield and triggers a short scene. An influencer is also filming at the top; you can taunt them.

What you get: no trophy, just the scene and the view.

Sources: IBTimes UK (November 19, 2026), ComicBook.com (November 20, 2026), GamingBible (November 20, 2026), BuzzFeed (via Yahoo) (November 20, 2026), GAMES.GG (November 20, 2026)

Dexter Morgan's apartment

Where: Vice City, Washington Island, Trinity Apartments building near Vice Beach, next to the drone shop and the Pay N' Spray: 2nd floor, northwest corner.

How to do it: You can't go inside. Climb onto the roof: items belonging to the killer from the series are laid out there (knife, syringe, rope); one guide did not find them, so they seem not to appear every time. The boat moored right next to it recalls the one from the show, and you can steal it.

What you get: a few dollars and a boat.

Sources: Dexerto (November 19, 2026), Insider Gaming (November 19, 2026), ComicBook.com (November 20, 2026), GamingBible (November 20, 2026)

The Fast & Furious Mitsubishi Eclipse

Where: Vice City, Vespucci Island (Rialto Islands): a white house in the middle of the island, on the left as you head toward Vice Beach.

How to do it: Climb over the hedge (or go through the gate if it is open) and force the garage door: the green Eclipse, a tribute to Paul Walker, is yours. No mission or purchase needed. It doesn't always appear on your first visit: come back later.

What you get: an iconic car, for free.

Sources: Jalopnik (November 19, 2026), BGR (November 19, 2026)

Arthur Morgan's hat (Red Dead Redemption 2)

Where: south of Port Gellhorn, at a yard sale, near a couch.

How to do it: From chapter 2, buy the "Genuine Cowboy Hat" (the price differs between guides). If you get it early, only Lucia can wear it; Jason can later.

What you get: the hat of the hero of Red Dead Redemption 2.

Sources: Gfinity (November 19, 2026), BuzzFeed (via Yahoo) (November 20, 2026)

The conspiracy theorist's house in Belville

Where: Belville. This house already appeared in an image hidden in GTA San Andreas Definitive Edition, in 2021.

How to do it: Force the garage: inside you find the notes of a man convinced he lives in a video game. He can neither leave the state nor buy a ticket to Los Santos, and he talks about "J" and "L"... like Jason and Lucia.

What you get: nothing, except chills.

Sources: Dexerto (November 19, 2026), GAMES.GG (November 19, 2026), BuzzFeed (via Yahoo) (November 20, 2026)

The parrots that talk too much

Where: a liquor store in the Leonida Keys.

How to do it: During a brawl in the store, the parrots start talking. One of them repeats "generator": money is hidden near a generator.

What you get: cash.

Sources: GamingBible (November 20, 2026), BuzzFeed (via Yahoo) (November 20, 2026)

The rescue dog on a jet ski

Where: at sea, off the beaches, mostly in the Keys.

How to do it: If you swim too far from the coast, a dog on a jet ski may come to your rescue; it leads you to a small island where dogs gather. The encounter is not guaranteed.

What you get: a rescue, and one of the funniest scenes in the game.

Sources: ComicBook.com (November 20, 2026), GamingBible (November 20, 2026), BuzzFeed (via Yahoo) (November 20, 2026)

Nods to GTA 5

  • Michael's yacht is moored in front of Jason's house in the Keys: climb aboard and look at the GPS, it shows the map of Los Santos.
  • The "Wind Chimes" ringtone, Michael's, can be chosen in the phone settings (Settings, Ringtone).
  • The Mount Chiliad mystery comes back in a conversation between two passers-by outside a bar, early in the story (mission with Donny and Brian).
  • "Race and Chase 7": on the phone's social network, posts joke about the wait for... GTA 7.

Sources: BuzzFeed (via Yahoo) (November 20, 2026), GAMES.GG (November 20, 2026), GamingBible (November 20, 2026)

Small hidden details

  • JoySystem consoles: install one in your hideout and play, notably "Tenebrae Lux"; there are also arcade cabinets, for example at the Bargain World in Port Gellhorn.
  • The "Petty Forever" sticker, a tribute to the singer Tom Petty, is stuck on a store door.

Sources: GAMES.GG (November 20, 2026), BuzzFeed (via Yahoo) (November 20, 2026)

Frequently asked questions

Where can you find Bigfoot in GTA 6?

The Skunk Ape, the Bigfoot equivalent, is in the swamps south of Hamlet: it eats a deer carcass on a dirt path, then leads you to an effigy surrounded by mist. A quest from Kirk, the "stink ape" hunter, is also devoted to it.

How do you get the UFO trophy in GTA 6?

At night, after 2 a.m., near the Starlet Motel in Port Gellhorn: find the abandoned car with its emergency lights on, follow the clothes to the light and help the woman. The trophy is called "The Truth Is Out There".

Are there references to GTA 5 in GTA 6?

Yes: Michael's yacht, whose GPS shows Los Santos, the "Wind Chimes" ringtone, a conversation about Mount Chiliad and jokes about GTA 7.

Are these easter eggs official?

Rockstar does not publish a list. They were found by players since launch; we only keep those that several guides describe the same way.
