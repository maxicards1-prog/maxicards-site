GTA 6 Online: when does it come out?

Updated November 24, 2026

As of November 24, 2026, Rockstar has announced no date for a GTA 6 Online.

The game came out on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series as a single-player experience, and the current GTA Online is still running.

What Rockstar said, and what it did not say

Rockstar has always presented the PS5 and Xbox Series version of GTA 6 as a single-player experience. The studio has not officially announced an online mode, nor a date, nor a price, nor shown a single image of such a mode. Articles published since release, such as the GTABase one updated on November 22, confirm it: nothing official to date.

If you have read “GTA 6 Online comes out in 2027” or “comes out in such and such month”, those are deductions. The only dated lead comes from Dan Clancy, the head of Twitch, who said he expects this mode “next year” after talks with Rockstar. This is not an announcement from Rockstar or Take-Two, and it should be read as a hint, not a fact.

Key takeaway. No online mode at launch, no official date, and 2027 is only a guess from a third party.

Rumors not to mistake for facts

Multiplayer-related pieces of code found in the game files and renewed domain names (GTA 6, Vice City Online) fuel the discussion. These are not announcements: a renewed domain name proves neither content nor a schedule. Figures like “32 players” circulate with no official source. On this site, we do not repeat them.

What happens to GTA Online?

Take-Two stated, at its September shareholder meeting, that support for the current GTA Online would continue after the release of GTA VI. Take-Two’s CEO, Strauss Zelnick, had already said in February that he had good reason to believe this support would continue. According to GTABase, the current GTA Online is also still promoted on Rockstar’s official website and runs alongside GTA 6.

How do you leave GTA VI Online?

The question comes up often in searches, but the answer is simple: there is no GTA VI Online to leave for now. When you launch GTA 6, you play solo, with no online session. If you are thinking of the current GTA Online, it remains a separate game, on its own servers. We found no reliable source describing a way to leave a future GTA 6 online mode, and we would rather not make anything up.
