How to call your car in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

To call your car in GTA 6, phone Joey in chapter 1, then use the Scooter Bros phone app from the Moses mission in chapter 2.

Only registered vehicles can be delivered.

In chapter 1: the Joey contact

At the start of the story, there is no delivery app. The guides agree: a contact named Joey appears in your phone book. You call him, and he brings you your car without asking questions. The two guides that mention him also say he repairs the vehicle for free.

Open the phone with the up arrow of the directional pad, choose Contacts, call Joey and wait. Since chapter 1 does not yet let you register a vehicle, this service is the only way to get your car back from a distance during this part of the game.

From chapter 2: Scooter Bros

The Scooter Bros app unlocks after the main mission of chapter 2 called Moses (Take A Vehicle to Moses), when you register your first car. Its icon is a green car, in the phone.

  1. Open the phone (up arrow).
  2. Launch Scooter Bros.
  3. Select the car you want from your list of personal vehicles.
  4. Choose "Request", pay the fee and wait for the courier.

The vehicle comes to you: you do not need to be near a garage. The app does not deliver your character, only the car.

Requirements to know

  • Registered vehicle required. A stolen car that is not registered cannot be called, and if it is destroyed it is lost. See how to keep a stolen car.
  • No registration in chapter 1. Before the Moses mission, only Joey is available.
  • To keep several cars, consider buying a garage.

How much does it cost?

Every request costs money. The amount is not the same from one guide to another (some mention about $100, others give no figure): check the price shown on screen before confirming. The premium option, however, is quoted at the same price by three guides: $12,500, one time, for three free deliveries per game day. It is also used to get a car out of the impound lot, see our page on the impound lot.

What if the call does not work?

First check that the car is properly registered and that you have completed the Moses mission. A delivery also does not clear your wanted level: lose the police first.

Step by step

  1. Open the phone: Press the up arrow of the directional pad.
  2. Launch Scooter Bros: Choose the app with the green car icon (chapter 2 onward). In chapter 1, call Joey in Contacts.
  3. Choose the car: Select a registered personal vehicle from the list.
  4. Request the delivery: Press "Request", pay the fee and wait for the car to arrive.

Frequently asked questions

How do you call your car at the start of GTA 6?

Open the phone, go to Contacts and call Joey: he delivers your car for free and repairs it.

Where is the Scooter Bros app?

In the phone (up arrow), green car icon, after the Moses mission (Take A Vehicle to Moses) in chapter 2.

Can you call a stolen car?

No, only registered personal vehicles. A stolen car that is not registered is lost if you abandon it or if it is destroyed.

What does the Scooter Bros premium option give?

For $12,500 one time, three free deliveries per game day and the recovery of impounded vehicles.
