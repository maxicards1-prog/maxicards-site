What Do You Lose on Death in GTA 6?

Updated November 24, 2026

If you die or get arrested in GTA 6, you lose the cash you were carrying.

The money in your BuckMe account is almost untouched: a small sum is taken, with no confirmed amount.

What is lost

  • Cash in your pocket: guides say it all goes. In our games, we saw the same cash loss on the red death screen.
  • Unsold loot: loot bags you are carrying can be lost if you do not escape the police.
  • Weapons you do not own: temporary weapons disappear.

What is kept

  • The weapons and vehicles you own.
  • The money in your BuckMe account, apart from a small loss: several guides mention "maintenance" fees. None gives a precise amount, but all describe it as much smaller than a cash loss.
  • The money in family savings, which can never be lost (but cannot be withdrawn either).

How to protect yourself

  1. Deposit regularly. One stop at an ATM is enough: see the BuckMe bank.
  2. Sell loot quickly. At a fence, it turns into money (where to sell).
  3. Use Jason's hiding place early on. Before the BuckMe account, the floorboards in his bedroom protect money and items. This protection is temporary: story events can put it in question.
  4. Lose the police before delivering. Clear your wanted level, see losing the police.

Cash limit

Two guides agree on a limit of $20,000 in cash per character. The closer you get to it, the heavier the loss on death: a good habit is to deposit well before that.

Before a risky job: the checklist

  • Deposit all your cash at the nearest ATM (the BuckMe locator guides you there).
  • Sell the loot you already have, or drop it off at a garage: an undelivered bag has no value and can disappear.
  • Long guns can be stored in a vehicle's trunk (seen in our playthrough): remember this before you leave.
  • Plan an escape route: the shorter the escape, the lower the risk of arrest.

Cash or bank: what to carry?

Shops accept cash for small purchases, up to about $999. Above $1,000, BuckMe pays. So carry just enough for small purchases and leave the rest in the bank.

Frequently asked questions

Do you lose all your money when you die in GTA 6?

You lose all the cash you carry. Money in the bank (BuckMe) is barely touched: only a small sum is taken.

How much are the BuckMe maintenance fees?

No guide gives the exact amount. They are described as much smaller than the cash you lose.

How do you protect your money before getting BuckMe?

Early on, hide it under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom. The protection is not permanent.
