The Settings to Change First in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

In GTA 6, the useful settings are in the pause menu, under Settings.

Before playing, check the aiming mode, aim assist, blur and camera shake, subtitles and accessibility assists: these are the most useful ones.

Where are the settings?

Open the pause menu (Options on PS5, Menu on Xbox Series) then Settings. The guides mention several tabs: Gameplay, UI, Display, Camera, Controller and Audio. The first setting is the control scheme: Default, Classic or Left-Handed. The Classic scheme is closer to the controls of the older GTA games; see our page on the controls.

Aiming

In the Gameplay tab, there are three targeting modes: Lock-On, Partial (the reticle is pulled toward a nearby target) and Free Aim (never locks on). The strength of the assist is set separately, with “Aim Assist Strength”, from 0 to 4. Opinions differ: one guide recommends 4 to hit distant enemies, another suggests 0 for raw aiming. If you are starting out on a controller, begin high and lower it as you get used to it. Other assists exist, such as Shooting Assist and Drive-By Assist for aiming from a vehicle. We also saw the “aim assist” and “partial aim” settings in our own playthrough.

Camera, blur and comfort

  • Motion Blur Strength: can be set to 0 if the blur bothers you when driving.
  • Camera Shake, Camera Bob, Weapon Sway: can be reduced, down to zero, for a steadier image.

Subtitles

By default, subtitles may be limited to missions (“Mission Only”). Set them to “On” to follow all dialogue, and increase their size if you play from a distance. According to the guides, they are in the Interface or Audio menu: the exact location varies.

Accessibility assists

They are at the bottom of the Gameplay tab. You can replace holds with simple presses (Hold Assist, Tap Assist), or the other way around. The “Simplified mini-games”, “Accessible combat” and “Reduced vehicle damage” options clearly change the difficulty. We saw them in our playthrough: they help, but change the balance of the game. Also think about auto drive, which is set in the same menu.

Tip: change one or two settings at a time and test them while playing. If you don't like it, go back without losing anything. Remember to save before a tricky mission.

Frequently asked questions

Which aiming mode should you choose in GTA 6?

There are three modes: Lock-On, Partial and Free Aim. The guides differ: Lock-On is the simplest for beginners, Free Aim demands more precision.

How do you reduce motion blur?

In the Display menu, set “Motion Blur Strength”, which you can set to 0. You can also reduce camera shake in the Camera tab.

How do you get all subtitles?

Set “Subtitles” to On rather than Mission Only, and adjust their size in the settings.

Can you make the game easier?

Yes, with accessibility options such as accessible combat, reduced vehicle damage or simplified mini-games.
