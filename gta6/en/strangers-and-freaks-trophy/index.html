Strangers and Freaks in GTA 6: The Quests

Updated November 24, 2026

The Strangers and Freaks trophy requires you to finish 8 stranger mission series.

The guides only confirm four of them with certainty: Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete.

What the trophy requires

In the trophy list, "Strangers and Freaks" is a Silver trophy: "Complete 8 stranger mission series". The guides call it "Strangers & Freaks". Not all side quests count: the guides say that Shirley, Cal, Moses, Billy, Andy, Yasmani, Tyrone and Eddie Acosta are not part of the count. They sometimes have their own trophy.

The four confirmed series

SeriesChapterMissionsGood to know
Kirk (Skunk Ape)1, ends in chapter 23Played at night; one guide reports a contradiction about the character required for the last one.
Kyle (the farm)2, continues in chapter 33Check Out McKittrick Farm, Collect Chemicals for Kyle, Buddy?. The last one unlocks by message.
Yago (street races)34Race Against Yago is timed and missable.
Pilot Pete44 or 5 depending on the guidesReserved for Jason; plane deliveries.

The other four series are not identified with certainty: the guides we read cite other names (some mention Dr. Merle, Freddie or Alvan), but do not confirm them consistently. So we do not publish a complete list of eight.

How to check your progress

The surest way, according to all the guides we read: look at the trophy counter on your console before and after the last mission of each series. If the counter moves, the series counts.

Tips so you miss nothing

  • Do the hourglass missions before moving ahead in the story.
  • After one step, the quest giver may take several weeks of in-game time to contact you again: do something else in the meantime.
  • Make a manual save before the final missions of a series.
  • The missable missions (Shirley, Race Against Yago) are not needed for the trophy, according to the guides.

For the full list of side quests and how to start them, read the side quests. To avoid missing the current chapter's content, see chapter 1.

In which order should you do them?

No order is imposed, according to the guides we read. Kirk and Kyle start in chapters 1 and 2 and can be finished later without blocking the story. Yago appears in chapter 3 and Pilot Pete in chapter 4. So keep an eye on calls and messages in each new chapter, because a series started in one chapter can end in another. Most quests stay accessible after the end of the story, except the time-limited ones.

This page will be updated when the guides agree on the four missing series.

Frequently asked questions

How many series do you need for the Strangers and Freaks trophy?

Eight stranger mission series, according to the trophy description.

Which series definitely count?

Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete: these are the four series confirmed by all the guides we read.

Do Shirley's quests count?

No. Her two favors are missable and do not count toward the trophy.

How do you know if a series counts?

Look at the trophy counter on your console before and after the last mission of the series.
