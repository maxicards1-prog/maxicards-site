GTA 6 Characters: Jason, Lucia and the Others

Updated November 24, 2026

GTA 6 is played with two characters: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.

The first two chapters also have you meet a few recurring contacts, introduced here without revealing anything about what comes next.

The two playable characters

Jason works for smugglers in the Leonida Keys. He would like a calmer life, but his situation keeps him from it. He is the one you control in most of the early missions: the first mission played as Jason is Gone Fishing.

Lucia opens the game with the Prologue: she is getting out of prison and trying to rebuild her life. She reunites with Jason in the mission Coming Home, and she is the one you play alone at the start of chapter 2. The two heroes are partners as a couple: the guides say their partnership continues even if the romantic side stays optional.

To know who plays what, check the list of the 91 story missions, or the chapter 1 and chapter 2 pages for mission-by-mission tips.

The relationship between Jason and Lucia

The game tracks a relationship level between the two heroes, from 1 to 4 out of 4. The guides agree on three names: "Running on Empty" for level 1, "Crazy in Love" for level 3 and "Hot Together" for level 4. The name of level 2 varies by source.

What raises it, according to several guides:

  • answering messages and spending time together (outings, activities);
  • succeeding at missions and heists as a pair;
  • adding to the joint savings account in the BuckMe app (see the BuckMe bank);
  • doing the small gestures before Coming Home: groceries, flowers, clothes, haircut, necklace.

What lowers it: ignoring her calls and messages, abandoning your partner, failing joint missions, or running her over with a vehicle. A damaged relationship can temporarily make the affectionate gestures disappear; they return when the bond is rebuilt.

The guides do not agree on the real impact of this relationship on the story. It does not stop you from playing: play both characters, answer the calls and the gauge maintains itself.

On a question that comes up often: the sources we read say Lucia cannot leave Jason for good, and that the two heroes stay partners throughout the story.

Frequently asked questions

Can you play as both Jason and Lucia?

Yes, both are playable. The story assigns you a character depending on the mission: Jason for most of the early missions, Lucia for the prologue and the start of chapter 2.

Can Lucia leave Jason?

According to the guides we read, no: Lucia cannot break up for good and the two heroes stay partners throughout the story.

How do you raise the relationship between Jason and Lucia?

By answering her messages, going on outings together, succeeding at joint missions, adding to the joint savings account and doing the small gestures before Coming Home.

Do the two heroes have different endings?

The guides do not agree on how the relationship affects the story. We give no information about the ending of the game.
