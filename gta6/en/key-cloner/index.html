The Key Cloner in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

The key cloner unlocks in the story, in chapter 3, with a Randy mission.

It opens cars that need a key, but it does not remove their tracker.

How to get it

You cannot buy it. It is given by the main mission "Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS (Steal the Zorrusso for Randy)", about halfway through chapter 3. Randy sends you an app: install it afterward from your phone settings. Once unlocked, the tool stays available.

How to use it

  1. Scan the car with WAiNK and read the access, security and tracker fields.
  2. Walk up to the driver's door and choose "Clone key".
  3. Fill the gauge with the analog stick.
  4. Get in the car and start it.

Cloning the key does not remove the tracker

This is the key point: the cloner deals with the lock, not the tracker. The supercars that require it often have an advanced tracker, which can report your position to the police when you slow down. The guides disagree on the details: one says driving fast stops your position from being sent, another says stopping or slowing down fills the alert gauge. The advice is the same: keep moving and plan your route before stealing.

A standard tracker does not spy on your position, but it blocks modifications to the car until it is in your name.

Pitfalls to avoid

The mission does not end when you install the app: you still have to steal the target car and deliver it to Randy in good condition. If you slow down too much in a car with an advanced tracker, the police arrive fast and gather in the area. So pick a clear route, preferably a big road, before you start. The cloner remains a help for modern keyless cars: for the others, Moses' Slim Jim is enough to open a locked door.

What next?

A car with an advanced tracker cannot be registered just anywhere: you need to have progressed with Randy, see registering a vehicle. You can also sell it to Randy. To keep your hauls more simply, read keeping a stolen car. One last tip: save before a risky mission, see saving your game.

Frequently asked questions

When do you unlock the key cloner?

In chapter 3, during the mission "Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS", about halfway through the chapter.

Does the key cloner remove the tracker?

No. It opens and starts the car, but the tracker stays active.

How do you know if a car needs the cloner?

Scan it with WAiNK: the screen shows its access, security and tracker.
