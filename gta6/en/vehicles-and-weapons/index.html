GTA 6 Vehicles and Weapons: The Complete Guide

Updated November 24, 2026

In GTA 6, you steal most vehicles, then register them to keep them; weapons are bought in gun shops.

This page sums it all up and links to each detailed guide.

Vehicle types

The game has cars, motorcycles, planes, helicopters and boats. Each type is obtained in its own way. Cars are stolen in the city, aircraft are found in specific places, and the fighter jet means getting into a military base.

How to keep a vehicle

A stolen vehicle is not yours: if you abandon it or it gets destroyed, you lose it. To keep it, you have to register it. That means Moses' garage in Port Gellhorn (cars without a tracker), then the Pay 'n' Spray shops later in the story (standard tracker). Advanced trackers require even more progress.

Air and sea

The fighter jet is stolen at Mile Zero Air Station: see how to steal a fighter jet. For a light plane or a helicopter early in the game, check where to find a helicopter or a plane.

Weapons in brief

In our playthrough, we saw several gun shops: Ammu-Nation, Kloss Guns, Pawn & Gun and Chuck's Tackle & Shot. An assault rifle was listed at $1,450 in one of them (seen in our playthrough). Long guns are stored in a vehicle's trunk, which ties the two topics together.

Early in the game, steal a vehicle with no tracker, then head to Moses in chapter 2 to register it. In the meantime, our beginner tips will save you from several mistakes.

Frequently asked questions

Can you buy a car in GTA 6?

The guides we read describe only one path: steal a vehicle, then register it. None of them mentions a dealership. Details are on the dedicated page.

Where can you find a fighter jet?

At Mile Zero Air Station, in Mariana County, in the southwest of the map, reachable from chapter 1 according to the guides.

Where do you store a long gun?

In a vehicle's trunk: we saw this in our playthrough.
