GTA 6 Gun Stores: Where to Buy Weapons

Updated November 24, 2026

Weapons in GTA 6 are bought at gun stores, the best known being Ammu-Nation.

Stock changes from one store to another and as you progress in the game.

The stores we saw

In our PS5 games, we came across four gun stores: Ammu-Nation, Kloss Guns, Pawn & Gun and Chuck's Tackle & Shot. Ammu-Nation is the one every guide mentions for buying a weapon, its ammo and gear, and then managing it.

Buying a weapon means owning it

A weapon you buy becomes owned: you can modify it, and get it back if you lose it, from the trunk of your personal vehicle or at a locker. That is the whole difference with a picked-up weapon. All the details are in saving your weapons.

Stock varies

Not every weapon is offered everywhere: the catalog and the prices depend on the store and on your progress. One guide sorts weapons by store type (all gun stores, standard or higher, premium), and some only show up once you are further into the story. If the weapon you want is missing, try another store later.

A sample price

In our game, an assault rifle was priced at $1,450 in a gun store. One guide gives the same price for the Duke 556, an assault rifle. Take this number as a benchmark, not as a single price: it can vary.

After the purchase

Ammo and armor you buy go to the trunk of your personal vehicle: see storing long guns. The wheel that holds your weapons is explained in the weapon wheel.

Money

Weapons are expensive: think about making money fast before you start. One guide says to pay in cash or with a BuckMe account, another notes that some purchases require a bank account. See also heists.

The section index is vehicles and weapons, and cheats are in weapon cheats.

Good habits

  • Buy the weapons you want to keep: they are the only lasting ones.
  • Compare stores: stock and prices change.
  • Keep some money for ammo.

Frequently asked questions

Where do you buy weapons in GTA 6?

At gun stores, mainly Ammu-Nation. In our games, we also saw Kloss Guns, Pawn & Gun and Chuck's Tackle & Shot.

Are weapons the same price everywhere?

No: stock and prices vary by store and by your progress.

How much does an assault rifle cost?

We saw one at $1,450 in a gun store, and a guide gives that price for the Duke 556. The price can change from one store to another.
