Dubious Provenance: the statue and Halifax

Updated November 24, 2026

Give the statue to Halifax: you get a watch and unlock the rest of the mission.

Killing or sparing Halifax at the end changes neither the reward nor the story, according to the guides.

Warning, light spoilers. This page details a chapter 3 mission (Vice City). It reveals nothing about the end of the story.

The mission in brief

Dubious Provenance is a mandatory chapter 3 mission, unlocked after "Dealer's Choice", given by Jason or Lucia. You bring a statue to an antiques dealer in Capri to find out what it is worth. The owner, Evelyn, refuses to appraise it; her colleague Benedict Halifax offers to do it in private and leaves his watch as a guarantee. The mission is one of the story missions, and the numbers the guides give it vary (46, 47 or 48), so it is better to spot it by name.

Choice 1: give the statue or refuse

Your choiceOutcome (3 guides agree)
Give the statue to HalifaxYou receive the Crowex Octane "Bobby Ambrose" watch. The second and third parts of the mission open up.
RefuseNo watch. An optional objective appears: call him back later to hand over the statue.

If you never call Halifax back, the objective disappears at the end of chapter 3 and you lose parts 2 and 3, and the money that goes with them. Two guides say this does not block the main story. The simplest option is therefore to give the statue right away. The watch is then equipped from the wardrobe, in the accessories menu (see also clothes and outfits).

Choice 2: trust Halifax at the motel

After you hand over the statue, Halifax stops answering. You enter the shop, find the statue in a desk drawer and look for Halifax at the nearby motel. A scene then asks whether you trust him. Two guides say you must trust him to reach part 3; a third says both answers lead to the same chase. When in doubt, choose trust.

Choice 3: kill or spare Halifax

Part 3 is a chase that ends when Lucia wounds Halifax in the leg. You then call Jason, and decide whether to shoot or spare him. No guide describes a difference in reward or story outcome between the two. So you can choose based on how you like to play.

What the mission pays

  • About $2,500 in jewelry and cash, in a storage container (parts 2 and 3). For other income ideas, see fast money.
  • A car, the '32 Hustler, which you can keep or resell.
  • The Bobby Ambrose watch if you gave the statue.

What to do if you slip up

Remember to save beforehand if a choice worries you. The mission is played with both characters; to switch between them, see how to switch characters. Quests tied to other characters are gathered in side quests.

Frequently asked questions

Should you give the statue to Halifax?

Yes if you want the Crowex Octane watch and the rest of the mission (parts 2 and 3, about $2,500). If you refuse, you can call him back later, but the option disappears at the end of chapter 3.

What happens if you kill Halifax?

The guides we read describe no difference in reward or story between killing and sparing. One guide says the choice only affects the criminal profile.

Can you miss the mission?

Parts 2 and 3 can be lost if you refuse the statue and never call Halifax back before the end of chapter 3. The main story continues without them.

What reward does the end of the mission give?

About $2,500 in jewelry and cash, plus a '32 Hustler car to keep or sell.
