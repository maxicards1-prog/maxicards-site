Pelican Valley Crab Lab Safe Code

Updated November 24, 2026

At the Pelican Valley research lab, the computer password is CLEAVES, the safe code is 0072 (double 0036) and the dock door code, found in the safe, is 0212.

The three lab codes

  • Computer: password CLEAVES (letters, not digits).
  • Safe: 0072.
  • Dock building door: 0212, which the safe reveals to you.

Three guides agree on these values. They also agree on the logic: nothing is random, everything can be worked out from the clues on site. The lab is in the swampy area of Pelican Valley, near Gullet Marsh, in Mariana County, northwest of Key Lento. One guide says you get there by boat.

The puzzle, step by step

  1. Restore the power. The switch is outside, on the side of the house according to one guide, behind the building near a large white tank according to another.
  2. Find the password. In the bedroom, look at the floor (the hole in the floorboards): several words are carved there and crossed out. The only one that is not is CLEAVES.
  3. Turn on the computer in the main room, near the safe, and enter CLEAVES. It says the safe code is “double success.”
  4. Spot the successful experiment. The lab's notes and papers mark one experiment as the most successful: number 0036. Double it: 0072.
  5. Open the safe with 0072. It contains money and a note that gives 0212.
  6. Go to the dock. Enter 0212 on the keypad of the building at the end of the pier.
If nothing works: first check that the power is back on. One guide also notes that values may differ from one save to another; in that case, trust the numbers your game shows.

Why this page and not the general list?

Because this is a puzzle safe, one of the few where you can understand the reasoning: keeping it in mind saves you from relying on a list if the digits change in your game. Step by step

  1. Restore the power: Flip the switch outside the lab.
  2. Read the password: In the bedroom, spot the word CLEAVES, the only one not crossed out, carved into the floor.
  3. Turn on the computer: Enter CLEAVES: it says the safe code is “double success.”
  4. Double experiment 0036: The notes mark experiment 0036 as the most successful: 0036 x 2 = 0072.
  5. Open the safe, then the door: Enter 0072 in the safe, read the note: 0212 opens the dock building.

Frequently asked questions

What is the crab lab safe code in GTA 6?

0072. The lab computer, unlocked with CLEAVES, says “double success”; the most successful experiment being 0036, its double gives 0072.

What is the computer password?

CLEAVES. It is carved into the bedroom floor, visible through the hole in the floorboards: it is the only word that is not crossed out.

What is the 0212 code for?

It is the keypad code for the building at the end of the pier. It is written on a note in the safe.
