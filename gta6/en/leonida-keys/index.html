The Leonida Keys in GTA 6: Locations and Secrets

Updated November 24, 2026

The Leonida Keys are the archipelago in southern Leonida, where the game starts, around Key Lento.

You can dive, fish and find several secrets: talking parrots, a rescue dog on a jet ski and Michael's yacht.

Where the Keys are and where you start

The Leonida Keys are a chain of islands linked by long bridges in the south of the map, inspired by the Florida Keys. Two guides say the game starts at Key Lento, the main area. To compare with the other regions, see the map and locations page.

Getting around: boat, seaplane, diving

On an archipelago, a car is not enough. We took off in a seaplane from a Keys dock, and crossed long bridges in a car at sunset. For water vehicles, check the boats; for planes, see helicopter and plane.

Diving for the Kenton treasures

The Collections menu in our playthroughs contains a “Kenton Treasures” entry. One guide published since release ties these dives to the Keys, and two guides give 33 underwater finds. The details are on the diving page.

Fishing

Fishing is one of the activities mentioned for the Keys. We have a fishing boat (a Lure Predator) in our playthroughs, and a “fishing” setting in the options. See the fishing page.

The archipelago's secrets

  • The liquor store parrots: during a fight, they talk. One of them says “generator,” which points to money hidden near a generator (two guides).
  • The rescue dog on a jet ski: if you go too far from the coast, a dog on a jet ski may come to rescue you and take you to a small island where dogs gather. The encounter is not guaranteed (three guides).
  • Michael's yacht: moored in front of Jason's house, its GPS shows the Los Santos map from GTA 5 (two guides).

The list of easter eggs is on the easter eggs page.

Good to know before you go

  • The Keys are the ideal place to try out the plane: see also flying a fighter jet, which we did at an air base.
  • For money early in the game, see fast money.
