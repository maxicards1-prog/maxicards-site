GTA 6 Chapter 1: Missions and Tips

Updated November 24, 2026

Chapter 1, "Just Like Paradise", has 22 main missions, from the Prologue to Discharge Time.

Before Tourist Trap, put your money in the bank and make a manual save: the game does not let you replay a mission.

The chapter 1 missions in order

The guides count differently: some say 21 missions (without the prologue), others 22, and others still add side tasks up to 26 or 32 journal entries. Here we follow the list of 22 missions, the same as on our missions page. Some early missions can be done in any order (Gear Up, Lab Fees, Just a Scratch, Unwanted Attention).

To do before Coming Home

Several guides recommend doing a set of small tasks before Lucia gets out of prison: buying groceries, buying flowers, picking up her clothes, getting a haircut and buying a necklace. Each one improves the couple's starting relationship (see the characters). The necklace can be used later, in chapter 2.

Do not miss: the missable items

  • Shirley's two favors (Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter): limited to chapter 1, do them before Tourist Trap.
  • The meditation with Lucia: the morning after Down to Earth, accept her invitation on the balcony. It only exists in chapter 1 and may count toward a trophy (see the trophies).
  • Clock Out: Lucia calls you, and you need to pick her up in a vehicle before 4 PM (16:00).

On the other hand, these can be done later: Kenton's treasure dives and, according to the guides, Kirk's hunts (see the side quests).

Money: why you should bank everything

Jason loses his cash near the end of the chapter, and the game does not let you go back. So deposit your bills in BuckMe before Tourist Trap: several guides recommend at least $5,000 in savings, and in our playthrough we saw the $5,000 savings goal in the app. To earn money at this stage, see our methods by chapter.

Do not waste money by "farming" at the end of the chapter: it is lost anyway. It is better to spend it or deposit it in the bank beforehand.

Frequently asked questions

How many missions are in chapter 1 of GTA 6?

22 main missions, from the Prologue to Discharge Time. Some guides count 21 without the prologue, others more by adding side tasks.

Can you replay a chapter 1 mission?

No, the guides say the game does not let you replay completed missions. Make a manual save before Tourist Trap.

Do you lose your money at the end of chapter 1?

Yes: several guides say Jason loses his cash at the end of the chapter. Deposit it in the BuckMe app before Tourist Trap.

Which quests are missable in chapter 1?

Shirley's two favors, the meditation with Lucia after Down to Earth, and Clock Out. Kenton's treasure dives can still be done later.
