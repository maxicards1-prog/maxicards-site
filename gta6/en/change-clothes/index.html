How to Change Outfits in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

To change outfits in GTA 6, open the wardrobe in a safehouse or use a store's fitting room; you can also keep outfits in your vehicle's trunk.

Changing your look helps you lose the police.

Where to change outfits

Three places come up in every guide. The wardrobe in your safehouse holds the clothes you bought or got, for Jason as well as for Lucia. Most stores have a fitting room where you put on clothes you already own. Finally, your vehicle's trunk can hold outfits: you change without going back to the safehouse.

Clothing stores

Leonida has 67 clothing stores spread across five categories: casual and streetwear, sportswear, formal wear, souvenirs and swimwear, and thrift stores. Shoes are sold in separate stores. Stores show up on the map with a T-shirt icon: open the map, choose the Style category, then the type of store. In our games, we visited the Thrillbilly Mud Club store, during the mission described on the page races and motocross.

The guides don't agree on access to the stores reserved for the Ultimate edition. So we don't give a list: see our page Ultimate edition and bonuses instead.

Changing your look for the police

Your outfit matters during a chase: one guide says it affects how aggressive the police are, and that changing clothes is the fastest way to lower your wanted level. Another recommends keeping outfits in the car to escape. See also how to lose the police and the wanted level.

Finding the right store

Most stores are concentrated in Vice-Dale County, especially around Ocean Beach, but you can also find them in Capri, Waning Sands and Port Gellhorn. The stores range from thrift shops to formal wear: the selection covers casual style as well as smart clothes. Gun stores and vehicle modification garages also sell a few clothes according to one guide, but dedicated stores remain the best choice for a real change of look. Get into the habit of keeping a spare outfit in the trunk before a heist: it's useful for losing the police afterward.

Going further

Customizing your look doesn't stop at clothes: hairstyles and tattoos are explained on tattoos and hair, and vehicle customization on customizing your car. All the activities are listed in the tour of activities.

We don't publish clothing prices: no guide gives them reliably.

Frequently asked questions

Where can you change clothes in GTA 6?

At the wardrobe in a safehouse, in the fitting room of most stores, or in your vehicle's trunk if you have stored outfits there.

Does changing outfits help against the police?

Yes: your outfit affects the chase, and changing clothes is described as the fastest way to lower your wanted level.

How many clothing stores are there?

67 stores in five categories, according to two guides. Shoes are sold in separate stores.
