The GTA 6 UFO: Where and When to See It

Updated November 24, 2026

The UFO is near Port Gellhorn, in Kelly County: you need to be there at night, after 2 a.m. in-game, and spot a parked car with its emergency lights on.

This scene is random: if the car is missing, leave and come back.

Where the encounter happens

The encounter takes place on a country road near the Starlet Motel safehouse, in Port Gellhorn (Kelly County). The guides do not place it at exactly the same spot: one says east of town, another northwest of the motel. The easiest way is to patrol around the motel at night, preferably alone. The area is covered on our Port Gellhorn page.

Conditions for it to appear

  • The time: after 2 a.m., game time. The three guides we read agree on this.
  • Chance: the car is not there every time. If you do not see it, move away and come back a little later.
  • Progress: the guides disagree. One asks you to have unlocked chapter 2, another says it is enough to have finished chapter 1 and be free to roam, and the third says nothing. If nothing shows up, progress a bit in the story before trying again.

The steps of the encounter

Here is what the guides describe, in order. We do not spoil what comes next.

  1. Spot the car stopped at the side of the road: emergency lights on, doors open, trunk open, clothes scattered around.
  2. Follow the clothes on foot, in the direction of the headlights, to a beam of light above the trees.
  3. Watch the scene, then talk to the woman left on the ground once the UFO has gone.
  4. Go through the whole conversation and agree to help her: she asks you to bring back her car, left on the road.
  5. Head back to the car. This is where the UFO takes over again, and where the trophy unlocks.
Tip: finish the whole dialogue with the woman before heading back to the road. Two guides insist on this.

The “The Truth Is Out There” trophy

This trophy (an achievement on Xbox) is tied to this scene. None of the guides we read says the encounter can be missed for good: if nothing happens, check the time first and try again. The trophy is one of the 55 trophies in the game.

What comes next? The research site

Two guides mention a follow-up for Jason, at an abandoned research site in Pelican Valley, near the swamps. The code for a door circulates in two different versions: we are not publishing it until it is confirmed. The summary is on our easter eggs page, and the neighboring area is on Grassrivers.

Another secret at night

For another secret that only triggers at night, see the Skunk Ape hunt and Kirk's missions, which also require waiting for nightfall.

Step by step

  1. Go to Port Gellhorn at night: Head to the Starlet Motel after 2 a.m. game time, preferably alone.
  2. Spot the car: Look for a stopped car with its emergency lights on and doors open. Not there? Leave and come back.
  3. Follow the clothes: Walk on foot toward the headlights, up to a beam of light.
  4. Talk to the woman: Go through the whole dialogue and agree to help her get her car back.
  5. Bring back the car: Return to the road: the scene continues and the trophy unlocks.

Where do you find the UFO in GTA 6?

The car does not appear. What should I do?

Which trophy do you get?

The “The Truth Is Out There” trophy, unlocked at the end of the encounter, when you bring back the woman's car.

Do you need chapter 2?
