Getting your car out of impound in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

To get an impounded car back in GTA 6, pay for delivery with the Scooter Bros app (chapter 2 onward) or go and take it back yourself, for free but with the police on your tail.

Why your car gets impounded

Your personal vehicle can be seized if you abandon it inside a police search zone after a crime. Everything in the trunk goes with it. Driving toward that zone restarts the search: do not rush there. If you lose money and your car at once, see also what you lose when you die.

Option 1: Scooter Bros (legal, paid)

The app unlocks in chapter 2, with the Moses mission. Pick the seized car in your vehicle list, confirm, pay the fee and it is delivered to your position. The guides do not give the same amount: $550 to $700 according to one, no amount according to the others. So expect more than the price of a simple delivery, and read the price shown before paying.

Option 2: the premium option

The premium subscription costs $12,500 one time. It gives three free deliveries per game day, and these daily credits can also be used to get a car out of the impound lot. Worth it if you often lose your car, useless at the start.

Option 3: take it back yourself (free, risky)

Go to the impound lot shown on the map, get past the fence, then drive out with the car. The guides agree on the risk: the police are alerted. Two of them mention a wanted level of one or two stars. Be ready to lose them: a Pay 'n' Spray and an outfit change, explained in losing the police.

  • Take the trunk contents at the same time: everything stays inside.

Finding the impound lot

An icon on the map shows the impound lot that holds your car: open the map, spot the icon and set a route. Several impound lots exist in the cities (Port Gellhorn and Hamlet are mentioned by the guides). To go there, pick a quiet moment, with no wanted level.

The "Impounded Vehicle" mission

Do not mix them up: in chapter 2, a side mission called Impounded Vehicle has you pick up a car from the Port Gellhorn impound lot. It is a small story objective, not the general service. Our chapter 2 page places it.

Unregistered car

Delivery only works for registered vehicles. To keep a stolen car, register it first.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to get a car out of the impound lot?

Through Scooter Bros, a higher price than a simple delivery ($550 to $700 according to one guide, no figure in others). Free if you take it back yourself.

Can you get your car back without paying?

Yes: by going to take it back yourself at the impound lot, at the risk of alerting the police.

When do you unlock Scooter Bros?

In chapter 2, after the Moses mission (Take A Vehicle to Moses).

What happens to the trunk contents?

The trunk contents are seized with the car and you get them back with it.
