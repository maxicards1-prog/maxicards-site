Selling Your Loot in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

In GTA 6, a pawn shop is mostly a robbery target: loot is sold to a fence, not to the shop.

Each fence pays a different price for drugs, jewelry, marked bills or cars.

Pawn shops: to rob, not to use

Guides list pawn shops as "Smash 'n' Grab" targets. You break the display cases to take the jewelry and search the back room for the safe. The optional mission "Hit Avery Pawn", in Port Gellhorn, is one example: the loot is estimated between $1,000 and $10,000. Breaking a display case or a door gives two wanted stars, see losing the police. The loot is then sold to a fence.

Fences and their prices

A fence turns loot into dollars. Prices vary and are rated in the guides from "very low" to "high".

With the first fences, the money sometimes arrives in Jason's mailbox the day after delivery.

Marked bills

Marked bank bills are only accepted with a dye pack jammer ("Dye Pack Jammer"), obtained in chapter 3. Without it, do not try to sell them. See robberies to find out where to get them.

Selling well

  • Fences are marked by green icons on the map, with a price estimate.
  • Spread the loot across several fences to aim for the best price per type of loot.
  • Lose the police before delivering and keep the bag in your vehicle's trunk (stolen cars).
  • After delivery, deposit the money (BuckMe bank).
A rare detail: two guides claim that a rare collectible card, the Leroy Rook, sells for about $6,000 at a pawn shop. See collectible cards.

For an overview of income, read making money.

Frequently asked questions

Can you sell loot to a pawn shop in GTA 6?

No, guides present them as robbery targets. Drugs, jewelry and marked bills are sold to fences.

Which fence pays the best?

Tyrone pays the best for drugs, Yasmani for jewelry and marked bills, both in chapter 3 in Vice City.

Why are marked bills refused?

You need the dye pack jammer, obtained in chapter 3. Without it, fences refuse them.
