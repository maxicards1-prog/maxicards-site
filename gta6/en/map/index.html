GTA 6 Map: Regions and Places to See

Updated November 24, 2026

The six regions of Leonida in gameplay images, the places not to miss and the secrets hidden in each one.

The State of Leonida, inspired by Florida, is split into large regions, each with its own atmosphere. Here is each region with our gameplay images, the places to see and the secrets hidden there.

The six regions of Leonida

  • Vice City, the big city
  • The Leonida Keys, the southern archipelago
  • Grassrivers, the swamps
  • Port Gellhorn, the port town
  • Ambrosia, the inland
  • Mount Kalaga National Park, the wilderness

Vice City

The game's main city, with its towers, beaches and islands linked by bridges. Places to see: Vice Beach, Washington Island and Vespucci Island. The best way to discover it: a biplane flight over the beaches, then the highway at sunset.

Secrets here: Dexter's apartment, the Fast & Furious Mitsubishi Eclipse, the radio tower Easter egg (south of the city).

The Leonida Keys

A chain of islands in the south, linked by long bridges over turquoise water. Ideal by seaplane, by boat, or diving for the underwater discoveries.

Secrets here: the liquor store parrots, Michael's yacht (GTA 5), the dog on a jet ski.

Grassrivers

The swamps, Everglades style: airboats, alligators, wooden piers and small dirt roads. Hamlet and its surroundings sit at the edge of the swamps.

Secrets here: the Skunk Ape, Kirk's quest, the Pelican Valley research base.

Port Gellhorn

A port town with its motels and country roads. The Starlet Motel serves as your hideout there.

Secrets here: the UFO, Arthur Morgan's hat, the arcade cabinets at the Bargain World.

Ambrosia

The inland: small-town streets and biker gangs, as in the official image. Our screenshots of this region are coming soon.

Mount Kalaga National Park

The big nature park: forests and dirt tracks for motorcycles and 4x4s. Several mysteries are reported there but not yet cross-checked: see the secrets page.

Frequently asked questions

What are the regions of the GTA 6 map?

Leonida has six main regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.

How do you see the whole map quickly?

Take a plane or a helicopter: a biplane flight over Vice City and then along the coast shows a large part of the map in a few minutes.
