Diving in GTA 6: Shipwrecks, Treasure and Kenton

Updated November 24, 2026

Diving unlocks in chapter 1 with Kenton Rees and his Island Dreams mission, east of Key Lento.

Equip the diving suit at the surface, follow the metal detector underwater, then sell the relics to Kenton.

Unlocking diving

The diving job goes through Kenton Rees, a treasure hunter whose Island Dreams shop is on the east side of Key Lento. You need to reach the point in chapter 1 where his marker appears, then do the Island Dreams mission. No particular level or item is required. This diving is optional for the story, but it counts toward 100% according to GTABase.

The gear

Equip the diving suit at the surface before diving. Underwater, take out the metal detector, which shows the direction of the nearest relic. Watch the oxygen bar: it is a key mechanic of the activity. If you are lost, look at the objective marker.

In our games, a dive club is set up on a pier, and we dived on the seabed off Key Lento: the area is full of shipwrecks.

The shipwrecks around Key Lento

Six shipwrecks are spread around Key Lento, including the one from the Island Dreams mission (Nuestra Señora del Ocio). Two of them are well documented:

ShipwreckNumber of relics
Nuestra Señora de la Ética Laxa5
Nuestra Señora de la Noche7

Each site has its own counter, visible on the dive site map. The relics are scattered around the wreck, not all inside it; don't limit yourself to the hull. When the detector points away from the wreck, trust it.

Selling what you find

Relics count as cargo until you bring them to Kenton, who buys them. There's no need to empty a wreck all at once: you can keep collecting, sell later and come back. We don't publish prices: the guides don't give the same amounts. For other ways to earn money, see making money and fast money. The full map is on our Leonida Keys page, and the start of the adventure is in chapter 1.

Step by step

  1. Go to Kenton's : In chapter 1, head to the Island Dreams shop, east of Key Lento, when its marker appears.
  2. Gear up : Equip the diving suit at the surface before diving.
  3. Search : Underwater, take out the metal detector and follow it to the nearest relic.
  4. Sell : Bring the relics to Kenton, who buys them.

Frequently asked questions

How do you unlock diving in GTA 6?

In chapter 1, with Kenton Rees's Island Dreams mission; his shop is east of Key Lento.

What do you need to dive?

The diving suit, equipped at the surface, and the metal detector to find relics underwater. Also watch the oxygen bar.

Where do you sell shipwreck treasure?

At Kenton's, who buys the relics. They count as cargo until you sell them.

How many shipwrecks are there?

Six around Key Lento, according to one guide. Ética Laxa holds 5 relics and Noche holds 7.
