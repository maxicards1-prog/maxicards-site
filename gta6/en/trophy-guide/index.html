GTA 6 Trophies and Achievements

Updated November 24, 2026

All 55 trophies, how to earn them, and tips for the platinum.

How many trophies are in GTA 6?

55 trophies on PS5: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze, 28 of them hidden. On Xbox, there are 55 achievements worth 1,000 points. All of them can be earned solo, and none is tied to online play. The names below are translated from the French names.

Platinum: expect around 200 hours according to early estimates (about fifty hours for the story alone). Cheat codes block trophies during the session: keep a playthrough without cheats.

Tips for the platinum

  • Aim for gold at the shooting ranges from the start: it helps for "The Gunslinger" and to unlock the ammo codes.
  • Make regular manual saves in different slots: there is no mission replay.
  • Be careful with "Staying Positive": one guide says a meditation offered by Lucia in chapter 1 does not come back later. Accept it to be safe.
  • The 100% objectives remain doable after the end of the story.
