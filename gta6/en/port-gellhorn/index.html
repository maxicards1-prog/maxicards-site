Port Gellhorn in GTA 6: Motel, UFO and Secrets

Updated November 24, 2026

Port Gellhorn is the port town of Kelly County, with the Starlet Motel as a safehouse.

It has the UFO secret, Arthur Morgan's hat and a garage to buy; you can also race off-road there.

Where Port Gellhorn is

Port Gellhorn is in Kelly County, according to SVG, Beebom and Gamesfuze. It is a seaside town. The game places an important safehouse there, the Starlet Motel. The whole map is described on the map and locations page.

The Starlet Motel

The motel serves as a safehouse. It is also a landmark for the UFO encounter, which takes place at night on the nearby roads. Everything is explained on the UFO page.

The town's secrets

  • The UFO: on a country road near the motel, at night after 2 a.m. It earns you the “The Truth Is Out There” trophy.
  • Arthur Morgan's hat: a nod to Red Dead Redemption 2, at the yard sale south of town, near a couch. It can be bought in chapter 2.

Buying a garage

In our playthroughs, the phone showed a listing for a garage in Port Gellhorn: the Mitchel Auto Center, $5,000, one slot. The details are on the buying a garage page.

Races and off-road

In Port Gellhorn we saw a Thrillbilly track where monster trucks race, and a motocross track. To take part in a race, you need to talk to the person in charge on site. See races and motocross.

Do not mix them up

Do not confuse Port Gellhorn with “VC Port,” the port of Vice City: they are two different places. For the city, see Vice City.

Tips

  • Go at night to try for the UFO.
  • For money to spend on the garage, check fast money.
