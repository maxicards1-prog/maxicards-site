How to open a convertible roof in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

To open a convertible roof in GTA 6, stay in the driver’s seat, hold left on the directional pad, push the right stick up, then confirm the roof option with Cross (PS5) or A (Xbox).

The roof moves when you close the menu.

How to do it on PS5 and Xbox

There is no dedicated button. The roof is controlled from a menu, and the two guides we cross-checked describe the same route. You have to be driving a convertible, that is, a car whose roof opens and closes.

  1. Get in the car, on the driver’s side.
  2. Hold left on the directional pad: the radio menu opens.
  3. Push the right stick up to switch to the vehicle functions menu.
  4. Choose the roof option (“Convertible Roof” in English), then confirm with Cross on PS5 or A on Xbox.
  5. Close the menu: the roof folds or unfolds at that moment.

One guide says the roof option is in first position, the other that you get to it with L1 and R1 on PS5 (LB and RB on Xbox). If you do not see it right away, scroll through the menu list with these triggers.

To close the roof, do the same thing again

To put the roof back up, repeat the same steps. One guide says this alternates between opening and closing; the other only describes opening, without saying whether the same option is used to close it. If nothing happens, try while stopped.

Which cars?

Only cars with a movable roof offer this option. Fixed-roof models, including some with a sunroof, do not have it. The guides give no official list of convertibles: we would rather name no model than invent one. To find a car you like, see our page on vehicles, or see how to buy a car and customize it.

Do you have to be stopped?

The sources disagree, or do not say. One guide says nothing about it and only notes that the game keeps running slowly while you browse the menu. The safest thing is to stop first: you avoid ending up in the scenery while you navigate the options, especially with the police on your tail (see how to lose them).

Good to know: the exact button may be different if you changed the button layout in the settings. Check in the controls menu. All the game’s buttons are gathered on our PS5 and Xbox controls page.

And on PC?

GTA 6 has not been released on PC, see our update on the PC version. The sources we read only describe PS5 and Xbox controllers anyway.

Step by step

  1. Get in the convertible: Sit on the driver’s side of a car with a movable roof.
  2. Open the radio menu: Hold left on the directional pad.
  3. Switch to the vehicle menu: Push the right stick up.
  4. Choose the roof: Select the roof option, then confirm with Cross (PS5) or A (Xbox).
  5. Close the menu: Exit the menu: the roof folds or unfolds.

Frequently asked questions

How do you put the roof down on a car in GTA 6?

Hold left on the directional pad, push the right stick up, choose the roof option and confirm with Cross (PS5) or A (Xbox), then close the menu.

Which button opens the roof on Xbox?

There is no single button: use the vehicle menu (left on the directional pad, right stick up) and confirm with A.

Can every car have its roof opened?

No, only those with a movable roof. Fixed-roof cars do not have the option.

Can you open the roof while driving?

The sources do not settle it. The safest thing is to stop before opening the menu.

Can you open a convertible roof on PC?

GTA 6 does not exist on PC to date, and the guides only describe PS5 and Xbox controllers.
