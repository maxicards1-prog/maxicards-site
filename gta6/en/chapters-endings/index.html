GTA 6: how many chapters and endings?

Updated November 24, 2026

GTA 6 is split into 5 chapters.

The number of endings is not official: depending on the guide it ranges from 4 to 14, because each one counts something different. This page reveals nothing about the content of the endings.

Chapters: 5

Jason and Lucia’s story unfolds over five chapters, and the end of the game is played in chapter 5. Insider Gaming says Rockstar had mentioned this split before release, and Beebom also speaks of “all five chapters”. For details on what you do in them, see chapter 1 and chapter 2, as well as the full missions list.

How many missions in these chapters?

The numbers vary slightly depending on the count: our list has 91, some guides announce 93. The gap comes from how certain mandatory activities are counted. As for how long that takes, it is another question, covered in GTA 6’s length.

Endings: why the numbers do not match

Rockstar has not published any official number of endings. Guides published since release therefore give very different answers:

SourceNumber givenNote
Detonated (Nov. 22)5The text also hesitates between “more than 5” and 4; information attributed to journalists
GTABase (Nov. 24)8Variants of the same ending are counted separately elsewhere
Beebom (Nov. 23)14“Spotted so far”, some seen only on social media

This gap has an explanation: some only count the main endings, others add every variant. Our honest answer is therefore: several endings exist, the exact total is not confirmed. If a site gives you a single figure with certainty, be wary.

What decides the ending?

Without giving anything away: according to GTABase, the ending depends on three things built up during the adventure, the relationship between the two characters, the money set aside in the joint account and the criminal profile. A late choice then splits the story into two paths. What you do before chapter 5 therefore matters, including how you manage money (see how to make money).

No spoilers. We deliberately describe no ending. Play first, compare afterwards: the site’s characters and guides help you without revealing anything.

Frequently asked questions

How many chapters are there in GTA 6?

Five chapters, with the end of the story played in chapter 5.

How many endings does GTA 6 have?

Rockstar has not given a figure. Guides claim from 4 to 14 endings depending on whether they count variants; the total is not confirmed.

Can you miss the best ending?

Guides say the ending depends on your couple’s relationship, the money saved and your criminal profile, so yes, your choices matter.

Is there a bonus chapter after chapter 5?

No reliable source says so for now; we do not claim it.
