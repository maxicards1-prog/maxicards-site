How Many Hours to Beat GTA 6?

Updated November 24, 2026

Reviewers finished the GTA 6 story in 42 to 115 hours, most around 60 to 90 hours with side content. 100% is estimated at around 200 hours, but that figure is not verified.

What the reviewers say

The times published at review time (November 18) vary a lot depending on how people play. Here are the figures cited by name:

Reviewer (source)Time
Pure Xbox (GameRiv)about 42 hours
Jeff Gerstmann (Kotaku)45 to 50 hours
Eurogamer (GameRiv)about 65 hours
IGN, one reviewer (GameRiv)about 70 hours
Kotaku, Zack Zwiezen (Kotaku)about 90 hours with a lot of side content
IGN, another reviewer (GameRiv)about 115 hours

One guide averages four reviewers: 73 hours. Kotaku also notes that a faster player could finish in about 60 hours, and that its reviewer had spent 150 hours on the game without seeing everything.

Story only, with side content, 100%

  • Main story: about 50 to 65 hours according to the reported estimates. Our missions page lists 91 missions spread over 5 chapters.
  • With side activities: about 80 to 120 hours. Part of these figures is an author's estimate, not a measurement.
  • 100%: about 200 hours according to unspecified “reports”. This assumes finishing missions with gold, scanning all 170 animal species, finding the collectibles and doing every challenge. We found no reliable measurement: see our list of trophies.

And according to Rockstar?

A Rockstar executive had put forward about 80 hours before release for his own playthrough, doing optional objectives that affect the story. This is not an independent review time.

How to gain or lose time

The length depends mostly on what you do outside missions: fishing, secrets and easter eggs, collectibles, exploring the map.

For comparison, one guide cites about 30 to 32 hours for the GTA 5 story, and 50 to 60 hours for Red Dead Redemption 2.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to finish the GTA 6 story?

Between about 42 and 70 hours according to the reviewers cited, with a common estimate of 50 to 65 hours.

How many hours for 100% completion?

About 200 hours according to unspecified reports. No reliable measurement is confirmed.

Is GTA 6 longer than GTA 5?

Yes, according to estimates: about 30 to 32 hours for the GTA 5 story, versus 50 to 65 for GTA 6.
