Hunting in GTA 6: Unlock and Sell

Updated November 24, 2026

Hunting unlocks in chapter 2, with Jason only, thanks to Bret Vogel, who lives near Mount Kalaga National Park.

You study the animal, aim for the head or the heart, then sell the carcass to Bret.

Unlocking hunting

Hunting arrives in chapter 2. The contact is called Bret Vogel, a former friend of Jason who runs a meat and hunting gear business from a cabin near Mount Kalaga. The first outing is playable with Jason only: if you are playing Lucia, you first have to switch characters. According to one guide, Bret lends you a scoped rifle for this first hunt and Jason keeps it afterward.

Study before you shoot

Before shooting, study the animal with the binoculars or the rifle scope, holding the button until the circle fills up. The card that appears shows the animal, the recommended weapon and the estimated value of the carcass. This value is a range, not a guaranteed price: it depends on the condition of the carcass.

A clean shot

Use the weapon recommended by the card: a lighter weapon for small animals, a more powerful one for big game. Aim at a highlighted weak point, the head or the heart. Several shots to the body damage the carcass and lower its value. If you are looking for other weapons, see our page on the best weapons.

In our games, long guns are stored in a vehicle's trunk. To carry a carcass, preferably take a vehicle with room: see also storing long guns.

Carrying and selling

Carry the carcass to your vehicle and store it, then drive to Bret's cabin (Killtelligence Provisions) and drop it in the processing area. Damaged carcasses pay less; clean shots pay the most. The game tracks the number of animals killed, carcasses sold and money earned.

Challenges and what comes next

Bret then sends requests for specific animals, and the hunting objectives name the animal to bring back: follow the requested target. Hunting eight different animals counts toward a hunter challenge, and toward a trophy according to one guide. For other sources of income, see our fast money page.

We don't give exact selling prices: the guides don't publish any that agree.

Step by step

  1. Meet Bret : In chapter 2, play as Jason and answer Bret Vogel, near Mount Kalaga.
  2. Study the animal : Binoculars or scope: hold the study button to see the recommended weapon and the value.
  3. Shoot cleanly : Aim for the head or the heart with the recommended weapon.
  4. Carry : Carry the carcass to a vehicle and store it.
  5. Sell : Drop the carcass off at Bret's, in the processing area.

Frequently asked questions

When can you hunt in GTA 6?

From chapter 2, with Jason, after meeting Bret Vogel near Mount Kalaga.

Where do you sell carcasses?

At Bret's, at his Killtelligence Provisions cabin: you drop the carcass in the processing area.

Why is my carcass worth so little?

Several shots to the body damage the carcass. A shot to the head or the heart with the weapon recommended by the card keeps the highest value.

Can you hunt as Lucia?

The first outing with Bret is reserved for Jason. Switch characters before you start it.
