The GTA 6 Weapon Wheel Explained

Updated November 24, 2026

The GTA 6 weapon wheel opens when you hold L1 on PS5 or LB on Xbox.

It holds at most two handguns and two long guns: the rest stays in the trunk or the locker.

Opening the wheel

Hold L1 (PS5) or LB (Xbox) to show the wheel. Weapons and items are separate: items (outfits, accessories, consumables) have their own wheel, and a shoulder button lets you switch from one to the other. Our controls for each console are on the controls page.

Weapon slots

TypeSlots
Handguns (pistols, revolvers, submachine guns)2
Long guns (rifles, shotguns)2: one on your back, one in your hands

This limit of two plus two is given by several guides. Handguns are carried in one hand and are not visible.

Changing the weapons you carry

The wheel only holds what you are carrying. To change your selection, you have to go to the trunk of your personal vehicle or to a gun store locker. We cover the trunk in storing long guns, and the locker in saving your weapons.

Aiming settings

In our settings, we saw aim assist and partial aim options. They have nothing to do with the wheel, but they change combat a lot: see the recommended settings.

Each character has their own gear: Jason and Lucia each carry their own two handguns and their own long guns.

During a fight

You can still switch weapons from the wheel in the middle of the action, but only among the weapons you already carry. If you forgot the rifle you need, you will have to go back to your vehicle. So, before a mission, plan two handguns for stealth or close range, and two long guns for longer distances, leaving the rest in the trunk. The same rules apply to Jason and to Lucia.

Recap

  • Wheel: L1 (PS5) or LB (Xbox), held down.
  • Limit: 2 handguns and 2 long guns.
  • Storage: personal vehicle trunk and Ammu-Nation locker.

Frequently asked questions

How do you open the weapon wheel in GTA 6?

Hold L1 on PS5 or LB on Xbox.

How many weapons can you equip?

Two handguns and two long guns, according to the guides we read.

Where are items like outfits?

On a separate item wheel, distinct from the weapon wheel, reached with a shoulder button.
