Hard GTA 6 missions: the solutions

Updated November 24, 2026

In Swamped, follow Brian to the helicopter and never shoot Lori.

In Down to Earth, set the seaplane down gently on the water: a rough landing fails the mission.

Swamped, chapter 1

This chapter 1 mission is played with Jason and Brian. You go to the Kingfishers camp to try to make peace, but the camp turns into an ambush. Lori is kidnapped, and you have to rescue her by helicopter then by boat.

  • The escape: follow Brian. Staying in the middle of the camp is a mistake, because you take more and more fire there. There is no need to kill everyone.
  • The helicopter: watch the kidnappers but also Lori: hitting her fails the mission.
  • The boat chase: if the kidnapper flees in an airboat, you can jump onto his boat from another airboat, or shoot the driver.

Down to Earth, chapter 1

Brian gives you a series of seaplane deliveries with Lucia. You first clean his boat with a pressure washer (it is handled like a weapon, with R2 or R1), then you fly to Bobcat Bay and deal with the smugglers on board the ship.

  • The water landing: approach gently, on a shallow path, and keep some margin. Hitting the water too hard blows up the plane and fails the mission.
  • The smugglers: you can take them out without killing or kill them, both work.
  • The ending: the guides contradict each other on where to hide the drugs (washing machine or shed). In the final scene, Lucia's probation officer arrives.

The pilot missions

If you are looking for a pilot mission, it is probably one of Pilot Pete's missions in chapter 4: "The Last Mile: Passing the Torch" (picking up Pete's plane), "Backseat Pilot" and its deliveries. See side quests and switching characters.

General tips

  • Save before chase missions: saving the game.
  • Mission numbers vary from one guide to another (Swamped is the 17th or the 20th). Spot them by name in the mission list.
  • For another mission with choices, read Dubious Provenance.

Frequently asked questions

Why do you fail the Swamped mission?

The two causes the guides cite are letting Lori get away with the Kingfishers or shooting her by mistake.

How do you land successfully in Down to Earth?

Descend gently, on a shallow path with some margin. Hitting the water too violently blows up the seaplane.

Do you have to kill the smugglers in Down to Earth?

No: you can take them out without killing or kill them, both methods let you finish the mission.
