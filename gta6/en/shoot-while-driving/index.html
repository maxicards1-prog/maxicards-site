How to shoot while driving in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

To shoot while driving in GTA 6, equip a weapon before getting in, then press R1 (PS5) or RB (Xbox).

The brake stays on L2/LT: it is not the aim button.

The fire button

The four guides we read agree: you shoot from the driver's seat with R1 on PS5 and RB on Xbox, without stopping the car. You need to keep one hand on the steering: shooting while driving in a straight line is much easier than in a turn.

A weapon equipped before getting in

Two guides say you must have a firearm in hand in your selection before entering the vehicle. Otherwise nothing fires. See the weapon wheel and how to stow shoulder weapons so you do not leave empty-handed.

Aiming: the guides disagree

  • Two guides say L2 (PS5) or LT (Xbox) is the brake and reverse in a vehicle. Pressing it out of habit makes you slow down instead of aiming. You then steer your shot with the right stick, that is, the camera.
  • Two other guides instead describe holding L1 (PS5) or LB (Xbox) to aim, with the right stick, then R1/RB to shoot.

We do not take sides: try both in a quiet spot, and check the controls screen in the settings. Our PS5 and Xbox buttons page lists the controls all guides agree on.

Shooting from the passenger seat

Jason and Lucia can share the work: one drives, the other shoots from the passenger seat. Three guides say so, and some story scenes use it during a chase. Switching characters is done with the right arrow, see switching characters.

Ducking behind the wheel

Two guides mention the possibility of ducking in the vehicle to hide or reduce damage, but they do not give the same button (Circle/B held for one, Cross/A for the other). Check the controls screen.

A few tips

  • Make small steering movements: a big turn of the wheel swings the camera past your target.
  • Avoid shooting in turns.
  • If the police are chasing you, see how to lose them.
Lowering a convertible roof: none of the controls guides we read give the button to lower or raise the roof. So we do not state it; check the game's controls screen.

Frequently asked questions

Which button shoots from a car in GTA 6?

R1 on PS5, RB on Xbox, from the driver's seat.

Why can't I aim with L2 in a car?

Because L2 (LT on Xbox) is the brake in a vehicle: the crosshair moves with the camera.

Can you shoot as a passenger?

Yes: Jason or Lucia can drive while the other shoots from the passenger seat.
