BuckMe Bank: How to Deposit Money in GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

To deposit money in GTA 6, you need to unlock the BuckMe account in chapter 1, then go to an ATM: the app alone does not transfer cash.

A deposit protects your money if you die.

Unlocking the account

The BuckMe account opens in chapter 1 through a mission called "Buck Me", one of the first missions in the game. It installs the app on your phone. Before that, the only reliable hiding place is the floor of Jason's bedroom (see losing your money on death).

Depositing and withdrawing

You deposit at an ATM, never from the app. Just open BuckMe and choose "ATM Finder": the nearest one is marked on the map. The same ATMs let you withdraw money from the checking account.

In our playthrough, we saw that the checking account and the family savings account both appear in the app, with a savings goal and the option to transfer money between the two.

Family savings: careful, it is one-way

Money transferred to family savings can no longer be withdrawn, but it cannot be lost either. Each goal reached unlocks perks, including higher checking account limits. Think before you confirm: a transfer to savings cannot be undone.

What BuckMe changes about your purchases

  • Shops accept cash for items up to about $999; above $1,000, you have to pay with BuckMe.
  • Big purchases, such as garages, go through the account.

Limits to know

Cash is capped at $20,000 per character. If you go over it without visiting an ATM, you risk losing it when arrested. Note also that even money in the bank takes a small loss on death: see this page.

A real example

You have just sold loot to a fence: the cash is in your pocket. Open BuckMe, start the locator, go to the ATM, deposit, and only then head back out. This two-minute habit keeps you from losing everything at the next arrest. See where to sell loot.

Tip: deposit every time you walk past an ATM, especially before a robbery. See also making money.

Frequently asked questions

How do you deposit money in GTA 6?

Open the BuckMe app, find an ATM with the locator, then deposit your cash there.

Can you withdraw money from family savings?

No. That money can neither be withdrawn nor lost, and the transfer cannot be undone.

When does BuckMe become available?

In chapter 1, after a mission called "Buck Me", one of the first missions.
