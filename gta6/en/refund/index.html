How to get a refund for GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

On the PlayStation Store, a game can be refunded within 14 days as long as it has not been downloaded or streamed.

At Microsoft, a request is only possible after less than two hours of play. In both cases, approval is never guaranteed.

On PS5: the PlayStation Store

The official PlayStation help page sets clear conditions for a digital game:

  • the request must be made within 14 days of purchase;
  • the content must not have been downloaded or streamed;
  • a pre-order made more than 14 days before release must be requested before the release date; a pre-order made less than 14 days before release must be requested before release, or within 14 days of purchase if the release date has passed;
  • for a pre-order, the main part of the content must not have been downloaded.

Note: the download is the sticking point. If you have already downloaded GTA 6, a refund is in principle no longer possible, unless the content is faulty. The refund generally goes back to the original payment method, sometimes after a delay of up to 10 days.

On Xbox: the Microsoft Store

The Microsoft help page lists several conditions: less than two hours of total play, across all devices; the game must not be part of a bundle; you must be signed in to the right account; and the page also notes that your diagnostic settings must be set to “Full”, otherwise the request may be refused. The Xbox help page on refunds also details the case of pre-orders. Read it again before acting: the rules change. Also keep your order confirmation and, if possible, the time of your first play session: this is the information support will ask for first to review your case.

A boxed code: who do you ask?

A code bought in a store is not refunded by PlayStation: a code sold by a retailer is refunded by that retailer. For boxes with a missing or already used code, see our page on the physical version.

No promises

These rules are the stores’ rules, not ours. The decision rests with Sony or Microsoft, and any doubt is settled with their support. Before buying, check that your console is suitable, that you have the disk space needed and that the 30 FPS smoothness works for you. To know how long the game is: game length.

Step by step

  1. Check the conditions: PlayStation: within 14 days and nothing downloaded. Xbox: less than two hours of play.
  2. Open the order details: On PlayStation, open the order details and choose the transaction concerned.
  3. Request the refund: On PlayStation, choose “Cancel purchase”; on Xbox, use the refund help page.
  4. Track the refund: The amount generally goes back to the original payment method, sometimes within 10 days.
  5. Contact support if needed: If you get stuck, write to PlayStation or Microsoft support.

Frequently asked questions

Can you get a refund for GTA 6 on PS5?

Yes, within 14 days, as long as you have not downloaded the game or streamed it; a faulty game is an exception.

Can you refund GTA 6 after downloading it?

On the PlayStation Store, in principle no, unless the content is faulty. On Xbox, a request is possible within two hours of total play.

How long does a refund take?

PlayStation says it can take up to 10 days, generally back to the original payment method.

How do you cancel a GTA 6 pre-order?

On PlayStation, before release (or within 14 days of purchase if the release date has passed), as long as the main part is not downloaded.
