Death or Arrest in GTA 6: What You Lose

Updated November 24, 2026

In GTA 6, when you die or get arrested, your character returns to the nearest safe place, without going through a hospital.

Cash is lost; money in the bank is protected, as are weapons stored in a trunk or a locker.

Where do you respawn?

Two guides published on November 19 say the same thing: no hospital to patch you up. After “Wasted” (death), you reappear at the nearest safe place to where you fell. For an arrest (“Busted”), it is also a nearby place. In our games, death shows a red screen and makes you lose your cash.

What you lose

DeathArrest
CashLostConfiscated
Carried weaponsLost if not storedConfiscated

Both guides also mention the loss of storage bags carried in a vehicle, and a small “Buck Me” fee with no amount given; they don't agree on which cases it applies to, so we don't detail it.

What is protected

  • Money deposited in the bank (BuckMe app) is not touched. See how to deposit money.
  • Stored weapons in the trunk of a personal vehicle (if it is not in the impound lot) or in an Ammu-Nation weapons locker. See how to store your weapons.

No hospital: what does that change?

In the older GTA games, you woke up in a hospital after paying a bill. Here, the two guides we read describe a return to the nearest safe place, which saves you travel time. In exchange, everything you carry is at risk: cash, unstored weapons and, on death, storage bags left in the vehicle. So remember to store or deposit things before dangerous moments. Ammu-Nation gun stores offer lockers for weapons, as the guides note; we also came across several gun shops there, see our gun stores page.

How to limit your losses

Deposit your money before a heist or a risky fight, store your weapons in the trunk, and save before a hard mission. The phone lets you manage the bank at any time. If you are being chased, apply our tips on how to lose the police. To earn money back after a bad streak, see fast money.

These rules come from guides published on release day: they may be corrected if the game changes. What we saw in our playthrough: cash is indeed lost on death.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a hospital in GTA 6 when you die?

The guides we read say no: you respawn at the nearest safe place to where you died.

Do you lose all your money when you die?

Only the cash you carry. Money deposited in the bank is protected.

What happens to the weapon you carry?

It is lost if it is not stored in a personal vehicle trunk or a gun store locker.

What happens when you get arrested?

Cash and carried weapons are confiscated, according to the guides we read.
