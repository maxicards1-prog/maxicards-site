GTA 6 radio stations and music styles

Updated November 24, 2026

GTA 6 has about 21 to 23 radio stations depending on the guides, from rap to classical, plus country, metal and two talk stations.

Here is each one with its style.

How many stations?

The guides do not count the same thing: 21 stations for one, 22 for another, 23 for a third (including two stations with no music). The difference mostly comes from what counts as a station and a few less common names. Below, we only keep the stations mentioned by several guides, with a style they agree on. We do not publish a song list.

Two stations with an unclear genre

Stockyard FM and Kaleidoscope FM are described very differently from one guide to another (pop, dance, indie, electro, synth pop). We do not choose for you. Also note that Emotion 98.3 is listed as ballads by two guides and as pop by a third.

Which station for which mood?

For upbeat driving music, Flash FM (pop) or CircoLoco Records Radio (electronic) fit. For the game's atmosphere, Vice City FM goes for the 1980s. Rock or metal fans will head to V-Rock or The Chamber 106.6, hip-hop fans to Dirty South Classics or VCRR, and to relax there is jazz, blues and classical music.

The talk stations

Two stations do not play music: The Wipe, a talk program, and WKTT, with speech and commentary. They are mentioned by at least two guides.

Not everything is published yet

One guide says the full track lists have not all been officially revealed, and that some of the station names come from leaks. So we do not detail songs or hosts. To go further in the game, check our Vice City and Leonida Keys pages, or try auto-drive while listening to the radio.

Frequently asked questions

How many radio stations are in GTA 6?

Between 21 and 23 depending on the guides, including two talk stations with no music.

Which station plays rap?

Dirty South Classics for Southern rap and VCRR for modern hip-hop.

Is there 80s music?

Yes, on Vice City FM, according to the guides.

Are there stations with no music?

Yes, The Wipe (talk) and WKTT (speech and commentary).
