How much did GTA 6 cost?

Updated November 24, 2026

Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has published the budget of GTA 6.

Analysts estimate it between $1 billion and $1.5 billion, a rough range and not an official figure.

The budget: an estimate, not an official figure

No budget has been disclosed. Take-Two’s CEO, Strauss Zelnick, only said that the game was expensive and, during the November 9 earnings call, that the company had “not talked about budgets at all”. Every figure therefore comes from third parties.

You will also come across “$2 billion” or more. This figure goes back to a press article from July 2025, with no known basis, and Rockstar denied that the budget exceeds $1.5 billion. We do not use it. The safe figure to keep: in the range of $1 to $1.5 billion, estimated.

How many years of development?

Rockstar has never said when development began. The first official confirmation dates from February 4, 2022, when the studio described a game “well underway”. Since the release of GTA V in September 2013, about 13 years passed before that of GTA VI. Articles that talk about “more than ten years” or a start in 2018 are guesses: an exact duration cannot be given.

The official launch figures

On November 20, 2026, Take-Two announced:

  • $3.79 billion in worldwide retail sales on day one (the publisher’s estimate, through the end of November 19);
  • 32.5 million copies sold. According to articles, this total covers pre-orders alone or pre-orders plus launch day: the exact wording is not always the same.

Note: $3.79 billion is what players spent in stores, not Take-Two’s revenue, which will be given in its quarterly results. Take-Two did not split PS5 and Xbox, or digital and physical. An analyst cited by TweakTown estimates more than $4 billion and about 45 million copies after the opening weekend: this is a third-party estimate, not an official figure. A first estimate of $2.36 billion, repeated on November 20, was overtaken by the publisher’s announcement.

Frequently asked questions

How much did GTA 6 cost to develop?
