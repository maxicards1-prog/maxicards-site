GTA 6 Controls on PS5 and Xbox

Updated November 24, 2026

In GTA 6, you sprint by clicking the left stick, aim with L2/LT and shoot with R2/RT, open the phone with up on the D-pad and the map with the touchpad (PS5) or the View button (Xbox).

Here are all the buttons the guides agree on.

The basic controls

The guides we read don't agree on every button, sometimes because of differences between schemes. We only keep here what is cited consistently. For everything else, use the game's controls screen: it is the reference.

Sprinting: a change

In the default scheme, sprinting is no longer done by tapping a button: you click the left stick. Longtime GTA players may prefer the Classic scheme, which is closer to the habits of the previous games.

Driving

In a car, the accelerator is on R2 (PS5) or RT (Xbox) and the brake on L2 or LT, like in most recent driving games. To get in or out of a vehicle, press Triangle (PS5) or Y (Xbox): two guides agree on this point. The handbrake, radio and horn are not described consistently, so we don't give them; look for them in the game's controls screen. The auto drive buttons are detailed in our dedicated page.

Aiming and shooting

Aiming is done by holding L2 or LT, and shooting on foot with R2 or RT. The aiming settings (Lock-On, Partial, Free Aim) change how the reticle behaves: see the recommended settings.

The three schemes

In Settings, you choose between Default, Classic and Left-Handed. You can change it at any time: see also our recommended settings.

Frequently asked questions

How do you sprint in GTA 6?

By clicking the left stick (L3 on PS5) in the default scheme. Tap-to-sprint is no longer part of that scheme.

How do you open the map?

With the touchpad on PS5 or the View button on Xbox Series.

How do you open the phone?

With up on the D-pad, on both consoles.

Can you change the control scheme?

Yes, in the settings: Default, Classic or Left-Handed.
