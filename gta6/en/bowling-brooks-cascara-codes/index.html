Kingpin, Brooks Boatworks and Cascara Safe Codes

Updated November 24, 2026

The Kingpin Bowling code is 0300, the Brooks Boatworks code is 6823 and the La Cascara Motel code is 6969.

All three are recorded at once by studying the wall map at Brooks Boatworks, in Port Gellhorn (chapter 2).

The three codes at a glance

  • Kingpin Bowling (office, south Port Gellhorn, along US-9): 0300.
  • Brooks Boatworks (shipyard, northeast of town): 6823.
  • La Cascara Motel (west of town, near the railroad bridge to Draper Island): 6969.

These three places are illegal operations of the Kolt & Associates gang, unlockable in chapter 2. All the guides we read give the same digits. The bowling code starts with a zero: enter all four digits, or it will be refused.

Where to find the clue: the wall map

You do not need to search every location. At Brooks Boatworks, a map hangs on the wall in a room upstairs. Study it (hold L2 on PS5, according to one guide): all three codes are added to your game and then show up every time you approach the safes concerned.

The route on foot

  1. Head to Brooks Boatworks, northeast of Port Gellhorn.
  2. Enter through the gap in the fence, on the east side of the hideout.
  3. Go up the stairs of the gray building, follow the walkway around and go through the door.
  4. Study the map on the wall, then open the safe.
Bowling alley trap: one guide reports that a note in the Kingpin office has several crossed-out codes. Do not use them; only 0300, from the map, is correct.

What if the code is refused?

One guide says the digits may vary between saves, and another specifies that these are the ones from their own test. Compare with what your wall map shows: that is what counts. Failing that, a sticky explosive, bought at a gun shop, opens the safe, but it alerts nearby enemies and may bring the police.

What you get

The guides do not give a reliable amount for these three safes, so we are not announcing one. To know what to do with the cash, see our pages on money stashes and robberies, and find the area on the Port Gellhorn page. The other combinations are in the full list of safe codes, and chapter 2 progress is on the missions.

Step by step

  1. Go to Brooks Boatworks: Head to the shipyard, northeast of Port Gellhorn, and go through the gap in the fence on the east side.
  2. Go upstairs: Take the stairs of the gray building, follow the walkway and go through the door.
  3. Study the wall map: Hold L2 (PS5) in front of the map: all three codes are recorded.
  4. Open the safes: Enter 6823 at Brooks, 6969 at La Cascara Motel and 0300 at Kingpin Bowling.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Kingpin bowling alley code in GTA 6?

0300. The safe is in the bowling alley office, south of Port Gellhorn. Enter all four digits, zero included.

Where do you find the Brooks Boatworks and La Cascara codes?

On the wall map upstairs at Brooks Boatworks: it records 6823 (Brooks), 6969 (La Cascara) and 0300 (bowling alley) all at once.

Do you need to have progressed in the story?

The guides place these three operations in chapter 2. They do not say whether they are accessible earlier.
