Beginner Tips for Starting GTA 6

Updated November 24, 2026

The advice several guides agree on for your first hours in Leonida.

The tips below are the ones several player guides give; we left out any that only one guide claims.

Money

  • Cash is lost if you die or get arrested. Before you have an account, hide it under the floorboards in Jason's room.
  • The BuckMe account opens with the "Buck Me" mission in chapter 1; after that, deposit at an ATM.
  • Don't wear yourself out farming early on: the big money comes later (fast money).
  • Do the Key Lento activities before leaving the area (diving, fishing, requests from the neighbor Shirley): coming back takes time.

Controls to know

  • Focus mode (concentration): click both sticks (L3 + R3). It highlights enemies, useful objects and money; in combat, it briefly slows time.
  • Phone: up on the D-pad.
  • Put your weapon away: L1 on PS5, LB on Xbox.

Police and criminal profile

  • To shake off the police: change your outfit, change or repaint the car, put your weapon away, destroy surveillance camera footage.
  • Your criminal profile has five levels, from "Professional" to "Irredeemable". Killing civilians, police officers or animals lowers it; at the "Psycho" level, you can no longer surrender.
  • Knocking people out instead of killing them keeps a good profile and fills the focus gauge.

Cars and getting around

  • Scan cars with the WAiNK app before stealing them (alarm, tracker, value).
  • There is no fast travel on the map: use the RideMe app (about $100 to skip the trip), public transport or character switching.

Training Jason and Lucia

  • Weight training increases health, cardio (running, cycling, kayaking) increases stamina, and calm activities like fishing increase focus.
  • If you stop training, health and stamina drop back down.
  • Eating, drinking, sleeping and showering give temporary bonuses.

Weapons

  • Weapons bought at Ammu-Nation come back after a death; those picked up from an enemy are lost.
  • A silencer delays the police response.

Frequently asked questions

How do you activate Focus mode in GTA 6?

Click both sticks at the same time (L3 + R3 on PS5, both sticks on Xbox).

Is there fast travel in GTA 6?

There is no teleporting from the map. The RideMe app lets you pay about $100 to skip a trip, and you can switch characters.

Where should you keep your money early in GTA 6?

Under the floorboards in Jason's room, then in the BuckMe account as soon as you do the "Buck Me" mission.
