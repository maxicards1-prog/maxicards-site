Roblox Username Generator

Pick a style, then generate.

Rules checked on November 23, 2026. Sources: username rules (Roblox help). They can change: check them on Roblox before you act.

  • Runs in your browser
  • Free
  • No sign-up
  • No data sent

Pick a style and, if you like, a word: the tool suggests 12 usernames that follow Roblox's format rules.

Everything is calculated in your browser: nothing is sent, nothing is stored, and there is no account or sign-up.

How to use it

  1. Type a word if you like (a nickname, an animal, a color), with no accents or spaces.
  2. Pick a style: cute, gamer, funny, mystery or epic.
  3. Click “Generate 12 ideas” as many times as you want.
  4. Copy the one you like, then try it in Roblox to see if it is free.

How the tool builds usernames

The generator mixes your word with prefixes and suffixes that match the chosen style, sometimes adds a number or an underscore, then rejects anything outside the format: 3 to 20 characters, letters without accents and numbers, a single underscore that is neither at the start nor at the end. Accented letters are simplified (“Zoé” becomes “Zoe”). Everything happens in your browser: neither your word nor the results are sent.

The five styles

  • Cute: soft and sweet, with plushies, clouds and unicorns.
  • Gamer: Pro, Turbo, Master, Ninja… the players' classic.
  • Funny: Captain Potato, Doctor Pickle: made to make people smile.
  • Mystery: shadows, mist and ghosts.
  • Epic: dragons, titans and knights.

Before you choose

A good username contains no personal information (full name, address, school) and does not look like a banned word. The tool does not know whether a username is free: Roblox tells you when you try to create it. You can also check it with the username checker. On an existing account, a username change costs Robux; the display name is free (see the difference).

Frequently asked questions

Are the suggested usernames available?

Maybe, but the tool cannot know: it does not contact Roblox. Try them when you sign up; if a name is taken, Roblox tells you.

Why are there no accents or spaces?

Roblox only accepts letters A to Z, numbers and one underscore in a username. The tool follows these rules.

Can I generate a username from my first name?

It is not a good idea: a username should not contain personal information. Use a nickname or a word you like instead.

How many usernames can I generate?

As many as you want: every click gives 12 new ideas.

What if a username is refused?

Try another idea or add a number. A username can be refused because it is taken or because moderation blocked it.
