What is this site for?

Roblox is a world of games made by players, with its own currency, Robux. This site answers the practical questions players, creators and parents ask: how much Robux cost, what a sale or DevEx really pays, how to find a username in the right format, how to secure an account or set up parental controls.

Checked figures, honest tools

Every figure in the tools (pack prices, DevEx rate, creator share, username rules) comes from official Roblox pages, with the check date shown under each tool and the full list on the Sources page. Pack prices are shown in euros, the only currency we could verify on an official page. Calculations run in your browser, with no account and no data sent.

What we will never do: promise Robux without paying, offer a tool that makes Robux or codes, or share cheats. Those are scams; our guide on “no-payment Robux” promises explains how to spot them.
