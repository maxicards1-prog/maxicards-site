Roblox Tax Calculator

Enter the Robux price of your pass or item: the tool shows your share, Roblox's share and the DevEx value in US dollars.

Everything is calculated in your browser: nothing is sent, nothing is stored, and there is no account or sign-up.

How to use it

  1. Type the sale price in Robux.
  2. Pick the type of sale: game pass or developer product, avatar item sold on the Marketplace, or avatar item sold in your own game.
  3. Keep the suggested DevEx rate, or change it.
  4. Read your share per sale and the price to set to receive about 1,000 Robux.

What players call the “Roblox tax”

The “tax” is not a government tax: it is the part of each sale that Roblox keeps. Its size depends on what you sell.

The official shares

  • Game passes and developer products: you receive 70% of the Robux spent; the remaining 30% is Roblox's marketplace fee.
  • Avatar item (UGC) sold on the avatar Marketplace: the creator receives 30%; Roblox keeps the 40% “seller” share and a 30% fee.
  • Avatar item sold in your own game: 30% as the creator plus 40% as the seller, so 70% in total.

These percentages come from Roblox's official documentation, read on November 23, 2026. To sell avatar items, Roblox requires an age-verified creator subscribed to Roblox Plus or Premium. If Roblox changes a share, pick “Other share” and type the new percentage.

From Robux to dollars

Earned Robux only become money through DevEx, with a minimum of 30,000 earned Robux. So the tool converts your share at the official rate, to show what one sale is worth. The calculation is an estimate: Roblox may round, and taxes are not deducted. Setting a price also means thinking about players: too high scares them away, too low earns little. Our guide to making a game pass and the DevEx calculator go with this tool.

Nothing guarantees sales: these numbers show what a sale would earn, not what you will earn.

Frequently asked questions

How much do you get from a 100 Robux game pass?

70 Robux, based on the official 70% share; Roblox keeps 30 Robux.

Why does an avatar item earn less?

On the avatar Marketplace, the creator receives 30%: Roblox takes the 40% seller share and a 30% fee. In your own game, you also get the seller share, so 70% in total.

How do I receive about 1,000 Robux per sale?

Divide 1,000 by your share (0.7 for a pass): you need a price of about 1,429 Robux. The tool works it out for you.

Can these shares change?

Yes, Roblox adjusts its rules from time to time. The “Other share” field lets you type the current value; check it in the official documentation.

Are the Robux I receive real money?

Not directly: you have to exchange them through DevEx, which requires at least 30,000 earned Robux and other conditions.
