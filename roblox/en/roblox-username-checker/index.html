Roblox Username Checker

Type a username to check its format.

Rules checked on November 23, 2026. Sources: username rules (Roblox help) · changing your username (Roblox help). They can change: check them on Roblox before you act.

  • Runs in your browser
  • Free
  • No sign-up
  • No data sent

Type a username: the tool checks each Roblox format rule and tells you which one is the problem. It does not check whether the name is taken.

Everything is calculated in your browser: nothing is sent, nothing is stored, and there is no account or sign-up.

How to use it

  1. Type the username you want.
  2. Read each rule: a green tick means “OK”, a red cross means “fix this”.
  3. Edit the username until everything is green.
  4. Then try it in Roblox: that is the only place to find out if it is free and accepted.

The format rules

According to Roblox's official help (read on November 23, 2026), a username must:

  • be 3 to 20 characters long;
  • use only letters (A to Z, no accents) and numbers;
  • contain at most one underscore (_), which cannot be at the start or the end;
  • contain no inappropriate words;
  • contain no personal information: full name, phone number, address.

The tool checks the first three rules automatically. The last two depend on what the words mean, so they are up to you: read your username the way a parent or a moderator would.

What the tool cannot know

A username in the right format can still be refused: another player may already have it, or Roblox's moderation, which filters text all the time, may block it. To know if it is free, try it in Roblox: when signing up, or in Settings, then Account Info, with the username change button. Careful: changing the username of an existing account costs Robux (see the guide), while the display name is free. Need ideas? Try the username generator.

Tips for a good username

Pick a username that is easy to read and remember, with no link to your real identity. Avoid your birth date, your town or your school. Never give your password to a website that promises to “check” a username for you.

Frequently asked questions

Which characters are allowed in a Roblox username?

Letters A to Z without accents, numbers 0 to 9 and a single underscore that is neither at the start nor at the end, according to the official help.

What is the maximum length?

20 characters, and at least 3.

The tool says my username is valid: is it available?

Not necessarily. The tool only checks the format. Availability and moderation can only be checked in Roblox.

Can I use spaces or accents?

No: no spaces, no accents, no symbols. For a freer name, use the display name, which is free.

Is the username I type sent anywhere?

No, the check runs entirely in your browser.
