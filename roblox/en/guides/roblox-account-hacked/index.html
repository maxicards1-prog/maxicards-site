My Roblox account was hacked: what do I do?

The short answer

First clean your device, reset your password, turn on two-step verification, then contact Roblox support, ideally within 30 days.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Clean the device. Run a full antivirus scan, restart, and run it again. Remove suspicious browser extensions.
  2. Reset the password. Use “Forgot Password or Username?” on the login page and pick a new, unique password.
  3. Turn on two-step verification. In Settings, on the Security tab, turn it on, ideally with an authenticator app.
  4. Contact support. If the reset fails, write to Roblox support using the account's original email.

Stay calm, act in order

Losing your account is stressful, but the order matters: if you reset the password on an infected computer, the hacker can take the account back. So follow the steps above in that order, as Roblox's official page recommends.

1. Remove what caused the hack

If you downloaded a file or installed an extension from a shady site, remove it. Run a full scan of the computer, restart and run it again. Delete any browser extension you do not recognize, and only install extensions from trusted sources.

2. Reset the password

On the login page, click “Forgot Password or Username?” and enter your email or phone number. The detailed steps are in Roblox forgot password. Choose a password you use nowhere else.

3. Turn on two-step verification

It protects the account even if the password is stolen again. See how to secure your Roblox account.

4. If the reset does not work

Contact Roblox support with proof that the account is yours. The official help page mentions two useful items: the first email address added to the account and the billing address of the very first payment. Even if the hacker changed the email, this information helps. With no email or purchase, Roblox may explore other ways of verifying. Getting the account back is never guaranteed.

Lost items and Robux

Roblox states that it is not obliged, except where the law requires, to help with compromised accounts, and does not guarantee returning items or Robux. In rare cases, it may offer to restore an inventory or its approximate value. For that, you must contact it within 30 days of the hack. So do not wait.

Preventing it from happening again

  • Do not share your password, codes or cookies.
  • Be wary of promises of free Robux: it is a scam.
  • If a minor is involved, tell their parents: see Roblox parental controls.

Frequently asked questions

How do I recover a hacked Roblox account?

Reset the password after cleaning your device, turn on two-step verification, then contact Roblox support if needed.

The hacker changed the account's email, what do I do?

Contact support with the account's first email address or the billing email of the first payment.

Can I get my stolen Robux back?

Roblox does not guarantee it. In rare cases it may help, as long as you contact it within 30 days.

How do I know if my computer is infected?

Run a full antivirus scan and remove browser extensions you do not recognize, as Roblox advises.
