How to get Robux the legitimate way

The short answer

Roblox says it plainly: Robux are bought, or earned as a creator. There is no way to get them for free, but you can earn them by creating games or items.

Updated November 24, 2026

What Roblox says

Roblox's official “Ways to Get Robux” page sums it up: Robux are purchased with money, or earned as a creator. It adds that a “Robux generator” does not exist and that any offer of free Robux is a scam. If you are looking for a shortcut, read our guide Free Robux: why it is a scam first.

The real ways to earn Robux

According to Roblox's help center, “earned Robux” come mainly from:

  • selling items on the Marketplace or the Creator Store;
  • purchases made in your experience (game passes, items, developer products);
  • Creator Rewards, linked to player engagement;
  • ad revenue from the Publisher program;
  • affiliate commissions on sales.

Every method takes work and nothing is guaranteed: Roblox states that only creators who succeed reach high earnings.

Make a game or a game pass

Anyone can learn to create with Roblox Studio, which is free. A game pass or an in-experience purchase earns Robux when a player buys it. Roblox keeps a commission: use our sales earnings calculator to estimate the creator's share. To get started, see how to make a Roblox game.

Sell avatar items

You can sell clothing and accessories on the Marketplace. Roblox now requires a Roblox Plus subscription (or an existing Premium 1000/2200) to publish and keep avatar items on sale. The detailed conditions change, so check the official documentation before you start.

The Roblox Plus referral program

Roblox runs an official referral program for Plus subscribers. Its rewards are promotional perks with no cash value, set by Roblox, and the program can change. Never share your password to “refer” someone: only a link generated by Roblox is valid.

Turning earned Robux into money

The Developer Exchange (DevEx) program lets you cash out earned Robux under strict conditions. Robux that you bought or received as a gift are not eligible. Read how DevEx works and run numbers with our DevEx calculator.

What you should never do

Never give out your password or security codes, never complete a survey in exchange for Robux, and never use cheat scripts or tools: you could lose your account.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get free Robux on Roblox?

Roblox states that free Robux do not exist: they are bought, or earned by creating games or items. Any free offer is a scam.

Can I get Robux without paying or creating anything?

Not through an official method. The only sources Roblox recognizes are purchases and creator earnings.

Are earned Robux worth real money?

Under conditions, earned Robux can be exchanged for money through DevEx. Nothing is guaranteed and you need a minimum amount of earned Robux.

Can kids earn Robux?

Any player can make a game with Roblox Studio. DevEx requires being at least 13, and a parent's agreement is advisable for minors.
