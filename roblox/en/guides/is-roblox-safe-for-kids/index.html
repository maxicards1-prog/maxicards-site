Is Roblox safe for kids?

The short answer

No platform where strangers talk to each other is risk-free. Roblox offers protections (filtered chat, parental controls, reporting), but they don't replace talking with your child or setting the account up properly.

Updated November 24, 2026

The real risks

  • Contact with strangers: text or voice chat, friend requests.
  • Scams: fake Robux generators, fake sites. See free Robux: the scam.
  • Unplanned spending: see limit spending.
  • Content that doesn't suit the age, depending on the game.
  • Screen time.

What Roblox has in place (11/24/2026)

According to its page "Safety Features: Chat, Privacy & Filtering", filtered chat is the standard for all eligible communication. It targets inappropriate content (harassment, violence, sexual content) and, for players 12 and under, personal information. Account owners can limit or turn off who can chat with them, message them or follow them into games.

The page "Roblox Kids and Roblox Select" states that users under 16 are placed in catalogs of games that have passed an extra safety check, with chat off until an age check is done. Details in how old do you have to be to play Roblox and Roblox Kids and Select.

Five things for parents

  1. Link your account to your child's: Roblox parental controls.
  2. Check their age and chat settings together.
  3. Teach them to block and report: report a player.
  4. Protect the account: secure your account.
  5. Choose suitable games: games for younger players.

Golden rules to repeat to your child

Never give out an address, a phone number or a password. Never leave the platform for another chat at a stranger's request. Talk to an adult as soon as something feels wrong. Roblox itself notes that its safety rules don't remove every risk: parents' judgment matters.

Sources

Roblox help pages: Safety Features: Chat, Privacy & Filtering, Safety & Civility at Roblox, Roblox Kids and Roblox Select (checked 11/24/2026).

Frequently asked questions

Is Roblox dangerous or not?

It carries risks of contact, scams and spending, which parental controls and conversation reduce. No protection is absolute.

Why is Roblox dangerous for kids?

Mostly because of exchanges with strangers, scams and content that doesn't suit their age.

Is Roblox's chat dangerous?

Chat is filtered, and off by default for under 16s without an age check, but it is still worth talking about with your child.

What can parents do?

Link their account, set up parental controls, limit spending, and teach their child to block and report.
