How to change your username on Roblox

The short answer

You change your username in Settings, on the Account Info tab, with the pencil button, and it costs Robux. Your display name, on the other hand, changes for free.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Open settings. While logged in, click the gear at the top right (website) or the three dots “More” (app).
  2. Account Info. Open the Account Info tab.
  3. Change the username. Click the Change Username button (pencil icon).
  4. Enter the new username. Type the new username, then your current password.
  5. Buy the change. Click Buy. You will be able to log in with the new username and the same password.

How much does it cost?

Roblox says every username change costs Robux. The official help page we checked on Nov 23, 2026 gives no amount: the price appears in the purchase window, before you confirm. It adds that no discount is possible for a simple change, such as switching the name to capital letters. So plan the amount in advance, and ask a parent if the account is theirs to fund.

Rules to know

  • You cannot take a name already used by another account.
  • You cannot swap your username with that of another of your accounts.
  • The account creation date stays the same.
  • Your old usernames stay visible in your settings, on your profile and in people search.
  • You can go back to a previous username, but that costs Robux again.
  • All text, usernames included, goes through a filter: an inappropriate name is refused.

Choosing a good username

Pick a username that is easy to remember, with no real name, birth date or address. For ideas, try our username generator, then check the format with the username checker. It checks length and characters, but not whether the name is free: only Roblox confirms that when you make the change.

Username or display name?

The username is the unique identifier of the account. The display name is a freer name, which other players can also use, and it can be changed for free. If you only want to change what people see, do not pay for a username change: read our guide to the Roblox display name.

Your Robux and items are safe

A username change does not affect your items or inventory. To secure the account before any change, turn on two-step verification: see how to secure your account.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to change a Roblox username?

Roblox says the change costs Robux, but the help page gives no figure. The price appears before you confirm the purchase.

Can I change my username for free?

Not the username. Only the display name can be changed for free.

Can I get my old username back?

Yes, by following the same steps, but the change costs Robux again.

Can other players see my old username?

Yes, your past usernames are visible in people search. For removal options, check Roblox's privacy and support pages.
