Can you play Roblox on Nintendo Switch?

The short answer

As of 11/24/2026, Roblox does not list the Nintendo Switch among its platforms. Its help pages name computers, mobile, Xbox, PlayStation (in select countries) and Meta Quest, and give no date for other devices.

Updated November 24, 2026

What Roblox says

The help page "When will Roblox be on other devices?" (checked 11/24/2026) simply answers that the company has no further details to give about other devices for now, and invites you to follow its blog. It points to the system requirements pages to see where Roblox is available.

Where can you play, then?

  • Computer: Windows 10 and 11, macOS: see how to install Roblox.
  • Mobile: iPhone, iPad, Android, Amazon Fire tablets.
  • Xbox: you play with friends who are on computer, Mac or mobile.
  • PlayStation 4 and 5: in select countries only.
  • Meta Quest: in virtual reality.

Players on these platforms share the same experiences, according to Roblox's Xbox and PlayStation pages. Check the system requirements before buying hardware.

Beware of "tricks" for the Switch

You can find unofficial methods online to "get Roblox on Switch". Roblox mentions none of them, and we don't relay them. They may ask you to enter your login details on third-party sites, a classic way to get hacked. See what to do about a hacked Roblox account and secure your account. A site or video promising a "Roblox Switch" with free Robux is a scam: read why free Robux are a scam.

Alternatives for a child

A phone or tablet is often enough. Parents can manage use with parental controls and pick games suited to younger players.
