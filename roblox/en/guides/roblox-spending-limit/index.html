How to limit your child's spending on Roblox

The short answer

With a linked parent account, open Settings, Parental controls, Spending restrictions and set a monthly limit. For an unauthorized purchase, contact support before any bank dispute.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Log in. Log in with the account that has parent rights (linked to the child's).
  2. Open the settings. Go to Settings (gear), then Parental controls, and choose the child's account.
  3. Spending restrictions. Scroll to “Settings you manage” and click Spending restrictions.
  4. Set the limit. Click Monthly spending limit, enter the monthly amount and click Update.
  5. Turn on alerts. Under Spending notifications, choose: all transactions, or high-spending alerts.

What the monthly limit covers

The limit sets what the child can spend per month on their account. Roblox states that it does not apply to redeeming a gift card and may not apply on some consoles. On a console, you need to set the limit on the device itself, and Roblox's limits do not control purchases made through Xbox: to require a parent's approval on Xbox, follow Xbox's own settings. From age 13, spending limits no longer apply to the child's account, but you can keep notifications.

Spending notifications

Under Spending notifications there are three choices: all transactions, high-spending alerts (an email if your child spends $100, $250 and/or $500 US dollars, or the local equivalent, in the month, then every ten transactions), or none. Even without a Roblox notification, a payment provider or a recurring subscription may write to you.

An unauthorized purchase: what to do

Roblox reminds players that they must always ask permission from the payment method's owner. For unauthorized charges by card, PayPal or Google Play, contact Roblox support first, before disputing the payment: once it is disputed, Roblox can no longer refund directly. Prepare the list of charges (date, amount), the billing name and the last four digits of the card. If the statement shows “Xsolla*Roblox”, contact Xsolla support.

Purchases made through Apple, Microsoft, PlayStation, Meta Quest, Samsung or Amazon cannot be refunded by Roblox: the request goes through that provider.

What is not refunded

  • Roblox says there is no way to get Robux back once a purchase is made, including by mistake. Use the Try On feature before buying an item.
  • Gift cards are not refundable: see how to redeem a Roblox gift card.

Good habits

  • Do not leave your card saved on the child's device, or turn on password protection in the App Store or Google Play.
  • Keep an eye on subscriptions: see Premium and Plus.
  • A fixed budget makes things easier: the Robux converter helps you see what you are paying.
  • For the other settings: Roblox parental controls.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set a spending limit on Roblox?

With a linked parent account: Settings, Parental controls, the child's account, Spending restrictions, Monthly spending limit, then Update.

Does the limit block gift cards?

No, Roblox states that monthly limits have no effect on redeeming gift cards.

My child bought Robux without permission, can I get a refund?

Contact Roblox support before disputing the payment. For Apple, Microsoft, PlayStation or Amazon, the request goes through those providers.

Can I get back Robux spent by mistake?

Roblox says there is no way to get Robux back after a purchase, even an accidental one.
