How to secure your Roblox account

The short answer

Add a verified email, turn on two-step verification (ideally with an authenticator app), use a unique password and never give out your codes.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Verify your email. A verified email address is required to turn on two-step verification. It must be yours or a parent's.
  2. Open Security. Click the gear (website) or the three dots then the gear (app), then open the Security tab.
  3. Choose a method. Switch on the method you want: authenticator app, email or security key.
  4. Follow the instructions. The steps shown vary depending on the method you choose.
  5. Change your password. Choose a long password, different from all your other accounts.

Why two-step verification?

It adds a layer of protection: even if someone knows your password, they cannot log in without the second code. Roblox offers three methods: authenticator app, email and security keys, and recommends the authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Duo or Authy, for example). You can only pick one method at a time.

The three methods at a glance

  • Email: a code arrives in your inbox each time you log in with a password.
  • Authenticator app: a code that changes regularly on your phone. This is the method Roblox recommends.
  • Security key: a physical key (such as a YubiKey or Titan) or a method managed by your device, like face recognition. Roblox asks that you also have the authenticator app turned on, in case you lose the key.

Roblox also offers passkey login. Careful: logging in with a code sent by email or with a passkey bypasses two-step verification, according to the help center.

The habits that matter most

  • Never give your password, browser cookies, verification codes or backup codes. Roblox employees will never ask for them.
  • Use a different password for each account, hard to guess, without your username.
  • Never add someone else's email address: they would receive the reset messages and could take your account.
  • Log out on a shared computer, at school or at a friend's.
  • Do not click links from unknown sites, and check the address of the login page.

Scams to know

The most common way to lose an account is to hand over your own login details, often for fake Robux or a “trick”. Read Free Robux scams to recognize the traps.

What if it is already too late?

See our guide to a hacked Roblox account. If you simply lost access, follow forgot password. Parents can also check parental controls.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn on two-factor authentication on Roblox?

In settings, open the Security tab and turn on the method you want. A verified email is required.

What is the best two-step verification method?

Roblox recommends the authenticator app, which is safer than email.

Do I need an email address on my Roblox account?

Yes, it is strongly advised: it is used to reset the password and to turn on two-step verification.

Can a Roblox employee ask me for my password?

Never. Roblox states that its staff do not ask for passwords, codes or cookies.
