Roblox Premium and Roblox Plus: price, perks, cancelling

The short answer

Since April 30, 2026, Roblox Premium is no longer sold: Roblox Plus replaces it, at $4.99 USD per month. Existing Premium subscribers can keep it, and cancel at any time.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Open settings. Log in to the account that holds the subscription, then click the gear at the top right.
  2. Go to Subscriptions. In Settings, choose Subscriptions.
  3. Cancel renewal. Click “Cancel Renewal” and confirm.
  4. If you pay through a mobile app. If you paid through the App Store or Google Play, cancel from the subscriptions of your Apple or Google account.

Premium is over for new subscribers

Roblox's help center announces that, since April 30, 2026, the Roblox Premium subscription is no longer available to buy. Existing subscribers can keep it, but if they cancel they cannot subscribe again. On May 30, 2026, the 10% bonus on Robux purchases and the Premium logo disappeared. What remains: the monthly Robux allowance, trading and reselling of items, and publishing avatar items for the Premium 1000/2200 plans.

Roblox Plus: the perks

Roblox Plus is a subscription at $4.99 USD per month (price on Roblox's newsroom page, checked Nov 24, 2026). Its perks:

  • 10% off in-experience items and avatars, and 20% from the third consecutive month;
  • free private servers in compatible experiences;
  • sending Robux without fees, with age conditions: the people involved must be verified 18+, and a parent must approve transfers for under-18s;
  • unlimited trading of Limited items, and reselling;
  • publishing and selling avatar items;
  • a badge on your profile.

Discounts do not apply to items over one million Robux, resold items, ad credits or some fees.

Plus does not include Robux by default

The basic subscription includes no Robux. Three plans add them: Plus 500, Plus 1000 and Plus 2000, with 500, 1,000 and 2,000 Robux each month. We could not read their prices on an official page, so we do not quote them: the price appears when you subscribe. To compare with buying Robux alone, see Robux prices.

Cancelling and tracking your subscription

You can cancel a recurring subscription at any time: the perks stay active until the end of the period already paid. Subscriptions bought through the app on Windows or Amazon are not recurring. A subscription cannot be transferred from one account to another. If the cancel button is missing, it is often because the subscription is already canceled or was not bought with that payment method.

For families

A subscription is a purchase: get the payment method owner's agreement and set limits, see how to limit spending and parental controls. If a code or gift card pays for the subscription, read how to redeem a Roblox gift card.

Frequently asked questions

Does Roblox Premium still exist?

It has not been sold since April 30, 2026. Existing subscribers can keep it, but cannot resubscribe after a cancellation.

How much does Roblox Plus cost?

$4.99 USD per month for the base subscription (Roblox newsroom, checked Nov 24, 2026). Taxes or local prices may apply when you subscribe.

How do I cancel Roblox Plus or Premium?

On the website: gear, Settings, Subscriptions, then Cancel Renewal. If you paid through the App Store or Google Play, cancel from your Apple or Google account.

Does Roblox Plus give Robux?

Not in the base plan. The Plus 500, 1000 and 2000 plans include 500, 1,000 or 2,000 Robux per month.
