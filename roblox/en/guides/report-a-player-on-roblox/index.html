How to report a player on Roblox

The short answer

In the game, open the menu at the top left, click the flag icon next to the player's name, choose the reason and submit. Report the specific message or content, not just the profile: otherwise moderators can't see what happened.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Open the game menu. Click the Roblox icon at the top left of the screen.
  2. Pick the player. Click the flag icon next to their name, or the Report tab.
  3. Select Person. Then choose the player under Which Person?
  4. Give the reason. Choose the reason under Reason for Abuse? and add a short description.
  5. Submit. Click Submit. To cut off all contact, block the player too.

Why report from inside the game

The official help page (checked 11/24/2026) explains that Report Abuse sends the report straight to moderators. It insists on one point: report the specific chat, private message or content that breaks the rules. If you only report a user from their profile page, moderators can't see what they did wrong.

Reporting outside a game

  • A whole experience: the red Report Abuse button in the description, on the game's About page.
  • An item (shirt, pass, model): the three dots at the top right of its info box, then Report item.
  • A chat message: in the mobile app, the (i) icon at the top right, then the participant, then Report Abuse.

Blocking a player

To block, open the profile, click the three dots at the top right, then Block User. During a game, you can also block from the player list or the Esc menu, People tab. When you block someone, they are not notified, you are no longer friends, and you can't message or invite each other. Parents can block their child's friends from parental controls: see Roblox parental controls.

Serious cases

For threats, harassment or shocking content aimed at a minor, keep screenshots and also tell a trusted adult. To see how Roblox protects younger players, read is Roblox safe for kids.

Sources

Roblox help pages: How to Report Rule Violations and How to block another user (checked 11/24/2026).

Frequently asked questions

How do I block someone on Roblox?

Open the player's profile, click the three dots, then Block User. In a game, use the player list or the Esc menu, People tab.

Does the blocked player get notified?

No, Roblox states they are not notified.

How do I report someone in voice chat?

Use Report Abuse and specify Voice Chat: the report goes to Roblox's moderators.

Can a parent block their child's friend?

Yes, from Settings, Parental Controls, the Friends section, Manage button, then Block.
