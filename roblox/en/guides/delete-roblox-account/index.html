How to delete a Roblox account

The short answer

In Roblox's privacy settings, choose “Delete” under “Account data, deactivation & deletion”. A verified email is needed, and Roblox checks your identity.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Verify the email. Your account must have a verified email address.
  2. Open privacy. While logged in, open settings, then the privacy page.
  3. Find the section. Look for “Account data, deactivation & deletion”.
  4. Choose Delete. Select Delete and follow the instructions. Roblox verifies your identity before processing the request.

Deactivate or delete: the difference

Roblox offers two options. Deactivation locks your account and hides your profile, but some information stays visible, such as items you put on sale on the Marketplace. You can reactivate it whenever you want by logging back in at Roblox.com. Deletion erases the personal information Roblox collects or keeps about you, and it requires deleting the account. In both cases you go through the “Account data, deactivation & deletion” section of your privacy settings.

The conditions

  • The account needs a verified email address.
  • Roblox takes steps to verify your identity before processing the deletion, to protect your privacy.
  • If there is a problem, Roblox gives you additional information.

The processing time is not stated on the help page we checked: so we give no duration.

Cannot log in?

If you no longer have access to the account or need help, use Roblox support's contact form. First try a password reset.

Before you delete

  • Your Robux, items, subscriptions and game access are tied to the account. Roblox does not refund Robux already spent or gift cards: see spending limits and refunds.
  • Cancel your subscriptions to avoid another charge: see Premium and Plus.
  • If you only want to change your name, there is no need to delete: see change your username.
  • For a child, it is better to discuss it with them, and a parent can also limit use with parental controls before deleting.

Why delete rather than deactivate?

Deactivating suits a break: nothing is erased. Deleting aims to erase your data. To understand your rights over personal data, check Roblox's privacy policy and the data protection rules that apply where you live.

Frequently asked questions

How do I permanently delete my Roblox account?

In privacy settings, under “Account data, deactivation & deletion”, choose Delete. A verified email is required.

What is the difference between deactivating and deleting?

Deactivation hides the profile and can be undone by logging back in. Deletion erases the personal information collected.

Can I delete an account without a verified email?

Roblox says a verified email address is required. Otherwise, contact support.

How long does deletion take?

Roblox's official help page does not give a specific time.
