“Free Robux” offers: how to spot the scam

The short answer

Roblox says it in black and white: every offer of free Robux is a scam, including generators, tricks and codes. Their goal is to steal your password.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Do not click. Leave the page, video or game that promises free Robux. Do not download anything.
  2. Never give your login details. No page needs your password, cookies or security codes.
  3. Report it. Use the Report Abuse button in the game, on the profile or on the message.
  4. Check your account. If you typed anything in, change your password right away and turn on two-step verification.

What Roblox says

Roblox's official help page about generators is unambiguous: any offer of free Robux, subscriptions or valuable items is a scam. It exists to get you to hand over your password or personal information, or to click a dangerous link. The goal is to take your account, your Robux and your items. Roblox also states that there is “no trick, code or generator” of this kind.

Warning signs of a scam

  • You are asked for your password, a two-step verification code or “just” your username.
  • A site, video or game promises a precise number of Robux, fast and effortlessly.
  • A stranger offers a “trade” or a “trick” that needs a link or a file.
  • You are rushed: “offer valid for 10 minutes”, “only 3 spots left”.
  • The site looks like Roblox but the address is not roblox.com.

The real ways to get Robux

There are two: buy them (see Robux prices) or earn them by creating games and items (see how to get Robux legitimately). Roblox also issues official codes during events: read where to find real codes.

The fake purchase trap

Roblox also warns that making false claims about purchases to get Robux or subscriptions for free breaks its rules and can lead to your account being deleted.

If you already clicked or gave information

Act fast. Change your password, turn on two-step verification, and follow our guide on a hacked Roblox account if you can no longer log in. Our page on securing your account covers good habits for the future.

Good habits

  • Only enter your password on Roblox's real login page.
  • Do not click links to sites you do not know.
  • Ask a parent or an adult before any purchase.
  • Roblox's official promotions are announced on its blog, in the Notifications section or in a banner on the site.

The site you are reading does not give out Robux and does not promise any: it explains the rules and offers calculators.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Robux generator?

No. Roblox states that a Robux generator does not exist: any site, video or game that advertises one is a scam.

Can a code give me free Robux?

Not on a permanent basis. Roblox sometimes publishes codes during official events, but no third-party site can create them.

I gave away my password, what do I do?

Change your password right away, turn on two-step verification and contact Roblox support if you cannot get into the account.

Are YouTube videos about free Robux real?

No. Roblox states that no video can give you free Robux: it is a scam and a violation of its rules.
