How to turn on voice chat on Roblox

The short answer

To talk on Roblox you must be at least 13 and pass an age check (ID verification or facial age estimation). Voice chat is never on by default: you switch it on inside a supported experience, with the headphone icon or the experience settings.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Be 13 or older. Voice chat is only for accounts aged 13 and over with a completed age check.
  2. Complete the age check. Verify your ID or do facial age estimation. Phone verification is no longer valid for voice.
  3. Join a supported experience. Not every game has voice chat: the experience's description page says whether it does.
  4. Turn voice on. Click the headphone icon, or open the experience settings and set Voice Chat to Connected.
  5. Manage your mic. Mute or unmute with the microphone icon at the top left of the screen.

Who can use voice chat?

According to Roblox's help center (checked 11/24/2026), age-checked users aged 13 and over can talk with players of similar age groups in any experience that supports voice. Voice chat is not on by default, and it is not available under 13. To understand age checks, read how old do you have to be to play Roblox.

I can't see the icon: why?

Roblox warns that the feature may not be available in every region, and that verification methods can differ from one account to another. If, after verifying, you see neither the headphone icon nor the setting in the experience, voice may not be open in your region. Another possible cause: the game itself has no voice chat, since creators decide whether to add it.

On console and mobile

Voice chat exists on computer, mobile, in VR, on PlayStation and on Xbox. On PlayStation, you choose Join Voice in the experience settings.

Staying safe while you talk

  • To mute someone, click the speaker icon above their avatar or next to their name. This only lasts for the session and does not affect text chat.
  • A red dot on the microphone means Roblox is recording audio, for safety and moderation purposes.
  • To report bad behavior, use the report feature and choose Voice Chat: see how to report a player.
  • Never say personal information out loud: address, phone number, banking details.

What about a child?

Voice chat with friends is not available under 13. Between 13 and 17 it only works with trusted friends in the same age group. Parents will find the other settings in Roblox parental controls.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn on voice chat on Roblox?

Be 13 or older, complete the age check, then click the headphone icon or turn voice on in the settings of a supported experience.

How do I get voice chat on Roblox?

Voice chat is not on by default: you need an age check (ID or facial age estimation) and a game that offers it.

What age do you need for Roblox voice chat?

At least 13, with a verified age. A phone number alone is no longer enough for verification.

How do I mute my mic in a Roblox game?

Tap the microphone icon at the top left of the screen: it shows a red slash when your mic is muted.
