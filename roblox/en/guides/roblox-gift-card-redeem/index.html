How to redeem a Roblox gift card

The short answer

You redeem a Roblox gift card at roblox.com/redeem, logged in to your account. Depending on the card, you get Roblox credit or Robux.

Updated November 24, 2026

Robux to euro

How to use it

  1. Open the redeem page. In a web browser, go to roblox.com/redeem.
  2. Log in. Log in to the Roblox account that should receive the card.
  3. Enter the code. Type the card's code in the text box (scratch off the coating if needed).
  4. Confirm. Click Redeem. A message confirms that the credit or item was added.

Credit or Robux?

According to Roblox's help center, depending on the type of code you get credit, Robux or a virtual item. Amazon gift cards and “Party Packs” can only be redeemed for Robux, not Roblox credit. Credit is used to buy Robux or, on the website, a subscription such as Roblox Plus. As of Nov 23, 2026, buying Robux with a gift card gives the web bonus (up to 25% more Robux), see Robux prices.

Where to enter the code

Redeeming is generally done from a browser at roblox.com/redeem. It is not available in most mobile apps or on consoles. Roblox mentions an in-app option on Samsung Galaxy and, in the United States, on iOS.

The code is rejected: what to check

Letters and digits can look alike. Roblox suggests trying these swaps: 0 (zero) for O, 1 for I (capital i), 2 for Z, 5 for S, 6 for G or Q, and 8 for B. Also check that you are logged in to the right account. If the problem persists, contact Roblox support with your username, the purchase date and amount, where you bought it, your country, the card's PIN and its serial number.

Cards from other countries, several cards

Roblox says all codes can be used worldwide: the value is converted automatically into your account's currency. You can redeem as many cards as you like, one at a time.

Refunds

Roblox does not refund gift cards. The exact terms are on roblox.com/giftcardterms. Spending limits set by parents do not block gift card redemption: see how to limit spending.

Buying a card safely

  • Choose a well-known retailer, in a store or on its official website.
  • Never pay for a card from a stranger on a messaging app or in a game.
  • Do not share the code on social media: anyone who sees it can use it.
  • Beware of “free cards”: read Free Robux scams.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I redeem a Roblox gift card?

At roblox.com/redeem, in a browser, while logged in to your account.

Can I redeem a gift card on mobile?

In most mobile apps and on consoles, no: you need a browser. Exceptions exist (Samsung Galaxy, iOS in the United States).

Is a Roblox gift card refundable?

No, Roblox does not refund gift cards, according to its help center.

Why doesn't my gift card code work?

Mixing up letters and digits is common (0 and O, 1 and I, 5 and S). Try the swaps, then contact Roblox support.
