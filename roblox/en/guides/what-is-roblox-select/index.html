What are Roblox Kids and Roblox Select?

The short answer

Roblox Kids and Roblox Select can't be switched on by hand: Roblox places under-16s in one of the two based on their verified age. To change, you need an age check, or a parent can correct the age if the child was misplaced.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Find out which type you're in. Without an age check, Roblox uses the birthday entered at sign-up.
  2. Complete the age check. By ID, or by facial age estimation with the camera.
  3. Correct the age if needed. In Settings, Account Info, the pencil next to the birthday.
  4. For a child: go through the parent. A linked parent can change the child's age from Parental Controls (once).

What are they?

According to the help page "What are Roblox Kids and Roblox Select?" (checked 11/24/2026), these are two account types for under-16s, giving access to games that have passed a multistep safety check. Ages vary by region.

AccountIndicative agesContent
Roblox Kids5 to 8Minimal or Mild games
Roblox Select9 to 15Minimal, Mild or Moderate
Roblox16 and overall rated content, except Restricted

Why is my account in Kids or Select?

Without an age check, Roblox goes by the age entered at sign-up: under 9 for Kids, 9 and over for Select, with chat off in both cases. To access chat or games outside these catalogs, you need an age check. See also how old do you have to be to play Roblox.

How to move out of them

  • Complete the age check: official ID or a quick facial age estimation (images and video are deleted after processing, according to Roblox).
  • Fix a wrong birthday: in Account Info you can correct your birthday, but Roblox may ask for an ID or a parent's consent.
  • Linked parent: they can correct the child's age once, unless the child has already verified their age with an ID.

Lying about your age is not a solution: it removes protections meant for younger players. Parents should read Roblox parental controls and how to remove parental controls.

Chat that doesn't work

If chat stays closed, it is often for lack of an age check. For voice, see how to turn on voice chat.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove Roblox Kids?

You can't remove it by hand: you need an age check, or a correction of the age if the child was misplaced.

How do I remove Roblox Select?

Same principle: the type depends on the verified age. At 16, the account becomes standard Roblox.

How do I get Roblox Kids?

Roblox places children there based on their age, generally 5 to 8 depending on the region.

Can a parent correct their child's age?

Yes, once, from the linked account, unless the age was already verified with an ID.
