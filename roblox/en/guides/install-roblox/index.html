How to install Roblox on PC, mobile or console

The short answer

On a computer, log in on the Roblox website, open a game and click the green Play button: Roblox Player installs. On mobile or PlayStation, you install it from the device's app store.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Go to the Roblox website. Use a recent browser (Edge, Firefox or Chrome) and log in.
  2. Open a game. Pick any experience.
  3. Click Play. A window says Roblox Player is installing.
  4. Launch the player. If a window asks which program to use, select Roblox and confirm.
  5. Play again later. Once the player is installed, the Play button opens the game directly.

On Windows PC and Mac

As of 11/24/2026, the help page "How to Install and Play Roblox" describes exactly these steps. On requirements, Roblox installs on 64-bit versions of Windows 10 and 11, and on macOS 10.13 (High Sierra) or newer. Roblox states that Linux is not supported. For recommended hardware, read the system requirements to play Roblox.

On phones and tablets

The official pages say you need at least iOS 14 or iPadOS 14 on iPhone and iPad, and Android 8.0 or higher on Android (with OpenGL ES 3.0). Roblox recommends an internet connection of at least 4 to 8 Mb/s, and Wi-Fi rather than 3G or 4G if the connection is unstable. Install the app from your device's store, then log in.

On PlayStation

According to the PlayStation page, the Roblox app exists on PS4 and PS5 in select countries. You find it on the PlayStation Store, add it to your library, then log in with Quick Login from a computer or phone. The app is rated "T for Teen", recommended for ages 13 and up. PlayStation players play with those on Xbox, iOS, Android, desktop and Meta Quest.

On Switch?

The Switch does not appear on these pages: see Roblox on Nintendo Switch.

Installing Roblox Studio

To make games, open one of your games on the website, click at the top right then Edit: Roblox Studio installs, then opens. Our guide how to make a Roblox game takes over from there.

If the installation fails

Roblox publishes pages about common installation errors. First check that your device is compatible, then your connection, and that the app is up to date.

Sources

Roblox help pages: How to Install and Play Roblox, Computer Hardware & Operating System Requirements, Roblox Mobile System Requirements, PlayStation FAQ (checked 11/24/2026).

Frequently asked questions

How do I install Roblox on PC?

Log in on the Roblox website, open a game and click the green Play button: Roblox Player installs.

How do I install Roblox on PS5?

Search for Roblox on the PlayStation Store, add it to your library, then log in with Quick Login. Available in select countries.

How do I install Roblox on a phone?

Through the device's app store. You need iOS 14 or higher on iPhone, Android 8.0 or higher on Android.

How do I download Roblox on Mac?

Same method as on PC, with macOS 10.13 or newer.
