How to make a Roblox game pass

The short answer

A game pass is created in the Creator Hub, under Monetization then Passes, once the game is published. It is bought once with Robux, and the creator receives 70% of the Robux spent, per the official documentation.

Updated November 24, 2026

Roblox tax calculator

How to use it

  1. Publish your game. A pass is created for an already published game. See our guide if needed.
  2. Open the passes page. In Creations, select your game, then go to Monetization > Passes.
  3. Create the pass. Click Create pass. Upload an icon (512 x 512 pixels at most, .jpg, .png or .bmp, with important details inside the circle).
  4. Name and describe. Enter a clear name and description, then choose a category: it helps organize your in-game shop.
  5. Confirm and put it on sale. Click Create pass. In the game, the sale goes through MarketplaceService (for example PromptGamePassPurchase()). Outside the game, the pass must be marked “Listed” in Monetization > Shop.

What a game pass is

A pass gives a permanent perk: access to an area, an exclusive item, a username color. The documentation states that it can only be bought with Robux and only once per player. For a repeatable purchase (in-game currency, for example), Roblox offers “developer products”, which generally earn more.

How much does the creator earn?

As of Nov 23, 2026, the “How do I make money?” page states, for in-experience purchases: “you receive 70% of the Robux spent”, with the remaining 30% being Roblox's marketplace fee. To estimate what a price represents, use our sales earnings calculator. This rule applies to in-experience purchases; selling avatar items on the Marketplace follows other rules, explained in our guide on clothing and UGC items.

How to choose the price

The official guide sets no general minimum or maximum price. One reference point: for a pass to be featured on the Robux purchase page, it must cost more than 49 Robux and less than 801 Robux. For the rest, some common-sense tips:

  • offer a clear perk, without hurting players who do not pay;
  • start with a modest price, you can change it later;
  • describe exactly what the pass contains.

Points of attention

  • From May 30, 2026, cross-experience pass sales are disabled, according to the documentation.
  • Never promise earnings: many games sell almost nothing at first.
  • Players are often minors: avoid pressure to buy. Parents will find our tips to limit spending.

To sell, you first need a public game: see how to publish a game then how to promote it. And to convert your earned Robux into cash, read our page on DevEx.

Frequently asked questions

How many Robux does a creator get from a game pass?

According to the official documentation checked on Nov 23, 2026, the creator receives 70% of the Robux spent in their game, with Roblox keeping 30%.

What price should a Roblox pass be?

The official guide imposes no price. To appear on the Robux purchase page, a pass on sale must cost more than 49 and less than 801 Robux.

Can a player buy the same pass several times?

No, a pass can only be bought once. For repeatable purchases, Roblox offers developer products.

Do I need a published game to make a pass?

Yes, the game must already be published to create a pass in the Creator Hub.
