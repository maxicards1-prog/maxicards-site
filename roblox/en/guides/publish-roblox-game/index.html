How to publish a Roblox game and make it public

The short answer

In Roblox Studio, open File then Publish, fill in the name, icon and description, then answer the maturity questionnaire to make the game public. By default, only you (and people you allow) can play.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Publish the game from Studio. Go to File > Publish. Fill in the icon, thumbnail, name, genre and description, then click Save. Roblox then creates your experience.
  2. Keep it private to test. At first, only you, people with Edit access and allowed group members can get in. Use this time to test with a few friends.
  3. Start going public. In File > Publish, click Continue (or Invite to invite testers first).
  4. Answer the questionnaire. The first question asks whether the game contains content meant for older ages. Answer honestly: the game then receives a content label.
  5. Set the audience. In the Creator Dashboard, open your game, then Publish settings > Manage Audience > Manage access. The Playtest tab limits it to invited testers; the Public tab lets you restrict it to friends.

The maturity questionnaire: what it changes

As of Nov 23, 2026, the official documentation explains two cases:

  • You answer “No” to content for older ages: the game gets the “Content May Vary” rating and is only available to users 16 and up verified by age estimation.
  • You answer “Yes”: you must complete the whole questionnaire. The game then gets a matching rating, available to users 16 and up (age estimation) or 18 and up (age verification).

To reach all ages (Roblox Kids and Select), you must complete the entire questionnaire and get a Minimal, Mild or Moderate (9+) rating. This also requires extra conditions: a verified account, two-step verification, a Roblox Plus or Premium subscription for two consecutive months (or a one-time refundable fee), and passing a “Kids and Select” review. The official page mentions a publishing fee of 1,000 Robux (or 50,000 Robux for an expedited review). Check these conditions on the official page before you start, as they change.

Limits to know

  • You can publish at most 5 never-before-published experiences per day.
  • To publish for ages 16 and up, the account must be in good standing, at least 2 days old, and pass an age check (facial estimation or ID).
  • Users under 9 cannot invite testers.

Before making the game public

Play it yourself from start to finish, on computer and on phone if possible. Check that the name, icon and description really match the game. Then think about your audience: if you are aiming at younger players, re-read the age rules in our page What age is Roblox for?.

Is the game online? Now you need to promote it or make a game pass. If you do not have a game yet, start by making a Roblox game.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make my Roblox game public?

In Roblox Studio, File > Publish then Continue, and answer the maturity questionnaire. Access can then be set in the Creator Dashboard under Manage Audience.

My Roblox game is published, why can't anyone play it?

By default, a published game is limited to you, people with Edit access and allowed group members. You need to make it public and check the Manage access tab.

How many games can I publish per day?

The official documentation lists a limit of 5 never-before-published experiences per day, as of Nov 23, 2026.

What is the “Content May Vary” label?

It is the label of a game published without completing the whole questionnaire. According to Roblox help, it is available from age 16 in most regions and may contain minimal, mild or moderate content.
