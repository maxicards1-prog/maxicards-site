What age is Roblox for?

The short answer

Roblox asks that you be at least 5 to have an account. The games and chat you can access then depend on your verified age: Kids (5-8), Select (9-15), then Roblox (16 and up).

Updated November 24, 2026

The minimum age

In the summary of its terms for the European Economic Area, Roblox writes that you must be at least 5 to have an account. Under 18, some features are unavailable, and a parent or guardian must permit use. These ages can vary by country.

Three types of accounts

AgeAccount typeContent
5 to 8Roblox KidsMinimal or Mild games
9 to 15Roblox SelectMinimal, Mild or Moderate
16 and upRobloxEverything except Restricted (18+ only)

All Kids and Select games have passed a multi-step safety check. A linked parent can allow a specific game.

Content labels

  • Minimal: occasional mild violence, light unrealistic blood, mild fear.
  • Mild: repeated mild violence, light crude humor.
  • Moderate: moderate violence, light realistic blood, moderate crude humor, moderate fear.
  • Content May Vary: the creator did not finish the questionnaire; limited to age-verified users 16 and up in most regions.
  • 16+: sensitive social, political or religious topics and places to meet without private spaces.
  • Restricted (18+): strong violence, realistic blood, strong language and more, for verified adults.

Roblox also adopts the international IARC framework. We do not give equivalents in other rating systems such as ESRB or PEGI, for lack of an official source.

Age checks

They are used to access chat, 16+ and 18+ content, games outside the Kids and Select catalogs, and some creation features. They work by facial age estimation (images are deleted after processing) or by ID, available from age 13. Without a check, Roblox uses the age entered at sign-up: under 9 it is Kids; from 9 it is Select, in both cases with chat off. If the estimated age is wrong, a linked parent can correct the birth date once, or you can verify with an ID.

And chat?

For under-9s, chat needs a parent's agreement. From 9, after the check, you can chat with players in the same age range or with trusted friends, with parental permission up to age 12.

What to do as a parent

Set the maturity level, chat and spending: see Roblox parental controls. For game ideas, see Roblox games for younger kids and horror games and their labels.

Frequently asked questions

What age can you have a Roblox account?

Roblox says you must be at least 5. Rules may vary by country.

Is Roblox allowed for kids under 13?

Yes, with suitable accounts (Kids for ages 5 to 8, Select for ages 9 to 12) and parental controls. ID verification is only open from age 13.

What does Content May Vary mean?

The creator did not finish the maturity questionnaire. These games are limited to verified users 16 and up, in most regions.

How does Roblox check age?

By camera-based facial age estimation or by ID. The estimation images are deleted after processing.
