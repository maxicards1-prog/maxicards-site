What are Roblox's system requirements?

The short answer

Roblox runs on most recent computers, on iPhone, iPad, Android phones and some consoles. The requirements of Roblox Studio, the creation tool, are a little higher than those of the game.

Updated November 24, 2026

On a computer (Windows and Mac)

According to the official help article on computers, checked Nov 23, 2026, the minimums are modest: Windows 10 or 11 in 64-bit, or a Mac running macOS 10.13 (High Sierra) or later, a recent processor of at least 1.6 GHz, 1 GB of RAM, DirectX 10 for Windows and about 300 MB of free space. These are minimums to launch the game: some very heavy experiences run better with more memory and a real graphics card. If the game stutters, close other programs and lower the graphics quality in the options.

On phone and tablet

  • iPhone and iPad: iOS 14 or iPadOS 14 or later. The official list notably includes the iPhone 6s and the models after it.
  • Android: Android 8.0 or later, with OpenGL ES 3.0 support. Devices with a Tegra 2 chip or a processor older than ARMv7 are blocked.
  • Widgets: they need iOS 16 or iPadOS 17 or Android 13 or later.

On console and headset

  • PlayStation: the help center says Roblox is available on PlayStation 4 or 5 in some countries.
  • Xbox: the Xbox help pages are old and mention an Xbox One and an online Xbox account; they also state that the game is offered to ages 10 and up on Xbox. Check your console's store.
  • Meta Quest: the help center asks for software version v57 or later.

To create with Roblox Studio

Studio is free and runs on Windows and Mac. The official documentation lists a minimum of Windows 10 or macOS 10.14 with 3 GB of RAM, and recommends Windows 11 or macOS 14 and later, 8 GB of RAM and a screen of at least 1600 x 900. To go further, read our guide to making a Roblox game.

If Roblox will not launch

  • Check that your system is up to date.
  • Update Roblox; on mobile, go through the app store.
  • Check your connection: an unstable connection causes disconnections.
  • For any purchase or account setting, stick to the official sites: see how to secure your account.

Requirements change with Roblox updates: if in doubt, the official help center prevails. For parents, our page on parental controls explains the settings to make before handing a device to a child, and the page on minimum age explains the access rules.

Official sources: computers, mobile and Roblox Studio.

Frequently asked questions

Is Roblox free?

Playing Roblox is free. Purchases with Robux are offered in games, but they are not required.

What version of Android does Roblox need?

According to the official help center, Android 8.0 or later, with OpenGL ES 3.0 support.

Can I play Roblox on PlayStation?

The Roblox help center says the app is available on PlayStation 4 or 5 in some countries.

What PC do I need for Roblox Studio?

The documentation lists a minimum of Windows 10 or macOS 10.14 with 3 GB of RAM, and recommends Windows 11 or macOS 14 and 8 GB of RAM.
