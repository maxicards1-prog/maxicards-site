Roblox forgot password: how to recover it

The short answer

On the login page, click “Forgot Password or Username?”, then get a link by email or a code by text message to choose a new password.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Open recovery. On Roblox's login page, click “Forgot Password or Username?”.
  2. Enter the email. Enter the email address linked to the account, exactly as it is saved, then click Submit.
  3. Open the email. Click the Reset Password button in the message you receive. Check your spam folder.
  4. Choose the account. If several accounts use this address, select the one to recover.
  5. Create the password. Type the new password twice, then confirm.

By email: common pitfalls

Roblox sends a message to the address currently linked to the account. If nothing arrives after a few minutes, look in your spam folder. Type the address as it is saved: if the account uses “examplename@domain.com” without a dot, typing “example.name@domain.com” will not work. Create a brand-new password: do not reuse the old one or one from another account.

By phone number

If the account has no email but has a verified phone number, open the Phone tab on the recovery page, choose the country code, enter the number and click Continue. Roblox then sends a 6-digit code: enter it and click Verify, then create the new password.

With no email or phone

With no recovery method, you have to use Roblox support's contact form. That is why Roblox strongly recommends adding an email address to your account. For a child, the simplest option is to use a parent's address, with their agreement.

Forgot your username?

The same recovery page, “Forgot Password or Username?”, also lets you find your username.

After the reset

  • Turn on two-step verification: how to secure your account.
  • If you did not ask for a reset and get unexpected messages, someone may be trying to get into your account: read hacked Roblox account.
  • Only follow the link in the official message: beware of emails that ask for your password.

Good habits

A password manager, or a notebook kept safe by an adult, prevents lockouts. Never write your password in a message or on a “rewards” site: see Free Robux scams.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my Roblox password?

Click “Forgot Password or Username?” on the login page, enter your email or phone, then follow the link or code you receive.

Why am I not getting the reset email?

Check your spam folder and that the address you typed is exactly the one on the account, including any dots.

Can I recover my Roblox account without an email?

By phone number, if one is saved. Otherwise, contact Roblox support.

How do I find my Roblox username?

Use the same “Forgot Password or Username?” link on the login page.
