How to make and sell Roblox clothes or UGC items

The short answer

To sell clothing on Roblox you need an ID-verified account and a Roblox Plus or Premium 1000 or 2200 subscription, then pay an 80 Robux fee per upload. The creator's share ranges from 30% to 70% depending on the price.

Updated November 24, 2026

Roblox tax calculator

How to use it

  1. Check the requirements. As of Nov 23, 2026, the official Marketplace FAQ says creators must be ID-verified and have Roblox Plus or Roblox Premium 1000 or 2200.
  2. Prepare the image. For a shirt or pants, use the official template: each area has its own size (for example 128 x 128 pixels for the front and back). A T-shirt is a square image (the official example is 512 x 512).
  3. Upload. In the Creator Dashboard, open Avatar Items then the Classics tab, drop the image or click Upload Asset.
  4. Fill in the item. Choose the type (T-Shirt, Shirt or Pants), enter a name and description, then click Upload. An 80 Robux fee applies to each upload.
  5. Set the price and put it on sale. On the item's Configure page, set the price (Set a price) and turn on On Sale so players can buy it.

How much does a sale earn?

The official page on fees and commissions says creators earn at least 30% of an item's gross Marketplace sales. This share is progressive: it rises with the price relative to the floor price for the item type.

Price relative to the floorCreator's share
1 times30%
2 times50%
3 times62%
6 times and more70%

The page states that this system only applies to purchases made on the Marketplace, not to purchases made inside an experience. Each purchase is also subject to a 30-day hold before being credited. Our earnings calculator helps estimate what that means.

The fees

  • 80 Robux per upload for a standard upload, which is generally not refunded if moderation rejects the item;
  • 500 Robux for an item with an emissive mask and 200 Robux for a background;
  • a refundable “publishing advance”, paid when putting the item on sale (the fees page lists 10 Robux for non-limited T-shirts, shirts and pants; check the amount when you publish).

Classic clothing or 3D UGC item?

Classic clothing is an image applied to the avatar's body. A 3D UGC item (an accessory, for example) is modeled in software like Blender and needs 3D skills. The Creator Hub offers an official tutorial, “Create your first avatar item”. To start, classic clothing is simpler.

Rules to follow

  • Follow Roblox's community rules and Marketplace policy; copied or inappropriate items are rejected.
  • ID verification concerns the account holder: if you are a minor, talk to your parents first.
  • Do not promise any earnings: sales are never guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to publish Roblox clothing?

As of Nov 23, 2026, a standard upload costs 80 Robux per upload, per the official fees page. A refundable publishing advance is added when you put it on sale.

Do I need Roblox Premium to sell clothes?

According to the official Marketplace FAQ, you must be ID-verified and have Roblox Plus or Roblox Premium 1000 or 2200.

How much does a clothing creator earn?

At least 30% of gross sales, up to 70% depending on the price relative to the floor price. Earnings go through a 30-day hold.

What image size for a Roblox T-shirt?

A T-shirt is a square image; the official documentation's example is 512 x 512 pixels.
