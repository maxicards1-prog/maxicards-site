How much do Robux cost?

The short answer

Roblox sells Robux in nine packs, and the price per Robux drops a little on bigger packs. On Roblox's European page on Nov 23, 2026, 1,000 Robux cost €11.99. Prices show in your own currency, so check the page before you pay.

Why the same pack gives more Robux on the website

Roblox's help center says that buying Robux on the web, on desktop and with a gift card gives “up to 25% more Robux”. According to that page, this is not a limited-time promotion. Purchases made inside a mobile app or on a console can therefore give fewer Robux for the same money, so compare the Robux amount before you pay.

Gift cards and credit

A gift card is paid for in a store, then redeemed on the website. Roblox says gift cards, even ones bought before November 2024, get the same bonus. Details are in our guide to redeeming a Roblox gift card.

What about Roblox Plus?

Since April 30, 2026, Roblox Plus replaces Roblox Premium for new subscribers. Roblox's newsroom lists it at $4.99 USD per month (checked Nov 24, 2026). Bundles that add monthly Robux also exist (Plus 500, Plus 1000, Plus 2000). We did not find their prices on an official page we could read, so we do not quote them. Everything is explained in our guide to Roblox Premium and Plus.

How to buy without getting scammed

  • Only buy Robux on Roblox's official website or app, or with a gift card from a reputable retailer.
  • Always get permission from the owner of the payment method. Parents can set a limit: see how to limit spending.
  • No site gives away free Robux: read why free Robux offers are scams.

Frequently asked questions

How much is 1000 Robux?

On Roblox's European page on Nov 23, 2026, the 1,000 Robux pack was €11.99 (website price). The price in your currency may differ and can change, so check the official page.

Why do I get fewer Robux on mobile?

Roblox says that buying on the web, on desktop and with a gift card gives up to 25% more Robux. Compare the Robux amount shown before you pay.

Do Robux expire?

Roblox's purchase page has a section called “Do Robux expire?”. Read it on the official site, because we do not quote a duration without a source.

What is the cheapest Robux pack?

On Nov 23, 2026, the smallest pack was 270 Robux for €3.49. Bigger packs give a better price per Robux.
