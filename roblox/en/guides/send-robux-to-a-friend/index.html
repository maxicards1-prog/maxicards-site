How to send Robux to a friend

The short answer

As of 11/24/2026, you must subscribe to Roblox Plus to send Robux: you do it from the recipient's profile or the Buy Robux page. A transfer is final, limited per day and per month, and users under 16 need a parent's approval.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Subscribe to Roblox Plus. Only the sender must be subscribed; the recipient does not need to be.
  2. Meet the age rules. From 16, an age check is enough. Under 16, a linked parent account must approve every transfer.
  3. Start the transfer. From the recipient's profile or the Buy Robux page.
  4. Enter the amount. Type the number of Robux, then click Continue.
  5. Review and send. Check the details, then click Send. The recipient then accepts the transfer from their notification.

The official requirements

According to the help page "Sending and Receiving Robux" (checked 11/24/2026), sending is instant and free for Roblox Plus subscribers. To see what that subscription costs, read Roblox Premium and Plus. Before sending or receiving, users aged 16 and over must have completed an age check; users under 16 must have a linked parent account, which approves every transfer. In some regions, a parent's approval is required up to age 18.

The limits

Roblox states caps of 500 Robux a day and 1,000 Robux a month, which may change. With 2-Step Verification on and an account in good standing, they rise to 5,000 Robux a day and 10,000 a month. See secure your account to turn it on.

Can a transfer be undone?

No. Roblox states that all transfers are final: once sent, they cannot be cancelled or reversed. A transfer goes back to the sender only if the recipient doesn't accept it, the parent declines it, or no parent account is linked within 7 days.

Watch out for scams

  • Roblox will never ask you to send Robux to someone to verify an account or for any other reason.
  • Only send Robux to people you truly know.
  • Anyone promising to multiply your Robux is a scammer: see "free Robux": the scam.

To give something else, a Roblox gift card is another route, and Robux prices help you choose an amount.

Frequently asked questions

How do I receive Robux from a friend?

The recipient gets a notification of a pending transfer, then selects Accept. The Robux reach their balance right away.

Can you send Robux without Roblox Plus?

No: the sender must be subscribed to Roblox Plus. The recipient does not need to be.

What is the Robux sending limit?

500 Robux a day and 1,000 a month, or 5,000 and 10,000 with 2-Step Verification (as of 11/24/2026, subject to change).

Can you cancel a Robux transfer?

No, all transfers are final once sent.
