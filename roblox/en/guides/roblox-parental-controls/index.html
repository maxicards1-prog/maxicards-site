Roblox parental controls: how to set them up

The short answer

Link your Roblox account to your child's, then open Settings, Parental controls to manage allowed games, chat, screen time and spending.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Create or use your account. You need a Roblox account, verified as an adult by facial age estimation, ID or credit card.
  2. Link the child's account. In the child's settings, open Parental Controls, choose Add parent and follow the steps, or accept the request email.
  3. Open the settings. On your account: Settings (gear), Parental controls, then choose your child's account.
  4. Pick the settings. Scroll to “Settings you manage”: content, chat, spending, screen time, friends.

Two ways to manage

Roblox offers two options. The first is a linked parent account, which lets you manage settings from your own device and receive emails about activity. The second is Parent mode on the child's device, with no personal account, when they ask for permission (“Ask in Person”). In this mode, you create a 6-digit PIN to approve later requests faster, but settings that send real-time alerts, such as spending notifications, need a linked account. Roblox notes that Parent mode is rolling out gradually.

What you can set

  • Content: maturity level of games (Minimal, Mild, Moderate, Restricted), blocked or allowed experiences, sensitive topics, preview games.
  • Chat: who the child can chat or party with.
  • Spending: monthly limit, notifications, Robux transfers. See how to limit spending.
  • Screen time: daily limit and most played games.
  • Friends and privacy: trusted friends, blocking, activity visibility.

Settings depend on age

Roblox gives more independence as kids grow. From age 13, the child manages their own screen time, blocking and privacy, and spending limits no longer apply (you can still receive notifications). From 16, the “Settings you manage” section is limited to notifications, spending alerts and activity updates. For content levels and age, read what age is Roblox for?.

The child's requests

The child can ask you to allow a game or change a setting. You get an email and can answer “Allow” or “Don't Allow”, from the email, from Parental controls (Pending requests) or on the device.

Who can create a parent account?

In most regions, a parent of a child under 18, verified as an adult, can create and link a parent account. The account's email address must match the one on the email you received.

Beyond the settings

Talk with your child about what they do and protect the account: how to secure your account. To pick suitable games, see Roblox games for younger kids. Roblox's own help center, at en.help.roblox.com, has the official guidance for parents.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn on parental controls on Roblox?

Link your Roblox account to your child's, then go to Settings, Parental controls and choose their account.

Can I use parental controls without a Roblox account?

Yes, with Parent mode on the child's device, but that mode is not yet available to everyone and does not handle real-time alerts.

How do I block chat for my child?

In Parental controls, choose the child's account and open the chat settings. For under-9s, chat is off by default.

At what age does the child manage their own settings?

From 13, some settings move to the child: screen time, blocking, privacy. Spending limits no longer apply.

Does my parent account have to be verified?

Yes: you must verify that you are an adult by facial age estimation, ID or credit card.
