How to change the language on Roblox

The short answer

Open your account settings, go to the Account Info tab, and change the language in the Personal section. If your language isn't listed, it is not supported yet.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Log in. If you can't, start by recovering your password.
  2. Open settings. On the website: the gear icon at the top right. On mobile: the three dots, More.
  3. Account Info. Open the Account Info tab.
  4. Pick the language. In the Personal section, open the language drop-down and select it.
  5. Save. The page reloads. If not, click Save at the bottom right of the Personal section.

What Roblox specifies

The help page "Changing Your Account Language" (checked 11/24/2026) recommends always checking that your account's language is correct. It states that, if the language you want is not in the list, it is not currently supported; you then need to check back later to see if it has been added.

The same steps, one by one

  1. Log in. If you have trouble, see forgotten password.
  2. Go to your account settings: the gear at the top right on the website, the three dots in the mobile app.
  3. Choose the Account Info tab.
  4. In the Personal section, the current language is shown: click the arrow to open the list.
  5. Select the language: the page reloads in that language.

What about one game's language?

This setting changes the account interface. Some games show their text in the account's language, others in the language chosen by their creator: it depends on the game. An automatic translation option also exists in account settings, according to the help page "Enabling Automatic Translations in Account Settings".

On console

Roblox has separate help pages for changing the language on Xbox and PlayStation: search for "How to Change Language" in its help center.

Careful

Don't look for a "mod" or a file to translate the game: they are often traps to steal logins. If you have doubts about your account's security, read how to secure your Roblox account. If your goal is to pick a suitable name, see how to change your username.

Sources

Frequently asked questions

How do I change a Roblox game's language?

Change the account language in Settings, Account Info, Personal. A game's text also depends on its creators.

Why doesn't my language appear in the list?

Roblox states that in this case, the language is not supported yet.

Where do I change the language on mobile?

In the three dots More, then Settings, Account Info, the Personal section.

The page doesn't update, what should I do?

Click the Save button at the bottom right of the Personal section.
