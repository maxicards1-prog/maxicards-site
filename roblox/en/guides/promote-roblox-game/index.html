How to promote your Roblox game

The short answer

To promote a Roblox game, polish the icon and thumbnails, ship regular updates, show it in videos, and if you want to pay, use the official Ads Manager tool. No method is guaranteed.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Polish the storefront. A clear name, a readable icon, 16:9 thumbnails and a description that explains the game in two sentences make people want to click.
  2. Update regularly. A small addition every week or two (an area, an item, a mode) gives a reason to come back. Announce it in the description.
  3. Make a video. A short gameplay clip on YouTube or another platform shows the game in action. Be honest about what is in it.
  4. Try the official advertising. In Ads Manager, create an ad account, add a payment method then a campaign. Start small.
  5. Read the feedback. Look at reviews and comments, fix the reported flaws, then repeat.

Official advertising: Ads Manager

Roblox offers an official tool to sponsor a game. Campaigns show your thumbnails on the home page and in search results. As of Nov 23, 2026, the documentation describes three goals:

  • Plays: target the players most likely to start a session;
  • Earnings (Limited): target those who spend Robux;
  • Qualify for Kids and Select: reach the threshold of engaged players needed for Roblox Kids and Select.

You can attach up to 10 16:9 thumbnails per campaign and choose an audience (all players, new players, and others depending on thresholds). Moderation generally takes up to 24 hours.

Requirements and budget

  • Access to Ads Manager requires being at least 13 and having a verified email.
  • Paying by credit card requires being at least 18.
  • Converting Robux into ad credits requires being at least 13.
  • The page lists no minimum campaign budget, but you must buy at least 1 ad credit. For a first card, a $5 charge is made and then deducted from the first invoice.

If you are a minor, ask your parents' permission before spending any money. An ad does not guarantee that players stay: without a fun game, it is useless.

Free ways

  • The thumbnail and icon: this is what the player sees first.
  • Updates: a game that evolves is recommended more often by its players.
  • Videos: talking about your game on YouTube helps, as long as you do not promise false rewards.
  • Friends: your first players give your first reviews.

What to avoid

Do not offer “free Robux” in exchange for visits: it is against the rules and a breeding ground for scams, as our page on free Robux explains. If you want to earn money legitimately, read how to get Robux.

Before promoting a game, make sure it is properly published and think about the game pass if you want to monetize it. For inspiration, look at the best Roblox games.

Official source: Ads Manager.

Frequently asked questions

How do I advertise my Roblox game?

With Ads Manager, the official Creator Hub tool: an ad account, a payment method, then a campaign with a goal, audience, budget and thumbnails.

Do I need to be 18 to advertise on Roblox?

Access to Ads Manager requires being at least 13, but paying by credit card requires being 18, according to the official documentation.

What is the minimum budget for a Roblox ad?

The official page lists no minimum campaign budget, but you must buy at least 1 ad credit.

Can I promote my game for free?

Yes: a polished thumbnail, regular updates, honest videos and sharing with friends cost nothing.
