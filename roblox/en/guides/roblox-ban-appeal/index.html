How to appeal a Roblox ban

The short answer

There is no trick to "get back" a banned account: you can only appeal to Roblox's Appeals team, within 30 days. The appeal link is in the moderation notice you received, and an appeal never guarantees the ban will be lifted.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Re-read the moderation notice. Roblox sent you a notification: it says whether you appeal through Violations & Appeals or through the support form.
  2. Open Violations & Appeals. If your case qualifies, the notice has a direct link. Choose Appeal and explain why the decision deserves a second look.
  3. Otherwise, use the support form. Give your account username and a short statement. Write from the email address linked to the account.
  4. Respect the deadline. Send the appeal within 30 days of the action (6 months for users in the European Union).
  5. Wait, without duplicates. A decision can only be reviewed once, and duplicate appeals are not reviewed.

What Roblox's help center says

As of 11/24/2026, the help page "Appeal Your Content or Account Moderation" explains that the Appeals team reviews a decision again, taking into account everything you add, then adjusts your account's status if needed. It states that submitting an appeal does not guarantee the decision will be reversed.

The rules to know

  • The appeal must be sent within 30 days of the action. Users in the European Union have 6 months for an initial action.
  • Once a decision has been reviewed, it cannot be reviewed again. Duplicate appeals for the same action are ignored.
  • Only the account owner can send an appeal, from a valid email address for that account.

This is not the same as a hacked account

A banned account was sanctioned by Roblox; a hacked account was taken over by someone else. If someone took your account, follow what to do about a hacked Roblox account instead. If you only forgot your password, see forgotten password.

Banned from one game only?

Being kicked by a game's creator is not a platform ban. Roblox publishes pages about error codes of this kind (for example 267 and 600, "banned from an experience"): the game's creator decides, not the Appeals team.

Avoiding another ban

  • Read Roblox's community rules before you play again.
  • Protect your account: secure your account.
  • Beware of "free Robux" offers: see why they are scams. Cheat sites can cost you your account.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my banned Roblox account back?

By appealing: through the Violations & Appeals link in your moderation notice, or through the support form, within 30 days.

How can I recover a Roblox account that was banned?

Roblox only offers an appeal. It reviews the decision but does not guarantee it will be lifted.

How do I recover a 1-day banned Roblox account?

A temporary ban ends on the date in your moderation notice: re-read the notification to find that date.

Can you appeal more than once?

No. A decision that has already been reviewed is not reviewed again, and duplicate appeals are ignored.
