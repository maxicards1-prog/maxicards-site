How to remove parental controls on Roblox

The short answer

Only the parent can change a child's settings: from Settings, Parental Controls, pick the child and adjust each setting, or unlink the accounts. A child can't remove them alone, but some settings move to them as they grow up.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Log in with the parent account. The account linked to the child's.
  2. Open Parental Controls. Settings (gear or More), then Parental controls.
  3. Choose the child. Select their account: the page shows their requests, balance and screen time.
  4. Change a setting. Scroll to Settings you manage and change the setting you want.
  5. Or unlink the accounts. Click the pencil at the top right, then Unlink accounts.

Change a setting rather than remove everything

According to the page "Parental Controls Overview" (checked 11/24/2026), parental controls manage content, chat, spending, screen time and friends, and parents can change their settings at any time. Often the simplest thing is to loosen one specific setting (a game, a screen-time limit) rather than remove everything. For the settings one by one, see Roblox parental controls.

Unlinking the parent account

The official FAQ explains the steps: Settings, Parental Controls, the child, the pencil at the top right, Unlink accounts. Careful: once unlinked, the parent can no longer give permission for actions, set privacy controls, set screen-time or spending limits, or see the child's Robux balance.

What changes with age

The settings a parent manages depend on the child's age. From 13, the child manages their own screen-time limit and blocking, and spending limits no longer apply. For 16 and over, the parent is only left with notifications, spending notifications and activity updates.

I'm the child: can I remove it myself?

No, not without the parent's approval: a child can request a change, which the parent accepts or declines. Getting around the controls (a fake age, a second account) risks the account being blocked and losing access to games: talk to your parents instead. To understand the ages, see how old do you have to be to play Roblox.

If the parent account is no longer reachable

Roblox also offers a parent mode on the child's device, with a 6-digit PIN, rolling out gradually. A lost parent account is recovered like any account: forgotten password.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove parental controls on Roblox?

From the parent account: Settings, Parental Controls, the child, then change the settings or choose Unlink accounts.

How do I remove Roblox parental controls without the parent?

It isn't designed that way: the child can request a change, which the parent accepts or declines.

What happens if you unlink the accounts?

The parent loses control of permissions, privacy, screen-time and spending limits, and sight of the balance.

At what age does the child manage their own settings?

From 13, they manage screen time and blocking; spending limits no longer apply.
