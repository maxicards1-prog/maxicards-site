Where to find real Roblox promo codes

The short answer

Real Roblox codes come from official events, gifts or partnerships, never from a list copied on a website. We list no codes: they expire fast and fakes are everywhere.

Updated November 24, 2026

How to use it

  1. Log in. Open roblox.com and log in to the account that should receive the code.
  2. Open the redeem page. Go to roblox.com/redeem.
  3. Enter the code. Type the code exactly as shown.
  4. Confirm. Click Redeem. Depending on the code, you get credit, Robux or a virtual item.

Where do real codes come from?

Roblox's help center explains that a promo code can come from events or contests run by Roblox. Official promotions are announced on Roblox's blog, in the Notifications section of your messages or in a banner at the top of the site. Some toy boxes or gift cards also contain a virtual item code. Outside these sources, be suspicious.

Why we list no codes

Codes often have a very short lifespan and can be reserved for one event. A list of “valid codes” is therefore almost always out of date, or fake. Some sites use these lists to lure you to dangerous pages.

What to do if a code does not work

  • Check that you are logged in to the right account.
  • Go back to the official redeem page and copy the code carefully.
  • Look in your inventory to see whether you already own the exclusive item.
  • Remember that many codes are time-limited: an expired code no longer works.

Fake code scams

A code that asks you to give your password, fill in a survey, download a program or use a “generator” is a scam. Roblox says so: there is no trick, code or generator for free Robux or subscriptions. Read our guide Free Robux scams.

Gift cards and store codes

A code printed on a gift card is redeemed in the same place: see how to redeem a Roblox gift card. And if your goal is to earn Robux, you should know the legitimate methods.

Games have their own codes

Some creators give rewards inside their game through a code typed in the game itself. These codes do not go through Roblox, give no Robux, and should only be looked up on the game's official page or group. To discover good games, see our best Roblox games.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I enter a Roblox promo code?

At roblox.com/redeem, while logged in to your Roblox account.

Where do I find official Roblox codes?

In Roblox's official announcements: its blog, the Notifications section and site banners, as well as certain events.

Can a code give free Robux?

Depending on its type, a code gives credit, Robux or an item. Offers of free Robux in bulk are scams.

Why isn't my promo code working?

It may be expired, already used or mistyped. Also check that you are on the right account.
