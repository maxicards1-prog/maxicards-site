Roblox display name: how it differs from your username

The short answer

Your display name is the name others see in games and on your profile. Unlike the username, it can be changed for free, once every 7 days.

Updated November 24, 2026

The free tool that goes with it:
Username generatorUsername checker

How to use it

  1. Open settings. While logged in, click the gear at the top right of the site, or the three dots (More) in the app.
  2. Account Info. Open the Account Info tab.
  3. Change Display Name. Click the Change Display Name button, a pencil-and-paper icon.
  4. Enter the name. Type the new display name.
  5. Save. Click Save. Refresh the page: the new name appears next to your username.

Username vs display name: the difference

Your username is the unique identifier of your account: nobody else can have the same one, and changing it costs Robux. Your display name is a more personal name, which Roblox shows in experiences and on your profile, and which can be changed for free. In some versions of the app, the display name is simply called “Name”.

Display name rules

  • It can only be changed once every seven days.
  • It must follow the community rules and the Terms of Use, otherwise Roblox can replace it with your username.
  • It is filtered like any text on the platform: an inappropriate name is refused.
  • Your display name and username both remain visible on Roblox and in connected experiences.
  • Roblox is testing support for Japanese characters in beta for some users.

Finding a good name

A display name should stay respectful and contain no personal information: no real name, school or address. For inspiration, use our username generator. The username checker checks the format of a username, not of a display name, and does not say whether a name is free.

When to change the username instead

If the goal is only to show another name to other players, the display name is enough. If you want to change your login identifier, follow the guide to changing your Roblox username.

Security

A display name does not protect the account. Remember to turn on two-step verification: read how to secure your Roblox account.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change my Roblox display name for free?

Yes, it is free, but only once every 7 days.

What is the difference between a username and a display name?

The username is the account's unique identifier and changes for Robux. The display name is a visible, free name that other players can also use.

Why did my display name go back to my username?

Roblox can reset a display name that does not follow the community rules or the Terms of Use.

Where do I change my display name on mobile?

In the app: More (three dots), gear, Account Info, then Change Display Name.
