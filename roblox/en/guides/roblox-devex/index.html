How does Roblox DevEx work?

The short answer

DevEx (Developer Exchange) turns Robux you earned as a creator into real money. As of Nov 23, 2026, you must be at least 13 with 30,000 earned Robux, at a standard rate of $0.0038 per Robux.

Updated November 24, 2026

DevEx calculator

How to use it

  1. Check the requirements. Be 13 or older, have a verified email, at least 30,000 earned Robux, and follow Roblox's rules.
  2. Send the request. In the Creator Hub, submit a DevEx request under your real name (not a username).
  3. Wait for the review. The DevEx team checks your eligibility. If you are accepted, you receive an invitation to the DevEx portal.
  4. Fill in the portal. Enter your tax information (W-9 or W-8 form) and your payout method.
  5. Get paid. Once registration is complete, the payment is made. Allow several business days.

What are the requirements?

According to Roblox's help center and Creator Hub documentation (checked Nov 23, 2026), you must:

  • be at least 13 years old;
  • have at least 30,000 earned Robux;
  • have a verified email address;
  • have a valid account on the DevEx portal (the invitation arrives after your first request is reviewed);
  • provide a tax form (W-9 for US taxpayers, W-8 for others);
  • follow the Terms of Use and the community rules.

Premium is not listed among these requirements in the documentation we read.

Only earned Robux count

Robux from Marketplace sales, purchases in your experience, Creator Rewards, advertising or affiliate programs count. Robux you bought, received through a gift card, got as a monthly subscription allowance or obtained by trading are not eligible. You can estimate your balance on the DevEx page of the Creator Hub: it can lag by up to 72 hours.

The exchange rate

The standard rate is $0.0038 per earned Robux, or about $114 for 30,000 Robux (as of Nov 23, 2026). An older rate of $0.0035 applies to Robux earned before September 5, 2025, until used up. A higher rate exists for some purchases by verified US players aged 18+. Roblox states that rates can change. Use our DevEx Robux calculator to see the result: it was built to convert into euros, and you enter the exchange rate yourself.

Good to know before you apply

  • One completed request per calendar month.
  • A request cannot be canceled once sent.
  • The Robux you request are removed from your account during the review and returned if the request is declined.
  • Processing takes about 10 business days for a first payout and 5 for later ones, not counting bank delays.
  • Roblox cannot guarantee your eligibility in advance, and a request can be refused, for example in case of a scam or a stolen account.

Minors and families

The request must be made under the account holder's real name. A minor should talk to their parents, notably because of the tax forms and the payout account. To understand how to earn these Robux, read how to get Robux legitimately, and for Roblox's commissions, use the sales earnings calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum number of Robux for DevEx?

As of Nov 23, 2026, you need at least 30,000 earned Robux to send a request.

How old do you have to be for DevEx?

You must be 13 or older, according to Roblox's help center.

How much do 30,000 Robux pay out with DevEx?

About $114 at the standard rate of $0.0038 per earned Robux (Nov 23, 2026). The amount in other currencies depends on the exchange rate.

Can I use DevEx with purchased Robux?

No. Only Robux earned as a creator are eligible, not those bought or received with a gift card.

How long does it take to get paid?

Roblox says about 10 business days for a first request and 5 for later ones, not counting bank delays.
