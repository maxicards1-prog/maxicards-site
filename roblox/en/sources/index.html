What we could not find

Robux pack prices in US dollars: Roblox’s US page builds its prices with JavaScript and the App Store listing only shows Roblox Premium subscriptions, so on November 24, 2026 we had no official page we could read. That is why the Robux calculator stays in euros. The exact cost of a username change is not on the official pages we read either, so we do not show it. Pack prices in the mobile apps may also differ from the website.
