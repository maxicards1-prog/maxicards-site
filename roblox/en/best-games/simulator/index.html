The best simulator games on Roblox

In short

A Roblox simulator has you repeat a simple action (plant, fish, collect) to progress and unlock rewards. Eight simulators to know, with what makes them addictive.

Updated November 24, 2026

The principle of a simulator is that of an “incremental game”: you do an action, earn the means to improve, and the action then pays more. It is relaxing, but it can push you to spend Robux to go faster. Set a limit with your parents: see how to limit spending. Editorial selection, with no ranking.

Grow a Garden

You buy seeds, plant them on your farm, then harvest the profits once the plants have grown. The garden keeps growing while you are offline. Up to 4 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: patient players who like a quiet pace.

What people like: A simple loop: plant, wait, harvest.

Fisch

You cast your line, wait for a bite and reel in the fish by following a timing bar. You catch rare fish across an expanding world. Up to 20 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: fans of fishing and collecting.

What people like: The timing makes fishing active and collecting gives a goal.

Pet Simulator 99

You gather a big army of pets, more than 3,000 according to its page, that help you earn currency. Up to 10 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: collectors.

What people like: The huge number of pets makes you want to get them all.

Bee Swarm Simulator

You grow a swarm of bees, collect pollen, make honey and complete quests from bear characters. You explore the mountain and fight insects and monsters. Up to 6 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like exploring and quests.

What people like: A cute world with real progression.

Bubble Gum Simulator INFINITY

You blow bubble gum bubbles to grow and float up to the clouds, where you find islands and secrets, then hatch pets. Up to 12 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like colorful worlds.

What people like: Flying to the islands gives a real sense of getting somewhere.

Strongman Simulator

You pull objects along a line to gain energy, then spend it at the gym to get stronger. Removing the exit unlocks new areas. Up to 10 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like step-by-step progression.

What people like: The strength gains are visible on screen.

Mining Simulator 2

You dig mines to collect ores and materials to sell, then upgrade your backpack and tools to carry more. Up to 12 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like optimizing.

What people like: Every upgrade earns more money.

Legends Of Speed

You run, unlock trails, collect pets and take on parkour challenges across several maps to become the fastest. Up to 15 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like speed.

What people like: The action is more dynamic than in many simulators.

Frequently asked questions

What is a simulator on Roblox?

A game where you repeat a simple action (plant, fish, mine) to progress and unlock upgrades.

Which simulator should I start with?

Grow a Garden for a quiet pace, Fisch for fishing, or Bee Swarm Simulator for exploring.

Do I have to pay to progress in a simulator?

No. Many games offer Robux purchases to go faster, but they are not required.

Are simulators suitable for kids?

Often yes, but check the maturity label and limit purchases with parental controls.
