The best tycoon games on Roblox

In short

A tycoon is a management game where you grow your business. On Roblox you can run a theme park, a shop, a restaurant or a car dealership. Eight games to try.

Updated November 24, 2026

In a tycoon, you earn money to buy upgrades, which earn more money, and so on. It is a calm genre, ideal for players who like building and optimizing. The selection below is editorial, with no ranking.

Theme Park Tycoon 2

You get a plot of land to build a theme park with friends: rides, roller coasters you design yourself and plenty of scenery. Up to 6 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: creative players, from the youngest to the oldest.

What people like: The freedom to build is huge.

Retail Tycoon 2

You manage and expand a shop. Recent updates added decorations, paper products and one-way gates to guide customers. Up to 6 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like organizing and optimizing.

What people like: You watch your shop grow and run by itself.

Restaurant Tycoon 2

You create a restaurant, cafe or diner, cook for customers and visit your friends' menus. Free VIP servers are offered. See the game page.

Who it is for: fans of cooking and decorating.

What people like: You customize the place and the menu as you like.

Car Dealership Tycoon

You build a dealership, collect and drive more than 600 vehicles, customize them, race and explore an open world. Up to 15 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: car fans.

What people like: The choice of vehicles is very wide.

Lumber Tycoon 2

You cut down forests and build structures in the land of Lumberland. Up to 6 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like building and exploring.

What people like: It is more a tinkering and adventure game than a classic tycoon.

Elemental Powers Tycoon

You take a tycoon and build your empire using elemental powers. Controls include dodging and double jumping. Up to 12 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like combat and progression.

What people like: It blends management and action.

Raft Tycoon

You start with a small raft, earn money and expand your vessel by adding parts, upgrades and boats. Sharks, friend bonuses and rebirths add challenge. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like a survival-at-sea mood without too much stress.

What people like: The growing raft gives visible progression.

My Restaurant!

You create and run your own restaurant. Its page invites players to follow the game for updates. Up to 10 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like running a kitchen.

What people like: It focuses on atmosphere and service more than combat.

Tips for playing a tycoon well

  • Invest first in what pays off fastest.
  • Do not spend Robux without talking to a parent if you are a minor: see our tips to limit spending.
  • Play with others: some tycoons let you build together.

Frequently asked questions

What is a tycoon on Roblox?

A management game where you earn money to buy upgrades and grow your business.

Which tycoon should I start with?

Retail Tycoon 2 or Restaurant Tycoon 2 are easy to understand; Theme Park Tycoon 2 suits creative players.

Can tycoon games be played with others?

Many can, with a player limit depending on the game (6 for Theme Park Tycoon 2, for example).

Do I have to pay to progress in a tycoon?

No, but some games offer Robux purchases. Set a limit with your parents.
