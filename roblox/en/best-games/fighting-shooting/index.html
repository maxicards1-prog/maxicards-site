The best fighting and shooting games on Roblox

In short

Arsenal, Rivals, Blox Fruits, Phantom Forces: Roblox has plenty of fighting and shooting games. Here are eight, with their idea and recommended audience. Check the maturity label of each title.

Updated November 24, 2026

Fighting and shooting games are among the most played, but also among the most sensitive for young players. Each game shows a maturity label on its page: see our page What age is Roblox for?. This selection is editorial, with no ranking.

Arsenal

A fast arcade shooter with a wide choice of weapons, from a bazooka to a spellbook. You earn BattleBucks to customize your character, melee weapons, kill effects and skins. Up to 16 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who want instant action.

What people like: Rounds are short and the weapons are very varied.

RIVALS

A first-person shooter built around 1v1 to 5v5 duels: the first to five wins takes it. You unlock weapons and skins with keys and complete contracts. Up to 40 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: competitive players.

What people like: Fair duels and a very tense pace.

Blox Fruits

An action RPG: you train as a swordsman or fruit user, fight enemies and bosses, and sail the ocean to find secrets. Max level shown: 3000. Up to 12 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: those who like adventure and progression games.

What people like: A world to explore, with combat and a long progression.

Phantom Forces

A first-person shooter with thousands of possible weapon combinations, several game modes and very varied maps. Up to 32 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: fans of realistic shooting.

What people like: Weapon customization goes very deep.

Combat Warriors

A violent combat game that mixes melee and ranged weapons, with premium rewards and community-made items. Up to 35 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: older players: check the maturity label.

What people like: The mix of melee and ranged makes for varied fights.

BIG Paintball 2

The sequel to a paintball game. Its page says little about how it plays, but the theme is paintball shooting. Up to 10 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: those who prefer more colorful shooting.

What people like: Paintball is less serious than a war game.

Bad Business

A first-person shooter focused on skill-based combat, with deep weapon and clothing customization. Up to 30 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: experienced players.

What people like: Accuracy and weapon mastery matter a lot.

The Strongest Battlegrounds

A fighting game where you train and face other players to become the strongest. It runs on computer, console and mobile. Up to 15 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: fans of arenas and anime.

What people like: A dynamic fighting style and random rewards.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best shooting game on Roblox?

There is no official ranking. Arsenal, RIVALS and Phantom Forces are very well-known shooters, with different styles.

What age for shooting games on Roblox?

It depends on each game's maturity label, shown on its page. Some games may be limited to older ages.

Can I block violent games on Roblox?

Yes, parental controls let you limit access to games by maturity label.

Is Blox Fruits a fighting game?

It is an action RPG: you train, fight enemies and bosses, and explore the ocean, according to its page.
