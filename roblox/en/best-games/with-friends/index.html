The best Roblox games to play with friends

In short

Here are eight Roblox games that are better with other people, from a deduction game to fashion and survival. Each entry says who it suits. Always check the content label before letting a child play.

Updated November 24, 2026

This selection is an editorial choice, with no ranking or statistics: games change fast. To play with friends, you usually just add them, then join their game from your friends list. On each game's page Roblox also shows a content label: check it before you start.

Don't Get the Poop Egg (Hold the Egg) (our game)

A party game for up to 24 players: an egg goes around, holding it earns money, but the poop egg drains your wallet if you do not pass it on quickly. Silly humor and short rounds. See the game page.

Who it is for: groups of friends who like funny, fast games.

What people like: You understand the rules in a minute, and you have to watch your friends as much as the egg. It is the game of the Best Map Roblox YouTube channel, which publishes this site.

Murder Mystery 2

A deduction game: each round, one player is innocent, another is the sheriff, another is the murderer. Innocents survive, the sheriff tries to stop the murderer, and the murderer tries to eliminate everyone. Knives can be collected and traded. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like bluffing and guessing, from the age shown on its page.

What people like: Every round is different and the suspense builds fast. Its page is listed under horror, so check its label.

Dress To Impress

A fashion game: you create an outfit on a theme, then show it off on a runway, alone or with friends. The other players vote for their favorite looks. See the game page.

Who it is for: people who like creativity and style.

What people like: New items are added regularly, and the theme changes every round, so it never gets boring.

Flee the Facility

A team hide-and-seek game: survivors hack computers to open the exits while a Beast hunts them. Its page mentions matches of up to 5 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: small teams of 3 to 5 who like tension. The game is listed under horror: check the label.

What people like: You need to cooperate and keep calm. Getting caught and rescued by a friend makes for very funny moments.

Natural Disaster Survival

You must survive natural disasters that hit the map, moving constantly. Up to 30 players per match. See the game page.

Who it is for: all players, including beginners, who want a simple game with friends.

What people like: The rules fit in one sentence and every round is short.

Blade Ball

A homing ball speeds up and you must deflect it with the right timing, using abilities and blocks. The last players alive win. See the game page.

Who it is for: quick-reaction players who like reflex duels.

What people like: A ranking system, weapon skins and victory effects make you want to play again.

Work at a Pizza Place

You work together to prepare and deliver pizza orders, then spend your earnings on upgrading your house and buying furniture. See the game page.

Who it is for: friends who prefer to cooperate than to fight.

What people like: It is an older, calm game where everyone has a job: cashier, cook, delivery driver.

Jailbreak

An open world where you pick a side: rob or arrest criminals, alone or in a team. Fast cars play a big part. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who want action and chases with friends.

What people like: The open world gives total freedom: you can each play a role, then meet up.

How to choose well

Look at the maximum number of players, the content label, and read the page's comments. For younger kids, see our selection of games for younger kids. Parents will find the settings in parental controls. Like obstacle courses to do together? See the best obbies.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play with friends on Roblox?

Add them as friends, then join their game from the friends list or the game page. Many games also offer private servers.

Which Roblox game should I pick with friends?

It depends on your tastes: deduction (Murder Mystery 2), fashion (Dress To Impress), cooperation (Work at a Pizza Place) or party (Don't Get the Poop Egg).

Can several people play Roblox on the same console or device?

Each player generally needs their own account and device. Check Roblox's help center for your platform.

Are Roblox multiplayer games safe for kids?

It depends on the game, the chat and the settings. Use parental controls, check the game's content label and talk with your child.
