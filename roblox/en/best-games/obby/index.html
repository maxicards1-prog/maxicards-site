The best obbies (obstacle courses) on Roblox

In short

An obby is an obstacle course to get through. Here are eight to try, from the easiest (Mega Easy Obby) to the toughest (Tower of Hell), with what sets them apart and who they suit.

Updated November 24, 2026

An obby (short for “obstacle course”) is one of the easiest genres to understand: jump, dodge, reach the finish. Difficulty varies enormously. This selection is editorial: it relies on no numbered ranking.

Tower of Hell

A randomly generated tower with no checkpoints, which you climb racing against other players. Up to 20 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: persistent players who like competition.

What people like: Each tower is different, so it cannot be memorized, and the reward is winning.

Mega Easy Obby

An 825-level course where you earn gems and can “prestige” (only your stage progress is reset). An easy mode makes you immune to lava, and cosmetic pets are offered. See the game page.

Who it is for: beginners and younger kids.

What people like: The name says it all: progression is gentle and reassuring.

Spiral Difficulty Chart Obby

A 260-level obby that starts easy and gradually gets harder. The creator says every level is possible, even if few players finish. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who want to measure their skill.

What people like: You see your progress step by step, with a real challenge at the end.

Obby But You're On a Bike

An obby on a bike: 100 levels spread over 111 worlds, with speedrun leaderboards. Its page notes that the game may contain independent ads. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like speedrunning and new ideas.

What people like: The idea of a bike obby is a change from classic jumping.

Speed Run 4

A fast platformer with dozens of levels, each with its own music and style. You can race friends or the clock. Up to 30 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: fans of speed and platforming.

What people like: Each level has its own identity, and the game rewards mastery.

Obby Creator

A building tool to make your own obbies, showcases or cart rides, alone or with friends. You can also play other people's obbies. See the game page.

Who it is for: creative players who want an introduction to making things.

What people like: It is a stepping stone toward making games, before moving to Roblox Studio.

BARRY'S PRISON RUN! (OBBY)

A prison escape as an obby: the guard Barry is not very vigilant on duty. Designed for first-person view, but you can change perspective. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like themed obbies and a bit of humor.

What people like: A simple storyline that makes the course fun to follow.

NO JUMPING OBBY

A course where you cannot jump, inspired by difficulty-chart obbies. Its page says about 1% of players finish it. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like original rules.

What people like: The opening constraint completely changes how you play.

Tips to improve

  • Start with an easy obby to get your bearings.
  • Play on a comfortable device: a precise course is easier on a computer.
  • Take breaks: frustration is normal, but there is no need to keep forcing it.

Frequently asked questions

What is an obby on Roblox?

It is an obstacle course: you jump, dodge traps and reach the finish. The word is short for “obstacle course”.

Which obby should I pick to start?

Mega Easy Obby: as its name says, it is built for gentle progression, with an easy mode.

What is the hardest obby on Roblox?

There is no official answer. Tower of Hell and difficulty-chart obbies are known to be demanding.

Can I make my own obby?

Yes, with games like Obby Creator, then with Roblox Studio to go further.
