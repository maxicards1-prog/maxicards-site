The best roleplay games on Roblox (Brookhaven, Bloxburg…)

In short

On Roblox, a roleplay (RP) game lets you play a character in a town: live, work, drive, chat. Here are eight games to know, from Brookhaven to Bloxburg.

Updated November 24, 2026

Roleplay (RP) has no fixed goal: you invent your own story, with friends or other players. Since you meet strangers, remember to set chat and privacy: see our parental controls guide. This selection is editorial, with no ranking.

Brookhaven RP

A town life simulation: you buy houses, drive cars, take roles and explore. Up to 30 players; its page mentions a LEGO building event. See the game page.

Who it is for: players of all levels who like inventing stories.

What people like: You can play it a thousand ways: family, jobs, cars or just strolling.

Welcome to Bloxburg

A life simulation centered on building and decorating houses, customizing your character, jobs, driving and skills such as cooking. Up to 12 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: creative players who like building and decorating.

What people like: It is a very complete building and daily life game.

Berry Avenue RP

A roleplay town where you can go to high school, work in a grocery store, become a police officer or treat patients at the hospital. Up to 31 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like jobs and school life.

What people like: Roles are very varied and easy to understand.

Livetopia RP

A roleplay town where you pick roles and jobs, own houses and vehicles (boats and planes included), keep pets and look for hidden secrets. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like having lots of choice.

What people like: The choice of vehicles and pets is wide.

Royale High

A fashion and storytelling game with themed outfits, winter mini-games (snowball fight, snowboarding) and new avatar items. Up to 12 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like fashion and fairy tales.

What people like: The aesthetic world is very polished.

Deermont RP

A roleplay town where you choose your path: start a family, rob the bank, join the police, go shopping or discover secrets. Up to 30 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like varied scenarios.

What people like: Lots of possible paths on the same map.

Dreamville RP

A town roleplay to socialize, own houses and vehicles and play the role of your choice, with customizable cats and dogs. Up to 25 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: animal lovers.

What people like: The customizable animals give it a warm feel.

Prison Life

An escape roleplay: you try to break out of prison as an inmate or defend the prison as a guard. Up to 24 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like opposing roles.

What people like: The inmates-versus-guards duo gives each round a clear goal. Check the game's maturity label.

Playing RP well

  • Respect the server's rules and other players.
  • Never give out personal information (address, school, number).
  • Want clothes for your avatar? Read our guide to making Roblox clothes.

Frequently asked questions

What is an RP game on Roblox?

RP means “roleplay”: you play a character in a town, with no imposed goal.

What is the best roleplay game on Roblox?

There is no official ranking. Brookhaven RP and Welcome to Bloxburg are two very well-known games, with different styles.

Can I play RP with friends?

Yes, by joining the same server. Some games also offer private servers.

Are RP games suitable for kids?

It depends on the game, the chat and the settings. Use parental controls and check the maturity label.
