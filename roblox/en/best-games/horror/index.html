The best horror games on Roblox

In short

Doors, Piggy, The Mimic, Apeirophobia: horror is everywhere on Roblox. Each game shows a maturity label that decides who can play; parents, check it before letting a child jump in.

Updated November 24, 2026

Read this first: the maturity label

As of Nov 23, 2026, Roblox rates each game with a label: Minimal and Mild (games open to Roblox Kids, ages 5 to 8, and Roblox Select, ages 9 to 15), Moderate (Select and users 16 and up), “Content May Vary” (published without the full questionnaire, available from age 16 in most regions, closed to under-9s) and Restricted (only for verified users 18 and up). A horror game can therefore be limited to certain ages or verified accounts, and the label can change. Always look at it on the game's page. Details are in our guide What age is Roblox for? and in parental controls.

The entries below describe the idea of each game, not how scary it is: that varies with how sensitive you are. The links lead to the official roblox.com pages.

DOORS

You move from room to room opening doors, with up to 50 players per server. The developers advise learning from each death rather than looking for a guide. See the game page.

Who it is for: players comfortable with jump scares, alone or in a group.

What people like: Every door holds a surprise, and you replay to get further.

Piggy

You try to escape the monster Piggy and uncover the mysteries around it, with items, traps and the ability to crouch. Up to 6 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: small teams who like escape and cooperation.

What people like: You cooperate to get out, which makes the fear easier to bear.

The Mimic

Four horror stories inspired by Japanese history and urban legends, where you play different characters in worlds that echo reality. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like dark stories and atmosphere.

What people like: A real story, split into chapters, rather than just jump scares.

Apeirophobia

You explore endless rooms in the style of the “Backrooms”, solving puzzles, hiding from entities and documenting what you find. Up to 40 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: fans of eerie atmosphere and exploration.

What people like: The atmosphere is carefully crafted and you can explore alone or with others.

Pressure

An exploration game where you play “expendable” characters nobody expects back. Its page says the game is in alpha and may contain bugs. See the game page.

Who it is for: players willing to try a game still under construction.

What people like: Frequent updates and an original atmosphere, with dark humor.

Rainbow Friends

A school trip goes wrong: the Rainbow Friends decide to play with you. You must flee and survive. Up to 50 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: fans of colorful but unsettling characters.

What people like: The look recalls a kids' game, which explains its success, but the mood stays tense.

99 Nights in the Forest

You build a camp with friends while something unseen watches you. Up to 25 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: groups of friends who like survival and cooperation.

What people like: A mix of survival, building and tension.

Evade

A parkour game: you need to move fast to escape the “Nextbots”, pursuers that rush at you. Up to 15 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: fast, nervy players who like chases.

What people like: The fear comes from speed more than scenery, and rounds are short.

Tips for parents

  • Look at the maturity label on each game's page, not just the title.
  • Play a few minutes with your child to judge the mood.
  • Set chat and limits in parental controls.

For gentler games, see games for younger kids. With friends, also discover games to play together.

Frequently asked questions

Are there horror games on Roblox?

Yes. Horror games are plentiful on Roblox, with maturity labels that limit access by age.

What age for horror games on Roblox?

It depends on each game's label. A “Content May Vary” game is closed to under-9s; a “Restricted” game is only for verified users 18 and up.

How do I block horror games on Roblox?

Parental controls let you limit access to games by maturity label. See our parental controls guide.

Which Roblox horror game should I start with?

Pick a team game like Piggy, where you can cooperate, before trying more immersive ones.
