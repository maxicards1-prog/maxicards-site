Which Roblox games are good for kids?

In short

For kids, look at the maturity label first: Roblox says Minimal and Mild games are open to Roblox Kids (ages 5 to 8) and Select (ages 9 to 15). Here are seven games to look at with your child.

Updated November 24, 2026

Check the label before playing

As of Nov 23, 2026, according to Roblox's help center, games labeled Minimal or Mild are eligible for Roblox Kids (ages 5 to 8) and Roblox Select (ages 9 to 15). “Content May Vary” games are not available to under-9s. A series of games can have a different label from one title to another, and it can change: check it on the game's page rather than relying on our list. We do not state the label of each title here. See also what age Roblox is for and parental controls.

The games below are described from their pages. They are ideas to try with a parent, not a guarantee of suitability.

Adopt Me!

You raise and trade pets, collect rare ones, decorate your house and roleplay with friends. Up to 35 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: kids who like animals and decorating.

What people like: You care for a pet as in a care game, with no combat.

MeepCity

A social game where you chat, play mini-games, earn coins and decorate your house. Up to 40 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: players who like social play and decorating.

What people like: Chat is a real social space: turn on the safety settings.

Build A Boat For Treasure

You build a boat then set off on an adventure. Belonging to a group gives perks. Up to 7 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: kids who like tinkering and sharing.

What people like: You invent your boat and laugh at the shipwrecks.

Epic Minigames

141 mini-games to earn coins and buy gear and pets, or a board game, Epic Party, to collect crystals. Up to 12 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: those who like varied mini-games.

What people like: Rounds are short and varied.

Wacky Wizards

You mix ingredients to brew potions with varied effects, then drink them to see what they do to your character. Up to 28 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: curious, mischievous kids.

What people like: The surprising, funny effects make you want to try everything.

Super Golf!

You play varied golf courses, unlock rewards, customize your ball and build your own courses. Up to 20 players. See the game page.

Who it is for: those who like skill games without violence.

What people like: You can play as a family or with friends.

Tower Defense Simulator

A tower defense game: you place defenses to stop waves of enemies, alone or with other players. We did not note details from its page: read it before playing. See the game page.

Who it is for: kids who like strategy.

What people like: Thinking matters more than fast action. Check the game's maturity label.

How to guide a child

  • Create an age-appropriate account and link it to a parent account.
  • Limit chat and spending: see how to limit spending.
  • Play a few minutes with them before leaving them alone.

For gentle course ideas, see obbies, or browse all the best games.

Frequently asked questions

What age is Roblox for?

Roblox applies age rules based on game labels: Roblox Kids (ages 5 to 8) and Select (ages 9 to 15) are open to Minimal and Mild games.

Which Roblox games for a 6-year-old?

Look for Minimal or Mild games, open to Roblox Kids, and play with them first. Check the label on each game's page.

How can I tell if a Roblox game is suitable?

Look at the maturity label on the game's page, read the comments and test it with your child.

How do I limit a child's chat on Roblox?

With parental controls: a linked parent account and chat settings. See our dedicated guide.
