Robux to Euro Calculator

Enter Robux or a budget in euros.

Prices checked on November 23, 2026. Sources: Roblox's Robux packs page (France). They can change: check them on Roblox before you act.

  • Runs in your browser
  • Free
  • No sign-up
  • No data sent

Enter a number of Robux or a budget in euros: the tool finds the cheapest mix of official packs, based on the prices on roblox.com.

Everything is calculated in your browser: nothing is sent, nothing is stored, and there is no account or sign-up.

How to use it

  1. Pick a direction: “Robux to euros” to see what Robux cost, “Euros to Robux” to see what a budget buys.
  2. Type the amount (for example 1,000 Robux, or 10 euros).
  3. Pick the packs: with the roblox.com bonus, or with the base amount (crossed out on Roblox's page).
  4. Read the price, the suggested mix of packs and the cost of 100 Robux.

Why this calculator works in euros

Roblox shows its prices in the currency of your country. The only prices we could read on an official Roblox page are the ones on Roblox's page for France, in euros, on November 23, 2026. We could not read the US dollar prices on an official page, so we do not show any: a converted guess could be wrong. If you pay in dollars, pounds or another currency, use this tool to compare packs with each other, and always check the amount shown by Roblox before you pay.

Website or app price?

Roblox promotes purchases made on its website, which give more Robux for the same price. App stores (Apple, Google) take a commission, so Roblox may give the base amount there, or even a different price. The page we read does not give the exact details for each store: always check the amount and the price shown when you pay. A gift card is redeemed on Roblox; see our guide to Robux prices.

What are Robux for?

Robux are Roblox's currency: outfits and accessories for your avatar, game passes, upgrades inside experiences. If you are under 18, talk to a parent before you pay: it is the family's money. Be wary of any website or video that promises Robux without paying: it is a scam, see the guide to “no-payment Robux” scams.

Frequently asked questions

How much is 1,000 Robux in euros?

The 1,000 Robux pack was listed at €11.99 on November 23, 2026 on Roblox's page for France. Depending on the base amount, you may need a slightly bigger pack: the tool works out the cheapest mix.

How much is 1,000 Robux in US dollars?

We do not know for sure: we could not read the US dollar prices on an official Roblox page, so we do not give a figure. Check the price shown on roblox.com or in the app in your country.

Why does the tool sometimes suggest more Robux than I asked for?

Robux are only sold in whole packs. To get at least the amount you want, you sometimes need a slightly bigger pack or an extra one: the extra Robux stay in your account.

Can I buy Robux for less?

The price per Robux drops as packs get bigger. Beyond that, only Roblox's official offers and gift cards sold by authorized retailers are safe. Avoid any site promising Robux without paying or at slashed prices.

Does this tool sell Robux?

No. It only calculates, in your browser, without sending anything. Purchases happen only on Roblox.
