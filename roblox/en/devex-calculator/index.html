DevEx lets creators exchange the Robux they earned for real money. Enter your Robux: the tool gives the amount in US dollars at the official rate.

Everything is calculated in your browser: nothing is sent, nothing is stored, and there is no account or sign-up.

How to use it

  1. Type the number of earned Robux you want to cash out (at least 30,000).
  2. Keep the official rate ($0.0038 per Robux on November 23, 2026) or change it if Roblox updates it.
  3. Read the amount in US dollars and the value of 1,000 earned Robux.
  4. Check whether the cash-out minimum is reached.

What DevEx is

The Developer Exchange (DevEx) is the Roblox program that lets creators convert earned Robux into real money. The standard rate is $0.0038 per earned Robux. In practice, 30,000 earned Robux are worth $114. Robux earned before September 5, 2025 are paid at the old rate of $0.0035, and they are cashed out first. A higher rate ($0.0054) exists for some Robux earned from US players aged 18 and over; the tool does not apply it automatically, but you can type it in the rate field.

The official requirements

  • Be at least 13 years old and have at least 30,000 earned Robux in the account.
  • Have a verified email address and an account on the DevEx portal.
  • Fill in the required tax form (W-9 for US persons, W-8 for everyone else).
  • Follow Roblox's Terms of Use and Community Standards.
  • Only one completed cash-out request per month.

Roblox estimates about 10 business days for a first request and 5 for the following ones. The amount calculated here is gross: it does not include any tax you may owe, nor bank or currency fees. If you are a minor, a parent's agreement and help are essential. Our DevEx guide explains the steps.

Earned Robux and bought Robux

Only earned Robux count for DevEx: the ones you receive when players buy your passes or products. Robux you buy for yourself do not count. To see what you get from one specific sale, use the Roblox tax calculator. If you are paid in euros, the Robux to euro calculator shows Roblox's euro pack prices.

Frequently asked questions

How much is 100,000 Robux in USD with DevEx?

At the standard rate of $0.0038 per Robux, 100,000 earned Robux make $380, before taxes and fees. Enter your own numbers in the tool.

What is the minimum to cash out?

30,000 earned Robux, which is $114 at the standard rate. You also need to meet the program's other requirements.

How old do you have to be to use DevEx?

At least 13, according to Roblox's official page. You also need a verified email address, a DevEx portal account and a tax form.

Why is my payout different from the result?

The calculation stops at the gross amount. Your bank may charge fees or convert the currency, and taxes may apply depending on where you live.

Is the rate fixed?

No. Roblox has already changed it ($0.0035 before September 5, 2025, $0.0038 after). The rate field can be edited to follow any change.
