Privacy policy

What you type never leaves your device. Here are the details.

Last updated: November 24, 2026.

Data controller

MAXICARDS, SASU, 60 rue François Ier, 75008 Paris, France. Contact: contact@maxicards.net.

What you type stays on your device

The tools (Robux prices, DevEx, sales earnings, usernames) calculate in your browser. The numbers and usernames you type are not sent, stored or analyzed by MAXICARDS or by anyone else.

What the site does not collect

The site has no accounts, no forms and no audience measurement of its own (apart from advertising, see below), and it saves nothing in your browser's storage.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). To serve the pages, GitHub processes technical data such as the IP address and browser type, possibly outside the European Union. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Google AdSense advertising

This site shows ads provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and identifiers to serve ads, limit how often you see them, measure their results and, with your consent, personalize ads based on your visits to this site and other sites. Many of our readers are minors: personalized ads are not meant for people under 18, and Google's rules apply.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Union and the United Kingdom), a Google consent message asks for your choice before any advertising cookie. You can change it at any time from the consent link shown on the site.
  • Learn more: how Google uses cookies in advertising.
  • To adjust or turn off ad personalization: Google's ad settings.

Links to YouTube and Roblox

Links to our YouTube channel and to Roblox are plain links: no content from those sites is loaded until you click. Their own privacy policies then apply.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict your data, and object to its processing, by writing to contact@maxicards.net. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), or to the authority of the country where you live.
